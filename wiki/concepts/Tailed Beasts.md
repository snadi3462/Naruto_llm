# Tailed Beasts

The nine tailed beasts (bijū): immensely powerful chakra entities, each sealed within a
human jinchūriki, whose shared origin and fate drive most of the *Naruto* series' large-
scale plot. [[Kurama]] (Nine-Tails, access restricted) is the most story-central member;
this page covers the shared lore and history common to all nine.

## Shared origin

All nine were created by Hagoromo Ōtsutsuki (the Sage of Six Paths), who split the
chakra of the ancient Ten-Tails into nine separate beings before his death, foretelling
that they would one day be reunited by someone who could show them "true power." Each
beast has powerful regenerative/healing chakra it can transfer to others, and is
functionally immortal — killed beasts eventually revive in a younger form rather than
dying permanently. Generations later, Hashirama Senju (access restricted) captured all
nine and distributed eight of them among the hidden villages as gifts of peace at a Five
Kage Summit, keeping Kurama for Konoha; this is the origin of each village's jinchūriki
tradition.

## Shared history through the Fourth Shinobi World War

Every tailed beast except Kurama and Shukaku is hunted down and extracted by
[[Akatsuki]] over the course of Part II, each extraction killing that beast's jinchūriki.
The extracted beasts are stored in the Demonic Statue of the Outer Path until [[Obito
Uchiha]] (as "Tobi") forces the reincarnated jinchūriki of six of them to fully
transform and fight the Allied Shinobi Forces during the war's climax. [[Naruto
Uzumaki]] (access restricted) — already trusted by Kurama — talks each beast down and
frees it from Obito's chakra receivers, winning their collective trust and eventually
their combined power against [[Madara Uchiha]] and then [[Kaguya Ōtsutsuki]]. After
Kaguya's defeat, all nine beasts are freed for the first time in generations and choose
where to live out their lives.

## New Era

Split loyalties define the tailed beasts' current-era behavior: some (Gyūki, Shukaku,
Isobu, Son Gokū) trust Naruto enough to answer a telepathic summons when Urashiki
Ōtsutsuki begins hunting their chakra during the One-Tail Escort Arc; others (Matatabi,
Kokuō, Chōmei) refuse any human contact at all out of lingering fear of recapture,
despite trusting Naruto personally. All of them sense the moment
Kurama's chakra vanishes during the Kawaki Arc, when Kurama sacrifices itself in Baryon
Mode against Isshiki Ōtsutsuki (access restricted) — the most current shared status this
ingest covers.
