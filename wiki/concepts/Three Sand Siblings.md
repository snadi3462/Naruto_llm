# Three Sand Siblings

Sunagakure's three children of the Fourth Kazekage: [[Gaara]], [[Temari]], and
[[Kankurō]]. Sunagakure's counterpart to Konoha's genin teams, though they operate as
siblings and bodyguards rather than a formally assigned three-person cell.

## Roster

- **[[Gaara]]** (youngest) — former jinchūriki of Shukaku, later Suna's Fifth Kazekage.
- **[[Temari]]** (eldest) — ambassador and battle strategist; later marries into
  Konoha's Nara clan.
- **[[Kankurō]]** (middle) — puppet master; Gaara's closest bodyguard and confidant.

## Shared history

All three were raised under the shadow of their father's decision to make Gaara a
weapon rather than a son, which shaped their relationships with each other as much as
his own personality. Temari and Kankurō spent Gaara's childhood alternately controlling
and fearing him; both come to fully trust and protect him only after his reform,
following his defeat by [[Naruto Uzumaki]] (access restricted). From that point the
three function as a genuinely close family unit — Temari and Kankurō both risk their
lives during the Kazekage Rescue Mission to save Gaara from [[Akatsuki]], and Kankurō in
particular remains Gaara's bodyguard at Kage Summits for years into his reign.

The siblings' individual paths diverge over time: Gaara becomes Kazekage and stays in
Suna; Temari marries into Konoha's Nara clan and raises her family there, visiting Suna
regularly; Kankurō stays in Suna as Gaara's closest protector and inherits [[Sasori]]'s
puppets after killing him in the Fourth Shinobi World War. All three remain recurring
figures in the *New Era*'s Ōtsutsuki-era arcs, fighting together against Urashiki
Ōtsutsuki in the One-Tail Escort Arc.
