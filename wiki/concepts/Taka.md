# Taka

[[Sasuke Uchiha]] (access restricted)'s team of three, originally called "Hebi": each
member recruited from Orochimaru's captivity or experiments, united by loyalty to Sasuke
rather than any formal village assignment.

## Roster

- **[[Suigetsu Hōzuki]]** — Kirigakure swordsman, obsessed with collecting the Seven
  Ninja Swordsmen of the Mist's blades.
- **[[Karin]]** — Uzumaki-clan sensor and healer, infatuated with Sasuke.
- **[[Jūgo]]** — gentle recluse whose clan's power causes uncontrollable rages; the
  team's heaviest hitter.

## History

Formed as "Hebi" immediately after Sasuke's defeat of Orochimaru, tasked first with
hunting down [[Itachi Uchiha]] (access restricted). After Itachi's death and Sasuke's
recruitment by [[Obito Uchiha]], the team is renamed "Taka" and allies with [[Akatsuki]],
tasked with capturing Killer B's Eight-Tails — a mission that ends in defeat and
Sasuke's near-death, saved by Jūgo's healing. All three fight alongside Sasuke through
the Fourth Shinobi World War. Karin alone has confirmed *New Era* content, remaining
close to Sasuke's family and helping deliver [[Sarada Uchiha]]; Suigetsu and Jūgo's
post-war status isn't covered by the sources ingested so far.
