# Obito Uchiha

Childhood teammate of [[Kakashi Hatake]] and [[Rin Nohara]] on Team Minato, under
[[Minato Namikaze]].

## Background

Grew up parentless, driven by a need to be acknowledged — hence his own Hokage dream, a
detail that mirrors a similarly-orphaned peer's parallel origin (see [[Naruto Uzumaki]] —
access restricted). A late bloomer at the Academy, chronically late to things because he
stopped to help villagers along the way, and nursed an unconfessed crush on Rin Nohara.

Believed teamwork and loyalty to comrades matter more than rigidly following mission
orders — a philosophy at odds with a young teammate's (see [[Kakashi Hatake]] — access
restricted). During the Third Shinobi World War's Kannabi Bridge mission, that teammate,
then in command, chose to abandon a captured Rin to complete the mission; Obito objected and
left alone to save her, telling him he was "worse than trash" for abandoning a friend — **the
origin of the "trash/comrades" line**, said by Obito to that teammate rather than the
reverse. Moved by this, the teammate saved Obito from an enemy ninja, losing his own left
eye — the trigger for Obito's Sharingan awakening. That teammate later adopts Obito's line as
his own defining belief and teaches it to his own students, including [[Team 7]] (access
restricted).

During the cave-in that follows, Obito is fatally crushed while shoving that teammate clear of
a falling boulder. While trapped, he has Rin transplant his Sharingan into the teammate's
empty socket, framed as an apology for forgetting to get him a promotion gift, and apparently
dies — his last thought is regret at never telling Rin he loved her.

## Rescue and manipulation by Madara

Per [[Madara Uchiha (source)]], Obito's "death" is not the end of the story: [[Madara Uchiha]] finds him badly injured, rebuilds his body with transplanted cells (see
[[Hashirama Senju]] — access restricted) — his right side reconstructed from White Zetsu
matter plus Hashirama-cell grafts, granting Wood Release and rapid regeneration — and
deliberately deepens Obito's despair by orchestrating [[Rin Nohara]]'s death (see
[[Kakashi Hatake]] — access restricted). A broken Obito offers his services to Madara, who
spends his final years grooming him as an heir — teaching him of the Sage of Six Paths, the
Ten-Tails, and the Eye of the Moon Plan, and, with his dying breath, handing Obito his own
name and identity as "Madara Uchiha." [[Zetsu (source)]] adds that White Zetsu personally
cared for and befriended Obito during this rehabilitation period, becoming a genuine (if
strange) friendship.

Obito later took a Rinnegan from [[Nagato]]'s corpse and implanted it himself, granting Outer
Path techniques (Demonic Statue summoning, chakra chains, Preta Path absorption, and the
Samsara of Heavenly Life revival technique).

## Later revelation

Obito is later revealed to have survived, becoming the masked man ("Tobi") behind much of
[[Akatsuki]]'s scheming and the mastermind who orchestrated a tailed beast's attack on Konoha
the night of a pivotal birth (see [[Naruto Uzumaki]] — access restricted) — carrying out the
plan Madara entrusted to him, unaware (as Madara himself was) that Black Zetsu had been
manipulating the entire chain toward reviving Kaguya Ōtsutsuki. He later sealed the Ten-Tails
into himself as its jinchūriki; initially struggled for control before mastering it, becoming
powerful enough to fight three reincarnated Hokage simultaneously, with senjutsu remaining his
one clear weakness.

He eventually returns to his original values, fighting alongside [[Team 7]] (access
restricted) against Kaguya Ōtsutsuki in the Fourth Shinobi World War. When Kaguya pins two
of his old teammate's own students and attacks with All-Killing Ash Bones (see
[[Naruto Uzumaki]], [[Sasuke Uchiha]] — access restricted), Obito and that same teammate —
moved by a shared memory of their old friendship — shield the two together (see
[[Kakashi Hatake]] — access restricted): Obito uses Kamui to teleport away the strike aimed
at his teammate, deliberately letting the one aimed at himself land instead, an explicit echo
of the original Kannabi Bridge boulder sacrifice. He warns his teammate "I won't be around to
save you a third time" and entrusts the future to one of the two students he shielded (see
[[Naruto Uzumaki]] — access restricted), dying smiling and fully reconciled. His spirit
briefly reunites with Rin in the afterlife's limbo before returning once more via Kamui to
lend his teammate's power a final boost against Kaguya.

The Uchiha clan wanted Obito's Sharingan-bearing eye back after his presumed Kannabi Bridge
death; clan head Fugaku Uchiha let his old teammate keep it in his honor — part of the basis
for that teammate's "Copy Ninja" nickname (see [[Kakashi Hatake]] — access restricted).
