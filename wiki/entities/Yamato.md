# Yamato

Wood Release-wielding Anbu (codename "Tenzō") who leads [[Team 7]] (access restricted) as a
temporary replacement captain (see [[Kakashi Hatake]] — access restricted).

## Background

As an infant, Yamato was abducted by [[Orochimaru]] and made one of sixty children injected
with an early Hokage's DNA in an attempt to replicate his Wood Release (see
[[Hashirama Senju]] — access restricted). Every other subject died; Yamato alone survived,
emerging with an unquantified Wood Release and the ability to suppress tailed beasts' chakra.
Having lost all memory of his identity, he was found and raised by Danzō Shimura, recruited
into Root under the codename Kinoe. Sent to assassinate the Third Hokage, he was defeated but
spared by a young Anbu member (see [[Kakashi Hatake]] — access restricted) — the beginning of
a mentor-student relationship that eventually pulls Yamato away from Root entirely. He takes
the name "Tenzō," later "Yamato," and serves in that Anbu unit alongside a young fellow agent
(see [[Itachi Uchiha]] — access restricted).

## Personality

Discreet, cautious, and stoic even under pressure — a contrast to his captain's more relaxed
leadership style (see [[Kakashi Hatake]] — access restricted); Yamato admits he's willing to
"rule by fear" if his team misbehaves, usually via a deliberately terrifying "ghoul-eyes"
expression that unsettles his teammates (see [[Naruto Uzumaki]] — access restricted).
Genuinely playful underneath the severity, and has a real hobby/interest in architecture that
dovetails with his Wood Release — he builds instant wooden houses for the team to camp in and
later helps rebuild Konoha after Pain's attack.

## Abilities

Possesses an early Hokage's Wood Release kekkei genkai (unrefined compared to the original —
see [[Hashirama Senju]], access restricted), letting him grow trees and structures for
offense, defense, tracking, and shelter, plus a regeneration ability from the same genetic
source. Most strategically important: using the First Hokage's Necklace as an amplifier,
Yamato can suppress a jinchūriki's tailed-beast chakra — the specific reason he's assigned to
lead Team 7 in his captain's absence (see [[Tsunade]], [[Kakashi Hatake]] — access
restricted). This suppression ability is lost once the necklace is destroyed partway through
Part II.

## Key arcs

- **Tenchi Bridge Reconnaissance Mission**: leads the reformed Team 7 (access restricted) to
  meet a spy in [[Orochimaru]]'s ranks; restrains and later earns the trust of Sai after
  uncovering his true assassination mission against a teammate (see [[Sasuke Uchiha]] —
  access restricted).
- **Fourth Shinobi World War**: captured and drained by Kabuto/Obito to strengthen the White
  Zetsu Army using his cells (see [[Hashirama Senju]] — access restricted); freed when
  Madara's Infinite Tsukuyomi is broken.
- **New Era**: assigned to covertly surveil the pardoned [[Orochimaru]], eventually becoming
  famous as the only Konoha shinobi who can approach him without fear.

## Status

The ingested source's coverage ends in the *New Era* (Mitsuki's Disappearance Arc); unlike
[[Sai]], it does not follow Yamato into the *New Era: Part II* crisis chapters, so his status
during that crisis is unconfirmed (see [[Naruto Uzumaki]] — access restricted) — flagged as
an open thread.
