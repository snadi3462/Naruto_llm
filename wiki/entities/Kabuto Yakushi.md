# Kabuto Yakushi

[[Orochimaru]]'s spy-turned-right-hand-man, whose entire arc is a search for identity
after childhood amnesia erased his own past.

## Background and personality

Found as an amnesiac child and raised as a Root spy under [[Danzō Shimura]], Kabuto's
defining trait is an ongoing identity crisis — every persona he's had was constructed
for someone else's purposes, first Root's, then Orochimaru's. After Orochimaru's death
he sets out to surpass him entirely, convinced he must become "perfect" to finally
belong to no one but himself.

## Abilities

Grafts himself with Orochimaru's remains and the genetic traits of numerous test
subjects (including Suigetsu Hōzuki, Karin, and Jūgo), becoming a nearly unkillable,
shapeshifting threat with an unmatched mastery of medical ninjutsu. Claims to have
perfected the Impure World Reincarnation technique, reincarnating dozens of powerful
shinobi at once with their full living strength intact.

## Key arcs and status

Orchestrates much of the Fourth Shinobi World War's chaos as the mastermind behind mass
reincarnation, including [[Madara Uchiha]]'s revival. Defeated and freed from his
identity crisis by [[Itachi Uchiha]] (access restricted)'s Izanami, discovering his true
self was the orphan Nonō Yakushi once trusted; returns to run the Konoha orphanage that
raised him after the war.
