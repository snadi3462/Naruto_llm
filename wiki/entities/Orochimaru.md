# Orochimaru

One of Konoha's legendary Sannin (see [[Tsunade]], [[Jiraiya]] — access restricted, for the
other two). Former missing-nin and human experimenter; [[Sasuke Uchiha]]'s onetime mentor
(access restricted); later a semi-reformed researcher and father (via cloning) to Mitsuki.

## Background

Orphaned young, Orochimaru trained under the Third Hokage (see [[Hiruzen Sarutobi]] — access
restricted) alongside his two future fellow Sannin — his talent, insight, and determination
marked him as a once-in-a-generation prodigy, more so than either teammate. The three earned
the "Sannin" title together after surviving an encounter with Hanzō during the Second Shinobi
World War. Driven by a life-ambition to learn every secret of the shinobi world and achieve
immortality (a fixation attributed to his parents' early deaths), Orochimaru was eventually
caught performing unethical human experimentation — including the Hashirama-DNA experiments
on kidnapped children that produced [[Yamato]] — and defected rather than face justice (see
[[Hiruzen Sarutobi]] — access restricted, for that side of the story).

## Personality

Widely described as "twisted": charismatic enough to gather devoted followers he'll
nonetheless send on suicide missions without real attachment, treacherous by nature
(assassinating and impersonating the Fourth Kazekage), and possessed of a near-messianic god
complex about his own worth. A perfectionist who values genuine development in his subjects
and experiments over blind loyalty — genuinely impressed rather than threatened by his
former students' growth even when it works against him (see [[Sasuke Uchiha]],
[[Naruto Uzumaki]] — access restricted). His
post-war characterization softens considerably: he comes to value the "natural development"
of people (particularly his synthetic son, Mitsuki) over control and manipulation, though he
still uses deception when it serves that goal.

## Key arcs

- **Chūnin Exams / Konoha Crush**: brands [[Sasuke Uchiha]] with the Cursed Seal of Heaven
  (access restricted) as part of a long-term plan to take his body; murders the Fourth
  Kazekage and impersonates him to orchestrate an invasion of Konoha, killing the Third
  Hokage in the process (see [[Hiruzen Sarutobi]] — access restricted).
- **Search for Tsunade**: attempts to force a former teammate to heal his arms in exchange
  for reviving her dead brother and lover; she instead nearly kills him, precipitating a major
  turn in her own story (see [[Tsunade]] — access restricted).
- Was an early member of [[Akatsuki]] before the main story begins, defecting (and taking his
  ring, why he's never officially replaced) before another member joined (see
  [[Itachi Uchiha]] — access restricted) — corroborated by [[Akatsuki (source)]].
- **Sasuke Recovery Mission onward**: continues training [[Sasuke Uchiha]] as his intended
  next body, until his student turns the body-transfer technique against him and absorbs him
  during the Itachi pursuit (access restricted) — see [[Sasuke Uchiha (source)]] for the same
  events from Sasuke's side. Later revived and fights alongside Konoha during the Fourth
  Shinobi World War, citing curiosity about his former student's "different path."
- **New Era**: pardoned under 24-hour Konoha surveillance; reestablishes Otogakure as a
  legitimate village and creates synthetic humans (Log, Mitsuki) through cloning rather than
  kidnapping. Sends Mitsuki to live in Konoha, forming a genuine (if unconventional)
  parental bond with him.

## Status

The ingested source's coverage ends in the *New Era* (Kara Actuation Arc) and does not reach
the *New Era: Part II* crisis chapters covered in [[Sai (source)]] — Orochimaru's status
during that crisis is unconfirmed, flagged as an open thread (see [[Naruto Uzumaki]] — access
restricted).
