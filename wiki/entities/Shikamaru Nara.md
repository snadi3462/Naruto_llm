# Shikamaru Nara

[[Team 10]] member, Naruto's Hokage advisor — and, as of the *New Era: Part II* crisis, Konoha's
provisional Eighth Hokage.

## Background

Only child of Shikaku and Yoshino Nara. Met [[Chōji Akimichi]] as a child after defending him
from kids who excluded him as "too stupid and bad," inviting him to cloud-watch — the origin
of a friendship that predates even the Academy. Naturally lazy, slept through Academy
lessons, and skipped test questions out of laziness rather than inability, masking a genius
IQ his poor grades obscured until Asuma discovered it by disguising intelligence tests as
games. Catchphrase "How troublesome" precedes any attempt to avoid conflict or commitment; he
calls himself "the number one coward," but a strong moral compass repeatedly forces him into
leadership he doesn't want.

## Team 10 and the Chūnin Exams

Placed on [[Team 10]] with Chōji and [[Ino Yamanaka]] under [[Asuma Sarutobi]]. In the Chūnin
Exams, passed the written test via Ino's Mind Body Switch, used Formation Ino–Shika–Chō in
the Forest of Death, won his preliminary match via shadow manipulation, and then forfeited a
winning semifinal against [[Temari]] — judging it the smarter tactical choice. He was the
only genin from his class promoted to chūnin, based on performance and decision-making rather
than a win.

## Asuma's death and revenge

During the Akatsuki Suppression Mission, Hidan bound himself to Asuma via a blood ritual, so
damage to Hidan also struck Asuma; Shikamaru had objected to Asuma's battle plan as too
risky, and Asuma died of the resulting wounds — his dying words entrusted Shikamaru with "the
king" from their shōgi metaphor (the next generation). Shikamaru then planned and led the
revenge mission with Chōji, Ino, and Kakashi: separating Hidan from Kakuzu, luring Hidan into
Nara clan forest, and detonating a ring of explosive tags with one of Asuma's own cigarettes
to kill him. He later took on mentoring Asuma and Kurenai's daughter Mirai as her sensei.

## War arc and marriage to Temari

Served as deputy/field commander of the Fourth Division in the Fourth Shinobi World War;
after his father Shikaku's death at Allied HQ, became the Alliance's chief strategist,
coordinating the mass Ino–Shika–Chō restraint of the Ten-Tails.

His relationship with [[Temari]] sparked at their Chūnin Exams match and deepened across
missions; in *Shikamaru Hiden*, her arrival breaks a genjutsu-induced manipulation over him,
after which they date and eventually marry, with Temari relocating to Konoha as a member of
the Nara clan. Their son is Shikadai. Temari is characteristically the stricter, more
assertive half of the marriage — a dynamic compared to Shikamaru's own parents.

## Post-war career

Chief coordinator of the Shinobi Union after the war, then assistant to Sixth Hokage
[[Kakashi Hatake]], then chief aide to Seventh Hokage [[Naruto Uzumaki]] — handling
paperwork, intelligence, and kage summits, and running the village whenever Naruto is away.

## Current crisis (New Era: Part II) — provisional Eighth Hokage

After Naruto's apparent death/disappearance (sealed away via Kawaki's Daikokuten), Shikamaru
was appointed **Eighth Hokage on an explicitly provisional, three-year basis** — a more
specific status than earlier sources' "acting/interim Hokage" framing. As Hokage he issued a
formal **execution order against Boruto** (believed to have killed Naruto) and **re-listed
Sasuke Uchiha as a wanted criminal**, decisions Sarada Uchiha repeatedly and unsuccessfully
demanded he rescind.

**Why he believes Boruto is guilty**: everyone's memories, his own included, were altered by
an antagonist technique — **Ada's Omnipotence** — which swapped Boruto's and Kawaki's
histories in the village's collective memory and suppressed lingering doubts over time. His
belief was genuine, not political theater.

**The secret rescue operation**: his resolve cracked when Sai's surveillance revealed that
Amado had independently concluded memories had been altered. Combined with pressure from
Konoha elders Koharu and Homura to permanently claim the Hokage title, this pushed Shikamaru
to secretly support Boruto while publicly maintaining the hunt for him. He works through a
covert network: [[Ino Yamanaka]] (off-record communications, later refuses further protocol
breaches as too risky), Mitsuki (secretly given the code to free Boruto from his cuffs,
staged to look like an escape), Inojin Yamanaka (mind-transmission contact with the
mysterious Koji Kashin, infiltrates elder Kobu's body during an interrogation crisis),
[[Sai]] (learns of and reluctantly backs the plan once confronted), and Konohamaru Sarutobi
(told the truth and asked to help both Shikamaru and Boruto).

**Ongoing political threat**: the Council of Elders, backed by the Fire Daimyō's advisor
Kobu, opened a formal inquiry into Shikamaru's "validity as village leader," and the
investigation has traced Boruto's escape from custody back to Shikamaru himself — Kobu vows
to expose it. Unresolved as of the Mamushi Arc, the most current chapter reached by any
source in this wiki. See [[Team 7]]'s "Current crisis" section for how this connects to
Naruto, Sasuke, and Sakura's own threads.
