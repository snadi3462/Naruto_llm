# Matatabi

The Two-Tails, one of the nine [[Tailed Beasts]] — sealed within Yugito Nii of
Kumogakure.

## Background and personality

Unusually formal and polite compared to most tailed beasts, addressing even
[[Naruto Uzumaki]] (access restricted) with honorifics when introducing itself. See
[[Tailed Beasts]] for the shared origin all nine tailed beasts trace back to.

## Abilities

A flame-wreathed, two-tailed cat beast combining great physical strength with Fire
Release, and — alongside the other beasts' combined power — capable of shattering a
senjutsu-enhanced complete Susanoo. Also produces the Tailed Beast Ball common to all
nine.

## Status

Extracted from Yugito Nii by [[Hidan]] and [[Kakuzu]] well before the Fourth Shinobi
World War; refuses Naruto's telepathic call for help against Urashiki Ōtsutsuki during
the *New Era* One-Tail Escort Arc, wary of renewed human contact despite trusting Naruto
personally; later senses Kurama's death during the Kawaki Arc — the most current status
the ingested source covers.
