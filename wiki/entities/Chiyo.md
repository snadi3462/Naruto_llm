# Chiyo

Retired Sunagakure elder, legendary puppet master, and grandmother of [[Sasori]] — the
woman who sealed Shukaku into [[Gaara]] and later gave her life to revive him.
**Deceased.**

## Background and personality

A renowned puppeteer and poison expert whose rivalry with [[Tsunade]] (access
restricted) dates back to the Second Shinobi World War, Chiyo personally sealed Shukaku
into infant Gaara and raised her grandson Sasori after his parents' deaths, teaching him
puppetry — only to watch him grow increasingly detached and eventually abandon both her
and Sunagakure to join [[Akatsuki]]. Mischievous and initially detached from the
outside world in her retirement, she's moved to reengage by her love for Sasori and her
respect for [[Sakura Haruno]] (access restricted).

## Abilities

One of the greatest puppeteers in Sunagakure's history, capable of controlling ten
puppets simultaneously via the Chikamatsu Collection; also a skilled medical-nin and
poison specialist, and a master of fūinjutsu.

## Death and status

During the Kazekage Rescue Mission, fights alongside Sakura to defeat Sasori, then
sacrifices her own life using a forbidden technique to revive Gaara, dying at peace and
hopeful for lasting Suna-Konoha friendship. Reincarnated via Impure World Reincarnation
during the Fourth Shinobi World War, one of the few reincarnated shinobi never defeated
in battle — only released voluntarily.
