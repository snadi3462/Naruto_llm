# Darui

The Fourth Raikage's right-hand man and bodyguard; later Fifth Raikage of Kumogakure.

## Background and personality

Easy-going and reflexively apologetic — his catchphrases "dull" and "sorry" are running
gags — Darui is nonetheless fiercely loyal to the Fourth Raikage, quick to defend him
both verbally and physically. Trained by the Third Raikage in the rare Black Lightning
technique.

## Abilities

Combines Water and Lightning Release into Storm Release, firing guidable beams of light,
and wields a foldable cleaver sword with exceptional kenjutsu skill.

## Key arcs

- **Five Kage Summit**: defeats [[Suigetsu Hōzuki]] in swordplay while escorting the
  Fourth Raikage.
- **Fourth Shinobi World War**: leads the First Division; battles the Gold and Silver
  Brothers for their Treasured Tools of the Sage of Six Paths, and fights at the war's
  climax against the Ten-Tails.

## Status

Eventually succeeds A as Fifth Raikage; as an adult, skilled enough to compete with
Ōtsutsuki-level threats like Momoshiki.
