# Son Gokū

The Four-Tails, one of the nine [[Tailed Beasts]] — sealed within Rōshi of Iwagakure.

## Background and personality

Prideful and initially contemptuous of humans for enslaving and sealing its kind, Son
Gokū is gradually won over by [[Naruto Uzumaki]] (access restricted)'s honesty and
genuine effort to free it during the Fourth Shinobi World War, becoming one of the more
openly trusting tailed beasts by the war's end. See [[Tailed Beasts]] for the shared
origin all nine tailed beasts trace back to.

## Abilities

A monkey/gorilla-like beast that combines Fire and Earth chakra into Lava Release,
breathing streams of green fire and forming volcanoes, alongside the Tailed Beast Ball
all nine beasts share.

## Status

Extracted from Rōshi by [[Kisame Hoshigaki]] during the Itachi Pursuit Mission, well
before the Fourth Shinobi World War; answers Naruto's telepathic call for help against
Urashiki Ōtsutsuki during the *New Era* One-Tail Escort Arc, and later senses Kurama's
death during the Kawaki Arc — the most current status the ingested source covers.
