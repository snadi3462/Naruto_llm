# Hinata Hyuga

Heiress-turned-outcast of the Hyuga clan's main house; later [[Naruto Uzumaki]]'s wife.

## Background and personality

Raised as heiress to the Hyūga clan, Hinata is disinherited by her father Hiashi after
her younger sister Hanabi shows superior talent and Hinata proves unwilling to fight her
seriously in a clan trial — she's given over to [[Kurenai Yūhi]]'s care as a result. Her
meek, self-doubting personality as a child is directly reshaped by watching Naruto's
refusal to give up despite the village's low expectations of him; she consciously adopts
his philosophy as her own path to change.

## Overview

Meets Naruto as children on her first day at the Ninja Academy; her admiration grows into
love over the course of the series, mutual only much later (see [[Naruto Uzumaki]] — access
restricted, for his side of it). During Pain's Assault on Konoha, she openly confesses her
love and is nearly killed protecting him. Two years after the war, plays a crucial role in
the events of *The Last: Naruto the Movie* before her relationship with Naruto becomes
mutual. They marry in *Konoha Hiden: The Perfect Day for a Wedding* and have two children,
Boruto and Himawari — as a wife and mother, she becomes the structured, assertive center
of the household, turning down a jōnin promotion to prioritize family.

## Later status

Per the Kawaki Arc / New Era chapters of [[Naruto Uzumaki (source)]], Hinata is trapped,
unconscious, for three years in a dimension tied to the current crisis — her status is
unresolved as of the Flashforward section (see [[Naruto Uzumaki]] — access restricted, for
the fuller picture).
