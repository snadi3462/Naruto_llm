# Gaara

Youngest of the [[Three Sand Siblings]], former jinchūriki of Shukaku, and Sunagakure's
Fifth Kazekage.

## Background and personality

Sealed with Shukaku before birth so Sunagakure would have a weapon, Gaara was raised in
near-total isolation, protected from all physical harm by a sand shield but starved of
real affection. When his uncle and caretaker Yashamaru was sent to assassinate him and,
dying, told Gaara that neither he nor his late mother ever loved him, Gaara resolved to
live only for himself — killing to prove his own existence, feared by his own village as
"Gaara of the Sand Waterfall."

His defeat by a fellow jinchūriki who found strength in friendship rather than isolation
(see [[Naruto Uzumaki]] — access restricted) breaks this worldview. From that point Gaara
consciously emulates his example: repairing his relationships with his siblings [[Temari]]
and [[Kankurō]], and eventually becoming Suna's Fifth Kazekage so he can protect the
village that once feared him. As Kage he becomes a quiet, empathetic diplomat — advocating
for cooperation between villages, forgiving his father for ordering his death, and later
extending the same compassion to other jinchūriki-like figures, including [[Kawaki]].

## Abilities

A sand-manipulation prodigy: commands a personal supply of chakra-infused sand for both
overwhelming offense (Sand Waterfall Funeral, Sand Shuriken) and a near-impenetrable
automatic defense (Shield of Sand) that kept him unscathed in battle for years. Also
wields the inherited Magnet Release kekkei genkai as a rarely-used trump card, and — while
he was Shukaku's jinchūriki — could partially or fully transform into the tailed beast
itself. Widely regarded as one of the most powerful Kage of his generation.

## Key arcs

- **Chūnin Exams / Konoha Crush**: nearly kills Rock Lee in the preliminaries; loses his
  composure for the first time when wounded by [[Sasuke Uchiha]] (access restricted); is
  ultimately defeated by Naruto Uzumaki (access restricted), the turning point of his
  life.
- **Kazekage Rescue Mission**: captured by Akatsuki's Deidara and Sasori shortly after
  becoming Kazekage; dies and is revived by Chiyo's self-sacrifice, with Team 7's help
  reaching him in time.
- **Fourth Shinobi World War**: commands the Allied Forces' Fourth Division alongside
  Shikamaru Nara; advocates for Five Kage unity, later strengthening after the Infinite
  Tsukuyomi is broken.
- **New Era**: adopts Shinki after finding him struggling with an uncontrolled kekkei
  genkai; remains a recurring Kage-level figure through the Ōtsutsuki-era arcs
  (Momoshiki, Urashiki) and the Kawaki Arc, where he argues for treating Kawaki with
  sympathy rather than as a weapon.

## Status

Severely injured defending Sunagakure from the sentient God Tree Matsuri during the
*New Era: Part II* Boruto's Return Arc; sealed away by his adopted son Shinki for
protection, then freed and returned to Suna to recover — the most current status the
ingested source covers.
