# Zabuza Momochi

"Demon of the Hidden Mist" — missing-nin swordsman, formerly of Kirigakure's Seven
Ninja Swordsmen of the Mist; mentor and partner of [[Haku]]. **Deceased.**

## Background and personality

A mercenary after a failed coup against Kirigakure's Fourth Mizukage, Zabuza raised
[[Haku]] as his personal weapon — outwardly treating him as a disposable tool while
secretly caring for him more deeply than he could admit. Bitter, cynical, and ruthless
on the surface, his facade cracks completely after Haku's death, leading to a final act
of loyalty against his own employer.

## Abilities

Wields the sentient, self-sharpening sword Kubikiribōchō; a master of near-silent
assassination and the Hiding in Mist Technique, skilled enough to fight
[[Kakashi Hatake]] (access restricted) to a standstill.

## Death and status

Killed in the Land of Waves confronting Team 7, in a battle culminating in his own death
turning on his employer Gatō to avenge Haku, then dying beside him. Reincarnated via
Impure World Reincarnation during the Fourth Shinobi World War, fighting alongside Haku
against the Allied Forces.
