# Karin

Uzumaki-clan sensor and healer; member of [[Sasuke Uchiha]] (access restricted)'s team
[[Taka]] after years under Orochimaru's protection.

## Background and personality

An Uzumaki clan descendant taken in by Orochimaru as a child after her own village was
destroyed, Karin developed a rare sensory ability to detect chakra signatures from great
distances, which made her valuable enough to be put in charge of one of Orochimaru's
hideouts. She develops an intense, largely unrequited infatuation with Sasuke from their
first meeting — a fixation that sours considerably after he stabs her and later orders
her killed during his darkest period, though she never fully stops caring what happens
to him. By the *New Era* she's made peace with it, content that he's happy with his own
family.

## Abilities

A powerful Uzumaki life-force grants her self-healing "Heal Bite" — biting herself (or
letting others bite her) to transfer restorative chakra — alongside an unmatched
long-range sensory ability. Also able to materialize an incomplete version of the
Adamantine Sealing Chains, an Uzumaki-clan ninjutsu, used to significant effect during
the Fourth Shinobi World War.

## Status

By the *New Era*, remains close to [[Sasuke Uchiha]]'s family despite their history —
helps deliver [[Sarada Uchiha]] and gives her her signature glasses.
