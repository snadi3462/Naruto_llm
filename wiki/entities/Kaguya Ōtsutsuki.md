# Kaguya Ōtsutsuki

Ancient Ōtsutsuki matriarch, Earth's first chakra wielder, and the true final villain
of the original series — sealed by her own sons and later revived to fight
[[Naruto Uzumaki]] and [[Sasuke Uchiha]]. **Deceased.**

## Background and personality

Sent to harvest a God Tree's power for her clan, Kaguya instead ate its forbidden fruit
herself, becoming the first chakra user on Earth and using her new power to unify a
war-torn world — before growing despotic and transforming its people into an army via
the Infinite Tsukuyomi. Once genuinely good and peace-seeking according to her son
Hagoromo, her total loss of faith in humanity curdles into a god-complex and an
obsessive belief that all chakra should belong to her alone.

## Abilities

As the first and most powerful chakra wielder ever, commands all five nature
transformations plus Yin–Yang Release, wields the Byakugan, and can absorb any jutsu she
encounters. Her strongest techniques (All-Killing Ash Bones, the Expansive
Truth-Seeking Ball) draw on the full power of the Ten-Tails after merging with the God
Tree.

## Death and status

Sealed by her own sons Hagoromo and Hamura after she tried to reclaim their chakra —
the Moon is literally the sealed remnant of that battle. Secretly leaves behind Black
Zetsu, her own manifested will, who spends centuries manipulating the Uchiha and Senju
clans toward reviving her. Revived during the Fourth Shinobi World War at
[[Madara Uchiha]]'s expense, ultimately defeated and resealed by Naruto Uzumaki and
Sasuke Uchiha.
