# Saiken

The Six-Tails, one of the nine [[Tailed Beasts]] — sealed within Utakata of Kirigakure.

## Background and personality

Kinder and less self-serving than most tailed beasts, Saiken cooperated willingly with
its jinchūriki rather than fighting for control — though it's still capable of killing
in self-preservation, as shown when it killed the sensei who tried to forcibly extract
it. See [[Tailed Beasts]] for the shared origin all nine tailed beasts trace back to.

## Abilities

An enormous slug-like beast able to secrete corrosive, disintegrating substances and
trap targets in an adhesive Water Release technique, alongside the Tailed Beast Ball all
nine beasts share.

## Status

Extracted from Utakata by [[Akatsuki]] well before the Fourth Shinobi World War; answers
Naruto's telepathic call for help against Urashiki Ōtsutsuki during the *New Era*
One-Tail Escort Arc, and later senses Kurama's death during the Kawaki Arc — the most
current status the ingested source covers.
