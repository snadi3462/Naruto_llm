# Isobu

The Three-Tails, one of the nine [[Tailed Beasts]] — most recently sealed within Yagura
Karatachi of Kirigakure.

## Background and personality

Timid-mannered but destructive. Isobu was once secretly sealed within [[Rin Nohara]]
(access restricted) as a planned weapon against Konoha; her choice to die rather than
let that plan succeed makes Isobu the first known tailed beast to "die" and revive
without a formal extraction. Later sealed within Yagura, the Fourth Mizukage, who spent
years secretly controlled via genjutsu until the spell was broken. See [[Tailed Beasts]]
for the shared origin all nine tailed beasts trace back to.

## Abilities

A turtle/crab-like beast with a near-impenetrable shell, capable of rolling into a
high-speed offensive ball, generating coral, and producing powerful tidal waves,
alongside the Tailed Beast Ball all nine beasts share.

## Status

Captured and sealed by [[Akatsuki]] before the Fourth Shinobi World War; answers
Naruto's telepathic call for help against Urashiki Ōtsutsuki during the *New Era*
One-Tail Escort Arc, and later senses Kurama's death during the Kawaki Arc — the most
current status the ingested source covers.
