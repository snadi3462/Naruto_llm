# Shizune

[[Tsunade]] (access restricted)'s devoted apprentice and assistant, serving as her
right hand through three successive Hokage administrations.

## Background and personality

Niece of Tsunade's late lover Dan Katō, Shizune accompanied Tsunade during her
years-long self-exile from Konoha as both attendant and medical apprentice. Level-headed
and practical where Tsunade is impulsive, she's often the one managing the fallout of
Tsunade's gambling and recklessness, though she's not afraid to voice disagreement even
when it strains their relationship.

## Abilities

A highly skilled medical-nin and poison specialist — once considered Tsunade's top
disciple before [[Sakura Haruno]] (access restricted) surpassed her — capable of complex
regenerative medical ninjutsu and lethal poison-based combat techniques.

## Status

Objects on moral grounds when Tsunade nearly accepts Orochimaru's offer to revive her
dead loved ones in exchange for healing him. Leads the Logistical Support and Medical
Division during the Fourth Shinobi World War, and continues serving as Hokage's
assistant across Tsunade's, Kakashi Hatake's, and Naruto Uzumaki's administrations (all
access restricted).
