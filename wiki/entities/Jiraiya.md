# Jiraiya

One of the three Sannin; [[Naruto Uzumaki]]'s godfather and mentor.

## Background

Trained as a genin alongside [[Orochimaru]] and [[Tsunade]] under Hiruzen Sarutobi; the three
were the sole survivors of a Second Shinobi World War clash with Hanzō of Amegakure, who
personally dubbed them the "Legendary Sannin." Discovered Mount Myōboku and trained under
toad elder Fukasaku in senjutsu; the Great Toad Sage prophesied he would train a "Child of
Prophecy" who would either save or destroy the world — Jiraiya was never able to achieve a
fully balanced Sage Mode himself.

Out of war-guilt, spent three years training three Amegakure orphans — [[Yahiko]],
[[Nagato]], and [[Konan]] — identifying Rinnegan-bearer Nagato as the prophesied child.
Nagato's idealism about ending the cycle of hatred directly inspired Jiraiya's debut novel,
*The Tale of the Utterly Gutsy Shinobi*, dedicated to Nagato. He left the three to return to
Konoha, never learning until his final mission that Yahiko had died and Nagato had become
"Pain," leader of [[Akatsuki]] — a legacy failure he confronts fatally in Amegakure.

Godfather to Naruto by virtue of being the author of the book Naruto was named after
(protagonist Naruto Musasabi); took Naruto's father [[Minato Namikaze]] on as a personal
disciple, suspecting *he* might be the Child of Prophecy, and contributed directly to his
development of the Rasengan. Trains Naruto directly after the Chūnin Exams, teaching him to
harness [[Kurama]]'s chakra via summoning and eventually Sage Mode, and takes him on the
search for Tsunade and the following two-and-a-half-year training journey.

## Personality

A boisterous, self-deprecating showman and self-proclaimed "super pervert" — his peeping
"research" fueled the wildly popular adult *Icha Icha* novels after his debut book flopped
commercially. Refused the Hokage title multiple times, feeling disqualified by his own
conduct. Privately driven by a sense of failure (losing Tsunade's heart, failing to redeem
Orochimaru, failing to save Hiruzen and Minato) balanced against an indomitable refusal to
give up — a philosophy he passed on to all three of his students, most consequentially Naruto.

## Death

Infiltrating Amegakure to confirm Akatsuki's leadership, fought Konan, then the Six Paths of
Pain — reanimated corpses remotely controlled by Nagato, including Yahiko's own body as the
Deva Path. Killed by Pain (Nagato) after being overwhelmed once all six Paths combined;
fatally wounded, he used his last strength to carve a coded dying message into Fukasaku's
back before dying. Naruto later decrypted the message using *Icha Icha Tactics* as a cipher
key, revealing Nagato as the true controller behind Pain — intelligence that proves decisive
in Naruto's own confrontation with Pain during Pain's Assault on Konoha.

His death is the emotional catalyst for Naruto's Sage Mode training at Mount Myōboku and his
choice to forgive rather than avenge Nagato — Naruto explicitly frames his fight with Pain as
finishing what Jiraiya started, fulfilling the anti-hatred message of Jiraiya's own novel.
The Great Toad Sage retroactively revises the prophecy to cover two Children of Prophecy
(Nagato and Naruto) that Jiraiya trained. Naruto later builds him a memorial.
