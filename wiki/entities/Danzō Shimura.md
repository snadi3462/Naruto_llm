# Danzō Shimura

Founder of the shadow organization Root; lifelong rival of [[Hiruzen Sarutobi]] (access
restricted) for the Hokage title, and the hidden hand behind many of the series'
darkest events. **Deceased.**

## Background and personality

A decades-long rival of Hiruzen Sarutobi, Danzō built Root as a black-ops organization
operating outside normal village oversight, convinced Konoha's survival required
methods its own Hokage were too soft to use. Cold, calculating, and utterly convinced
the ends justify the means, he directly engineers some of the series' darkest turning
points while genuinely believing himself the village's true protector.

## Key facts

- Manipulates [[Itachi Uchiha]] (access restricted) into massacring his own clan by
  offering to spare [[Sasuke Uchiha]] (access restricted), and secretly leaks a young
  jinchūriki's identity to turn the village against him.
- Augments his own body with stolen Sharingan (including Shisui Uchiha's), Hashirama
  Senju's cells (access restricted), and other illicit modifications obtained through
  his working relationship with [[Orochimaru]].

## Death

Killed by Sasuke Uchiha during the Five Kage Summit while attempting a suicide attack
against him and Tobi/Obito for what he genuinely believed was the village's greater
good.
