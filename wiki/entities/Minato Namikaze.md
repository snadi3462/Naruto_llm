# Minato Namikaze

The Fourth Hokage. Father of [[Naruto Uzumaki]], husband of [[Kushina Uzumaki]].

## Background

An Academy prodigy placed on Team 6 under [[Jiraiya]], who personally mentored him and came
to suspect he might be the "Child of the Prophecy." Rescued Kushina single-handedly from a
Kumogakure kidnapping as a young genin, instantly making him famous across multiple nations
as "Konoha's Yellow Flash" — years before he ever became Hokage. Later led his own genin
team — [[Rin Nohara]], [[Obito Uchiha]], and [[Kakashi Hatake]] — giving them a bell test
that taught the "teamwork above orders" lesson Obito internalized and Kakashi initially
didn't. During the Kannabi Bridge mission he transferred command to a newly-jōnin Kakashi and
arrived afterward to rescue Kakashi and Rin from Iwa-nin, with Obito already believed dead.
Rin's later death — secretly orchestrated by Madara Uchiha to corrupt Obito — happened on
Minato's watch while he was away on another mission, a second instance of the same tragedy
repeating under his command.

Chosen as Fourth Hokage over [[Orochimaru]] for his war performance. As Hokage, he assigned
Kakashi to guard the pregnant Kushina, hoping the responsibility would help pull him out of
the darkness following Obito and Rin's deaths.

## Death

When Obito Uchiha (masked, as "Tobi") attacked Konoha the night of Naruto's birth to seize
the newborn jinchūriki, Minato rescued Naruto, then fought and freed Kurama from Obito's
control — branding him with a Flying Thunder God seal in the process, which becomes the
mechanism for Minato's shadow-clone strike on "Tobi" decades later during the war. Facing a
fox too powerful to seal into an infant safely, Minato used the Dead Demon Consuming Seal to
split its chakra in half — sealing the Yin half in himself and sacrificing his soul to seal
the Yang half in Naruto — before dying. He wove in a chakra failsafe that could later
manifest to repair the seal or let him and Kushina speak to Naruto again, later reappearing
to Naruto during Pain's Assault to repair the seal and offer encouragement.

Because he could never explain what really happened that night, Konoha villagers wrongly
suspected the Uchiha clan — a root contributor to the resentment that fed into the Uchiha
Clan Downfall (cross-reference: [[Tobirama Senju]]'s Military Police Force is the other
identified root cause). The Third Hokage resumed the Hokage seat after Minato's death and hid
Naruto's parentage to protect him from his father's enemies.

## War-arc reincarnation

Reincarnated by Orochimaru at Sasuke's request alongside the other three deceased Hokage
during the Fourth Shinobi World War. Fights alongside Naruto, loses both arms in combat
against Madara/Obito as the Ten-Tails' jinchūriki, and is present (unable to help directly)
when Black Zetsu steals his half of Kurama's chakra during the attempt to save Naruto's life.
Reunites with Naruto once more after the war ends, congratulates him on his 17th birthday,
and returns to the afterlife.

Naruto strongly resembles him physically and inherited his habit of relying on jutsu over
hand seals.
