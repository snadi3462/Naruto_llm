# Jūgo

Gentle recluse prone to uncontrollable violent rages; the most physically dangerous
member of [[Sasuke Uchiha]] (access restricted)'s team [[Taka]].

## Background and personality

A naturally kind, animal-loving person whose clan's unique ability to absorb natural
energy comes bundled with random, uncontrollable fits of murderous rage, Jūgo secludes
himself to avoid hurting anyone until he meets Kimimaro — and later Sasuke — whose
presence alone can keep his violence in check. His clan's blood is the biological origin
of Orochimaru's cursed seal technique, made from enzymes isolated directly from Jūgo
himself. Fully devoted to Sasuke as Kimimaro's successor, he remains loyal even after
Taka disbands, describing his place as Sasuke's "shield."

## Abilities

His clan's kekkei genkai lets him instantly transform parts or all of his body using
absorbed natural energy — reshaping limbs into weapons, boosting speed and strength, and
transferring healing chakra to others. Fully transforming grants a massive power boost
at the cost of his sanity.

## Status

Fights alongside [[Taka]] through the Fourth Shinobi World War; the ingested source's
coverage of his post-war/*New Era* status is thin — his last confirmed appearances are
wartime, still at Sasuke's side.
