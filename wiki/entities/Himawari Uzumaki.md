# Himawari Uzumaki

Daughter of [[Naruto Uzumaki]] (access restricted) and [[Hinata Hyuga]]; younger sibling of
Boruto Uzumaki. Named after Neji Hyuga's favorite flower (the sunflower) in tribute to his
death protecting his loved ones.

## Later significance — new Nine-Tails jinchūriki, mechanism resolved

[[Sakura Haruno (source)]] describes Himawari, in the *New Era: Part II* chapters, as
"the new Nine-Tails' jinchūriki," able to fully heal a gravely injured Inojin.
[[Ino Yamanaka (source)]] independently corroborates this — during the Boruto's Return
Arc, an injured Boruto is healed by "Himawari, the new Nine-Tails jinchūriki." [[Kurama
(source)]] resolves how this connects to Kurama's death (see [[Kurama]] — access
restricted): the remaining kernels of Kurama's chakra found their way into Himawari after
its death, and Kurama itself reforms as a small cub within her, taking on a mentoring
role as she learns to control her newfound power.
