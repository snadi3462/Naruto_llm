# Anko Mitarashi

Konoha tokubetsu jōnin and former student of [[Orochimaru]], left permanently marked —
physically and psychologically — by his experiments.

## Background and personality

One of ten test subjects for Orochimaru's Cursed Seal of Heaven, and the only survivor;
he later erased her memory rather than kill her, leaving her to piece together a life
without knowing whether she ever truly rejected him. Laid-back, brash, and sweet-toothed
(dango is practically a defining trait), with a darker undercurrent of trauma from
Orochimaru's betrayal that fuels her determination to personally stop him.

## Abilities

Trained extensively by Orochimaru before his betrayal — skilled in his signature snake
ninjutsu, poison resistance, and excellent tracking, later put in charge of the team
searching for [[Kabuto Yakushi]].

## Status

Proctors the Chūnin Exams' Forest of Death stage, where she's the first to detect
Orochimaru's infiltration and confronts him directly. Later teaches at Konoha's Academy;
her cursed seal is eventually removed after the Fourth Shinobi World War.
