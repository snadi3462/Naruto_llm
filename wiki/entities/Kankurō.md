# Kankurō

Middle of the [[Three Sand Siblings]]; Sunagakure's foremost puppeteer and [[Gaara]]'s
most loyal bodyguard.

## Background and personality

Initially resentful of and intimidated by his own younger brother during Gaara's most
violent years, Kankurō's relationship with him transforms completely after Gaara's
reform — he becomes fiercely, almost paternally protective, rushing alone into danger to
try to rescue Gaara from [[Akatsuki]] and staying at his side as bodyguard for Kage
Summits for years afterward. Practical and averse to needless fighting, he's proud of his
village and, unlike Gaara, popular with Suna's rank-and-file for leading from the front
lines. An avid puppet collector and tinkerer outside of combat.

## Abilities

A puppet master trained on tools originally built by [[Sasori]] — commands multiple
puppets simultaneously via chakra threads, capable of disrupting an opponent's own
chakra flow and combining puppets' attacks into devastating traps (the Black Secret
Technique). After killing Sasori during the Fourth Shinobi World War, inherits his
"Mother and Father" puppets and takes Sasori's old body as a new puppet of his own.

## Key arcs

- **Kazekage Rescue Mission**: poisoned nearly to death by Sasori while trying to rescue
  Gaara alone; cured by [[Sakura Haruno]] (access restricted).
- **Fourth Shinobi World War**: leads the Surprise Attack Division; defeats and kills
  Sasori, inheriting his puppets.
- **Blank Period / New Era**: remains Gaara's bodyguard through repeated Kage Summits,
  including the summit that first debates Kawaki's fate; helps train Gaara's adopted son
  Shinki's genin team.

## Status

Fights Urashiki Ōtsutsuki's puppets during the *New Era: Part II* One-Tail Escort Arc in
a battle ending in a large explosion; confirmed alive and safe afterward but not followed
further — the most current status the ingested source gives.
