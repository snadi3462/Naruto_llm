# Gyūki

The Eight-Tails, one of the nine [[Tailed Beasts]] — sealed within Killer B of
Kumogakure.

## Background and personality

Once notoriously violent whenever its earlier jinchūriki lost control, Gyūki's behavior
settles considerably after Killer B bests it in single combat and earns its respect.
Tough, blunt, and quick to reprimand B's goofier habits (rapping mid-battle, perverted
asides), Gyūki nonetheless formed a genuinely close bond with him — severing part of
itself to help sustain B's life after an extraction attempt, and openly missing B's
"awful rapping" once separated from him. See [[Tailed Beasts]] for the shared origin all
nine tailed beasts trace back to.

## Abilities

An ox-headed, octopus-tailed beast of immense size, capable of producing a Tailed Beast
Ball like all its kind. Notably never successfully extracted by [[Akatsuki]] — its horns
and teeth carry visible battle damage from fights with the Fourth Raikage and the
Ten-Tails, but Gyūki and Killer B remain paired throughout Part II rather than being
separated like most other beast/jinchūriki pairs.

## Status

Answers Naruto's telepathic call for help against Urashiki Ōtsutsuki during the *New
Era* One-Tail Escort Arc, and later senses Kurama's death during the Kawaki Arc — the
most current status the ingested source covers.
