# Killer B

Jinchūriki of the Eight-Tails ([[Gyūki]]); adoptive brother of A (Fourth Raikage) — one
of the few jinchūriki to achieve genuine, willing partnership with his tailed beast.

## Background and personality

Chosen as Gyūki's jinchūriki as a child after its previous host lost control and killed
several people; endures prejudice similar to what [[Naruto Uzumaki]] (access restricted)
and [[Gaara]] faced, but stays relentlessly cheerful for his brother's sake. Aspires to
be the world's greatest rapper, constantly writing rhymes mid-battle — a running gag
alongside his trademark fist-bump greeting.

## Abilities

Achieves full, willing control of Gyūki through training at the Falls of Truth, letting
him access every transformation stage — including full Tailed Beast Mode — while
retaining his own mind, a rarity among jinchūriki.

## Key arcs

- Fights [[Sasuke Uchiha]] (access restricted) to a draw when Taka attempts to capture
  him.
- Mentors Naruto Uzumaki during the Fourth Shinobi World War, helping him mature and
  turn the tide against Akatsuki.
