# Haku

Orphaned Ice Release user devoted to [[Zabuza Momochi]]; the first genuine emotional
gut-punch of the series' Land of Waves arc. **Deceased.**

## Background and personality

Orphaned as a child after his own father killed his mother and tried to kill him for
their shared kekkei genkai, Haku was taken in by Zabuza, who offered him purpose as a
"tool" rather than a person — a role Haku embraces gladly, mistaking usefulness for
love. Gentle and reluctant to kill despite his lethal skill, he believes a person
becomes truly strong only when they have someone important to protect — a philosophy
that directly influences [[Naruto Uzumaki]] (access restricted) during their fight.

## Abilities

Wields Ice Release, combining Water and Wind chakra, including the signature Demonic
Mirroring Ice Crystals technique; considered by both Zabuza and [[Kakashi Hatake]]
(access restricted) to be more skilled than an Anbu member despite his young age.

## Death and status

Dies shielding Zabuza from a fatal blow during their battle with Team 7, having found
one final purpose in being useful to him. Reincarnated via Impure World Reincarnation
during the Fourth Shinobi World War, fighting alongside Zabuza against the Allied
Forces.
