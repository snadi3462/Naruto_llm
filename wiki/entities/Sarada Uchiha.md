# Sarada Uchiha

Daughter of [[Sasuke Uchiha]] and [[Sakura Haruno]] (both access restricted — see their pages
for the family's fuller story).

## Overview

Raised almost entirely by her mother, since her father is away for years at a time on
Ōtsutsuki investigation missions. As her Academy graduation approaches, Sarada presses her
mother for details about her father's past; her mother's reluctance to discuss his dark
history leads Sarada to doubt whether her parents are even really married, or whether he's
really her father. She leaves the village to find him herself. The doubt is resolved in the
*Sarada Uchiha Arc* (also adapted as *Naruto Gaiden: The Seventh Hokage and the Scarlet
Spring*): her mother rescues the family from the antagonist Shin Uchiha, and the
confrontation confirms the biological bond, after which the family reconciles.

She later becomes a genin partnered with Boruto Uzumaki and inherits the Uchiha Sharingan,
progressing to a Mangekyō Sharingan. By the *New Era: Part II* chapters, overuse of it has
begun visibly damaging her eyesight, but she pushes through it out of devotion to helping
Boruto — a parallel the wiki notes to her mother's own younger devotion (see
[[Sakura Haruno]], [[Sasuke Uchiha]] — access restricted).
