# Chōjūrō

One of the last Seven Ninja Swordsmen of the Mist; [[Mei Terumī]]'s bodyguard, later
Sixth Mizukage of Kirigakure.

## Background and personality

Shy and lacking confidence early on, Chōjūrō grows steadily more self-assured through
the Fourth Shinobi World War — where he single-handedly wounds Black Zetsu — and matures
into a calm, methodical, and selfless leader by the time he succeeds Mei as Mizukage.

## Abilities

Wields Hiramekarei, a shapeshifting chakra sword that can form into a hammer, a
longsword, or split into two blades for dual-wielding — regarded as one of the era's
finest swordsmen, able to hold his own even against Ōtsutsuki-level opponents as an
adult.

## Status

Accompanies [[Mei Terumī]] through the Five Kage Summit and the Fourth Shinobi World
War; succeeds her as Sixth Mizukage in the *New Era*, navigating diplomacy with the
other villages and, later, a revolt within his own Seven Swordsmen's next generation.
