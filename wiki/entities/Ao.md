# Ao

Veteran Kirigakure hunter-nin, [[Mei Terumī]]'s right hand; later recruited into the
criminal organization Kara.

## Background and personality

A stern, prideful traditionalist who took a defeated Hyūga clan member's Byakugan for
his own right eye decades earlier, Ao frequently scolds his younger colleague
[[Chōjūrō]] for lacking confidence, though the two share real mutual respect beneath the
friction.

## Abilities

A highly skilled sensor-type shinobi and tracker, leading the Allied Shinobi Forces'
Sensor Division during the Fourth Shinobi World War using his sensory skills and stolen
Byakugan. Later rehabilitated with cybernetic Scientific Ninja Tool prosthetics after
losing an arm, a leg, and the Byakugan itself in the war.

## Status

Sole survivor of a devastating Ten-Tails attack on the Allied Forces' headquarters,
though severely maimed; never returns to Kirigakure afterward, growing bitter until
recruited by Kara as an "Outer." Works against Konoha as a Kara agent in the *New Era*,
though his arc in the ingested source ends on a redemptive turn after being defeated and
shown mercy by Boruto Uzumaki.
