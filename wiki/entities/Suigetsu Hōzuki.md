# Suigetsu Hōzuki

Kirigakure swordsman freed from Orochimaru's imprisonment by [[Sasuke Uchiha]] (access
restricted); member of his team [[Taka]].

## Background and personality

Obsessed with collecting the blades of the legendary Seven Ninja Swordsmen of the Mist —
a dream inherited from his late brother Mangetsu — Suigetsu joins Sasuke's team as much
for the opportunity to pursue that goal (starting with taking Zabuza Momochi's sword,
then coveting Kisame Hoshigaki's Samehada) as out of loyalty. Outwardly mocking and
mercenary, especially toward teammate [[Karin]], he grows genuinely protective of his
team over time, risking his life against Killer B's tailed-beast form during the Fourth
Shinobi World War.

## Abilities

A member of the Hōzuki clan, able to liquefy his entire body at will — making him nearly
impossible to strike physically, able to merge with existing water sources, and capable
of rapid healing — though vulnerable to lightning-natured attacks. Also a skilled
swordsman, wielding Zabuza Momochi's Kubikiribōchō.

## Status

Fights alongside [[Taka]] through the Fourth Shinobi World War; the ingested source's
coverage of his post-war/*New Era* status is thin — his last confirmed appearances are
wartime.
