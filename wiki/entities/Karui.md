# Karui

Kumogakure kunoichi, member of Killer B's genin team; later marries [[Chōji Akimichi]]
and joins the Akimichi clan.

## Background and personality

Self-assured, outspoken, and impulsive, Karui is fiercely loyal to her sensei
[[Killer B]] and was initially hostile toward Konoha over [[Sasuke Uchiha]] (access
restricted)'s actions against her team's own sensei. Shares a comedically antagonistic
but close partnership with teammate Omoi.

## Abilities

Relies primarily on kenjutsu, wielding a long katana trained under Killer B; also
capable of Lightning Release.

## Status

Fights through the Fourth Shinobi World War in the Second Division. In the *New Era*,
marries [[Chōji Akimichi]] and relocates to Konoha, becoming a member of the Akimichi
clan and mother to Chōchō — corroborating [[Chōji Akimichi (source)]].
