# Mei Terumī

Fifth Mizukage of Kirigakure, who took over after the genjutsu-manipulated reign of
Yagura Karatachi and worked to reform the village's brutal history.

## Background and personality

Raised through Kirigakure's brutal academy graduation ritual during the "Village of the
Bloody Mist" era, Mei becomes a kind, diplomatically open-minded leader — the first Kage
to genuinely respect [[Gaara]] despite his youth, willing to defend him against the
other Kage's dismissiveness. Also flirtatious and notoriously sensitive about her age
and marital status, a running source of friction with her bodyguard [[Ao]].

## Abilities

Wields two rare kekkei genkai — Lava Release (acidic, corrosive mud) and Boil Release
(corrosive mist) — powerful enough to seriously wound [[Sasuke Uchiha]] (access
restricted) even through his Susanoo.

## Key arcs

- **Five Kage Summit**: confronts Sasuke directly when he infiltrates the summit hunting
  Danzō Shimura, and later helps expose Danzō's genjutsu manipulation of the proceedings.
- **Fourth Shinobi World War**: fights alongside the other Kage against the reincarnated
  Madara Uchiha, escorted throughout by her bodyguards [[Chōjūrō]] and [[Ao]].

## Status

Eventually succeeded as Mizukage by [[Chōjūrō]] in the *New Era*.
