# Konan

Amegakure kunoichi, [[Akatsuki]] co-founder alongside [[Yahiko]] and [[Nagato]]; Nagato's
steadfast partner and, after his death, de facto leader of Amegakure. **Deceased** — killed
by Obito Uchiha.

## Background

Orphaned in the Second Shinobi World War, Konan found and bonded with fellow orphan [[Yahiko]]
(with whom she shared reciprocated romantic feelings), and the two later found and rescued a
dying [[Nagato]]. Trained by a wandering Konoha shinobi alongside them (see [[Jiraiya]] —
access restricted), she became [[Akatsuki]]'s only consistently loyal, level-headed member —
the "angel" to Nagato/Pain's "god" in Amegakure's public mythology.

## Personality

Smart, stoic, and calm — a contrast to Yahiko's brashness and Nagato's early sensitivity —
Konan places her teammates' safety above her own without hesitation and remains the one
person capable of reading Nagato's true emotions beneath his god persona. After Nagato's
death, she becomes fiercely protective of both his legacy and the person he entrusted his
mission to (see [[Naruto Uzumaki]] — access restricted), declaring Amegakure's loyalty to him.

## Abilities

A paper-ninjutsu master (Dance of the Shikigami): transforms her entire body into controllable
sheets of paper, usable as weapons, clones, explosives, or wings for flight — durable enough
to survive direct hits from a Sannin's fire techniques (see [[Jiraiya]] — access restricted).

## Key arcs and death

Fights alongside Nagato during an infiltration of Amegakure against a formidable intruder
(see [[Jiraiya]] — access restricted). After Nagato's own death, formally defects from
Akatsuki, taking his and Yahiko's remains back to Amegakure for a shrine. During the Fourth
Shinobi World War, Konan nearly kills Obito Uchiha (Tobi) in a devastating paper-and-explosives
trap, but he survives via the forbidden Izanagi technique and kills her in return — her last
act is affirming her faith in the "flower of hope" she, Yahiko, and Nagato had all believed
in (see [[Naruto Uzumaki]] — access restricted).
