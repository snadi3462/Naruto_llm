# Deidara

Former Iwagakure Explosion Corps member, [[Akatsuki]]'s youngest member — an explosive-clay
artist partnered first with [[Sasori]], then Tobi (Obito). **Deceased** — died detonating
himself against [[Sasuke Uchiha]].

## Background and personality

Stole a forbidden Iwagakure technique to fuse his Explosion Release kekkei genkai with clay,
becoming a missing-nin bomber before being forcibly recruited into [[Akatsuki]] after a
humiliating duel loss (see [[Itachi Uchiha]] — access restricted) that curdles into lifelong
loathing of his opponent. Defines himself entirely through his art philosophy ("art is a
fleeting explosion," in constant argument with [[Sasori]]'s belief in eternal, unchanging
art), and takes both his craft and his rivalries extremely personally — his final battle
against [[Sasuke Uchiha]] is driven as much by wounded artistic pride as by strategy (see
[[Sasuke Uchiha]] — access restricted).

## Abilities

Explosion Release combined with a stolen kinjutsu gives him mouths on both palms and his
chest, letting him mold explosive clay into any shape — birds for transport, dragons for
long-range bombardment, and (as a last resort) turning his own body into a devastating bomb.

## Key arcs and death

Captures Gaara during the Kazekage Rescue Mission, losing both arms over the course of Part
II (one to Gaara's sand, one to a rival's dimensional technique — see [[Kakashi Hatake]] —
access restricted) before being reincarnated via Impure World Reincarnation to fight Team
Kakashi. His final battle is against [[Sasuke Uchiha]], seeking revenge by proxy for his
earlier loss (see [[Itachi Uchiha]] — access restricted) — cornered and out of options,
Deidara detonates his most powerful technique (C0) in a suicide attack, but his opponent
escapes (see [[Sasuke Uchiha]] — access restricted), leaving Deidara's sacrifice for nothing.
