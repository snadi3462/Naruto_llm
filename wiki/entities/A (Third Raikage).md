# A (Third Raikage)

Kumogakure's Third Raikage, regarded as its greatest leader ever — a legendary
strongman renowned for surviving nearly anything. **Deceased.**

## Background and personality

Spent his reign personally subduing the Eight-Tails' periodic rampages before finally
sealing it successfully in [[Killer B]]; mentored [[Darui]] in the rare Black Lightning
technique. Calm, proud, and deeply protective of his village and comrades.

## Abilities

Renowned as the "strongest shield" for his near-total physical durability, able to
shrug off nearly any technique; also wields the piercing Hell Stab lightning technique
and a unique Black Lightning passed down only to Darui.

## Death and status

Died fighting an army of ten thousand shinobi single-handedly for three days straight to
let his comrades escape. Reincarnated via Impure World Reincarnation during the Fourth
Shinobi World War, forced to fight the Allied Forces before being resealed after a
climactic duel with [[Naruto Uzumaki]] (access restricted).
