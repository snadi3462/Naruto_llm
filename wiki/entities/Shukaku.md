# Shukaku

The One-Tail, one of the nine [[Tailed Beasts]] — sealed within [[Gaara]] of
Sunagakure.

## Background and personality

Childish, bombastic, and prideful about being underestimated for having only one tail,
Shukaku carries a long-standing rivalry with [[Kurama]] over which beast is truly
stronger. Its centuries of mistreatment by humans made it genuinely dangerous to its own
jinchūriki — threatening to take over young Gaara's body if he ever fell fully asleep,
the direct cause of his childhood insomnia. As Gaara reforms and comes to see Shukaku as
part of what shaped him rather than resent it, Shukaku's own hostility softens into real
loyalty. See [[Tailed Beasts]] for the shared origin all nine tailed beasts trace back
to.

## Abilities

Grants its jinchūriki sand-based abilities and, like all tailed beasts, can produce a
Tailed Beast Ball. Unlike most of the other eight, Shukaku was never captured or
extracted by [[Akatsuki]] — it remained with Gaara throughout Part II.

## Status

Directly targeted by Urashiki Ōtsutsuki for its chakra during the *New Era* One-Tail
Escort Arc, at one point willingly sealing itself inside a kettle to avoid detection —
the most current status the ingested source covers.
