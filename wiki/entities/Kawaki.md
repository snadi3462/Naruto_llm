# Kawaki

A Kara defector taken in by the Uzumaki family (see [[Naruto Uzumaki]] — access restricted)
and central to the unresolved arc that closes out the current wiki coverage of that family's
story.

## Overview

Introduced as a tattooed, heavily-modified youth (Shinobi-Ware implants granting
Orochimaru/Jugo-like physiology alteration) brought into Kara by Jigen and bestowed a Kāma
mark to be turned into a weapon. After fleeing Kara, he's brought to Konoha by Boruto and
taken in by the Uzumaki family. A guardian figure mentors him — training him in ninjutsu,
giving him a prosthetic arm, defending him from Kara operatives (Delta, Jigen, Isshiki) — and
the two develop a father-figure bond (see [[Naruto Uzumaki]] — access restricted), in tension
with Kawaki's rivalry/found sibling relationship with Boruto.

## Later significance (access restricted — see [[Naruto Uzumaki]])

Per [[List of Naruto characters]], the franchise's prologue foreshadows Kawaki and Boruto
becoming mortal enemies, with an older Kawaki implicated in Konoha's eventual destruction.
Kawaki plays a central, still-unresolved role in the current-era crisis affecting the Uzumaki
family — see [[Naruto Uzumaki]] and [[Team 7]] "Current crisis" (both access restricted) for
the details, which aren't repeated here.
