# Temari

Eldest of the [[Three Sand Siblings]]; Sunagakure's ambassador and battle strategist;
later wife of [[Shikamaru Nara]] and mother of Shikadai.

## Background and personality

Prudent, blunt, and rarely afraid to speak her mind, Temari spent her childhood
protective of — and occasionally afraid of — her youngest brother [[Gaara]] during his
most violent years. As Suna and Konoha's relationship thaws, she takes on the role of
diplomatic ambassador between the two villages, and her frequent joint missions with
[[Shikamaru Nara]] gradually develop from rivalry into partnership, then romance. As an
adult she becomes the outspoken, strict matriarch of the Nara household, deeply loyal to
the clan she married into while retaining her own fierce independence.

## Abilities

A long-range specialist who fights with a giant iron war fan (tessenjutsu) combined with
Wind Release, capable of slicing whirlwinds strong enough to injure a Kage-tier opponent
or counter a tailed beast's shockwave. A sharp tactical analyst in her own right — she
was the one Shikamaru Nara had to out-think, not overpower, to win their Chūnin Exams
match — and by adulthood is recognized as one of the strongest Wind Release users alive.

## Key arcs

- **Chūnin Exams**: defeats Tenten; loses a close tactical match to Shikamaru Nara after
  he traps her with the Shadow Imitation Technique, the start of their recurring
  partnership.
- **Sasuke Recovery Mission**: kills Tayuya of the Sound Four while backing up Shikamaru.
- **Kazekage Rescue Mission / Fourth Shinobi World War**: fights alongside the Fourth
  Division (which Shikamaru effectively commands in Gaara's stead) against the Third
  Raikage and later the Ten-Tails.
- **Blank Period / New Era**: her relationship with Shikamaru Nara becomes explicit,
  leading to marriage and her move to Konoha as a member of the Nara clan; raises their
  son Shikadai, teaching him and Mirai Sarutobi her Wind Release techniques.

## Status

Wounded protecting Shikadai during the same Urashiki Ōtsutsuki confrontation that injures
Gaara and Kankurō (One-Tail Escort Arc); recovers and continues raising Shikadai in
Konoha — the most current status the ingested source covers.
