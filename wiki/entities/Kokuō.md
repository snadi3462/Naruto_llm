# Kokuō

The Five-Tails, one of the nine [[Tailed Beasts]] — sealed within Han of Iwagakure.

## Background and personality

Quiet, polite, and proud, Kokuō was notably the only tailed beast to briefly break free
of Obito's mind control during the Fourth Shinobi World War, however momentary. After
the war it declares a wish to withdraw and live secluded in the forest — a pacifism born
of exhaustion with being controlled and weaponized. See [[Tailed Beasts]] for the shared
origin all nine tailed beasts trace back to.

## Abilities

A horse-dolphin hybrid beast with immense physical strength (able to knock even
[[Gyūki]] back with a ram of its horns) and a Boil Release (steam-based) ninjutsu
affinity unique among the nine, alongside the shared Tailed Beast Ball.

## Status

Refuses Naruto's telepathic call for help against Urashiki Ōtsutsuki during the *New
Era* One-Tail Escort Arc, wary of renewed human contact despite trusting Naruto
personally; later senses Kurama's death during the Kawaki Arc — the most current status
the ingested source covers.
