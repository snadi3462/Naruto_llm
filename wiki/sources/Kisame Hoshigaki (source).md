# Kisame Hoshigaki (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Kisame Hoshigaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kisame_Hoshigaki)
Raw file: `raw/Kisame Hoshigaki.md`

## Summary

Biography of Kisame Hoshigaki, a partner from his Akatsuki days (see [[Itachi Uchiha]] —
access restricted) and former Seven Ninja Swordsmen of the Mist member: a disillusioned
killer who finds real, if guarded, friendship and purpose alongside that partner, and dies by
suicide to protect Akatsuki's secrets from interrogation.

## Key takeaways

- Deepens [[Itachi Uchiha (source)]]'s brief account of the partnership (access restricted) —
  Kisame's disillusionment after killing his own trusted superior Fuguki (who was secretly a
  traitor) parallels his partner's own comrade-killing history, the basis of their bond.
- Confirms Kisame's loyalty was ultimately to his partner personally, not to Akatsuki's goals
  (see [[Itachi Uchiha]] — access restricted) — after that partner's death, only Obito's
  manipulation (revealing his identity) keeps Kisame engaged with the organization's plans.
- Details his death: cornered during the Fourth Shinobi World War, Kisame chooses assisted
  suicide via a shark-filled water prison rather than risk revealing Akatsuki's secrets under
  interrogation — corroborating his general nihilistic outlook.
