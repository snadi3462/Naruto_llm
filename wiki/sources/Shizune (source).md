# Shizune (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Shizune](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shizune)
Raw file: `raw/Shizune.md`

## Summary

Biography of Shizune, [[Tsunade]] (access restricted)'s devoted apprentice and
assistant, who serves as her right hand through three successive Hokage administrations.

## Key takeaways

- Niece of Tsunade's late lover Dan Katō; accompanied Tsunade during her years-long
  self-exile from Konoha as both attendant and medical apprentice, becoming a
  level-headed foil to Tsunade's recklessness (especially her gambling).
- A highly skilled medical-nin and poison specialist in her own right, once considered
  Tsunade's top disciple before [[Sakura Haruno]] (access restricted) surpassed her;
  leads the Logistical Support and Medical Division during the Fourth Shinobi World War.
- Objects on moral grounds when Tsunade nearly accepts Orochimaru's offer to revive her
  dead loved ones in exchange for healing him, going as far as considering drastic
  measures to stop her.
- Continues serving as Hokage's assistant across Tsunade's, Kakashi Hatake's, and Naruto
  Uzumaki's administrations (all access restricted).
