# A (Third Raikage) (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — A (Third Raikage)](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/A_(Third_Raikage))
Raw file: `raw/A (Third Raikage).md`

## Summary

Biography of A, the Third Raikage, regarded as Kumogakure's greatest ever leader — a
legendary strongman renowned for surviving nearly anything.

## Key takeaways

- Spent his reign personally subduing the Eight-Tails' periodic rampages before finally
  sealing it in [[Killer B]] successfully; mentored [[Darui]] in the rare Black
  Lightning technique.
- Died fighting an army of ten thousand shinobi single-handedly for three days straight
  to let his comrades escape, cementing his legendary "strongest shield" reputation for
  near-total physical durability.
- Reincarnated via Impure World Reincarnation during the Fourth Shinobi World War,
  forced to fight the Allied Forces before being resealed after a climactic duel with
  Naruto Uzumaki (access restricted).
