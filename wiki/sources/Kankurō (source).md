# Kankurō (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Kankurō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kankur%C5%8D)
Raw file: `raw/Kankurō.md`

## Summary

Biography of Kankurō, the middle [[Three Sand Siblings|Sand Sibling]]: a puppet-master
who grows from being terrified of his own brother into one of [[Gaara]]'s most loyal
protectors and bodyguards.

## Key takeaways

- Initially resentful of and intimidated by young Gaara, Kankurō's relationship with him
  transforms after Gaara's reform, becoming fiercely protective — rushing alone to rescue
  Gaara from Akatsuki despite being outmatched, and staying by his side as bodyguard at
  Kage Summits for years afterward.
- A puppet master trained on tools originally built by [[Sasori]]; after killing Sasori in
  the Fourth Shinobi World War, inherits his "Mother and Father" puppets and his old body
  as a new puppet — corroborating [[Sasori (source)]]'s account of that battle's aftermath.
- Cured of Sasori's poison during the Kazekage Rescue Mission by [[Sakura Haruno]] (access
  restricted), corroborating her role in that arc.
- Never marries in the ingested source (a *Gaara Hiden* subplot in which the Suna Council
  tries to arrange a marriage targets Gaara, not Kankurō); remains Suna's most
  front-line-loved shinobi, contrasted with Gaara's more political role.
- Reaches the *New Era: Part II* One-Tail Escort Arc, fighting Urashiki Ōtsutsuki's
  puppets and surviving a major explosion — confirmed alive and safe but not covered
  beyond that point, the most current status this source gives.
