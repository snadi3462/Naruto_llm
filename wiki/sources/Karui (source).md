# Karui (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Karui](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Karui)
Raw file: `raw/Karui.md`

## Summary

Biography of Karui, a Kumogakure kunoichi who later marries [[Chōji Akimichi]] and joins
the Akimichi clan.

## Key takeaways

- Self-assured, outspoken, and impulsive — part of Killer B's genin team alongside Omoi
  and Samui, fiercely loyal to her sensei and initially hostile toward Konoha over
  Sasuke's actions.
- Fights through the Fourth Shinobi World War in the Second Division, wielding kenjutsu
  trained under Killer B.
- Marries [[Chōji Akimichi]] and relocates to Konoha in the *New Era*, becoming a member
  of the Akimichi clan and mother to Chōchō — corroborating [[Chōji Akimichi (source)]].
