# Ino Yamanaka (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Ino Yamanaka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ino_Yamanaka)
Raw file: `raw/Ino Yamanaka.md`

## Summary

Biography of Ino Yamanaka, [[Team 10]]'s mind-technique specialist: her childhood friendship-turned-rivalry with a classmate (see [[Sakura Haruno]] — access restricted), her clan's sensory and mind-affecting techniques, her marriage to [[Sai]], and — critically — the most current-era account yet ingested, running deep into the *New Era: Part II* crisis as Shikamaru Nara's telepathic communications officer.

## Key takeaways

- Confirms and expands the friendship/rivalry arc already on [[Sakura Haruno]]'s page (access restricted), and confirms Ino's marriage to [[Sai]] and their son Inojin, corroborating [[Sai (source)]] and [[Team 10 (Asuma) (source)]].
- Details her role as head of the Konoha Barrier Team and a telepathic communications specialist (Mind Body Transmission Technique), making her the operational hub for village-wide alerts throughout the Kawaki Arc.
- **Major new finding**: Ino's "New Era: Part II" section (Boruto's Return Arc) is the most detailed account yet of the current crisis, touching several restricted characters and pages directly — see [[Naruto Uzumaki]], [[Kurama]], [[Himawari Uzumaki]], and [[Team 7]] "Current crisis" (all access restricted) rather than repeating the details here.
- Also reveals a major new plot thread involving Boruto's treatment by Konoha, with [[Shikamaru Nara]] privately convinced of his innocence but unable to act openly, and Ino refusing his request to secretly reconnect him with Boruto out of loyalty to protocol and suspicion of Boruto herself.
