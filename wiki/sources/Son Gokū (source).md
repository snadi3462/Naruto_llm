# Son Gokū (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Son Gokū](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Son_Gok%C5%AB)
Raw file: `raw/Son Gokū.md`

## Summary

Biography of Son Gokū, the Four-Tails, sealed within Rōshi of Iwagakure — see [[Tailed
Beasts]] for the shared origin/history all nine tailed beasts share.

## Key takeaways

- Prideful and initially hostile toward humans, Son Gokū is won over by Naruto's honesty
  and genuine effort to free it, becoming one of the more openly trusting beasts by the
  war's end — even lending Naruto its Lava Release chakra to help defeat Madara Uchiha.
- A monkey/gorilla-like beast that can combine Fire and Earth chakra into Lava Release,
  breathing streams of green fire and forming volcanoes.
- Extracted from Rōshi by Akatsuki's Kisame Hoshigaki during the Itachi Pursuit Mission,
  killing its jinchūriki, well before the Fourth Shinobi World War.
- Reaches the *New Era* One-Tail Escort Arc as one of the beasts willing to answer
  Naruto's telepathic call for help against Urashiki Ōtsutsuki, and later senses Kurama's
  death during the Kawaki Arc — the most current status this source covers.
