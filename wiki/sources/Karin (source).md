# Karin (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Karin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Karin)
Raw file: `raw/Karin.md`

## Summary

Biography of Karin, an Uzumaki-clan sensor and healer recruited into [[Sasuke Uchiha]]
(access restricted)'s team [[Taka]] after years under Orochimaru's protection.

## Key takeaways

- An Uzumaki clan descendant with a powerful life-force and self-healing "Heal Bite"
  ability; taken in by Orochimaru as a child after her own village was destroyed, and
  later placed in charge of one of his hideouts for her unique sensory ability to detect
  chakra signatures from great distances.
- Develops an intense, unrequited infatuation with Sasuke from their first meeting, which
  sours considerably after he stabs and later orders her killed during his darkest period
  — though she never stops caring what happens to him.
- Materializes an incomplete version of the Adamantine Sealing Chains, an Uzumaki-clan
  ninjutsu, and uses it to significant effect during the Fourth Shinobi World War.
- *New Era*: reconciles with her feelings about Sasuke, content that he's happy with his
  own family; helps deliver [[Sarada Uchiha]] and gives her her signature glasses —
  corroborating [[Sarada Uchiha]]'s own page.
