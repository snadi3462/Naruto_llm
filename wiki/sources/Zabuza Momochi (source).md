# Zabuza Momochi (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Zabuza Momochi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Zabuza_Momochi)
Raw file: `raw/Zabuza Momochi.md`

## Summary

Biography of Zabuza Momochi, "Demon of the Hidden Mist," a missing-nin swordsman whose
confrontation with Team 7 in the Land of Waves is often considered where the series'
stakes first become real.

## Key takeaways

- A former Seven Ninja Swordsmen of the Mist member turned mercenary after a failed coup
  against Kirigakure's Fourth Mizukage; took [[Haku]] in as his personal weapon,
  outwardly treating him as a tool while secretly caring for him deeply.
- Fights [[Kakashi Hatake]] (access restricted) to a standstill in the Land of Waves
  before being defeated; his death scene — turning on his employer Gatō and dying beside
  Haku — is one of the series' formative emotional beats.
- Wields the sentient, self-sharpening sword Kubikiribōchō and is a master of
  near-silent assassination and the Hiding in Mist Technique.
- Reincarnated via Impure World Reincarnation during the Fourth Shinobi World War,
  fighting alongside Haku against the Allied Forces.
