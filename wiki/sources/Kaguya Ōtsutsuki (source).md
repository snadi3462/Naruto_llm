# Kaguya Ōtsutsuki (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Kaguya Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaguya_%C5%8Ctsutsuki)
Raw file: `raw/Kaguya Ōtsutsuki.md`

## Summary

Biography of Kaguya Ōtsutsuki, the ancient matriarch who became Earth's first chakra
user and the true final villain of the original series, sealed away by her own sons and
later revived to fight [[Naruto Uzumaki]] and [[Sasuke Uchiha]].

## Key takeaways

- An Ōtsutsuki clan member sent to harvest a God Tree's power, Kaguya instead ate its
  forbidden fruit herself, becoming the first chakra wielder on Earth and using her new
  power to unify a war-torn world — before growing despotic and turning it into an army
  via the Infinite Tsukuyomi.
- Sealed by her own sons Hagoromo and Hamura after she tried to reclaim their chakra by
  merging with the God Tree as the Ten-Tails; the Moon is literally the sealed remnant
  of that battle.
- Secretly leaves behind Black Zetsu, her own manifested will, who spends centuries
  manipulating the Uchiha and Senju clans (via Madara Uchiha) toward reviving her.
- Revived during the Fourth Shinobi World War at Madara's expense, ultimately defeated
  and resealed by Naruto Uzumaki and Sasuke Uchiha (both access restricted).
