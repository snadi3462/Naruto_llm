# Sai (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Sai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sai)
Raw file: `raw/Sai.md`

## Summary

Exhaustive fan-wiki biography of Sai, the Root-trained replacement for [[Sasuke Uchiha]]
(access restricted) on Team Kakashi: his emotionless Root conditioning and the loss of his
"brother" Shin, his gradual rediscovery of genuine emotion and friendship through Team 7
(access restricted), his marriage to Ino Yamanaka, and his active role as Anbu Chief straight
through the *New Era: Part II* crisis chapters — making this one of the most current sources
ingested so far.

## Key takeaways

- Explains Sai's Root background: raised by [[Orochimaru]]'s former handler Danzō Shimura to
  have no name, personality, or attachments; his one exception was art, encouraged by a Root
  "brother," Shin, whose death (and Sai's later encounter with Shin's reincarnated corpse in
  the war) is the emotional hinge of his character arc.
- Details his rocky introduction to two future teammates (nicknaming one "Ugly," secretly
  tasked with assassinating the other on Danzō's orders — see [[Naruto Uzumaki]],
  [[Sakura Haruno]], [[Sasuke Uchiha]] — access restricted) and his genuine change of heart,
  choosing to protect their bond instead.
- Confirms he marries Ino Yamanaka (taking the Yamanaka name), becomes Anbu Director/Chief,
  and has a son, Inojin — directly corroborating [[Team 10 (Asuma) (source)]]'s account
  of Ino's marriage.
- **Most significant find**: this source's "New Era: Part II" section (Boruto's Return Arc,
  Matsuri and Ryū Arc, Mamushi Arc) has significant current-era developments — see
  [[Naruto Uzumaki]] and [[Team 7]] "Current crisis" (both access restricted) rather than
  repeating them here. Sai serves as Shikamaru's security chief through this period, amid
  unresolved political tension with the Fire Daimyō's advisor Kobu.
- Also confirms Yamato and Sai served together under Kakashi/Yamato-led Team 7 configurations
  (access restricted) during Part II (Tenchi Bridge, Power arc), corroborating
  [[Yamato (source)]].
