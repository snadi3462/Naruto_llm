# Hinata Hyūga (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Hinata Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hy%C5%ABga)
Raw file: `raw/Hinata Hyūga.md`

## Summary

The first dedicated Hinata biography ingested — previously her wiki page was
synthesized entirely from [[Naruto Uzumaki]] (access restricted)'s own source. Covers
her own backstory, personality, and growth independent of his story.

## Key takeaways

- Originally raised as heiress to the Hyūga clan, Hinata is disinherited by her father
  Hiashi after her younger sister Hanabi shows superior talent and Hinata proves
  unwilling to fight her seriously in a clan trial — she's given over to
  [[Kurenai Yūhi]]'s care as a result.
- Her meek, self-doubting personality as a child is directly reshaped by watching
  Naruto's refusal to give up despite the village's low expectations of him; she
  consciously adopts his philosophy as her own path to change.
- Two years after the war, plays a crucial role destroying the Tenseigan during a crisis
  involving Toneri Ōtsutsuki (*The Last: Naruto the Movie*), before her relationship
  with Naruto becomes mutual.
- As a wife and mother, becomes the structured, assertive center of her household —
  turning down a jōnin promotion to prioritize family — while remaining Naruto's
  steadiest supporter through his Hokage tenure.
