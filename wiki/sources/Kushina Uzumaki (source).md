# Kushina Uzumaki (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Kushina Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kushina_Uzumaki)
Raw file: `raw/Kushina Uzumaki.md`

## Summary

First dedicated biography of [[Kushina Uzumaki]] (previously only covered secondhand through
[[Naruto Uzumaki (source)]]). Fan-wiki style: background, personality, appearance, abilities,
her one Part II appearance, and legacy.

## Key takeaways

- **Origin**: born into the Uzumaki clan of Uzushiogakure; sent to Konoha as a child after her
  home village's destruction and enrolled in the Academy specifically because her chakra made
  her the next candidate to inherit [[Kurama]] from the previous jinchūriki, Mito Uzumaki, who
  taught her that love is what tames the Nine-Tails' hatred.
- **Nickname origin**: teased as "Tomato" for her round face/red hair; earned "Red Hot-Blooded
  Habanero" by violently retaliating against bullies.
- **Kumo kidnapping**: as a young genin she was abducted by Kumogakure to exploit her chakra;
  secretly left a trail of plucked hair, which only [[Minato Namikaze]] (a classmate she'd
  dismissed as unreliable) noticed, leading him to rescue her alone. His admiration for the
  hair she hated is what made her fall in love with him — she calls it her "red thread of
  fate." His research into protecting her during the Third Shinobi World War directly produced
  the Rasengan, which she named.
- **Naruto's birth and her death**: matches and slightly deepens [[Minato Namikaze (source)]]'s
  account — the masked man (Obito Uchiha) killed the Anbu guards and midwives, took Naruto
  hostage, then extracted Kurama from Kushina's body once Minato reclaimed the baby. Uzumaki
  clan vitality let her survive the extraction itself, though gravely weakened. She initially
  volunteered to have Kurama resealed into herself to die with it, but Minato talked her into
  instead sealing it into infant Naruto (Yang half in Naruto, Yin half in himself), reasoning
  it would let both parents someday reconnect with their son through the seal. She spent her
  last living moments pouring love and advice into Naruto before dying.
- **Family/personality color**: doted on Minato's genin team, especially found Rin Nohara
  adorable and had "friendly fights" with Obito Uchiha — a detail that lands harder in
  hindsight, since Obito is later revealed as the masked man who kills her.
- **Part II cameo**: appears to Naruto inside his mindscape during his tailed-beast training
  with Killer B (*Fourth Shinobi World War: Countdown*), restrains Kurama with her signature
  Adamantine Sealing Chains, tells him the full story of his birth, and fades into the
  afterlife content after he reassures her he doesn't blame her.
- **Legacy**: she is one of only four known jinchūriki to survive a tailed-beast extraction
  (with Black Zetsu, Killer B, and her son Naruto). Confirms — independently from
  [[Kurama (source)]] — that Kurama's jinchūriki line runs through her direct descendants:
  Naruto, then Himawari Uzumaki after Kurama's rebirth. Posthumous grandmother to Boruto
  (inherits her headstrong energy) and Himawari (inherits her nurturing side and temper).
