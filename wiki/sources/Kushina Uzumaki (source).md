# Kushina Uzumaki (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Kushina Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kushina_Uzumaki)
Raw file: `raw/Kushina Uzumaki.md`

## Summary

First dedicated biography of [[Kushina Uzumaki]] (previously only covered secondhand through
[[Naruto Uzumaki (source)]]). Fan-wiki style: background, personality, appearance, abilities,
her one Part II appearance, and legacy.

## Key takeaways

- **Origin**: born into the Uzumaki clan of Uzushiogakure; sent to Konoha as a child after her
  home village's destruction and enrolled in the Academy specifically because her chakra made
  her the next candidate to inherit [[Kurama]] from the previous jinchūriki, Mito Uzumaki, who
  taught her that love is what tames the Nine-Tails' hatred.
- **Nickname origin**: teased as "Tomato" for her round face/red hair; earned "Red Hot-Blooded
  Habanero" by violently retaliating against bullies.
- **Kumo kidnapping**: as a young genin she was abducted by Kumogakure to exploit her chakra;
  secretly left a trail of plucked hair, which only a classmate she'd dismissed as unreliable
  noticed, leading him to rescue her alone (see [[Minato Namikaze]] — access restricted, for
  his side of the story). His admiration for the hair she hated is what made her fall in love
  with him — she calls it her "red thread of fate." She's the one who named the Rasengan, a
  technique he developed during the Third Shinobi World War (see [[Minato Namikaze]] —
  access restricted).
- **Naruto's birth and her death**: matches and slightly deepens [[Minato Namikaze (source)]]'s
  account, whose specifics belong to her husband's and son's own story (see
  [[Minato Namikaze]], [[Naruto Uzumaki]] — access restricted). The masked man (Obito Uchiha)
  forced a crisis that ended in Kurama being extracted from Kushina's body. Uzumaki clan
  vitality let her survive the extraction itself, though gravely weakened. She initially
  volunteered to have Kurama resealed into herself to die with it. She spent her last living
  moments pouring love and advice into her newborn son before dying.
- **Family/personality color**: doted on Minato's genin team, especially found Rin Nohara
  adorable and had "friendly fights" with Obito Uchiha — a detail that lands harder in
  hindsight, since Obito is later revealed as the masked man who kills her.
- **Part II cameo**: appears inside her son's mindscape during his tailed-beast training with
  Killer B (*Fourth Shinobi World War: Countdown*), restrains Kurama with her signature
  Adamantine Sealing Chains, shares the full story of that night, and fades into the afterlife
  content once reassured she isn't blamed (see [[Naruto Uzumaki]] — access restricted).
- **Legacy**: she is one of only four known jinchūriki to survive a tailed-beast extraction
  (with Black Zetsu, Killer B, and her son). Confirms — independently from
  [[Kurama (source)]] — that Kurama's jinchūriki line runs through her direct descendants:
  her son, then [[Himawari Uzumaki]] after Kurama's rebirth (see [[Naruto Uzumaki]],
  [[Kurama]] — access restricted). Posthumous grandmother to Boruto (inherits her headstrong
  energy) and Himawari (inherits her nurturing side and temper).
