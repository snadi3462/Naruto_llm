# Danzō Shimura (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Danzō Shimura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Danz%C5%8D_Shimura)
Raw file: `raw/Danzō Shimura.md`

## Summary

Biography of Danzō Shimura, founder of the shadow organization Root, lifelong rival of
[[Hiruzen Sarutobi]] (access restricted), and the hidden hand behind many of the
series' darkest events.

## Key takeaways

- A decades-long rival of Hiruzen Sarutobi for the Hokage title, Danzō built Root as a
  black-ops organization operating outside normal village oversight, convinced that
  Konoha's survival required methods its own Hokage were too soft to use.
- Directly engineers the Uchiha clan's downfall — manipulating [[Itachi Uchiha]] (access
  restricted) into massacring his own clan by offering to spare [[Sasuke Uchiha]] (access
  restricted) — and secretly leaks Naruto Uzumaki's jinchūriki status to turn the village
  against him as a child.
- Augments his own body with stolen Sharingan (including Shisui Uchiha's), Hashirama
  Senju's cells (access restricted), and other illicit modifications, all obtained
  through his working relationship with Orochimaru.
- Killed by Sasuke during the Five Kage Summit while attempting a suicide attack against
  him and Tobi/Obito for what he genuinely believed was the village's greater good.
