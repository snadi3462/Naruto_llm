# Matatabi (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Matatabi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Matatabi)
Raw file: `raw/Matatabi.md`

## Summary

Biography of Matatabi, the Two-Tails, sealed within Yugito Nii of Kumogakure — see
[[Tailed Beasts]] for the shared origin/history all nine tailed beasts share.

## Key takeaways

- Unusually formal and polite compared to most tailed beasts, using honorifics even when
  addressing Naruto.
- A flame-wreathed, two-tailed cat beast able to combine great physical strength with
  Fire Release, and — combined with the other tailed beasts' power — capable of
  shattering a senjutsu-enhanced complete Susanoo.
- Extracted from Yugito Nii by Akatsuki's Hidan and Kakuzu, killing its jinchūriki, well
  before the Fourth Shinobi World War.
- Reaches the *New Era* One-Tail Escort Arc as one of the beasts that refuses Naruto's
  call for help against Urashiki Ōtsutsuki, wary of renewed human contact despite
  trusting Naruto personally, and later senses Kurama's death during the Kawaki Arc — the
  most current status this source covers.
