# Saiken (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Saiken](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Saiken)
Raw file: `raw/Saiken.md`

## Summary

Biography of Saiken, the Six-Tails, sealed within Utakata of Kirigakure — see [[Tailed
Beasts]] for the shared origin/history all nine tailed beasts share.

## Key takeaways

- Kinder and less self-serving than most other tailed beasts — cooperates willingly with
  Utakata rather than fighting for control, though still capable of killing in
  self-preservation (it kills the sensei who tried to forcibly extract it from Utakata).
- An enormous slug-like beast able to secrete corrosive, disintegrating substances and
  trap targets in an adhesive Water Release technique.
- Extracted from Utakata by [[Akatsuki]]'s Six Paths of Pain, killing its jinchūriki, well
  before the Fourth Shinobi World War.
- Reaches the *New Era* One-Tail Escort Arc as one of the beasts willing to answer
  Naruto's telepathic call for help against Urashiki Ōtsutsuki, and later senses Kurama's
  death during the Kawaki Arc — the most current status this source covers.
