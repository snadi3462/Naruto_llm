# Minato Namikaze (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Minato Namikaze](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Minato_Namikaze)
Raw file: `raw/Minato Namikaze.md`

## Summary

First dedicated biography of [[Minato Namikaze]] (previously only covered secondhand through
[[Naruto Uzumaki (source)]]). Fan-wiki style: background, personality, appearance, abilities,
Part II war-arc appearances (as a reincarnated Hokage), and legacy.

## Key takeaways

- **Early life**: Academy prodigy, placed on Team 6 under [[Jiraiya]], who mentored him
  personally and came to suspect he might be the "Child of the Prophecy." Rescued
  [[Kushina Uzumaki]] single-handedly from a Kumogakure kidnapping as a young genin, which
  made him famous across multiple nations as "Konoha's Yellow Flash" even before becoming
  Hokage.
- **Team Minato**: led his own genin team — [[Rin Nohara]], [[Obito Uchiha]], and
  [[Kakashi Hatake]] — giving them a bell test that established the "teamwork above orders"
  lesson Obito internalized and Kakashi initially didn't. During the Kannabi Bridge mission,
  transferred command to a newly-jōnin Kakashi; arrived after the fact to rescue Kakashi and
  Rin from Iwa-nin, with Obito already believed dead. Rin's later death (orchestrated by
  Madara Uchiha to corrupt Obito) happened on Minato's watch while he was away on another
  mission — a second instance of the same tragedy repeating.
- **Becoming Hokage**: chosen as Fourth Hokage over [[Orochimaru]] for his war performance.
  The Third Hokage assigned Kakashi to guard the pregnant Kushina, hoping the responsibility
  would help pull him out of the darkness following Obito and Rin's deaths.
- **Naruto's birth and Minato's death**: corroborates [[Kushina Uzumaki (source)]] — Obito
  ("Tobi") attacked during the birth, took infant Naruto hostage, and abducted Kushina once
  Minato reclaimed the baby. Minato later rescued Kushina from being killed by the
  Obito-controlled Kurama, then fought and freed Kurama from Obito's control (branding him
  with a Flying Thunder God seal in the process — this seal becomes the mechanism for
  Minato's shadow clone strike on "Tobi" decades later at the war). With Kurama too
  dangerous to seal whole into an infant, Minato used the Dead Demon Consuming Seal to split
  its chakra, sealing the Yin half into himself and the Yang half into Naruto, weaving in a
  chakra failsafe that could later manifest to repair the seal or let him and Kushina speak
  to Naruto again. Died as his soul was consumed by the Shinigami.
- **Legacy set up by his death**: because he could never explain what really happened that
  night, Konoha villagers wrongly suspected the Uchiha clan — a root contributor to the
  resentment that fed into the [[Sasuke Uchiha|Uchiha Clan Downfall]] (cross-reference:
  [[Tobirama Senju]]'s Military Police Force is the other identified root cause). The Third
  Hokage resumed the Hokage seat after Minato's death and hid Naruto's parentage to protect
  him from his father's enemies.
- **War-arc reincarnation** (Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax / Kaguya Ōtsutsuki Strikes):
  reincarnated by Orochimaru at Sasuke's request alongside the other three deceased Hokage.
  Fights alongside Naruto, loses both arms in combat against Madara/Obito-as-Ten-Tails-
  jinchūriki, personally strikes down "Tobi" with a shadow clone via the old Flying Thunder
  God seal before realizing it's Obito, and is present (unable to help directly) when Black
  Zetsu steals his half of Kurama's chakra from him during the attempt to save Naruto's life.
  Reunites with Naruto once more after the war ends, congratulates him on his 17th birthday,
  and returns to the afterlife.
- **Pain's Assault cameo**: appears within Naruto's subconscious via his sealed chakra
  failsafe to stop Naruto from removing Kurama's seal, meets his son for the first time
  emotionally, and expresses faith in him before restoring the seal.
