# Kurama (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Kurama](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kurama)
Raw file: `raw/Kurama.md`

## Summary

Exhaustive dedicated biography of Kurama, the Nine-Tails — the first dedicated source on
the beast itself rather than one referenced only through [[Naruto Uzumaki]]'s (access
restricted) own story. See [[Tailed Beasts]] for the lore all nine tailed beasts share.

## Key takeaways

- **Resolves the wiki's open "successor" question**: after Kurama dies from Baryon
  Mode's toll during the Kawaki Arc, the remaining kernels of its chakra find their way
  into [[Himawari Uzumaki]], and Kurama itself reforms as a small cub within her — much
  as it first did when created by Hagoromo Ōtsutsuki. This directly resolves the
  mechanism [[Sakura Haruno (source)]] and [[Ino Yamanaka (source)]] had left unexplained
  on Kurama's and Himawari's pages.
- Details Kurama's *New Era: Part II* relationship with Himawari: it takes on a
  mentoring role, training her to control her newfound tailed-beast-level power, much as
  it once did for Naruto — and is shown to genuinely care for the wider Uzumaki family,
  not just its jinchūriki.
- Corroborates the character-page account of Kurama's antagonistic early relationship
  with Naruto, their eventual friendship, and Kurama's self-sacrifice against Isshiki
  Ōtsutsuki (access restricted) to protect him.
