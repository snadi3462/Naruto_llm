# Isobu (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Isobu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Isobu)
Raw file: `raw/Isobu.md`

## Summary

Biography of Isobu, the Three-Tails, most recently sealed within Yagura Karatachi of
Kirigakure — see [[Tailed Beasts]] for the shared origin/history all nine tailed beasts
share.

## Key takeaways

- Timid-mannered but destructive, Isobu was once secretly sealed within [[Rin Nohara]]
  (access restricted) as a planned weapon against Konoha; Rin's choice to die rather than
  let the plan succeed is the first known instance of a tailed beast "dying" and reviving
  without formal extraction.
- Later sealed within Yagura, the Fourth Mizukage, who was secretly controlled via
  genjutsu by [[Obito Uchiha]] for years until [[Ao]] broke the spell.
- A turtle/crab-like beast with a near-impenetrable shell, capable of rolling into a
  high-speed offensive ball and generating coral and powerful tidal waves.
- Captured and sealed by [[Akatsuki]] (Deidara and Tobi) before the Fourth Shinobi World
  War. Reaches the *New Era* One-Tail Escort Arc as one of the beasts willing to answer
  Naruto's telepathic call for help against Urashiki Ōtsutsuki, and later senses Kurama's
  death during the Kawaki Arc — the most current status this source covers.
