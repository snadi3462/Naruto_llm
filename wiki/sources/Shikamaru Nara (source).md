# Shikamaru Nara (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Shikamaru Nara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikamaru_Nara)
Raw file: `raw/Shikamaru Nara.md`

## Summary

First **dedicated** biography of [[Shikamaru Nara]] — previously only a stub built
secondhand from his teammates', colleagues', and wife's biographies. This is also, by a wide
margin, the wiki's deepest look yet into the *New Era: Part II* crisis, since Shikamaru is
now revealed to hold the Hokage seat itself. Full background, personality, Team 10 arc,
Asuma's death/revenge mission, War arc, marriage to Temari, post-war career, and an extensive
New Era section.

## Key takeaways

- **Origin of the Shikamaru–Chōji friendship**: predates the Academy — young Shikamaru
  defended Chōji from kids excluding him as "stupid and bad" and invited him to cloud-watch;
  they've been inseparable since. Corroborates but predates the account already on
  [[Chōji Akimichi]]'s page.
- **Genius hidden by laziness**: poor Academy grades were pure laziness, not inability — Asuma
  discovered his 200+ IQ by disguising intelligence tests as games. Sole genin promoted to
  chūnin out of the whole Chūnin Exams class, based on tactical judgment (forfeiting a won
  match against Temari) rather than a win.
- **Asuma's death, precisely**: Hidan's blood ritual linked his and Asuma's fates; Shikamaru
  had objected to Asuma's battle plan as too risky, and it cost Asuma his life. Asuma's dying
  words entrusted him with "the king" (the next generation, per their shōgi metaphor).
  Shikamaru then personally planned and led the revenge mission — trapping Hidan in Nara
  clan forest with explosive tags and detonating them with one of Asuma's own cigarettes.
- **War arc**: deputy/field commander of the Fourth Division; after his father Shikaku's
  death at Allied HQ, became the Alliance's chief strategist, coordinating the mass
  Ino–Shika–Chō restraint of the Ten-Tails.
- **Marriage to Temari**: deepens across the Sasuke Recovery aftermath, Sunagakure Support
  Mission, and *Gaara Hiden*; in *Shikamaru Hiden* her arrival breaks a genjutsu-induced
  manipulation (Gengo) over him, after which they date. Married by *Konoha Hiden*; one son,
  Shikadai. Confirms the "Shikamaru easygoing / Temari strict" household dynamic already on
  his stub page.
- **Post-war career**: chief coordinator of the Shinobi Union after the war, then Sixth Hokage
  Kakashi's assistant, then Seventh Hokage Naruto's chief aide — handling paperwork,
  intelligence, and running the village whenever Naruto is away.
- **New Era — the most significant find**: after Naruto's apparent death/disappearance
  (sealed away via Kawaki's Daikokuten), **Shikamaru was appointed Eighth Hokage on an
  explicitly provisional, three-year basis** — a more specific and higher-stakes status than
  the wiki's prior "acting/interim Hokage" framing from [[Sai (source)]] and
  [[Ino Yamanaka (source)]]. As Hokage he issued a formal **execution order against Boruto**
  (believed to have killed Naruto) and **re-listed Sasuke Uchiha as a wanted criminal** —
  decisions Sarada Uchiha repeatedly and unsuccessfully demanded he rescind.
- **Why Boruto is believed guilty — resolved**: everyone's memories, Shikamaru's included,
  were altered by an antagonist technique, **Ada's Omnipotence**, which swapped Boruto's and
  Kawaki's histories in the village's collective memory and suppressed lingering doubts over
  time. Shikamaru's belief in Boruto's guilt was therefore genuine, if uneasy — not
  political theater.
- **The secret rescue operation**: Shikamaru's resolve cracked when Sai's surveillance
  revealed that Amado had independently concluded memories had been altered. Combined with
  pressure from Konoha elders Koharu and Homura to permanently claim the Hokage title, this
  pushed Shikamaru to secretly support Boruto while publicly maintaining the hunt. Key
  operatives: **Ino Yamanaka** (off-record comms, later refuses further protocol breaches as
  too risky), **Mitsuki** (secretly given the code to free Boruto from his cuffs, staged as an
  escape), **Inojin Yamanaka** (mind-transmission contact with Koji Kashin, infiltrates elder
  Kobu's body during an interrogation crisis), **Sai** (learns of and reluctantly backs the
  plan once confronted), **Konohamaru Sarutobi** (told the truth, asked to help both sides),
  and **Koji Kashin** (a mysterious ally feeding intel via toad summons, who explicitly
  praises Shikamaru for "trusting his heart" over procedure).
- **Ongoing political threat**: the Council of Elders, backed by the Fire Daimyō's advisor
  Kobu, opened a formal inquiry into Shikamaru's "validity as village leader," and the
  investigation has traced Boruto's escape from custody back to Shikamaru himself — Kobu vows
  to expose it. Unresolved as of the Mamushi Arc, the most current chapter this source
  reaches.
