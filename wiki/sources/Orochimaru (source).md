# Orochimaru (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru)
Raw file: `raw/Orochimaru.md`

## Summary

Exhaustive fan-wiki biography of Orochimaru, one of the three legendary Sannin (see
[[Tsunade]], [[Jiraiya]] — access restricted, for the other two): his descent from prodigy to
human-experimenting missing-nin, his long antagonism toward [[Sasuke Uchiha]] (access
restricted) and Konoha, his eventual reform, and his role in the *New Era* as a
semi-reformed researcher and father to the synthetic human Mitsuki.

## Key takeaways

- Establishes the Sannin's shared origin: the three trained together under the Third Hokage
  (see [[Hiruzen Sarutobi]] — access restricted) and earned the "Sannin" title together after
  surviving Hanzō — the same team that later fractures completely, with Orochimaru as the one
  who defects.
- Explains why he defected: caught performing human experimentation (including the
  Hashirama-DNA experiments that produced [[Yamato]]) by his old sensei, who couldn't bring
  himself to kill his own student and let him escape (see [[Hiruzen Sarutobi]] — access
  restricted).
- Details his long campaign against Konoha: orchestrating the Chūnin Exams invasion (Konoha
  Crush) that kills the Third Hokage, mentoring [[Sasuke Uchiha]] as a body-vessel candidate,
  and being absorbed into his own body when his student turns the tables on him during the
  Itachi pursuit (all access restricted) — a plot beat [[Sasuke Uchiha (source)]] should be
  cross-checked against. Orochimaru is later revived and fights alongside Konoha during the
  war.
- Confirms his post-war pardon (under 24-hour surveillance) and reform into a legitimate
  researcher: reestablishes Otogakure, creates synthetic humans (Log, Mitsuki) via cloning
  rather than kidnapping, and sends his "son" Mitsuki to live in Konoha under a protector's
  watch (see [[Naruto Uzumaki]] — access restricted) — a striking contrast to his Part I/II
  characterization.
- This source's coverage ends in the *New Era* (Kara Actuation Arc) and does **not** reach
  the *New Era: Part II* crisis chapters — unlike [[Sai (source)]], it gives no
  indication of Orochimaru's status during that crisis.
