# Deidara (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Deidara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Deidara)
Raw file: `raw/Deidara.md`

## Summary

Biography of Deidara, Akatsuki's explosive-clay artist: an Iwagakure defector forcibly
recruited after a humiliating genjutsu defeat (see [[Itachi Uchiha]] — access restricted),
partnered first with [[Sasori]] and later with Tobi (Obito), whose obsessive belief that "art
is a fleeting explosion" defines both his fighting style and his death — a self-detonation
against a rival (see [[Sasuke Uchiha]] — access restricted).

## Key takeaways

- Explains his lifelong grudge (see [[Itachi Uchiha]] — access restricted) and how it
  transfers by proxy to his final opponent during their climactic battle (see
  [[Sasuke Uchiha]] — access restricted) — corroborating and expanding [[Sasuke Uchiha
  (source)]]'s brief account of the same fight.
- Details his artistic philosophy clash with [[Sasori]] (fleeting explosions vs. eternal
  puppets) as the defining friction — and mutual respect — of their partnership.
- Confirms his death: after losing both arms in escalating fights (Gaara's sand, a rival's
  dimensional technique — see [[Kakashi Hatake]], access restricted), Deidara detonates
  himself in a final suicide bombing, a sacrifice ultimately rendered pointless when his
  opponent escapes (see [[Sasuke Uchiha]] — access restricted).
