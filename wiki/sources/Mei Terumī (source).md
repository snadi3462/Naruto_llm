# Mei Terumī (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Mei Terumī](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mei_Terum%C4%AB)
Raw file: `raw/Mei Terumī.md`

## Summary

Biography of Mei Terumī, the Fifth Mizukage, who took over Kirigakure after the
genjutsu-manipulated reign of Yagura Karatachi and worked to reform the village's
brutal history.

## Key takeaways

- Kind, diplomatically open-minded, and the first Kage to genuinely respect [[Gaara]]
  despite his youth; also flirtatious and notoriously touchy about her age/marital
  status, a running joke throughout the source.
- Wields two rare kekkei genkai — Lava Release and Boil Release — powerful enough to
  seriously wound [[Sasuke Uchiha]] (access restricted) even through his Susanoo.
- Central to the Five Kage Summit and Fourth Shinobi World War: confronts Sasuke
  directly, helps expose Danzō Shimura's manipulation of the Summit, and fights
  alongside the other Kage against the reincarnated Madara Uchiha.
- Accompanied throughout by her two bodyguards, [[Chōjūrō]] and [[Ao]]; eventually
  succeeded as Mizukage by Chōjūrō in the *New Era*.
