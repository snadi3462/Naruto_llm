# Kokuō (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Kokuō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Koku%C5%8D)
Raw file: `raw/Kokuō.md`

## Summary

Biography of Kokuō, the Five-Tails, sealed within Han of Iwagakure — see [[Tailed
Beasts]] for the shared origin/history all nine tailed beasts share.

## Key takeaways

- Quiet, polite, and proud, Kokuō was the only tailed beast to briefly break free of
  Obito's mind control during the Fourth Shinobi World War, however momentarily.
- A horse-dolphin hybrid beast with immense physical strength and a Boil Release
  (steam-based) ninjutsu affinity unique among the tailed beasts.
- Declares after the war that it wants to withdraw and live secluded in the forest,
  explicitly wary of further human contact.
- Reaches the *New Era* One-Tail Escort Arc as one of the beasts that refuses Naruto's
  call for help against Urashiki Ōtsutsuki out of that same wariness, despite trusting
  Naruto personally, and later senses Kurama's death during the Kawaki Arc — the most
  current status this source covers.
