# Suigetsu Hōzuki (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Suigetsu Hōzuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Suigetsu_H%C5%8Dzuki)
Raw file: `raw/Suigetsu Hōzuki.md`

## Summary

Biography of Suigetsu Hōzuki, a Kirigakure swordsman freed by [[Sasuke Uchiha]] (access
restricted) from Orochimaru's imprisonment and recruited into his team [[Taka]].

## Key takeaways

- Obsessed with collecting the blades of the Seven Ninja Swordsmen of the Mist (a legacy
  from his late brother Mangetsu); joins Sasuke's team chasing that goal as much as
  loyalty, taking Zabuza Momochi's sword early on and coveting Kisame Hoshigaki's Samehada.
- Outwardly mercenary and mocking (especially toward teammate [[Karin]]), but grows
  genuinely protective of his team, risking his life against Killer B's tailed-beast form
  during the Fourth Shinobi World War.
- Can liquefy his entire body (Hōzuki clan Hydrification Technique), making him nearly
  impossible to strike physically but vulnerable to lightning-natured attacks.
- The ingested source's coverage of his post-war/New Era status is thin — the last
  confirmed appearances are wartime.
