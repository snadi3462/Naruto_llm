# Chōmei (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Chōmei](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%8Dmei)
Raw file: `raw/Chōmei.md`

## Summary

Biography of Chōmei, the Seven-Tails, formerly sealed within Fū of Takigakure — see
[[Tailed Beasts]] for the shared origin/history all nine tailed beasts share.

## Key takeaways

- Happy-go-lucky, sharing its jinchūriki's cheerful disposition; the only tailed beast
  affiliated with a village outside the Five Great Shinobi Countries (Takigakure).
- A beetle-like beast, uniquely undergoing a full larval-to-adult transformation over its
  lifetime — the only tailed beast known to permanently change appearance this way.
- Extracted from Fū by Akatsuki's Hidan and Kakuzu well before the Fourth Shinobi World
  War, after an earlier extraction attempt during a joint Chūnin Exams was foiled by Neji
  Hyūga.
- Reaches the *New Era* One-Tail Escort Arc as one of the beasts that refuses Naruto's
  call for help against Urashiki Ōtsutsuki, wary of renewed human contact despite
  trusting Naruto personally, and later senses Kurama's death during the Kawaki Arc — the
  most current status this source covers.
