# Shukaku (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Shukaku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shukaku)
Raw file: `raw/Shukaku.md`

## Summary

Biography of Shukaku, the One-Tail, [[Gaara]]'s tailed beast — see [[Tailed Beasts]] for
the shared origin/history all nine tailed beasts share.

## Key takeaways

- Childish, bombastic, and once genuinely murderous — Shukaku's threats of taking over
  Gaara's body if he ever slept were the direct cause of young Gaara's insomnia, and its
  hatred of humans (for being hunted and weaponized across centuries) initially made it
  one of the more dangerous tailed beasts to its own jinchūriki.
- Harbors a long-standing rivalry with [[Kurama]], resentful of the fox's belief that a
  beast's strength scales with its tail count (Shukaku, with only one tail, is considered
  the weakest by that measure).
- Its relationship with Gaara mirrors the arc covered in [[Gaara (source)]]: as Gaara
  reforms and comes to genuinely thank Shukaku for shaping who he became, Shukaku
  reciprocates by fighting earnestly to protect him rather than merely tolerating him.
- Reaches the *New Era* One-Tail Escort Arc as the direct target of Urashiki Ōtsutsuki's
  chakra-draining attacks, willingly sealing itself in a kettle at one point to evade
  detection — the most current status this source covers.
