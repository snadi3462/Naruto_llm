## Summary

Biography of Nagato, secretly the true identity behind "Pain," [[Akatsuki]]'s leader after
Yahiko's death: an Uzumaki-descended Rinnegan wielder secretly implanted with the eyes by
Madara Uchiha as a child, radicalized by Yahiko's death into believing only shared suffering
could force the world to peace, and ultimately redeemed by someone who reaches him (see
[[Naruto Uzumaki]] — access restricted).

## Key takeaways

- Reveals Nagato was secretly given Madara Uchiha's Rinnegan as an infant, without his
  knowledge — the origin of both his power and (unknowingly) his lifelong manipulation as a
  pawn in Madara/Obito's Eye of the Moon Plan, corroborating [[Madara Uchiha (source)]].
- Details the trio's shared founding of [[Akatsuki]] with [[Yahiko]] and [[Konan]] under
  a wandering Konoha shinobi's early mentorship (see [[Jiraiya]] — access restricted), and
  Nagato's transformation into "Pain" after Yahiko's death — turning Yahiko's own corpse into
  the Deva Path, the public face of Akatsuki's leadership for the rest of the story.
- Confirms his role in Konoha's destruction during Pain's Assault, before being talked down
  from Akatsuki's philosophy (see [[Jiraiya]], [[Naruto Uzumaki]] — access restricted, for
  what happens to each of them in this arc) — Nagato sacrifices his own life reviving everyone
  he killed in the attack.
- This is the first dedicated source on Nagato/Pain; previously he was only referenced in
  passing across other characters' pages (e.g. [[Asuma Sarutobi]], [[Might Guy]]).
