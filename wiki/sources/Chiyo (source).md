# Chiyo (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Chiyo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chiyo)
Raw file: `raw/Chiyo.md`

## Summary

Biography of Chiyo, retired Sunagakure elder, puppet master, and grandmother of
[[Sasori]] — the woman who sealed Shukaku into [[Gaara]] and later gave her life to
revive him.

## Key takeaways

- A legendary puppeteer and poison expert whose rivalry with [[Tsunade]] (access
  restricted) dates back to the Second Shinobi World War; personally responsible for
  sealing Shukaku into infant Gaara.
- Raised her grandson Sasori after his parents' deaths, teaching him puppetry — only to
  watch him grow increasingly detached and eventually abandon both her and Sunagakure to
  join [[Akatsuki]].
- During the Kazekage Rescue Mission, fights alongside [[Sakura Haruno]] (access
  restricted) to defeat Sasori, then sacrifices her own life using a forbidden technique
  to revive Gaara, dying at peace and hopeful for lasting Suna-Konoha friendship.
- Reincarnated via Impure World Reincarnation during the Fourth Shinobi World War, one
  of the few reincarnated shinobi never defeated in battle — only released voluntarily.
