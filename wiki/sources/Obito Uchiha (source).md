# Obito Uchiha (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Obito Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha)
Raw file: `raw/Obito Uchiha.md`

## Summary

First dedicated biography of [[Obito Uchiha]] (previously only covered secondhand through
[[Kakashi Hatake (source)]], [[Naruto Uzumaki (source)]], [[Madara Uchiha (source)]], and
[[Zetsu (source)]]). Adds precision to the Kannabi Bridge incident, the Obito/Kakashi
"comrades" philosophy's exact origin, ability specifics, and his final death, all previously
only summarized secondhand.

## Key takeaways

- **Background/personality**: grew up parentless, driven by a need to be acknowledged, hence
  his own Hokage dream — a detail that mirrors a similarly-orphaned peer's parallel origin
  (see [[Naruto Uzumaki]] — access restricted). A late bloomer at the Academy, chronically
  late to things because he stopped to help villagers along the way, and nursed an
  unconfessed crush on [[Rin Nohara]].
- **Kannabi Bridge, precisely**: with a young teammate in command (see [[Kakashi Hatake]] —
  access restricted), that teammate chose to abandon a captured Rin to complete the mission;
  Obito objected and left alone to save her, telling him he was "worse than trash" for
  abandoning a friend. **This is the origin of the "trash/comrades" line** — said by Obito to
  that teammate, not the reverse as later assumed from his own retellings. Moved by this, the
  teammate saved Obito from an enemy ninja, losing his own left eye — the trigger for Obito's
  Sharingan awakening. During the cave-in that follows, Obito shoves him clear of a falling
  boulder and is crushed in his place; while trapped, he has Rin transplant his Sharingan into
  the teammate's empty socket, framed as an apology for forgetting to get him a promotion
  gift. His last thought before presumed death is regret at never telling Rin he loved her.
- **The "comrades" philosophy's legacy**: that teammate adopts Obito's line as his own
  defining belief and later teaches it to his own students, including [[Team 7]] (access
  restricted) — resolving what the wiki previously only had from his side as an inherited
  belief with no clear origin point.
- **Rescue and Rinnegan**: corroborates [[Madara Uchiha (source)]]'s account of Madara
  rebuilding Obito's body (right side reconstructed from White Zetsu matter plus Hashirama-cell
  grafts, granting Wood Release and rapid regeneration) and orchestrating Rin's death to
  deepen his despair. Adds that Obito later took a Rinnegan from Nagato's corpse and implanted
  it himself, granting Outer Path techniques (Demonic Statue summoning, chakra chains,
  Preta Path absorption, Samsara of Heavenly Life revival).
- **Ten-Tails jinchūriki**: sealed the Ten-Tails into himself after taking it from Madara's
  plan; initially struggled for control before mastering it, becoming powerful enough to fight
  three reincarnated Hokage simultaneously — senjutsu remained his one clear weakness.
- **Final death, precisely**: fighting Kaguya Ōtsutsuki, she pins two of that teammate's own
  students and attacks with All-Killing Ash Bones (see [[Naruto Uzumaki]], [[Sasuke Uchiha]]
  — access restricted). Obito and that teammate, moved by a shared memory of their old
  friendship, shield the two together (see [[Kakashi Hatake]] — access restricted) — Obito
  uses Kamui to teleport away the strike aimed at his teammate, deliberately letting the one
  aimed at himself land instead, an explicit narrative echo of the original Kannabi Bridge
  boulder sacrifice. He warns his teammate "I won't be around to save you a third time" and
  entrusts the future to one of the two students he shielded (see [[Naruto Uzumaki]] — access
  restricted), dying smiling and fully reconciled. His spirit briefly reunites with Rin in
  the afterlife's limbo before returning once more via Kamui to lend his teammate's power a
  final boost against Kaguya.
- **Legacy**: the Uchiha clan wanted Obito's Sharingan-bearing eye back after his presumed
  death; clan head Fugaku Uchiha let his old teammate keep it in Obito's honor (see
  [[Kakashi Hatake]] — access restricted). That teammate's "Copy Ninja" nickname is partly
  built on the gifted eye.
