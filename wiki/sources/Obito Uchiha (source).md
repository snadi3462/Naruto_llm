# Obito Uchiha (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Obito Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha)
Raw file: `raw/Obito Uchiha.md`

## Summary

First dedicated biography of [[Obito Uchiha]] (previously only covered secondhand through
[[Kakashi Hatake (source)]], [[Naruto Uzumaki (source)]], [[Madara Uchiha (source)]], and
[[Zetsu (source)]]). Adds precision to the Kannabi Bridge incident, the Obito/Kakashi
"comrades" philosophy's exact origin, ability specifics, and his final death, all previously
only summarized secondhand.

## Key takeaways

- **Background/personality**: grew up parentless, driven by a need to be acknowledged, hence
  his own Hokage dream — a detail that mirrors [[Naruto Uzumaki]]'s parallel origin. A late
  bloomer at the Academy, chronically late to things because he stopped to help villagers
  along the way, and nursed an unconfessed crush on [[Rin Nohara]].
- **Kannabi Bridge, precisely**: with [[Kakashi Hatake]] in command, Kakashi chose to abandon
  a captured Rin to complete the mission; Obito objected and left alone to save her, telling
  Kakashi he was "worse than trash" for abandoning a friend. **This is the origin of the
  "trash/comrades" line** — said by Obito to Kakashi, not the reverse as later assumed from
  Kakashi's own retellings. Kakashi, moved by this, saved Obito from an enemy ninja, losing
  his own left eye — the trigger for Obito's Sharingan awakening. During the cave-in that
  follows, Obito shoves Kakashi clear of a falling boulder and is crushed in his place;
  while trapped, he has Rin transplant his Sharingan into Kakashi's empty socket, framed as an
  apology for forgetting to get him a promotion gift. His last thought before presumed death
  is regret at never telling Rin he loved her.
- **The "comrades" philosophy's legacy**: Kakashi adopts Obito's line as his own defining
  belief and later teaches it to his own students, including [[Team 7]] — resolving what the
  wiki previously only had from Kakashi's side as an inherited belief with no clear origin
  point.
- **Rescue and Rinnegan**: corroborates [[Madara Uchiha (source)]]'s account of Madara
  rebuilding Obito's body (right side reconstructed from White Zetsu matter plus Hashirama-cell
  grafts, granting Wood Release and rapid regeneration) and orchestrating Rin's death to
  deepen his despair. Adds that Obito later took a Rinnegan from Nagato's corpse and implanted
  it himself, granting Outer Path techniques (Demonic Statue summoning, chakra chains,
  Preta Path absorption, Samsara of Heavenly Life revival).
- **Ten-Tails jinchūriki**: sealed the Ten-Tails into himself after taking it from Madara's
  plan; initially struggled for control before mastering it, becoming powerful enough to fight
  three reincarnated Hokage simultaneously — senjutsu remained his one clear weakness.
- **Final death, precisely**: fighting Kaguya Ōtsutsuki, she pins Naruto and Sasuke and
  attacks with All-Killing Ash Bones. Obito and Kakashi, moved by a shared memory of their old
  friendship, shield the two together — Obito uses Kamui to teleport away the strike aimed at
  Kakashi, deliberately letting the one aimed at himself land instead, an explicit narrative
  echo of the original Kannabi Bridge boulder sacrifice. He warns Kakashi "I won't be around to
  save you a third time" and entrusts the future to Naruto, dying smiling and fully
  reconciled. His spirit briefly reunites with Rin in the afterlife's limbo before returning
  once more via Kamui to lend Kakashi his power for the final push against Kaguya.
- **Legacy**: the Uchiha clan wanted Obito's Sharingan-bearing eye back after his presumed
  death; clan head Fugaku Uchiha let Kakashi keep it in Obito's honor. Kakashi's nickname
  "Copy Ninja" is partly built on the gifted eye.
