# Anko Mitarashi (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Anko Mitarashi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Anko_Mitarashi)
Raw file: `raw/Anko Mitarashi.md`

## Summary

Biography of Anko Mitarashi, a Konoha tokubetsu jōnin and former student of Orochimaru,
left permanently marked — physically and psychologically — by his experiments.

## Key takeaways

- One of ten test subjects for Orochimaru's Cursed Seal of Heaven, and the only
  survivor; he later erased her memory rather than kill her, leaving her to piece
  together a life without knowing whether she ever truly rejected him.
- Laid-back, brash, and sweet-toothed (dango is practically a defining trait), with a
  darker undercurrent of trauma from Orochimaru's betrayal that fuels her determination
  to personally stop him.
- Proctors the Chūnin Exams' Forest of Death stage, where she's the first to detect
  Orochimaru's infiltration of the exams and confronts him directly, reawakening her
  dormant cursed seal in the process.
- Later teaches at Konoha's Academy; her cursed seal is eventually removed after the
  Fourth Shinobi World War.
