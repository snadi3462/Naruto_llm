# Jūgo (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Jūgo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%ABgo)
Raw file: `raw/Jūgo.md`

## Summary

Biography of Jūgo, a gentle recluse prone to uncontrollable violent rages, recruited into
[[Sasuke Uchiha]] (access restricted)'s team [[Taka]] as its most physically dangerous
member.

## Key takeaways

- A naturally kind, animal-loving person whose clan's unique ability to absorb natural
  energy comes with random, uncontrollable fits of murderous rage; secludes himself to
  avoid hurting anyone until Kimimaro, and later Sasuke, become people whose presence
  alone can keep his violence in check.
- His clan's blood is the biological origin of Orochimaru's cursed seal technique,
  branded onto various followers using enzymes isolated from Jūgo himself.
- Fully devoted to Sasuke as Kimimaro's successor/legacy — remains loyal even after Taka
  disbands, choosing to return to Sasuke's side as, in his own words, his "shield."
- The ingested source's coverage of his post-war/*New Era* status is thin — his last
  confirmed appearances are wartime, still with [[Taka]].
