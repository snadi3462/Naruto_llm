# Haku (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Haku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Haku)
Raw file: `raw/Haku.md`

## Summary

Biography of Haku, an orphaned Ice Release user devoted to [[Zabuza Momochi]], and the
first genuine emotional gut-punch of the series' Land of Waves arc.

## Key takeaways

- Orphaned as a child after his own father killed his mother and tried to kill him for
  their shared kekkei genkai; taken in by Zabuza, who offered him purpose as a "tool"
  rather than a person — a role Haku embraces gladly, mistaking usefulness for love.
- Gentle and reluctant to kill despite his lethal skill, believing a person becomes
  truly strong only when they have someone important to protect — a philosophy that
  directly influences [[Naruto Uzumaki]] (access restricted).
- Wields Ice Release (combined Water and Wind chakra), including the signature Demonic
  Mirroring Ice Crystals technique; considered by Zabuza and Kakashi Hatake (access
  restricted) to be more skilled than an Anbu member despite his young age.
- Dies shielding Zabuza from a fatal blow during their battle with Team 7, having found
  one final purpose in being useful to him; reincarnated via Impure World Reincarnation
  during the Fourth Shinobi World War alongside Zabuza.
