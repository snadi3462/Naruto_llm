# Jiraiya (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Jiraiya](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jiraiya)
Raw file: `raw/Jiraiya.md`

## Summary

First dedicated biography of [[Jiraiya]] (previously only covered secondhand through
[[Naruto Uzumaki (source)]]). Fan-wiki style: background, personality, abilities, and a
detailed account of his death in Amegakure.

## Key takeaways

- **Sannin origin**: trained as a genin alongside [[Orochimaru]] and [[Tsunade]] under
  Hiruzen Sarutobi; the three were the sole survivors of a Second Shinobi World War clash with
  Hanzō of Amegakure, who personally dubbed them the "Legendary Sannin."
- **Mount Myōboku / senjutsu**: discovered the toads and trained under toad elder Fukasaku;
  the Great Toad Sage prophesied he would train a "Child of Prophecy" who would either save or
  destroy the world.
- **The Ame orphans**: out of war-guilt, spent three years training three orphans in
  Amegakure — Yahiko, Nagato, and Konan — identifying Rinnegan-bearer Nagato as the prophesied
  child. Nagato's idealism about ending the cycle of hatred directly inspired Jiraiya's debut
  novel, *The Tale of the Utterly Gutsy Shinobi*, which he dedicated to Nagato. Left them to
  return to Konoha, never learning until his final mission that Yahiko had died and Nagato had
  become "Pain," leader of [[Akatsuki]] — a legacy failure that Jiraiya confronts fatally.
  Cross-references [[Yahiko]], [[Nagato]], [[Konan]].
- **Minato Namikaze**: took Minato on as a personal disciple, suspecting *he* might be the
  Child of Prophecy, and contributed directly to his development of the Rasengan. Minato later
  named his son after the protagonist of Jiraiya's novel, making Jiraiya Naruto's godfather.
  Cross-references [[Minato Namikaze (source)]].
- **Personality**: boisterous, self-deprecating showman and self-proclaimed "super pervert" —
  his peeping "research" fueled the wildly popular adult *Icha Icha* novels after his debut
  book flopped commercially. Refused the Hokage title multiple times, feeling disqualified by
  his own conduct. Privately driven by a sense of failure (losing Tsunade's heart, failing to
  redeem Orochimaru, failing to save Hiruzen and Minato) balanced against an indomitable
  refusal to give up — a philosophy passed on to all three of his students, most consequentially
  Naruto.
- **Final student**: took on Naruto during the Chūnin Exams arc, training him in the Rasengan
  and Sage Mode fundamentals and taking him on the search for Tsunade and the following
  two-and-a-half-year training journey.
- **Death**: infiltrating Amegakure to confirm Akatsuki's leadership, fought Konan, then the
  Six Paths of Pain — reanimated corpses remotely controlled by Nagato, including Yahiko's
  body as the Deva Path. Killed three Paths but was overwhelmed once all six combined; fatally
  wounded, he used his last strength to carve a coded dying message into Fukasaku's back
  before dying. Naruto later decrypted the message using *Icha Icha Tactics* as a cipher key,
  revealing Nagato as the true controller behind Pain — intelligence that proves decisive in
  Naruto's own fight with Pain.
- **Legacy**: his death is the direct catalyst for Naruto's Sage Mode mastery and his choice to
  forgive rather than avenge Nagato, fulfilling the anti-hatred message of Jiraiya's own novel.
  The Great Toad Sage retroactively revises the prophecy to cover two Children of Prophecy
  (Nagato and Naruto) that Jiraiya trained. Naruto later builds him a memorial stone.
