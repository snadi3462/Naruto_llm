# Ao (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Ao](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ao)
Raw file: `raw/Ao.md`

## Summary

Biography of Ao, a veteran Kirigakure hunter-nin who serves as [[Mei Terumī]]'s right
hand, survives the Fourth Shinobi World War at great cost, and is later recruited into
the criminal organization Kara.

## Key takeaways

- A stern, prideful traditionalist who took a defeated Hyūga clan member's Byakugan for
  his own right eye decades earlier; frequently scolds his younger colleague [[Chōjūrō]]
  for lacking confidence, though the two share real mutual respect.
- Leads the Sensor Division during the Fourth Shinobi World War, using his sensory
  skills and stolen Byakugan to track enemy movements — and is the sole survivor of a
  devastating Ten-Tails attack on the Alliance's headquarters, though severely maimed.
- Rehabilitated with cybernetic Scientific Ninja Tool prosthetics after the war, but
  never returns to Kirigakure, growing bitter and purposeless until recruited by Kara as
  an "Outer."
- *New Era*: works against Konoha as a Kara agent (extracting intelligence via
  genjutsu), but his final confirmed arc in this source ends with a redemptive turn after
  being defeated and shown mercy by Boruto Uzumaki.
