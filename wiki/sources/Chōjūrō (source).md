# Chōjūrō (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Chōjūrō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%8Dj%C5%ABr%C5%8D)
Raw file: `raw/Chōjūrō.md`

## Summary

Biography of Chōjūrō, one of the last Seven Ninja Swordsmen of the Mist, who serves as
[[Mei Terumī]]'s bodyguard before eventually succeeding her as Sixth Mizukage.

## Key takeaways

- Shy and lacking confidence early on, growing steadily more self-assured through the
  Fourth Shinobi World War (where he single-handedly wounds Black Zetsu) and into a
  calm, methodical leader by the time he becomes Mizukage.
- Wields Hiramekarei, a shapeshifting chakra sword that can form into a hammer, a
  longsword, or split into two blades — regarded as one of the era's finest swordsmen.
- Accompanies [[Mei Terumī]] through the Five Kage Summit and the war, then succeeds her
  as Sixth Mizukage in the *New Era*, where he navigates diplomacy with the other
  villages and a revolt within his own Seven Swordsmen's next generation.
