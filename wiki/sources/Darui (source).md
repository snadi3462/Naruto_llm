# Darui (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Darui](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Darui)
Raw file: `raw/Darui.md`

## Summary

Biography of Darui, the Fourth Raikage's right-hand man and eventual Fifth Raikage of
Kumogakure.

## Key takeaways

- Easy-going, self-deprecating, and reflexively apologetic (his verbal tics — "dull" and
  "sorry" — are running gags), but fiercely loyal to the Fourth Raikage and quick to
  defend him physically or verbally.
- Trained by the Third Raikage in the rare Black Lightning technique; combines Water and
  Lightning Release into Storm Release, firing guidable beams of light.
- A key combatant throughout the Fourth Shinobi World War: defeats Suigetsu Hōzuki at
  the Five Kage Summit, battles the Gold and Silver Brothers for their Treasured Tools,
  and fights at the war's climax.
- Eventually succeeds A as Fifth Raikage, skilled enough as an adult to compete with
  Ōtsutsuki-level threats like Momoshiki.
