# Killer B (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Killer B](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Killer_B)
Raw file: `raw/Killer B.md`

## Summary

Biography of Killer B, jinchūriki of the Eight-Tails ([[Gyūki]]) and adoptive brother of
A (Fourth Raikage) — one of the few jinchūriki to achieve genuine, willing partnership
with his tailed beast rather than mere control.

## Key takeaways

- Chosen as Gyūki's jinchūriki as a child after its previous host lost control and
  killed several people, including a close friend's father; endures prejudice similar
  to what [[Naruto Uzumaki]] and [[Gaara]] faced, but stays relentlessly cheerful for his
  brother's sake.
- Aspires to be the world's greatest rapper, constantly writing rhymes even mid-battle —
  a running gag alongside his fist-bump greeting and fondness for older women.
- Achieves full, willing control of Gyūki through training at the Falls of Truth,
  letting him access every transformation stage while retaining his own mind — a rarity
  among jinchūriki.
- Fights Sasuke Uchiha (access restricted) to a draw, later mentors Naruto Uzumaki
  (access restricted) during the Fourth Shinobi World War, helping him mature and turn
  the tide against Akatsuki.
