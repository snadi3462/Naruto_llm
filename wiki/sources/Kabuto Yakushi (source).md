# Kabuto Yakushi (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Kabuto Yakushi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kabuto_Yakushi)
Raw file: `raw/Kabuto Yakushi.md`

## Summary

Biography of Kabuto Yakushi, [[Orochimaru]]'s spy-turned-right-hand-man, whose entire
arc is a search for identity after childhood amnesia erased his own past.

## Key takeaways

- Found as an amnesiac child and raised as a Root spy under [[Danzō Shimura]], Kabuto's
  defining trait is an ongoing identity crisis — every persona he's had was constructed
  for someone else's purposes, first Root's, then Orochimaru's.
- Becomes Orochimaru's most trusted agent and, after Orochimaru's death, sets out to
  surpass him entirely — orchestrating much of the Fourth Shinobi World War's chaos as
  the mastermind behind mass reincarnation via Impure World Reincarnation, including
  Madara Uchiha's revival.
- Grafts himself with Orochimaru's remains and the genetic traits of numerous test
  subjects (including Suigetsu Hōzuki, Karin, and Jūgo), becoming a nearly unkillable,
  shapeshifting threat.
- Defeated and freed from his identity crisis by [[Itachi Uchiha]] (access restricted)'s
  Izanami, discovering his true self was the orphan Nonō Yakushi once trusted; returns to
  run the Konoha orphanage that raised him after the war.
