# Temari (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Temari](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Temari)
Raw file: `raw/Temari.md`

## Summary

Exhaustive biography of Temari, eldest of the [[Three Sand Siblings]]: her Chūnin Exams
rivalry-turned-partnership with Shikamaru Nara, her role as Sunagakure's ambassador, and
her eventual marriage into the Nara clan and life in Konoha as a mother.

## Key takeaways

- The pragmatic, blunt eldest sibling — protective of [[Gaara]] and [[Kankurō]] even when
  afraid of Gaara during his "Sand Waterfall" years — Temari grows into Suna's diplomatic
  liaison to Konoha, repeatedly crossing paths with Shikamaru Nara from their Chūnin Exams
  match onward.
- Their relationship develops gradually across Part II and the "Hiden" novels: mutual
  respect during joint missions, an unspoken attraction the story treats as an open
  secret, and finally a confirmed romance, marriage, and move to Konoha as a member of
  the Nara clan.
- Fights alongside the Fourth Division during the Fourth Shinobi World War (countering
  the Third Raikage, the Ten-Tails' shockwaves), and is present for the war's climax and
  the Infinite Tsukuyomi.
- *New Era*: raises her and Shikamaru's son Shikadai, shown as a strict but loving mother
  who teaches him (and later Mirai Sarutobi) her Wind Release techniques. Recurs
  throughout the Ōtsutsuki-era arcs protecting Shikadai and, alongside him, is injured
  protecting him during the same Urashiki confrontation that hospitalizes Gaara and
  Kankurō — the most current status this source covers.
