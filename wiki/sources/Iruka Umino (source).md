# Iruka Umino (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Iruka Umino](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iruka_Umino)
Raw file: `raw/Iruka Umino.md`

## Summary

Biography of Iruka Umino, an Academy instructor and the first adult in the village to treat
a young student with genuine care rather than fear or pity (see [[Naruto Uzumaki]] — access
restricted). Orphaned by the same Nine-Tails attack that made that student a jinchūriki,
giving the two a parallel loneliness that becomes the foundation of their relationship.

## Key takeaways

- **Mentor relationship** (see [[Naruto Uzumaki]] — access restricted, for the details):
  initially uneasy about his teaching assignment, Iruka becomes the first adult his student
  genuinely respects — shields him from danger early on, and vouches for him at a pivotal
  Academy milestone for effort rather than by the letter of the rules.
- **Mentored by Hiruzen**: the Third Hokage (see [[Hiruzen Sarutobi]] — access restricted)
  comforted Iruka after his parents' deaths and became his own model for what teaching should
  look like, which is why Iruka pursued a teaching career himself.
- **New Era**: continues teaching at the Academy for decades, is eventually promoted to
  Headmaster, and remains a recurring, grounding presence across the *Boruto*-era storyline
  (including watching over [[Himawari Uzumaki]] during an Academy trial-lesson arc).
