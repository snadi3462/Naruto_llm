# Kiba Inuzuka (source)

Source: [Naruto Fandom wiki — Kiba Inuzuka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kiba_Inuzuka)
Raw file: `raw/Kiba Inuzuka.md`

## Summary

Biography of Kiba Inuzuka, [[Team 8]]'s hot-headed tracker and Akamaru's partner: his childhood rivalry-turned-respect for a classmate (see [[Naruto Uzumaki]] — access restricted), his Inuzuka clan collaboration techniques, his protectiveness of [[Hinata Hyuga]], and his adult life as a top dog breeder and Military Police investigator, still active as of the Kawaki Arc.

## Key takeaways

- Establishes Kiba's core dynamic with [[Team 8]] teammates: friction with Shino over leadership, protective/brotherly closeness with Hinata (whom he's one of the first to learn has a crush on a teammate), and a long rivalry that turns to respect after a Chūnin Exams defeat (see [[Naruto Uzumaki]] — access restricted).
- Details the Inuzuka clan's human-canine collaboration fighting style with [[Akamaru]] (Fang Passing Fang, the Double/Three-Headed Wolf transformations) — corroborated in full by [[Akamaru (source)]].
- Confirms he lives with Tamaki as a couple in the *New Era*, becomes a renowned dog breeder/TV personality, and joins the Konoha Military Police Force as an investigator.
- Reaches the Kawaki Arc (fighting and being quickly defeated by Isshiki) but does not continue into the *New Era: Part II* crisis chapters — his status during that crisis is unconfirmed (see [[Naruto Uzumaki]] — access restricted).
