**Anko Mitarashi** (みたらしアンコ, *Mitarashi Anko*) is a [tokubetsu jōnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tokubetsu_j%C5%8Dnin "Tokubetsu jōnin") who teaches at [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure") 's [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy").

## Background

![Anko with Orochimaru.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/0a/Orochimaru_and_Anko.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240908012729)

Anko with Orochimaru.

When she was a [genin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genin "Genin"), Anko was on a [team](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Orochimaru "Team Orochimaru") led by [Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru").[^3] Their team took part in the [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams"); during the second round, they confronted a team consisting of [Hayate Gekkō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hayate_Gekk%C5%8D "Hayate Gekkō"), [Ibiki Morino](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ibiki_Morino "Ibiki Morino"), and [Tokara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tokara "Tokara").[^4] Orochimaru took a strong interest in Anko, teaching her several of his signature [jutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jutsu "Jutsu") and, in the anime, what he'd learned about immortality.[^5] Orochimaru eventually used Anko as a test subject for his [Cursed Seal of Heaven](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Cursed_Seal_of_Heaven "Cursed Seal of Heaven"). Of the ten subjects, Anko was the only survivor.[^6]

![Anko is found by Konoha's Anbu.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/ad/Anko_found_by_Anbu.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20251018223505)

Anko is found by Konoha's Anbu.

In the anime, Anko refused to continue following Orochimaru after she received her Cursed Seal of Heaven. Unwilling to kill her because she was so valuable as a research subject, Orochimaru erased Anko's memory, making her wonder if she'd been abandoned for lacking the ideal drive to fuel the cursed seal, rather than her choosing to reject him in the face of his evil experiments.[^7] An amnesiac Anko was found in the [Land of the Sea](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_the_Sea "Land of the Sea") by [Konoha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha "Konoha") 's [Anbu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Anbu "Anbu") and brought back in hopes of giving the village information regarding Orochimaru, but because of her tampered memory, nothing could be recovered. Distraught at being abandoned and having no memory, Anko was comforted by the [Third Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Hokage "Third Hokage"). Ever since then, Anko held a grudge against Orochimaru for what he did and believed it was her responsibility, as his former student, to stop his criminal actions.

## Personality

![Anko making a dramatic entrance.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/4b/Anko_entrance.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20251018223640)

Anko making a dramatic entrance.

Anko is laid-back, tomboyish, playful, stubborn, brave, rough, feisty, and sometimes hyperactive, to the point that [Sakura Haruno](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sakura_Haruno "Sakura Haruno") described her as being much like [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki") in many ways.[^8] Despite portraying several childish and somewhat juvenile behavior, Anko has nonetheless been shown to be very well-prepared and studious. However, she is also shown to have a bloodthirsty side of herself, as seen when she slashed Naruto's face with a [kunai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kunai "Kunai") and licked his blood (in the anime, she merely used her finger to wipe it off).[^9] Anko has also shown a somewhat sadistic side and is apparently not the most sympathetic of people, which is no surprise being a former student of Orochimaru. However, she is not above caring for her comrades or people in pain, as shown by her encouraging words to [Isaribi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Isaribi "Isaribi"). Anko is also a very strict and impatient person, wasting no time in announcing bad news and dislikes sugarcoating situations when there is no need for it. She regularly makes sure she has at least a basic understanding of the landscape so her next mission can be accomplished. Anko is also highly confident in her own abilities, often choosing to face a powerful opponent alone or continue a dangerous mission alone.

![Anko's scary demeanor.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/5a/Anko_scary.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20251018223535)

Anko's scary demeanor.

Anko's personality quirks are often commented by others, who consider her being crazy and somewhat deranged, such as Naruto and [Ino Yamanaka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ino_Yamanaka "Ino Yamanaka"). While completely aware of this, Anko doesn't enjoy when others point it out, scaring Naruto and leaving him creeped out when she overheard his negative comments about her, threatning him with a kunai, all the while licking her lips.[^10] Anko once held an extremely powerful admiration towards her teacher [Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru"), viewing him as a role model and striving to become just like him. However, after Orochimaru [branded](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru%27s_Juinjutsu "Orochimaru's Juinjutsu") her with a [Cursed Seal of Heaven](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Cursed_Seal_of_Heaven "Cursed Seal of Heaven"), Anko's admiration for her former teacher transformed into a deep-seated hatred.[^11] Her experience with Orochimaru, and the betrayal she suffered by his hand, left Anko with post-traumatic stress disorder, having nightmares about the effects of the Cursed Seal of Heaven, which often left her waking up drenched in sweat. As a result, Anko became determined to be the one to put an end to Orochimaru's evil ways, being even willing to [sacrifice herself](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Twin_Snakes_Mutual_Death_Technique "Twin Snakes Mutual Death Technique") in order to accomplish it.

![Anko's sweet tooth.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/c8/Anko%27s_sweet_tooth.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20251018224235)

Anko's sweet tooth.

One of Anko's defining characteristics is her strong and unyielding sweet tooth, spending almost all of her free time eating food, particularly [dango](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dango "wikipedia:Dango"), which is her favourite meal. Accordingly, she becomes extremely angry and hostile towards anyone who either threatens to interrupt her snack time, or dares to steal her food, seen when she almost killed Naruto by throwing several [skewers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skewer "wikipedia:Skewer") at him after he ate her dango right under her nose.[^12] Her unrelenting love for sweets and unwillingness to moderate her eating habits eventually caused Anko to gain a significant amount of weight, and around fifteen years after the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"), her previously slender frame had since become an overweight one, although she shows zero concern about it and takes no offense when others comment on her newfound weight.

## Appearance

- ![Anko as a genin.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/d/d9/Anko_Genin.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/250?cb=20251018224605)
	Anko as a genin.

1/2

Anko is an average-sized, young woman who was of slender frame. She has light brown, pupil-less eyes. Her hair - black with a blue tint in the manga [^13] and violet in the anime - is styled in a short, spiky, fanned-out ponytail. She usually wears a custom-made outfit that is crafted of thin metal mesh to fit the lines of her body,[^14] that covers her from neck to thigh. Over this, she typically wears a tan overcoat with a purple in-seam and a pocket on each side, a dark orange mini-skirt, a dark blue belt, and pale grey shin guards. In addition to the typical [forehead protector](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forehead_protector "Forehead protector"), she also wears a small pendant that looks like a snake fang.

As a child, Anko wore a sleeveless chainmail mesh shirt, chainmail armbands that reached to her elbows, dark shorts, and knee-length boots.[^4] Though during her time as a preteen when following Orochimaru in the anime, she instead wears a brown-red t-shirt with bracelets, white shorts and chainmail leggings. Years after the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"), she puts on weight and starts wearing red lipstick. Her chainmail bodysuit is replaced with an orange blouse with a white coat cape over it.

## Abilities

Being a [tokubetsu jōnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tokubetsu_j%C5%8Dnin "Tokubetsu jōnin"), Anko is indeed a highly skilled [kunoichi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kunoichi "Kunoichi"). Even as a child, her talent gained the attention of Orochimaru who would take her up as his disciple. Her prowess ultimately earned her recognition as a legendary kunoichi a decade after the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War").[^15]

### Ninjutsu

![Anko utilising snakes.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/28/Hidden_Shadow_Snake_Hands.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140916141159)

Anko utilising snakes.

While under Orochimaru's wing, she was taught many of his techniques, such as the [Hidden Shadow Snake Hands](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden_Shadow_Snake_Hands "Hidden Shadow Snake Hands") and its [stronger variant](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Many_Hidden_Shadow_Snake_Hands "Many Hidden Shadow Snake Hands"). Her skill with these techniques, in particular, allowed her to easily outmaneuver the collaborative efforts of the [Ino](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Inojin_Yamanaka "Inojin Yamanaka") - [Shika](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikadai_Nara "Shikadai Nara") - [Chō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%8Dch%C5%8D_Akimichi "Chōchō Akimichi") trio during their [Genin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genin_Exams "Genin Exams").[^16] She could also perform Orochimaru's [murder-suicide technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Twin_Snakes_Mutual_Death_Technique "Twin Snakes Mutual Death Technique") as well.[^17] She was also the first surviving recipient of his [Cursed Seal of Heaven](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Cursed_Seal_of_Heaven "Cursed Seal of Heaven"), which appears as three [tomoe](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tomoe "Tomoe") at the back of her neck. Though it flares up from time to time in its attempt to encourage her to use it, she refrains from doing so. It is eventually [removed](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Releasing_Method "Evil Releasing Method") from her body following the Fourth Shinobi World War.[^18] Anko has incredible aim, able to make a perfect [Konoha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha "Konoha") symbol out of dango sticks without even looking.[^19] She has excellent tracking skills, evidenced by her being placed in charge of [a team](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Infiltration_and_Reconnaissance_Party "Infiltration and Reconnaissance Party") tasked with finding [Kabuto Yakushi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kabuto_Yakushi "Kabuto Yakushi"). In the anime, she has been stated to have built up an immunity to [poisons](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Poison "Poison") with Orochimaru's help.[^20] She also has proficiency [Fire](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire "Fire") and [Yin Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yin_Release "Yin Release"), able to unleash [power flame streams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire_Release:_Dragon_Fire_Technique "Fire Release: Dragon Fire Technique") and [confuse her targets](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sly_Mind_Affect_Technique "Sly Mind Affect Technique") with the respective elements.[^21] [^16]

## Part I

### Chūnin Exams

Main article: [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams_\(Arc\) "Chūnin Exams (Arc)")

![Anko meets "Shiore".](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/e/e2/Anko_intimidates_Naruto.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140705131703)

Anko meets "Shiore".

Anko proctors the second stage of the [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams") held in [Konoha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha "Konoha"). She breaks into the room where the first stage was held and showily introduces herself to the assembled [genin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genin "Genin"). When she realises that she entered too soon, she tries deflecting blame by instead reprimanding the first stage's proctor, [Ibiki Morino](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ibiki_Morino "Ibiki Morino"), for how many genin were allowed to pass to the second stage. She leads the genin to the [Forest of Death](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forest_of_Death "Forest of Death") where she explains the rules for the second stage and the dangers they'll face in the forest. She attacks [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki") with a kunai when he suggests she's exaggerating, only for the kunai to be returned to her by [Shiore](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shiore "Shiore"), a [Kusa](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kusa "Kusa") -nin.

![Anko finds Orochimaru.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/d/d2/Anko_encounters_Orochimaru.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240817040258)

Anko finds Orochimaru.

Shortly after the second stage gets underway, Anko is alerted to the discovery of three dead Kusa-nin, among them Shiore. From examining the bodies it becomes clear to her that they've been dead for some time and, because they're all [missing their faces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Vanishing_Facial_Copy_Technique "Vanishing Facial Copy Technique"), she concludes that Orochimaru has infiltrated the Chūnin Exams. She orders that the [Third Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Hokage "Third Hokage") be informed of what's happened, that two teams of [Anbu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Anbu "Anbu") be deployed, and then goes into the forest to find Orochimaru on her own. She locates him shortly after nightfall and tries to use the [Twin Snakes Mutual Death Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Twin_Snakes_Mutual_Death_Technique "Twin Snakes Mutual Death Technique") on him, only to discover at the last moment that she's been attacking a [shadow clone](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_clone "Shadow clone"). He reactivates her dormant [Cursed Seal of Heaven](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Cursed_Seal_of_Heaven "Cursed Seal of Heaven"), immobilising her with the pain, and warns her not to cancel the Chūnin Exams before departing.

Despite the pain, Anko is eventually able to reach the centre of the Forest of Death. The Third Hokage treats her cursed seal and, after hearing what's happened, speculates that Orochimaru is after [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha"), who Orochimaru has branded with another Cursed Seal of Heaven.[^22] Anko tries to argue that Sasuke be removed from the exams and placed under guard, but the Third insists the exams continue per Orochimaru's instructions. During the preliminary matches, Anko stays by the Third's side, informing him of the participants' particulars; she is amazed by Sasuke's control of his cursed seal during his match.

A month after the preliminaries, as the final round matches near and it becomes increasingly clear that Orochimaru means ill to Konoha, Anko tries to apologise to the Third for her failure to kill him. She also rues over the death of the [Fourth Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Hokage "Fourth Hokage"), believing he could have stopped Orochimaru, but the Third tells her to neither blame herself nor to dwell on the dead..[^23] In the anime, around the same time, Naruto approaches her while she's at her favourite [Dango Shop](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Dango_Shop "Dango Shop") to ask her about the "Fourth Hokage's legacy". When he eats one of her dango without permission, she attacks him and chases him away.[^24]

Main article: [Kurosuki Family Removal Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kurosuki_Family_Removal_Mission "Kurosuki Family Removal Mission") In the anime, Anko meets with the Fifth Hokage after completing an escort mission in the [Land of Swamps](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Swamps "Land of Swamps"). ### Kaima Capture Mission

Main article: [Kaima Capture Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaima_Capture_Mission "Kaima Capture Mission")

![Anko and her team deploy.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/2f/Kaima_Capture_Team.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150121165823)

Anko and her team deploy.

In the anime, Konoha is hired to protect a shipping convoy in the [Land of the Sea](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_the_Sea "Land of the Sea"). ## Part II

### Akatsuki Suppression Mission

Main article: [Akatsuki Suppression Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki_Suppression_Mission "Akatsuki Suppression Mission") In the anime, Anko attended [Asuma Sarutobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Asuma_Sarutobi "Asuma Sarutobi") 's funeral.[^29]

Main article: [Itachi Pursuit Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Itachi_Pursuit_Mission "Itachi Pursuit Mission") In the anime, Anko was on a mission with [Mozuku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mozuku "Mozuku") and [Iwashi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iwashi "Iwashi") when she senses something behind her. ### Pain's Assault

Main article: [Pain's Assault](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Pain%27s_Assault_\(Arc\) "Pain's Assault (Arc)")

![Anko and her team tracking down Kabuto.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/e/e9/Anko%27s_team.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20251018225152)

Anko and her team tracking down Kabuto.

Before Pain attacked Konoha, Anko led the [Infiltration and Reconnaissance Party](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Infiltration_and_Reconnaissance_Party "Infiltration and Reconnaissance Party"), along with Sai and Yamato, to track down and locate [Kabuto Yakushi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kabuto_Yakushi "Kabuto Yakushi"), Orochimaru's former subordinate. Anko warned the rest of them not to let Kabuto detect them. However, shortly after discovering Kabuto's location, Yamato noticed that Naruto had achieved his six-tailed Nine-Tails transformation and apologizing for deserting the mission while claiming an emergency, rushed back to the village while leaving a confused Anko and Sai behind.

### Fourth Shinobi World War: Countdown

Main article: [Fourth Shinobi World War: Countdown](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War:_Countdown "Fourth Shinobi World War: Countdown")

![Anko and her team investigate the corpses.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/bc/Anko%27s_unit.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160223214619)

Anko and her team investigate the corpses.

Although Yamato is gone and Sai also returns to Konoha, a restaffed Reconnaissance Party continues to track Kabuto. They eventually come across three dead [Takigakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Takigakure "Takigakure") ninja. From the bite marks on their necks, it becomes clear to Anko that Kabuto has gained control of Orochimaru's powers, but she wonders why he's leaving behind bodies for them to find. Wary of a trap, they follow the trail of bodies to the [Mountains' Graveyard](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mountains%27_Graveyard "Mountains' Graveyard"). [Tokuma Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tokuma_Hy%C5%ABga "Tokuma Hyūga") scouts the area with his [Byakugan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Byakugan "Byakugan") and reports that Kabuto is in the company of [Tobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobi "Tobi"). Believing they have found the [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki") base, Anko immediately sends word of their discovery to Konoha, only briefly wondering why Kabuto would lead them there.

### Fourth Shinobi World War: Confrontation

Main article: [Fourth Shinobi World War: Confrontation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War:_Confrontation "Fourth Shinobi World War: Confrontation")

![Anko clashes with Kabuto.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/3e/Anko_and_Kabuto_clash.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120503132421)

Anko clashes with Kabuto.

Anko and her team continue to monitor the Mountains' Graveyard. When [Muta Aburame](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Muta_Aburame "Muta Aburame") detects the mobilisation of the [White Zetsu Army](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/White_Zetsu_Army "White Zetsu Army"), Anko instructs her team to inform the [Allied Shinobi Forces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Allied_Shinobi_Forces "Allied Shinobi Forces") while she goes after Kabuto. She tries to sneak up on Kabuto and attack him, but is quickly defeated. Tobi orders that Kabuto kill her, but he instead keeps her comatose, siphoning the chakra from her cursed seal to strengthen his [Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Impure_World_Reincarnation "Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation").

### Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax

Main article: [Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War:_Climax "Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax")

![Orochimaru revived from Anko's cursed seal.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/7/7c/Kaija_H%C5%8Din.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20131205121643)

Orochimaru revived from Anko's cursed seal.

After Kabuto is defeated by [Itachi Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Itachi_Uchiha "Itachi Uchiha") and [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha"), Anko's unconscious body is found by [Jūgo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%ABgo "Jūgo"). Wishing to speak with Orochimaru, Sasuke uses the [Evil Releasing Method](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Releasing_Method "Evil Releasing Method") to revive Orochimaru from her cursed seal, causing her cursed seal to disappear in the process. Anko remains unconscious throughout the ordeal and they leave her behind.

### Academy Entrance Arc

Main article: [Academy Entrance Arc](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy_Entrance_Arc "Academy Entrance Arc") Ten years after the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War") ends, Anko becomes an instructor at Konoha's Academy. During the entrance ceremony, Anko and the other instructors were left mortified when [Boruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Boruto_Uzumaki "Boruto Uzumaki") crashes a train into the [Hokage Rock](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage_Rock "Hokage Rock"). Two weeks later, when the students skipped [Shino Aburame](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shino_Aburame "Shino Aburame") 's class to watch a fight between [Iwabee Yuino](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iwabee_Yuino "Iwabee Yuino") and Boruto, Anko gave the class a severe scolding afterwards. Later, Anko checked on Shino when he began pouting about how little respect his students seemed to have for him.

Later, when classes ended early because of the [Five Kage Summit](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Kage_Summit "Five Kage Summit"), she invites [Chōchō Akimichi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%8Dch%C5%8D_Akimichi "Chōchō Akimichi") to the dango shop to eat some anmitsu.

### School Trip Arc

Main article: [School Trip Arc](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/School_Trip_Arc "School Trip Arc") In the anime, to improve relationships with other villages and symbolically show the warring era was over, the Seventh Hokage set up an official class trip for the Academy to go to Kirigakure. Anko joined Shino to watch over the class. Upon arriving, they were met with [Kagura Karatachi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kagura_Karatachi "Kagura Karatachi"), who was appointed by the Mizukage to act as the class' tour guide. The class was later taken to meet [Chōjūrō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%8Dj%C5%ABr%C5%8D "Chōjūrō"), the Sixth Mizukage.

![Anko and Shino discussing Kiri's affairs with Mei.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/34/Political_Talk.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20171022112702)

Anko and Shino discussing Kiri's affairs with Mei.

Later, Anko and Shino met with the Fifth Mizukage [Mei Terumi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mei_Terumi "Mei Terumi") to discuss the growing conflict between the Land of Water and neighbouring countries. As the Land of Water wishes to expand military power into the bordering seas, Mei feared that this could escalate into war again. Despite this, Mei assured them that Kirigakure was strongly against all this and that she with the Sixth Mizukage would work to prevent it all.

### Graduation Exams Arc

Main article: [Graduation Exams Arc](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Graduation_Exams_Arc "Graduation Exams Arc")

![Anko participating in the Genin Exams.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/86/Anko_Vs_Students.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20171213103202)

Anko participating in the Genin Exams.

During the practical and final test of the graduation exam, the class was brought to the training field. There, the entire class was set up to face Anko, Shino, and Konohamaru for 24 hours. While the teachers would still evaluate the students' respective improvements, the students' true test was against Kakashi Hatake. The Sixth Hokage quickly explained that he would only pass the first student who is able to take the single bell attached to his hip. As the test began, Anko quickly caught many students in a genjutsu. She was then attacked by the [Ino–Shika–Chō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ino%E2%80%93Shika%E2%80%93Ch%C5%8D "Ino–Shika–Chō") trio. While questioning their willingness to work together as there could only be one winner in the test, they insisted that it was more practical to work team up for now. Anko smirked at their choice. As she began overwhelming the trio with her [snake techniques](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden_Shadow_Snake_Hands "Hidden Shadow Snake Hands"), they were soon aided by more of their friends to face Anko. Ultimately, Anko defeated and subdued the group of students.

Later that night, following a plan from Boruto, the remaining students able to split up the proctors. Anko was then attacked by Lee and [Wasabi Izuno](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wasabi_Izuno "Wasabi Izuno"). While taking their attacks well, Anko then turned her attention to [Namida Suzumeno](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Namida_Suzumeno "Namida Suzumeno"), whose frightened nature was used to the advantage of Anko's intimidating battle nature, unleashing a scream powerful enough to emit a [sonic blast](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Calling_on_Tears_Technique "Calling on Tears Technique") that incapacitated Anko. By the end of the test, despite no one getting the bell from Kakashi, the Sixth Hokage chose to pass everyone as they succeeded in the true goal of the test; team-work and loyalty.

### Mujina Bandits Arc

Main article: [Mujina Bandits Arc](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mujina_Bandits_Arc "Mujina Bandits Arc") In the anime, months later, Anko helped in a one-day trail session for children to experience the Ninja Academy. She acted as a team captain for [Himawari Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Himawari_Uzumaki "Himawari Uzumaki"), [Yuina Itomaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yuina_Itomaki "Yuina Itomaki"), and [Ehō Norimaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eh%C5%8D_Norimaki "Ehō Norimaki"), and tasked them with the retrieval of a missing pet cat. She watched in amusement at how the team struggled to get along, but ultimately learned to cooperate and complete their mission.

### Ao Arc

Main article: [Ao Arc](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ao_Arc "Ao Arc") In the anime, Anko attended the memorial service for the fallen people of the Fourth Shinobi World War.

### Kawaki & Himawari Academy Arc

Main article: [Kawaki & Himawari Academy Arc](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kawaki_%26_Himawari_Academy_Arc "Kawaki & Himawari Academy Arc") In the anime, Anko attended the entrance ceremony for a new class of students in the Academy. When a new teacher, [Hana Kaka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hana_Kaka "Hana Kaka"), struggled with the job, she and Shino reassured her that every new teacher does so at first. When she felt dejected over the enthusiasm her students showed when Team 7 taught a class, Anko was confident about Hana's qualifications. After she successfully mended a rift between her students, Anko was happy for her. She asked Team 7 if they wanted to teach another class, but Boruto declined the offer.

When the Academy held a festival, they an explosion went off and they received a terrorist threat of more. The Academy staff discussed how to deal with the situation, and they accepted the help of former students in attendance to help deal with the situation, as no one could leave or contact the outside.

Following the attack, Hana was injured and hospitalised. As Shino took over Hana's class as she recuperated, Anko suggested to Shino they not tell Hana about Kae having been the target of the attack, not wanting to put any grief on the new teacher. After the threat on Kae was seemingly thwarted, Anko and Shino decided to tell Hana about it upon her return.

Anko later listened to Hana in the hospital as she explained how her other personality had been the assassin after Kae, and her decision to live with the other personality, teach her, and befriend her. Anko attended the class' Sannin play as a send off for Kae, who had to return to her country.

## Trivia

- "Anko" (餡子) is derived from the [sweet red bean paste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_bean_paste "wikipedia:Red bean paste") used in popular Japanese sweets such as [dango](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/dango "wikipedia:dango"), while her family name comes from mitarashi syrup that usually goes with dango.
- In an interview included with *[Naruto Anime Profiles, Vol. 1](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Anime_Profiles,_Vol._1 "Naruto Anime Profiles, Vol. 1")*, [Kishimoto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kishimoto "Kishimoto") states that Anko is his favourite female character, due to her "relationship with Orochimaru".
- Anko's highest ranking in the [Naruto Character Popularity Polls](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Character_Popularity_Polls "Naruto Character Popularity Polls") was eleventh place in the first poll.
- Studio Pierrot's Settei sheets of Anko show that she was 141 cm at her Chūnin Exams when she was first part of Orochimaru's genin team and 143 cm a year or so later.
- According to the databook(s):
	- She likes conducting [tea ceremonies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_tea_ceremony "wikipedia:Japanese tea ceremony") as a hobby.
		- Anko wishes to fight Orochimaru.
		- Anko's favourite foods are sweet bean soup and [dango](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dango "wikipedia:Dango"). Her least favourite type of food is spicy-tasting food.
		- Her favourite phrase is "Calling on" (参上, *Sanjō*).
		- Anko has completed 647 official missions in total: 72 D-rank, 86 C-rank, 398 B-rank, 89 A-rank, 2 S-rank.
- In the [anime](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jiraiya_Shinobi_Handbook:_The_Tale_of_Naruto_the_Hero "Jiraiya Shinobi Handbook: The Tale of Naruto the Hero"), a dream version of Anko was part of [Root](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Root "Root") and was one of its best members, alongside [Yūgao Uzuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Y%C5%ABgao_Uzuki "Yūgao Uzuki"), [Hayate Gekkō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hayate_Gekk%C5%8D "Hayate Gekkō"), and [Sai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sai "Sai"). She wore a mixture of her own clothing and the standard-issue [Anbu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Anbu "Anbu") uniform, including a black hooded cloak and an asymmetrical black and white mask.[^30]
- According to her profile in *[Naruto: Ultimate Ninja Online](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto:_Ultimate_Ninja_Online "Naruto: Ultimate Ninja Online")* and Rin no Sho, Anko is the most dangerous woman in Konohagakure.
