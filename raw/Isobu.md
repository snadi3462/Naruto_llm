**Isobu** (磯撫), more commonly known as the **Three-Tails** (三尾, *Sanbi*), is one of the nine [tailed beasts](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_beasts "Tailed beasts"). It was last sealed within [Yagura Karatachi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yagura_Karatachi "Yagura Karatachi") of [Kirigakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kirigakure "Kirigakure").

## Background

![The young tailed beasts with Hagoromo.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/bb/Young_Bijuu_and_Hagoromo.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150728221849)

The young tailed beasts with Hagoromo.

Isobu and the tailed beasts first came into being in the waning days of [Hagoromo Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hagoromo_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Hagoromo Ōtsutsuki"), who used his [Creation of All Things Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Creation_of_All_Things_Technique "Creation of All Things Technique") to divide the [Ten-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails "Ten-Tails") ' chakra into nine separate bodies and giving each a name. Some time after being created, the Sage explained to the young tailed beasts that a link existed between them despite their separation. He also foretold of a day when they would be brought together again - though not as they were - and that at that time someone would emerge to show them what true power is.[^2] [^3] In the anime, before his death, Hagoromo sent Isobu to live in a temple built for the purpose of protecting it in a region surrounding a large misty lake that would later become part of the [Land of Water](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Water "Land of Water").[^4]

![Rin as Isobu's jinchūriki.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/31/Rin_and_Isobu.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140807122036)

Rin as Isobu's jinchūriki.

During the [First Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/First_Shinobi_World_War "First Shinobi World War"), [Hashirama Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama_Senju "Hashirama Senju") captured eight of the tailed beasts and sold Isobu and [Saiken](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Saiken "Saiken") to Kirigakure at the [Five Kage Summit](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Kage_Summit "Five Kage Summit"), as a sign of friendship and to ensure a balance of power amongst their new villages.[^5] Decades later, Kirigakure — having been manipulated by [Madara Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Madara_Uchiha "Madara Uchiha") — sealed Isobu into the [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure") shinobi, [Rin Nohara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rin_Nohara "Rin Nohara"). They plotted for Rin to be taken back to her village as a [Trojan horse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trojan_Horse "wikipedia:Trojan Horse"), releasing the tailed beast to wreak havoc in Konoha. This failed, however, as Rin was fully aware of their schemes and opted to [kill](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_Cutter "Lightning Cutter") herself at hands of her friend and fellow teammate, [Kakashi Hatake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake "Kakashi Hatake"), before their objectives could come to fruition.[^6] Eventually, Isobu was revived and recaptured by Kirigakure, who sealed Isobu within Yagura, the Fourth [Mizukage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mizukage "Mizukage"), who managed to gain [complete control](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Control "Tailed Beast Control") and harmony with it.[^7] However, Yagura was placed under the genjutsu of [Obito Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha "Obito Uchiha"), who controlled the Mizukage, and Isobu by extension. The genjutsu was dispelled years later by [Ao](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ao "Ao"), which led to Yagura's death shortly after.[^8] Some time after Yagura's death, Isobu was revived once more, although it was not recaptured and therefore was free to live in the wild.

In the anime, [Tsunade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsunade "Tsunade") states that Isobu disappeared, its whereabouts unknown, after the [Third Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Shinobi_World_War "Third Shinobi World War").[^9]

## Personality

Isobu speaks in a timid manner, referring to itself as "boku" (ボク) which is usually used by young boys.

## Appearance

![A young Isobu.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/f1/Young_Isobu.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130913102943)

A young Isobu.

Isobu primarily resembles a large turtle, but with a crab-like shell, spikes all over its body, and three shrimp-like tails. Under its shell, it has red, muscle-like tissue. It has a pair of human-like arms and hands, but no hind-legs. His face is concealed between a large forehead and lower jaw — both of which have spikes. Its eyes are red and have crimson pupils. In the anime, the eyes are red with yellow pupils.

Its right eye is constantly closed. In the anime, [Guren](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Guren "Guren") speculated this was because its eyes were the only soft parts of its body, being heavily-armoured, and thus kept one eye closed to cover more vulnerable spots. During the last remaining days of the Sage of Six Paths, Isobu was essentially much the same in appearance, albeit smaller in size with shorter spikes around its body, but was still many times larger than the Sage.[^3]

## Abilities

*See also: [Jinchūriki Forms](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki_Forms "Jinchūriki Forms")*

![Isobu's destructive capability.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/3e/Isobu_destroying_Konoha.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150221100419)

Isobu's destructive capability.

As a [tailed beast](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_beast "Tailed beast"), Isobu is a powerful force to face. [Kirigakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kirigakure "Kirigakure") was so confident of its destructive potential that they, under the influence of [Madara Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Madara_Uchiha "Madara Uchiha"), intended to unleash Isobu in the midst of [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure") to destroy the village. As with any tailed beast, Isobu is effectively immortal, as when it dies, it will eventually revive. In the anime, it could produce a [hallucinogenic mist](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Illusionary_Mist "Illusionary Mist") that exploited the victim's insecurities and forced the victim to face them.

### Physical and Chakra Prowess

![Isobu rolled into a ball.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/1/19/Shadow_Stroke.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160110010949)

Isobu rolled into a ball.

Isobu can [roll into a ball](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Stroke "Shadow Stroke") to [move about quickly](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Leaping_Bullet_Shell_Tower "Leaping Bullet Shell Tower") and attack its opponents, making full use of the protrusions on its shell in a manner similar to the [Akimichi clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akimichi_clan "Akimichi clan") 's [Spiked Human Bullet Tank](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Spiked_Human_Bullet_Tank "Spiked Human Bullet Tank").[^10] Isobu's tough skin and shell also grant it additional defence, allowing it to withstand nearly all forms of attack. It can also any of its three spiked tails to strike its opponents and break through most defences. It also has the ability to [create coral](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Coral_Palm "Coral Palm") and swim at very high speeds. In the anime, Isobu's only known physical weakness is its eye; as tough as its skin and shell are, it cannot harden its eyes and thus seems to keep one closed at all times.

As a [tailed beast](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_beast "Tailed beast"), Isobu possesses an enormous amount of [chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra "Chakra") that it can [transfer](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Transfer_Technique "Chakra Transfer Technique"). It also has powerful healing capacities, able to mend injuries in themselves and others in seconds.[^11] Likewise, Isobu is effectively immortal, as when it dies, it will eventually revive, albeit in a younger form.

### Ninjutsu

![Isobu creating a Tailed Beast Ball.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/58/Isobu%27s_Tailed_Beast_Ball.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150917122825)

Isobu creating a Tailed Beast Ball.

In the anime, Isobu's abilities were greatly elaborated upon. It could create shockwaves to repel attacks and produce large tidal waves around it, enabling it to overwhelm [Guren](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Guren "Guren") 's [Crystal Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Crystal_Release "Crystal Release") several times over the course of their battle. When Isobu ate something, thousands of [miniature versions of itself](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Three-Tails_Mini_Clone "Three-Tails Mini Clone") would attack what was eaten within its stomach, presumably to speed up the digestion process. It was also shown capable of [Space-Time Ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Space-Time_Ninjutsu "Space-Time Ninjutsu"), materialising an entrance to a separate dimension in which it could hide its presence, until it was ready to return to wherever it chooses.[^12] Like the others Tailed Beasts, it can create the [Tailed Beast Ball](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Ball "Tailed Beast Ball").

#### Nature Transformation

As a turtle, Isobu has an affinity for [Water Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water_Release "Water Release").[^13] In the anime, it has shown to fire powerful [water blasts](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water_Release:_Great_Water_Mass_Bullet "Water Release: Great Water Mass Bullet") that are capable of destroying an entire cliff side.

## Part II

### Akatsuki Suppression Mission

Main article: [Akatsuki Suppression Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki_Suppression_Mission "Akatsuki Suppression Mission") In the manga, Tobi and Deidara confronted the beast around the same time [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki") had begun his [Wind Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Release "Wind Release") training, to which it responded by beginning to chase Tobi. With the Three-Tails distracted, Deidara moulded an explosive clay fish in an attempt to capture it. It was unclear if this marked the end of the battle, as the remainder of the battle went unseen. As the two towed the Three-Tails to the Akatsuki's lair after the battle, Tobi gloated at bringing the Three-Tails down with his special technique, but Deidara thought otherwise. It was later [sealed](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sealing_Technique:_Phantom_Dragons_Nine_Consuming_Seals "Sealing Technique: Phantom Dragons Nine Consuming Seals") in the [sealing statue](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Statue_of_the_Outer_Path "Demonic Statue of the Outer Path").

### Three-Tails' Appearance

Main article: [Three-Tails' Appearance](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Three-Tails%27_Appearance "Three-Tails' Appearance") **NOTE:** In the manga, the events of this arc occur during the [Akatsuki Suppression Mission](#Akatsuki_Suppression_Mission).

![The entrance to the dimension where the Three-Tails came from.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/f1/The_dimension%27s_entrance.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20151030201817)

The entrance to the dimension where the Three-Tails came from.

In the anime, [Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru") instructed [Kabuto Yakushi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kabuto_Yakushi "Kabuto Yakushi") and [Team Guren](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Guren "Team Guren") to take a young boy named [Yūkimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Y%C5%ABkimaru "Yūkimaru") to a lake. After they arrived, Kabuto forced Yūkimaru to release all of his chakra, which caused the Three-Tails to begin to surface from the lake. With [Guren](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Guren "Guren") 's help, the beast was completely revealed, and they attempted to capture it. Their attempts proved insufficient, however, and the beast created a large tidal wave to wash its attackers away.

The tidal wave gained the attention of a nearby group of [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure") ninja, who tried to use [Four-Corner Sealing Barrier](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Four-Corner_Sealing_Barrier "Four-Corner Sealing Barrier") to permanently trap the beast in its own dimension. Just as the sealing began to work, the Konoha ninja were interrupted by Guren, which prompted the beast to attack them all. Yūkimaru was able to calm the beast down temporarily, but, after he became tired, the Three-Tails turned its attention to him. Guren and [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki") came to his defence, but were swallowed in the process. With the help of their allies on the outside, they were able to escape, and the Konoha ninja tried once more to seal the beast.

![Akatsuki prepares to seal the Three-Tails.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/1/12/Isobu_captured_by_Akatsuki.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150209111508)

Akatsuki prepares to seal the Three-Tails.

Just when the sealing was almost complete, Yūkimaru, angered by the apparent death of Guren, empowered the beast to break free of its restraints. The Three-Tails went on a rampage, attacking everyone nearby and crushing [Nurari](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nurari "Nurari"), [Kigiri](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kigiri "Kigiri"), and [Kihō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kih%C5%8D "Kihō"). Its attempts to attack Yūkimaru, however, had no effect. It was only through the use of the [Wind Release: Toad Oil Flame Bullet](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Release:_Toad_Oil_Flame_Bullet "Wind Release: Toad Oil Flame Bullet") that the beast was driven away. The sealing attempt was left to [Anbu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Anbu "Anbu") members, and the remaining Konoha ninja returned home. [Deidara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Deidara "Deidara") and [Tobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobi "Tobi") of [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki") soon found these Anbu members and disposed of them.

### Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax

Main article: [Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War:_Climax "Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax")

![The remaining jinchūriki transform into their tailed beasts.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/59/Biju_full_transformation.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130905164030)

The remaining jinchūriki transform into their tailed beasts.

After [Son Gokū](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Son_Gok%C5%AB "Son Gokū") was resealed into the Demonic Statue of the Outer Path, Tobi, who was intent on going all-out from that point on, forced Yagura to fully transform into the Three-Tails. The opposing tailed beasts prepared to attack the shinobi, but they were [rebuffed](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Roar "Great Roar") by a fully-transformed [Naruto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto "Naruto"). Wasting no time in their attacks, the Three-Tails evaded Naruto and [Kurama](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kurama "Kurama") 's pile-drive attack by rolling out of the way to counter-attack after the [Six-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Six-Tails "Six-Tails") had incapacitated them, but the turtle was stopped by the [Eight-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight-Tails "Eight-Tails"). Freeing itself from the ox's grasp, the opposing beasts regrouped and prepared a collaborative Tailed Beast Ball, which was met by one from Naruto and Kurama.

![Isobu, alongside the other jinchūriki and tailed beasts, encounters Naruto.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/6b/Seishin_Sekai.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130905162801)

Isobu, alongside the other jinchūriki and tailed beasts, encounters Naruto.

As Naruto entered a deeper plane of the tailed beasts' consciousness, Isobu (who remembered [meeting](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Three-Tails%27_Appearance "Three-Tails' Appearance") him in the anime) introduced itself to the young shinobi. Later, as Isobu was freed from the [chakra receiver](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_receiver "Chakra receiver"), it affirmed Kurama's belief that Naruto was the person the [Sage of the Six Paths](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_of_the_Six_Paths "Sage of the Six Paths") had spoken about so long ago before being resealed into the [Demonic Statue of the Outer Path](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Statue_of_the_Outer_Path "Demonic Statue of the Outer Path").

### Birth of the Ten-Tails' Jinchūriki

Main article: [Birth of the Ten-Tails' Jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Birth_of_the_Ten-Tails%27_Jinch%C5%ABriki "Birth of the Ten-Tails' Jinchūriki")

![The tailed beasts being freed from Obito.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/21/Biju_released.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250919211345)

The tailed beasts being freed from Obito.

Isobu appeared alongside the other tailed beasts with the exception of Gyūki and [Shukaku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shukaku "Shukaku") when the [Ten-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails "Ten-Tails") looked down at Naruto. The image of the tailed beasts, along with a silhouette of the Sage of Six Paths, drove the Ten-Tails to a fit as it evolved further. Due to the combined teamwork of the [Allied Shinobi Forces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Allied_Shinobi_Forces "Allied Shinobi Forces"), Isobu, alongside the other imprisoned tailed beasts, was later pulled out of Obito and thus freed.

![The tailed beasts working together to attack Madara.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/53/Tailed_Beasts_vs_Madara.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250919131223)

The tailed beasts working together to attack Madara.

When Madara ran towards the tailed beasts, both Shukaku and [Gaara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gaara "Gaara") conjured [numerous sand bullets](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Release:_Sand_Buckshot "Wind Release: Sand Buckshot") and were able to restrain the Uchiha's movements. Using the opportunity to attack, the tailed beasts each took a swing at their opponent, giving sand-manipulating duo an opening to use their [Desert Layered Imperial Funeral Seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Desert_Layered_Imperial_Funeral_Seal "Desert Layered Imperial Funeral Seal"), but Madara managed to escape the sand pyramid with his Susanoo. As Naruto in [Tailed Beast Mode](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Mode "Tailed Beast Mode") pinned Madara down with a tail, Isobu and the other tailed beasts joined in and did the same to increase the damage, but the Uchiha survived their attacks and retreated to a safe distance. After he received his own right Rinnegan eye from a White Zetsu clone, Madara quickly summoned the Demonic Statue of the Outer Path from Obito's body, and used the Rinnegan to [repel](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Limbo:_Border_Jail "Limbo: Border Jail") the tailed beasts. With the tailed beasts stunned, Madara then [chained](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Statue_Chains "Demonic Statue Chains") them up and sealed them back into the Demonic Statue.

![The tailed beasts reunite with Hagoromo and Naruto.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/bd/Tailed_Beast_Reunion.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150730121101)

The tailed beasts reunite with Hagoromo and Naruto.

Later, the chakra Isobu transferred to Naruto earlier, manifested within Naruto's subconscious along with the other tailed beasts to speak with the young jinchūriki and the spirit of [Hagoromo Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hagoromo_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Hagoromo Ōtsutsuki"). There, they all voiced their shared belief that Naruto was the child of prophecy.

### Kaguya Ōtsutsuki Strikes

Main article: [Kaguya Ōtsutsuki Strikes](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaguya_%C5%8Ctsutsuki_Strikes "Kaguya Ōtsutsuki Strikes")

![The tailed beasts being freed from the Ten-Tails.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/af/Tailed_Beast_separate_from_Kaguya.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160901155526)

The tailed beasts being freed from the Ten-Tails.

After Madara's ambitions were cut short by [Black Zetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Zetsu "Black Zetsu"), who revealed to be the silent manipulator of shinobi history since it was first created by [Kaguya Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaguya_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Kaguya Ōtsutsuki"), the black entity used Madara's body as a medium to resurrect its creator. During the battle against the tyrannical princess, Isobu and the other tailed beasts continued to support Naruto by giving him [their combined power](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_Art:_Super_Tailed_Beast_Rasenshuriken "Sage Art: Super Tailed Beast Rasenshuriken") to overwhelm her. Ultimately, Kaguya was defeated by Naruto and [Sasuke](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke "Sasuke"), who used the [Six Paths — Chibaku Tensei](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Six_Paths_%E2%80%94_Chibaku_Tensei "Six Paths — Chibaku Tensei") to eject all the tailed beasts from her and seal her away inside a newly-created [moon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Moon "Moon") within her own [dimension](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaguya%27s_Dimensions "Kaguya's Dimensions").

After the spirits of Hagoromo and the past Kage summoned both the tailed beasts and [Team 7](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Kakashi "Team Kakashi") back to the real world, the tailed beasts began to discuss their plans for the future since they were finally free. Sasuke, however, made his own intentions known: removing as many people as possible who could get in the way of his plans of leading the world through a revolution to do away with current shinobi systems, including killing all the current Kage and tailed beasts. Just as Sasuke swiftly [subdued](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu:_Sharingan "Genjutsu: Sharingan") the tailed beasts with his Rinnegan and sealed them within his [Chibaku Tensei](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chibaku_Tensei "Chibaku Tensei"), Naruto reassured the tailed beasts that he would save them once again. After Naruto was able to defeat and reform Sasuke, the two dispelled the Infinite Tsukuyomi and Sasuke released Isobu, along with the other tailed beasts, from their prisons.

Isobu also appears as a boss in *[Naruto Shippūden: Ninja Destiny 3](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Shipp%C5%ABden:_Ninja_Destiny_3 "Naruto Shippūden: Ninja Destiny 3")*.

## Trivia

- 'Isobu' (磯撫) literally means 'beach stroker', and is also an adaptation of the name of the sea [yōkai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Y%C5%8Dkai "wikipedia:Yōkai") [Isonade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isonade "wikipedia:Isonade") (磯撫で). The Isonade had a hooked tail, similar to Yagura's staff.
- Since Rin died without having Isobu extracted, Isobu is the first known tailed beast to have "died" and revived.
- In the [Three-Tails' Appearance](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Three-Tails%27_Appearance "Three-Tails' Appearance"), Isobu's capture took place much later than in the manga, but played out in much the same way.
- Isobu's turtle-based appearance may be a reference to [Gamera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gamera "wikipedia:Gamera"); the *[Rough Sea Splash](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rough_Sea_Splash "Rough Sea Splash")* technique in *[Naruto Shippūden: Ultimate Ninja Storm 3](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Shipp%C5%ABden:_Ultimate_Ninja_Storm_3 "Naruto Shippūden: Ultimate Ninja Storm 3")* involves Isobu ramming into an opponent by emitting streams of water from his arms, strongly resembling Gamera's ability to fly with jets and ramming into opponents. Isobu also possesses a technique to [gain high mobility by spinning](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Leaping_Bullet_Shell_Tower "Leaping Bullet Shell Tower") while one of Gamera's signature abilities is also fly and rolling on the ground to ram into opponents by rapidly spinning.
	- References to classic kaiju medias have been made by [Masashi Kishimoto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Masashi_Kishimoto "Masashi Kishimoto") regarding Tailed Beasts on several occasions such as *[Tailed Beast Full Charge](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Full_Charge "Tailed Beast Full Charge")* and *[Tailed Beast Mode!!](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Mode!! "Tailed Beast Mode!!")*.
