This article is about the character. For the episode, see [Kaguya Ōtsutsuki (episode)](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaguya_%C5%8Ctsutsuki_\(episode\) "Kaguya Ōtsutsuki (episode)").

**Kaguya Ōtsutsuki** (大筒木カグヤ, *Ōtsutsuki Kaguya*) is a member of the [Ōtsutsuki Clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/%C5%8Ctsutsuki_Clan "Ōtsutsuki Clan") and was the matriarch of a branch family of her clan, including her children [Hagoromo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hagoromo "Hagoromo") and [Hamura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hamura "Hamura"). Long before the founding of the hidden villages, during an era of endless war, Kaguya betrayed her clan by consuming the fruit of the [God Tree](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/God_Tree "God Tree") and became the first wielder of [chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra "Chakra") on Earth, before later merging with the tree's [Tailed Beast](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails "Ten-Tails") form in a battle against her children that ended with her being sealed away.

Centuries later, during the Fourth Shinobi World War, Kaguya was brought back once again by her "son", Black Zetsu at the expense of Madara Uchiha, Kaguya resuming her attempt to claim all the chakra for herself, only for both Kaguya and Black Zetsu to end up being defeated by Team 7 and sealed away.

## Background

Long ago,[^8] Kaguya and [Isshiki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Isshiki "Isshiki") were both sent to cultivate a God Tree on the planet Earth and harvest its [Chakra Fruit](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Fruit "Chakra Fruit"),[^9] with which she was to provide for the main family of her clan from another world.[^10] According to [Amado Sanzu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Amado_Sanzu "Amado Sanzu"), Kaguya was a low-ranking member of the clan chosen as a sacrifice to the Ten-Tails, which would nurture the development of the God Tree.[^11] Instead, Kaguya desired the chakra fruit herself; betraying Isshiki in the process and leaving him for dead.

In the anime, Kaguya was discovered by the people of the [Land of Ancestors](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Ancestors "Land of Ancestors") during an arrival to Earth, who brought her before their emperor [Tenji](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tenji "Tenji"). Identifying herself as the God Tree's guardian, Kaguya used her abilities to wipe Tenji's memories of the encounter and subsequently settled among his people, becoming recognised as his concubine. Living among humans, she slowly developed an attachment for the planet she meant to destroy. Growing closer with the emperor, she eventually fell pregnant. When the [Land of That](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_That "Land of That") began to threaten the Land of Ancestors over a border dispute, Tenji attempted to prevent the outbreak of war by declaring that anyone who attacked the larger nation's representatives was to be executed, an order which she broke when she was threatened by them, resulting in her and her assistant [Aino](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Aino "Aino") becoming hunted by their land to be executed.[^12]

![Kaguya eats the chakra fruit.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/b2/Kaguya_Eats_Fruit.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160513111921)

Kaguya eats the chakra fruit.

Losing all faith in humanity but still longing for peace, Kaguya believed that it was necessary for her to attain god-like powers in order to put an end to all of humanity's conflicts. So, despite her clan waiting to harvest that world of its God Tree,[^13] she defied the taboo surrounding the fruit and ate it. In doing so, she awakened the [Rinne Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rinne_Sharingan "Rinne Sharingan") [^2] and became the sole person on the planet endowed with chakra. Using her new-found power, Kaguya single-handedly ended the wars,[^14] and took control of the nations.[^15] For her deed of stopping the conflict, she received the blessing of the people and was worshipped as the Rabbit Goddess (卯の女神, *Usagi no Megami*).[^8] [^16] After establishing peace and changing somehow, Kaguya used the [Infinite Tsukuyomi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Infinite_Tsukuyomi "Infinite Tsukuyomi") on humans to [transform](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/God:_Nativity_of_a_World_of_Trees "God: Nativity of a World of Trees") them into an army of [White Zetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/White_Zetsu_Army "White Zetsu Army") over the course of many years [^5] in fear of her clan's impending retaliation. In the anime, not wanting mankind to perish, she freed some victims of the [genjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu "Genjutsu") and erased their memories.[^17]

While giving birth to her twin sons Hagoromo and Hamura, she dispersed chakra to them,[^18] making the pair the first people born with chakra. Ruling by herself and often resorting to cruelty, her [dōjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/D%C5%8Djutsu "Dōjutsu") and [jutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Infinite_Tsukuyomi "Infinite Tsukuyomi") were regarded with fear, awe and reverence by humans. At some indefinite point, the Rabbit Goddess had instead become known as a Demon (鬼, *Oni*) for her despotic rule over the lands and its residents.[^8] [^19] Eventually, Kaguya found herself ruled by her own lust for power and desire to possess all chakra, which eventually turned her against her sons. Having devised a plan to absorb their chakra, she integrated with the God Tree and became the Ten-Tails itself, followed by her challenging the twins to battle.[^18]

![Kaguya sealed by her sons.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/c3/Kaguya_Sealed_By_Sons.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160606154212)

Kaguya sealed by her sons.

Assuming the Ten-Tails was an incarnation of the God Tree that attacked them to recover the chakra from its stolen fruit, Hagoromo and Hamura [sealed](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/F%C5%ABinjutsu "Fūinjutsu") the beast's chakra within the former's body while using its [husk](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Statue_of_the_Outer_Path "Demonic Statue of the Outer Path") as the core of [Six Paths — Chibaku Tensei](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Six_Paths_%E2%80%94_Chibaku_Tensei "Six Paths — Chibaku Tensei"), creating a celestial body that would become known as the [Moon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Moon "Moon"). Hamura later took leadership of the Ōtsutsuki clan and departed to the moon to guard the beast's remains, while Hagoromo remained on Earth in order to spread chakra to humanity and teach them the concept of [ninshū](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ninsh%C5%AB "Ninshū").[^20] However, just before her sons could completely seal her, Kaguya had manifested her [will](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Will_Materialisation "Will Materialisation") in the form of an artificial human called [Black Zetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Zetsu "Black Zetsu"). With the agenda of reviving its " *mother* ", Black Zetsu manipulated Hagoromo's eldest son, [Indra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Indra "Indra"), as well as his descendants within the [Uchiha clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uchiha_clan "Uchiha clan") and his [brother](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Asura_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Asura Ōtsutsuki") 's descendants within the [Senju clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Senju_clan "Senju clan") in an attempt to get one of them to awaken the [Rinnegan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rinnegan "Rinnegan") and [break](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Demonic_Statue_of_the_Outer_Path "Summoning: Demonic Statue of the Outer Path") the seal. [Madara Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Madara_Uchiha "Madara Uchiha") would eventually succeed in obtaining the Rinnegan, and Black Zetsu began secretly manipulating world events to have Kaguya revived.[^21]

## Personality

![Kaguya's mixed feeling towards her sons.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/c9/Kaguya_crying.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160526120922)

Kaguya's mixed feeling towards her sons.

As noted by her son Hagoromo, Kaguya was once truly a good and caring person. This was expanded on in the anime where despite displaying an apathetic personality, she longed for peace and eventually fell in love with Tenji, who shared a similar dream, even though she never openly displayed her feelings for him. She also cared deeply for her hand-maiden Aino, even though she never openly displayed emotions to her either until just before Aino's death. However, she was also treacherous, spiteful, and ruthless towards those she did not care about, as she betrayed her fellow clansman Isshiki and nearly killed him; Amado speculated it could either be personal enmity or her desire to have the chakra fruit for herself. In the anime, although she had affection for Tenji, it was not strong enough to stop her from trapping him in the Infinite Tsukuyomi when he betrayed her for his own pursuit of peace.

In the anime, Hagoromo and Hamura understood that their mother committed immoral acts for the sake of protecting them from the clan that she previously defected from, and they still felt affection for her because she was simply a mother doing what she could for her children. Even Kaguya herself told her sons prior to their battle that she had given them nothing but unconditional nurturing and protection, and willingly shared her chakra with them before their betrayal prompted her to try to claim it back. However, whatever love she harboured for her children was small, as shown where she was willing to take Hamura hostage and force him to fight Hagoromo in order to reclaim her chakra without remorse. Although, Kaguya did claim Hagoromo's love would prevent him from killing Hamura, suggesting she wanted to claim her chakra back without killing her children.[^7] It was later revealed she was on a mission to provide chakra to her clan, but after falling in love with a human and bearing his children, she created a White Zetsu Army to fight them off and protect not only her children, but the inhabitants of the Earth. This suggests that had it not been for her clan, she would have been a benevolent ruler.

After obtaining the ability to manipulate powerful chakra from the God Tree's fruit, Kaguya had grown despotic and developed both a god-complex and messiah-complex, believing that chakra rightfully belonged to her alone and that only she could protect and unite the world. Black Zetsu noted that Kaguya tried to love humans for the sake of peace, but her distrust in them proved far too great as she came to believe all they did was fight and destroy. Despite her initial desire for peace like her sons, grandsons, and in the anime, her lover, Kaguya ultimately caved into corruption from her power and lost faith in humanity's ability to do what is right. As a result of her losing faith in humanity, she opted to use her great and terrible power to submit everyone to her will. As Hagoromo noted, humanity around the time of her rule began to fear her as a demon for her repressive tyranny. She used the archaic pronoun "warawa" (ワラワ) when referring to herself — a sign of ancient nobility. While her belief that chakra should belong to her alone made her somewhat obsessive in retrieving it from everyone else, she was willing to listen to Black Zetsu's suggestion on killing Naruto to prevent him and Sasuke from sealing her away, though she expressed reluctance that she must destroy a large reservoir of chakra to preserve her freedom.[^22] Despite her sombreness, she could nonetheless be stunned by unexpected events, as one of [Naruto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto "Naruto") 's [perverted ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sexy_Reverse_Harem_Technique "Sexy Reverse Harem Technique") caught her off-guard, thereby allowing him to land a hit on her.[^23]

Naruto stated that unlike all his previous foes, he couldn't empathise with Kaguya at all, as he felt she completely lacked a heart and was too strange in nature to reconcile with. Hagoromo wasn't sure why his once benevolent mother ended up the way she did, but believed her eating the chakra fruit may have had something to do with it. Nevertheless, even after her resurrection, Kaguya displayed a certain degree of affection, as she appears to care very deeply about the Earth, viewing it as a nursery that she does not want humanity itself to damage any further.[^24] Although she openly stated that she hated her own sons for using their inherited chakra against her and created the Ten-Tails to take it back by force, she openly wept when looking at [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki") and [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha"), who reminded her of them, hinting that part of her still loved her children.[^21]

## Appearance

![Kaguya's full appearance.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/aa/Kaguya_full_appearance.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160507231422)

Kaguya's full appearance.

Kaguya had grey beige skin (pale in the anime) and soft facial features. She had extremely long, sweeping white [^25] hair (grey in the anime). In the anime, after consuming the forbidden fruit, her hair grew even longer, dragging across the ground and her fingernails grew long and dark.[^12] Most noticeable, she had two horns which have the same colour of her skin (brown in the anime) that stuck out from her head, and a [third eye](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rinne_Sharingan "Rinne Sharingan") formed in the centre of her forehead. Kaguya possessed white clear eyes and her eyebrows were cut very short and round— a symbol of nobility, and she wore a red shade of lipstick on her lips. She wore a high-collared hime-kimono which has [tomoe](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tomoe "Tomoe") running down the centre and edges of the sleeves, and black stripes on the edges of the gown (gold and purple lines in the anime).

When her power went out of control, Kaguya transformed into a conglomerate of the tailed beasts. Her original body turned into a gigantic rabbit creature with a humanoid upper body, jagged teeth and her signature Rinne Sharingan on its forehead. On its back was a great spherical mass of chakra with the heads of the nine tailed beasts emerging from it, along with chakra arms. When Kaguya had her powers stripped away, she briefly reverted to her Ten-Tailed form, with each tail ending with the head of one of the tailed beasts and the tenth ending with her rabbit head.

## Abilities

![Kaguya absorbing ninjutsu.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/e/ec/Kaguya_Absorbs_Amaterasu.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160606155402)

Kaguya absorbing ninjutsu.

Even before eating the chakra fruit of the God Tree, Kaguya was able to leave her fellow clansman Isshiki near death, albeit using a surprise attack.[^26] After eating the chakra fruit, Kaguya achieved such unrivalled power, as she was able to pacify the war-torn world all by herself, leading the people to worship her in fear.[^16] Hagoromo, being a legendary figure himself, praised his mother's might as superior to all others, including his own combined with his brother's.[^16] Even at the height of their powers, it took several months for Hagoromo and Hamura to defeat her.

### Chakra and Physical Prowess

After eating the God Tree's fruit, Kaguya gained unparalleled chakra reserves and strength, noted by both Naruto and Sasuke to surpass Madara's as the Ten-Tails' [jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki").[^27] In the anime, just a simple release of her chakra could create massive shockwaves capable of subduing Hamura.[^17] As the first person to ever wield chakra on Earth, Kaguya can absorb any jutsu she encounters.[^28] When making physical contact with an enemy, she is able to [halt their movements](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Temporary_Paralysis_Technique "Temporary Paralysis Technique") while [absorbing their chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Absorption_Techniques "Chakra Absorption Techniques").[^21] Through her bond with the God Tree, she becomes stronger by gaining chakra from the [tethered](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/God:_Nativity_of_a_World_of_Trees "God: Nativity of a World of Trees") victims of the Infinite Tsukuyomi.

Kaguya has shown a degree of unarmed combat skills, as seen from her being able to easily block several attacks from Naruto's omnidirectional [shadow clone](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_clone "Shadow clone") assault.[^22] She can also move very fast, shown when she suddenly appeared before [Sakura Haruno](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sakura_Haruno "Sakura Haruno") in the blink of an eye,[^29] and later when she reacted quickly to evade Sasuke's [teleportation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Amenotejikara "Amenotejikara") -initiated Chidori, even though she first had to switch dimensions.[^30]

Upon gaining the [Ten-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails "Ten-Tails") ' power from merging with the God Tree,[^31] then once again through Madara as a [vessel](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Six_Paths_Ten-Tails_Coffin_Seal "Six Paths Ten-Tails Coffin Seal"),[^32] Kaguya essentially became immortal, able to quickly regenerate any lost anatomy such as severed limbs, with Hagoromo lending Naruto and Sasuke the only means of defeating her, the [Six Paths — Chibaku Tensei](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Six_Paths_%E2%80%94_Chibaku_Tensei "Six Paths — Chibaku Tensei") through the [Yin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Six_Paths_Yin_Power "Six Paths Yin Power") and [Yang](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Six_Paths_Yang_Power "Six Paths Yang Power") Seals. She retained the ability to mould chakra even though she was hit with [nine different variations of the Rasenshuriken](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_Art:_Super_Tailed_Beast_Rasenshuriken "Sage Art: Super Tailed Beast Rasenshuriken"), escaping with merely superficial damage.

### Ninjutsu

Though she was active well before the dawn of shinobi and thus [ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ninjutsu "Ninjutsu"), Kaguya, as an Ōtsutsuki, is capable of casting various jutsu for which she needs not weave [signs](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hand_Seal "Hand Seal") and the natural capacity to fly. With her absolute mastery of chakra, she can utilise all five [nature transformations](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nature_transformation "Nature transformation") along with [Yin–Yang Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yin%E2%80%93Yang_Release "Yin–Yang Release").[^2] She can make use of her extremely long hair as a [makeshift weapon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hair_Binding_Technique "Hair Binding Technique") for capturing and tossing foes around the battlefield. She can also use the [Summoning Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning_Technique "Summoning Technique").[^2] Most notably, she can create life to serve on her behalf as her [will](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Will_Materialisation "Will Materialisation").[^33] [^29]

### Kekkei Mōra

![Kaguya's All-Killing Ash Bones.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/d/d1/All-Killing_Ash_Bones.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160814174157)

Kaguya's All-Killing Ash Bones.

Kaguya possesses a number of unique abilities, some of which predate those shown in the series and are far deadlier in comparison. The [Eighty Gods Vacuum Attack](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eighty_Gods_Vacuum_Attack "Eighty Gods Vacuum Attack") allows Kaguya to materialise enormous [chakra fists](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eighty_Gods_Vacuum_Attack "Eighty Gods Vacuum Attack") to attack the target, which are powerful enough to overcome a [jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki") 's [chakra arms](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Chakra_Arms "Tailed Beast Chakra Arms") and destroy a Complete Body — Susanoo with ease;[^34] [^35] this jutsu can also be used defensively. She can also harden her long hair to fire [countless needles](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rabbit_Hair_Needle "Rabbit Hair Needle") with enough ferocity to pierce [Susanoo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Susanoo "Susanoo"), an "absolute defence".[^36] With the [All-Killing Ash Bones](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/All-Killing_Ash_Bones "All-Killing Ash Bones"), Kaguya can harden her bones to launch as weaponised projectiles from her back or hands. When hitting living targets, these bones will cause the victim's body to disintegrate into piles of ash, killing them. Kaguya's strongest ability is the [Expansive Truth-Seeking Ball](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Expansive_Truth-Seeking_Ball "Expansive Truth-Seeking Ball"), formed by the accumulated chakra drawn from all the people connected to the God Tree. According to Black Zetsu, it houses every chakra nature, with the power to reshape the world by reducing it to nothing first, essentially serving as the start of a new dimension.

#### Byakugan

![Kaguya's activated dōjutsu.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/1/1c/Kaguya%27s_dojutsu.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160505125408)

Kaguya's activated dōjutsu.

Kaguya wields the [Byakugan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Byakugan "Byakugan") in both eyes,[^1] [^19] granting her a near 360° field of x-ray vision (with the presumed exception of a small blind spot at the upper thoracic vertebrae) and the ability to see the [chakra pathway system](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_pathway_system "Chakra pathway system"). She notes that her Byakugan is especially powerful, far more so than her younger son Hamura's.[^17] Using her Byakugan, she identified that Naruto and Sasuke had similar chakra to her sons and Hagoromo's children after seeing the chakra accumulated in their palms and pathway systems.[^37] In conjunction with her hair, she is able to see her opponent's vital points, accurately piercing them in order to immobilise them completely.[^36]

#### Rinne Sharingan

Kaguya wields her Rinne Sharingan as a third eye on her forehead, which is red in colour and contains several concentric circles and nine tomoe.[^38] Her third eye also grants her the [Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sharingan "Sharingan") 's powers,[^19] such as seeing the flow of chakra, casting and easily recognising genjutsu, heightened powers of perception, among the other abilities it held. Using the Rinne Sharingan's power, Kaguya could cast Infinite Tsukuyomi on select individuals.[^39] She is able to [manipulate](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/God:_Nativity_of_a_World_of_Trees "God: Nativity of a World of Trees") the roots of the God Tree to encase those caught within her Infinite Tsukuyomi, converting most of the populace into [White Zetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/White_Zetsu_Army "White Zetsu Army") by slowly draining them of their individuality, personalities and defining features.[^40] She can become stronger by rapidly absorbing the chakra of everyone connected to the God Tree.

With her third eye, Kaguya is able to [instantaneously teleport](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Amenominaka "Amenominaka") herself and others around her to a different [dimension](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaguya%27s_Dimension "Kaguya's Dimension"), while simultaneously erasing the presence of their chakra.[^41] Similarly, she is able to open and travel through [rifts in space](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yomotsu_Hirasaka "Yomotsu Hirasaka") as a means of instantaneous travel. She has demonstrated the unique ability to merge with nature to give her control of the environments of her dimensions, such as ice and snow.[^42]

### Tailed Beast Transformation

![Kaguya's unstable Tailed Beast Transformation.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/f5/Kaguya_Transformed.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160825114505)

Kaguya's unstable Tailed Beast Transformation.

Combined with the God Tree, Kaguya became the Ten-Tails to take back the chakra inherited by her sons. While fighting [Team 7](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Kakashi "Team Kakashi"), the [tailed beasts](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_beasts "Tailed beasts") ' chakra in Naruto's [Sage Art: Super Tailed Beast Rasenshuriken](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_Art:_Super_Tailed_Beast_Rasenshuriken "Sage Art: Super Tailed Beast Rasenshuriken") reacted to the chakra inside of Kaguya, resulting in her mutating into an unstable, rabbit-esque version of the Ten-Tails. Black Zetsu noted that the transformation was not humanoid and did not happen intentionally,[^43] implying that she's also able to transform into the Ten-Tails at will.

When she became an unstable Ten-Tails from Naruto's attack, she needed to absorb chakra from victims trapped within the Infinite Tsukuyomi to return to her normal form.

### Other skills

In the anime, Kaguya showed otherworldly powers long before she consumed the fruit of the God Tree, being able to perform interstellar travel, subdue people into sleep, hypnotise them, erase their memories, unleash a powerful shockwave from her eyes capable of blowing away or maiming people,[^12] and read a person's thoughts and feelings to certain extent.[^42]

## Part II

### Kaguya Ōtsutsuki Strikes

Main article: [Kaguya Ōtsutsuki Strikes](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaguya_%C5%8Ctsutsuki_Strikes "Kaguya Ōtsutsuki Strikes")

![Kaguya is revived.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/c1/Kaguya%27s_revival.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160505121840)

Kaguya is revived.

After the Infinite Tsukuyomi was cast by Madara Uchiha, he attempted to finally tie up loose ends by eliminating Naruto Uzumaki, Sasuke Uchiha, [Kakashi Hatake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake "Kakashi Hatake"), and Sakura Haruno. When he proclaimed himself as the [Saviour of the World](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Saviour_of_the_World "Saviour of the World"), Black Zetsu — who had been standing behind Madara prior — impaled the elder Uchiha in the chest with the left hand of Obito Uchiha whose body Black Zetsu had taken control of. Madara was left shocked and horrified, remarking that Black Zetsu was his will with Black Zetsu berating Madara. Black Zetsu then revealed that his will was that of Kaguya's, much to Madara's horror. Black Zetsu then began covering Madara's body with his own malleable mass. As Sasuke and Naruto attempted to intervene to stop the revival from going ahead, they were captured by [hair-like appendages](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hair_Binding_Technique "Hair Binding Technique") while Madara's body continued to expand, growing off of enormous reserves of chakra from the victims of the Infinite Tsukuyomi. His body grew until Black Zetsu's mass completely engulfed him, thereby completing Kaguya's revival who now stood in Madara's place and resulted in Kaguya having been successfully brought back into the modern ninja world. She looked on at the surroundings and, upon noticing Naruto and Sasuke and, more important, the marks on their hands before the hair-like appendages, now made up of her actual hair, threw them back to the ground. She activated her Byakugan, noticing that the two youths had similar chakra to her sons and Hagoromo's children, before coming to the conclusion that Hagoromo had bestowed his powers to them. Kakashi asked her about her motives, which led her to state that the world was her nursery and as such, she wanted no more fighting to damage it any further. She then teleported [Team 7](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Kakashi "Team Kakashi") and [Obito Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha "Obito Uchiha") into an active volcano, telling them that she would have them disappear.

Kaguya catches Naruto and Sasuke.

Seeing this, Kaguya struck them once again with her hair, this time aiming with her Byakugan and firing needle-like strands at Sasuke, who was saved by Naruto whom had just discovered his ability to fly. Noticing this, and Naruto's power resembling that of her son, Kaguya grew enraged and clashed with Naruto while declaring all the chakra in the world should once again be hers. As she knocked Naruto back, Sasuke appeared above her and attempted to strike her with his Susanoo's sword. The assault left Kaguya unscathed and she was able to rebuff Sasuke sending him reeling to where he almost fell into the lava, before teleporting behind Naruto and using one of his Truth-Seeking Balls as a platform for safety. Kaguya immediately entered a dimensional void and transported herself behind the duo, noticeably crying due to the resemblance they held to her own children, before forcing Black Zetsu over their bodies. Black Zetsu then began to tell the two youths the truth about past events, things that even Hagoromo was unaware of. He told them that Hagoromo and Hamura once sealed Kaguya using the same powers that Sasuke and Naruto inherited and that he had been manipulating the descendants of Indra and Asura for presumably all of time, attempting to get an Uchiha to awaken the Rinnegan and activate the Infinite Tsukuyomi so that the Ten-Tails — and subsequently, his mother, Kaguya — would be revived.

## Legacy

![Kaguya's ultimate ambition finally achieved with the Eye of the Moon Plan.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/d/d1/Kaguya_Plan_Complete.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150827121344)

Kaguya's ultimate ambition finally achieved with the Eye of the Moon Plan.

Despite Kaguya's genuine desire to bring forth peace, her methods only brought it about temporarily at the cost of much death. It also inspired the people of Hagoromo's era to [weaponise](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ninjutsu "Ninjutsu") chakra for wars instead of using it for peace, which would include the tailed beasts being seen as nothing more than masses of powerful chakra to exploit and control and the hosts to be seen as such.[^44] [^19] Before she was sealed away, she created Black Zetsu as an extension of her will to facilitate her future revival. Black Zetsu's actions throughout the history of the shinobi world included influencing the Uchiha and Senju clan for hundreds of years, and tampering with the [Naka Shrine](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naka_Shrine "Naka Shrine") [stone monument](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Stone_Tablet "Stone Tablet").[^21]

Kaguya is the progenitor of the [Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hy%C5%ABga "Hyūga"), [Kaguya](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaguya_clan "Kaguya clan"),[^45] Senju, [Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uzumaki "Uzumaki"), and Uchiha clans, as she is the mother or grandmother of each clans' ancestor and originator of their unique abilities. Eventually, Black Zetsu's efforts directly led Madara Uchiha to create and carry out the [Eye of the Moon Plan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eye_of_the_Moon_Plan "Eye of the Moon Plan"), and creating [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki") as a project to prepare the Infinite Tsukuyomi, which led to the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"). Madara would later succeed in casting the Infinite Tsukuyomi upon the entire world thinking he had accomplished his goal, but he had also unknowingly facilitated the revival of Kaguya. This came about when Black Zetsu manifested his will into Madara, using him as a vessel to revive Kaguya into the Mortal World, essentially bringing her back to life once more before she was finally and permanently defeated by both Naruto and Sasuke.

Despite her god-like might, she still felt the need to leave a scroll revealing the true nature of her coming to Earth and why. Many years after her defeat, Sasuke Uchiha began investigating Kaguya's reasoning behind creating the White Zetsu Army in the first place. His ultimate theory was that there was a threat which even Kaguya was afraid of, and spent a large majority of his life researching Kaguya's history and her separate dimensions. His suspicions were confirmed when he eventually met [Momoshiki Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Momoshiki_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Momoshiki Ōtsutsuki") and [Kinshiki Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kinshiki_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Kinshiki Ōtsutsuki"). During his travels, he found information regarding the threat documented in a highly encrypted scroll, stored inside a [castle](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/%C5%8Ctsutsuki_Castle "Ōtsutsuki Castle") within her [ice dimension](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaguya%27s_Dimensions "Kaguya's Dimensions"). When it was eventually deciphered in [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure"), it provided crucial information about Kaguya's enemies and was extremely valuable in cementing their defeat.[^46]

Originally, Kaguya was to be a sacrifice to prepare the chakra fruit on Earth for her clan in accordance with the Ōtsutsuki Will. However, her attachment to the planet along with betraying Isshiki and preparation of an army in her own defence led Momoshiki to condemn her a disgrace and a traitor. Along with not abiding her duties that led to Momoshiki's inconvenience of hunting down the Tailed Beasts' chakra personally, he stated his intention to punish her personally, and that her defeat has saved him the trouble of doing that.[^10] Indeed, one of Amado's suggestions was that Kaguya wanted the chakra fruit all to her self, hence her attempt at Isshiki's life.

In the anime, [Danzō Shimura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Danz%C5%8D_Shimura "Danzō Shimura") unknowingly got close to one of Kaguya's techniques, when he cultivated some of the First Hokage's cells to produce [Gozu Tennō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gozu_Tenn%C5%8D "Gozu Tennō").[^47]

## Influence

- Her name and title are references to Princess Kaguya, the main character from *[The Tale of the Bamboo Cutter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Tale_of_the_Bamboo_Cutter "wikipedia:The Tale of the Bamboo Cutter")* (竹取物語, *Taketori Monogatari*), which is said to be one of the oldest Japanese legends in written record. According to the legend, Princess Kaguya wears a *hagoromo* (羽衣, *"feather mantle"*) when she returns home to her kingdom. The name Ōtsutsuki (大筒木) roughly means "big bamboo tree" and is derived from an obscure passage in the second volume of the [*Kojiki*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kojiki "wikipedia:Kojiki") about the genealogies of [Emperor Suinin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emperor_Suinin "wikipedia:Emperor Suinin"), eleventh emperor of Japan, which states that he had a consort named Kaguyahime no Mikoto (迦具夜比売命), the daughter of a king named Ōtsutsukitarine no Miko (大筒木垂根王); tradition holds that these individuals served as the basis for Princess Kaguya and the old bamboo cutter in the aforementioned tale.
	- Her horn-like appendages symbolise rabbit ears, representing her connection to the moon by evoking the [moon rabbit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moon_rabbit "wikipedia:Moon rabbit"); pop culture depictions of Princess Kaguya and the moon-folk often incorporate features like rabbit ears for this reason. This also fits with her being known as the "Rabbit Goddess" (卯の女神, *Usagi no Megami*). This is even more cemented, with her taking a rabbit-like form after being hit by Naruto's Sage Art: Super Tailed Beast Rasenshuriken.
- Kaguya's appearance and attire are heavily inspired by noblewomen of the [Heian period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heian_period "wikipedia:Heian period"). Her [jūnihitoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C5%ABnihitoe "wikipedia:Jūnihitoe") (twelve-layered kimono), her extremely long hair, and her [hikimayu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hikimayu "wikipedia:Hikimayu") (short, round eyebrows) were all hallmarks of nobility and beauty standards for high-ranking women during that era.
- Aspects of Kaguya also bear resemblance to [Izanami-no-Mikoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Izanami-no-Mikoto "wikipedia:Izanami-no-Mikoto"), the Shintō goddess of the dead. How Kaguya desires peace with taking over humankind and suspending them into bliss is similar to the concept of death leading into the afterlife, while her White Zetsu Army are reminiscent of the [Yomotsu-shikome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shikome "wikipedia:Shikome"), Izanami's minions of [Yomi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yomi "wikipedia:Yomi").
- The concept of a woman eating a forbidden fruit may be based on the biblical [Book of Genesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Book_of_Genesis "wikipedia:Book of Genesis"), in which [Eve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eve "wikipedia:Eve"), the predecessor of humanity, eats the fruit of the [Tree of the Knowledge of Good and Evil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tree_of_the_knowledge_of_good_and_evil "wikipedia:Tree of the knowledge of good and evil"), bringing sin to humanity, with the consequence of death.
