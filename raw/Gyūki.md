**Gyūki** (牛鬼), more commonly known as the **Eight-Tails** (八尾, *Hachibi*), is one of the nine [tailed beasts](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_beasts "Tailed beasts"). It has been in the possession of [Kumogakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kumogakure "Kumogakure") for decades, where it has been sealed into many [jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki"). Its most recent jinchūriki, and the first to have any success in cooperating with it, is [Killer B](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Killer_B "Killer B").

## Background

![The young tailed beasts with Hagoromo.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/bb/Young_Bijuu_and_Hagoromo.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150728221849)

The young tailed beasts with Hagoromo.

Gyūki and the tailed beasts first came into being in the waning days of [Hagoromo Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hagoromo_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Hagoromo Ōtsutsuki"), who used his [Creation of All Things Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Creation_of_All_Things_Technique "Creation of All Things Technique") to divide the [Ten-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails "Ten-Tails") ' chakra into nine separate bodies and giving each a name. Some time after being created, the Sage explained to the young tailed beasts that a link existed between them despite their separation. He also foretold of a day when they would be brought together again - though not as they were - and that at that time someone would emerge to show them what true power is.[^2] [^3] In the anime, before his death, Hagoromo sent Gyūki to live in a temple built for the purpose of protecting it in a region surrounding a range of tall mountains between the clouds, that would later become part of the [Land of Lightning](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Lightning "Land of Lightning").[^4]

In an unknown time, [Hashirama Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama_Senju "Hashirama Senju") captured eight of the tailed beasts and sold Gyūki and [Matatabi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Matatabi "Matatabi") to Kumogakure at the [Five Kage Summit](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Kage_Summit "Five Kage Summit"), as a sign of friendship and to ensure a balance of power amongst their new villages.[^5] In a cavern behind the [Falls of Truth](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Falls_of_Truth "Falls of Truth"), where [jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki") learn to control their tailed beasts' power, there are wall murals of Gyūki and Matatabi, which indicates how long they were associated with the country.[^6] [^7] At some point in the past, one Kumo-nin attempted to mimic the [Gold and Silver Brothers](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_and_Silver_Brothers "Gold and Silver Brothers") by eating one of Gyūki's chakra meat tentacles in the hopes of gaining jinchūriki-like power, but died in the process.[^8]

![Gyūki and the Third Raikage collapse from exhaustion.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/bd/A_vs_Eight-Tails.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150821085634)

Gyūki and the Third Raikage collapse from exhaustion.

Throughout the years, none of Gyūki's former jinchūriki - such as [Blue B](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Blue_B "Blue B"), and his uncle and father before him - could control its power and the ox would constantly go on a rampage, destroying the village and killing many of its inhabitants. Despite these setbacks, the [Third Raikage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Raikage "Third Raikage") believed a jinchūriki for Gyūki was too valuable of a war deterrent and decided to keep looking for a compatible one, to which he would form a special coalition to subdue and seal it in the [Kohaku no Jōhei](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kohaku_no_J%C5%8Dhei "Kohaku no Jōhei"). During one of its rampages, the Third fought Gyūki one-on-one until they both collapsed from exhaustion. During another, Gyūki had one of its four horns lost to [A](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Raikage "Fourth Raikage"), the offending horn having killed [Furui](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Furui "Furui"). In the anime, Blue B's rampage was stated to be orchestrated by [Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru"), who gave Blue B genjutsu-inducing pills to draw out Gyūki. Due to the pills, Gyūki had no recollection of the rampage.[^9]

Gyūki was ultimately sealed within [Killer B](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Killer_B "Killer B"), who was five at the time,[^10] through the [Iron Armour Seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iron_Armour_Seal "Iron Armour Seal").[^11] Through rigorous training within the Falls of Truth, B managed to defeat and eventually befriend Gyūki.[^12]

## Personality

Gyūki is characterised as a tough and serious individual. It uses "ore" (オレ) when referring to itself and often speaks in a somewhat rude dialect. Gyūki was also shown to have a good analytical ability, as it told Killer B that if he left [Ponta](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ponta "Ponta") and [Sabu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sabu "Sabu") and ran away from them, [Kisame Hoshigaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kisame_Hoshigaki "Kisame Hoshigaki") and his water dome would follow him and they (Sabu and Ponta) would be freed. Although Gyūki mentioned that it and its brethren disliked [Kurama](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kurama "Kurama") for its arrogance and belief that their strength are based on their number of tails,[^13] it insisted that was no reason for not helping [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki") to control Kurama's power, hinting a benevolent side.[^14] Since then, both Gyūki and B have taken a liking to Naruto.

In the past, Gyūki was infamous in Kumo for being wild and violent, as it went on countless rampages whenever its former jinchūriki would lose control of it. This was most likely due to its hatred against humans for oppressing and sealing it away many times. Gyūki maintained this behaviour until it was sealed within B, who would later change it after he bested it in one-on-one combat. Since then, Gyūki's personality has become much more relaxed, becoming close friends with B; even allowing him to sit on top of its muzzle while talking to it leisurely. However, B tends to annoy Gyūki to comical effect, as its no-nonsense demeanour often contrasts with B's tendency to goof off whenever he likes, adding fuel to Gyūki's irritation to its vessel.

As a result, Gyūki is not above bad-mouthing B and is also known to reprimand him in serious occasions. This was seen when Gyūki criticised B for rapping in his fight with Kisame when he should have been completely focused on their opponent, and later when it comically bumped B in the head when the latter started to mention in a perverted manner how [Yugito](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yugito "Yugito") 's black receivers were embedded in her left breast and when Obito [disappeared](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kamui "Kamui") B was wondering if he escaped only for Gyūki to tell B to find him. This somewhat contradicts the original point of having a jinchūriki which is to control a tailed beast, but Gyūki seems to be more of the one in control than B. Because of this, Gyūki appears to be much more noteworthy than B as far as responsibility is concerned. Nonetheless, Gyūki formed a very strong bond with B, as even after being extracted from its jinchūriki, the tailed beast severed a piece of itself to help sustain B's life. Following its release from Kaguya, Gyūki expressed a desire to return to B, noting it had gotten used to his awful rapping and felt odd not hearing it.

Gyūki's relationship with its B's predecessor Blue B was unknown in the manga, other than the fact it would pounce on the "hole" in Blue B's heart and assume control over him. In the anime, however, Gyūki stated it had high hopes for Blue B, indicating it bore no ill will towards its former host.[^9]

## Appearance

![A young Gyūki.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/be/Young_Gyuki.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130913103418)

A young Gyūki.

Gyūki is an [ushi-oni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ushi-oni "wikipedia:ushi-oni") with four long horns on its head, similar to that of a [Jacob sheep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_\(sheep\) "wikipedia:Jacob (sheep)"). As a tailed beast, it is extremely massive, dwarfing entire forests and towers over large rock formations and buildings and matching the [Giant Squid](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Giant_Squid "Giant Squid") in size. The lower left part of its horns was permanently sliced off in a battle with [A](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Raikage "Fourth Raikage"), and the right horns were destroyed by its own point-blank Tailed Beast Ball when fighting the Ten-Tails. It also has straight teeth, one of which was blown off during the point-blank Tailed Beast Ball. Following the Fourth Shinobi World War, it grew another tooth, along with its horns (albeit not completely).[^15] Gyūki also has a muscular upper-body structure, with a hunched back similar to an [American bison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_bison "wikipedia:American bison"), arms with spiked protrusions on the elbows, and hands with opposable thumbs like that of a human. Instead of hind-legs, its lower half consists of its eight tails, which resemble the [cephalopod arms of an octopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cephalopod_arm "wikipedia:Cephalopod arm"). These tails will grow back in the event they are sliced off. During the last remaining days of Hagoromo Ōtsutsuki, Gyūki was smaller and its horns were much shorter.[^3]

## Abilities

*See also: [Jinchūriki Forms](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki_Forms "Jinchūriki Forms")*

![Gyūki rampages as several Kumo-nin attempt to seal it.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/09/Hachibi_subduing_team.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150821123445)

Gyūki rampages as several Kumo-nin attempt to seal it.

Gyūki was noted by Kisame and Kurama to be the second most powerful of the nine tailed beasts.[^16] [^13] Even the Third Raikage, hailed as the greatest shinobi in Kumo's history, formed a [special team](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight-Tails_Subduing_Team "Eight-Tails Subduing Team") to subdue and seal it during its various rampages, with the ushi-oni fighting the Third to a stalemate on one occasion.[^17] [^18] The [Fourth Raikage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Raikage "Fourth Raikage") also once forbade B from fully transforming into Gyūki due to its great power.[^19] During the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"), Gyūki held its own against two other tailed beasts despite suffering a past injury.[^20]

### Chakra and Physical Prowess

![Gyūki overwhelming Kokuō with its raw strength.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/41/Gyuki_punches_Kokuo.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130920041110)

Gyūki overwhelming Kokuō with its raw strength.

As a [tailed beast](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_beast "Tailed beast"), Gyūki possesses a massive supply of powerful [chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra "Chakra") and can [share it with others](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Transfer_Technique "Chakra Transfer Technique"). It can replenish its high reserves shortly after an entire day's usage.[^21] Should the need arise, it can disturb B's chakra with its own to dispel powerful genjutsu.[^22] Gyūki's tentacles, if severed, can be used as a medium for its chakra, such as creating a [clone](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Octopus_Leg_Clone_Technique "Octopus Leg Clone Technique") of its host, or transferring chakra to said host should the beast be extracted.[^23] It also has powerful healing capacities, able to mend injuries in themselves and others in seconds.[^24] Likewise, Gyūki is effectively immortal, as when it dies, it will eventually revive, albeit in a younger form.

Gyūki has immense physical strength, being able to temporarily restrain Kurama, contain its Tailed Beast Ball, and overwhelm two charging [tailed](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Isobu "Isobu") [beasts](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Koku%C5%8D "Kokuō") simultaneously.[^25] [^26] [^27] It is deceptively quite fast, able to strike quickly with its tentacles and block [Amaterasu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Amaterasu "Amaterasu") with its fist.[^28] [^29] Its incredibly sharp horns can easily impale a human being.[^10] Gyūki is also durable enough to survive its own Tailed Beast Ball,[^30] including a combined explosion of such attacks from itself and the Ten-Tails in the latter's mouth. It can rapidly spin its tentacles to create an [enormous tornado](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Eight_Twists "Tailed Beast Eight Twists"), or wrap them around its body for protection against enemy attacks, even holding back the Ten-Tails' massive Tailed Beast Ball. However, the tentacles can be severed by [sharp](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chidori_Sharp_Spear "Chidori Sharp Spear") [attacks](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flying_Thunder_God_Kunai "Flying Thunder God Kunai") with enough force.

### Ninjutsu

![Gyūki's Tailed Beast Ball.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/9f/Gyuki%27s_Bijudama.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130919142021)

Gyūki's Tailed Beast Ball.

Gyūki can perform the [Tailed Beast Ball](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Ball "Tailed Beast Ball"), being able to fire it normally, [consecutively](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Continuous_Tailed_Beast_Balls "Continuous Tailed Beast Balls") or as a massive wave. It is strong enough to devastate an entire [valley](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Valley_of_Clouds_and_Lightning "Valley of Clouds and Lightning"), penetrate nine [barriers](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Self-Repairing_Barrier "Self-Repairing Barrier") without exploding, and even push the Ten-Tails' own Tailed Beast Ball back into its body.[^31] [^32] Unlike most tailed beasts, Gyūki can supercharge it, creating one many times its own size for much greater power.[^33] Going with its octopus theme, it can produce [ink](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ink_Creation "Ink Creation") to blind its enemies or form [ink clones](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ink_clone "Ink clone") to [seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sealing_Technique:_Octopus_Hold "Sealing Technique: Octopus Hold") them.

### Intelligence

Gyūki is quite intelligent and can formulate tactics in the midst of battle, coaching B on how to counter Kisame Hoshigaki's techniques. It has great knowledge of the Tailed Beast Ball and explained the initial limitations of Naruto's [Nine-Tails Chakra Mode](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tails_Chakra_Mode "Nine-Tails Chakra Mode"). Gyūki is also very perceptive; from witnessing Naruto sense Kurama's chakra coming from outside the [Tailed Beast Temple](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Temple "Tailed Beast Temple"), it correctly deduced that the [Gold and Silver Brothers](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_and_Silver_Brothers "Gold and Silver Brothers") were [reincarnated](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Impure_World_Reincarnation "Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation") by [someone](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kabuto_Yakushi "Kabuto Yakushi") using Orochimaru's [kinjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kinjutsu "Kinjutsu") in the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War").

The Eight-Tails vs Taka.

When [Taka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Taka "Taka") arrived at Kumogakure in an attempt to capture Killer B, he battled them to the point of annoyance. With his great control over his tailed beast, Killer B transformed himself into the Eight-Tails, and proceeded to terrorise Taka. After [Sasuke](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke "Sasuke") activated his [Mangekyō Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mangeky%C5%8D_Sharingan "Mangekyō Sharingan") and released Amaterasu on B, it fell into pain by its flames. Killer B disguised himself as the cut part of the tail and escaped. Killer B was then seemingly captured by Taka and was given to [Tobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobi "Tobi"). Killer B's brother, the [Fourth Raikage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Raikage "Fourth Raikage"), swore that [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki") would pay for what they had done.

It was later revealed that Killer B faked his capture. After Taka left, he and the Eight-Tails left the lake and Killer B took this opportunity to take a "vacation" from being a guardian. As the Eight-Tails commented that a new era was coming, Killer B thought it was going to be his singing career.

![B in his Eight-Tails form attacks the Giant Squid.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/b1/Hachi-B_fights_Squid.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150202192221)

### Fourth Shinobi World War: Confrontation

Main article: [Fourth Shinobi World War: Confrontation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War:_Confrontation "Fourth Shinobi World War: Confrontation") When Naruto failed to fully transform into the Nine-Tails, the Eight-Tails requested that B let it speak with Naruto. It explained the risks of using a tailed beast's chakra, and how using it meant that the fox could deplete Naruto's chakra, because whenever Naruto used the Nine-Tails' chakra, it could take his. It explained that Naruto wouldn't be able to transform fully until he could reach an agreement with Nine-Tails, which it recognised as unlikely, noting that it and B used to be like that. It also said that because of that, he wouldn't be able to learn the Tailed Beast Ball. When it was done, B changed back to his normal self.

The Eight-Tails was later surprised at how Naruto was able to discern the chakra coming from Kinkaku and Ginkaku despite being secured in the training zone and then told B to stop Naruto from leaving the temple, but failed as Naruto was able to trick B into letting him out. Later, after B and Naruto encountered [Nagato](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nagato "Nagato") and [Itachi Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Itachi_Uchiha "Itachi Uchiha") on the battlefield, Itachi trapped [Killer B](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Killer_B "Killer B") in a genjutsu, but Eight-Tails broke the genjutsu for B by disturbing his chakra flow. Ultimately, Itachi is able to break free of his control thanks to one of his crow that he stored inside Naruto using a [powerful genjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kotoamatsukami "Kotoamatsukami"). Itachi then aided Naruto and B against Nagato, ultimately [sealing](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sword_of_Totsuka "Sword of Totsuka") away Nagato. After Itachi explained the situation, he left Naruto and B to stop the [Impure World Reincarnation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Impure_World_Reincarnation "Impure World Reincarnation").

The Eight-Tails later reprimanded B for taking too many breaks to urinate and for letting Naruto go ahead of them. When Naruto contacted them through [Inoichi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Inoichi "Inoichi"), the Eight-Tails talked to him about the time it fought the [Third Raikage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Raikage "Third Raikage"). When asked about the Third's scar, it initially thought that it had caused the scar with his [Tailed Beast Ball](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Ball "Tailed Beast Ball"), but then remembered that they both ended up collapsing from exhaustion during the battle, with both of their techniques still intact.

Later, in the anime, [Motoi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Motoi "Motoi") arrived to aid B. They were then approached by the reincarnated [Blue B](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Blue_B "Blue B"), B's predecessor as the Eight-Tails jinchūriki. Blue B revealed, to the Eight-Tails' shock, the ability to still use the tailed beast chakra cloak, entering his Version 2 form. As B entered Version 2 and clashed with Blue B, the Eight-Tails insisted to know how Blue B can still use that power. Blue B explained that the Eight-Tails' rampage from thirty years ago was in fact manipulated by Orochimaru, using a genjutsu-inducing drug to force out the ox. During the chaos, the Sannin member also harvested some of the Eight-Tails' severed horn. It was from the genetic material that Blue B obtained a recreation of the ox's chakra. As they battled, Motoi was defeated but saved by B. Seeing that B accomplished what he could not, Blue B was able to let go of his past and return to the afterlife.[^9]

### Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax

Main article: [Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War:_Climax "Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax")

![B transforms into the Eight-Tails to level the forest.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/d/d8/B_transformed_into_Gy%C5%ABki.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130815125448)

B transforms into the Eight-Tails to level the forest.

Later, when Naruto and B encountered [Tobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobi "Tobi") and his Six Paths of Pain, the Eight-Tails recognised [Yugito Nii](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yugito_Nii "Yugito Nii") among them and figured out the others were the other deceased jinchūriki. After the initial assault from the dead jinchūriki, it warned B to be careful or he would end up just like them. After the revived jinchūriki enter their tailed forms, the Eight-Tails explained to a bewildered B that Tobi must have turned them back into jinchūriki after reviving them. As B head out to attack them, it told him to be careful as he sensed a change in the other tailed beasts. After escaping Yugito's onslaught, the Eight-Tails surmised that with the aid of the Sharingan and the Rinnegan, the jinchūriki's abilities had been greatly enhanced. It asked to speak to Naruto as well begin to formulate a counter strategy. After B revealed the location of the [black receivers](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Black_receiver "Black receiver") and Naruto's failed attempt to destroy Rōshi's, B in his [Tailed Beast Mode](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Mode "Tailed Beast Mode") [levelled](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Eight_Twists "Tailed Beast Eight Twists") the entire forest to get rid of their disadvantage. He then attempted to [seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sealing_Technique:_Octopus_Hold "Sealing Technique: Octopus Hold") them, but Tobi forced his Paths to enter their Version 2 forms, freeing themselves, and they started attacking Naruto and B. The latter caught a transformed [Utakata](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Utakata "Utakata"), who quickly freed himself by [oozing alkaline](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Leech_Gap "Leech Gap"). After Naruto reassured them it'd be fine because they were a pair of duos, the Eight-Tails expressed shock after seeing Naruto entering his Tailed Beast Mode with [Kurama](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kurama "Kurama").

### Kaguya Ōtsutsuki Strikes

Main article: [Kaguya Ōtsutsuki Strikes](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaguya_%C5%8Ctsutsuki_Strikes "Kaguya Ōtsutsuki Strikes")

![The tailed beasts being freed from the Ten-Tails.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/af/Tailed_Beast_separate_from_Kaguya.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160901155526)

The tailed beasts being freed from the Ten-Tails.

After Madara's ambitions were cut short by [Black Zetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Zetsu "Black Zetsu"), who revealed to be the silent manipulator of shinobi history since it was first created by [Kaguya Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaguya_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Kaguya Ōtsutsuki"), the black entity used Madara's body as a medium to resurrect its creator. During the battle against the tyrannical princess, Gyūki and the other tailed beasts continued to support Naruto by giving him [their combined power](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_Art:_Super_Tailed_Beast_Rasenshuriken "Sage Art: Super Tailed Beast Rasenshuriken") to overwhelm her. Ultimately, Kaguya was defeated by Naruto and Sasuke, who used the [Six Paths — Chibaku Tensei](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Six_Paths_%E2%80%94_Chibaku_Tensei "Six Paths — Chibaku Tensei") to eject all the tailed beasts from her and seal her away inside a newly-created [moon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Moon "Moon") within her own [dimension](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaguya%27s_Dimensions "Kaguya's Dimensions").

After the spirits of Hagoromo and the past Kage summoned both the tailed beasts and [Team 7](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Kakashi "Team Kakashi") back to the real world, the tailed beasts began to discuss their plans for the future since they were finally free. Gyūki, who was asked by Yang-Kurama on the subject, surprised the fox when it expressed its desire to return to B. Sasuke, however, made his own intentions known: removing as many people as possible who could get in the way of his plans of leading the world through a revolution to do away with current shinobi systems, including killing all the current Kage and tailed beasts. Just as Sasuke swiftly [subdued](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu:_Sharingan "Genjutsu: Sharingan") the tailed beasts with his Rinnegan and sealed them within his [Chibaku Tensei](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chibaku_Tensei "Chibaku Tensei"), Naruto reassured the tailed beasts that he would save them once again. After Naruto was able to defeat and reform Sasuke, the two dispelled the Infinite Tsukuyomi and Sasuke released Gyūki along with the other tailed beasts from their prisons.

- "Gyūki" (牛鬼), which literally means "ox ogre", is an alternate pronunciation for the name of the [ushi-oni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ushi-oni "wikipedia:ushi-oni"). In Japanese folklore, an ushi-oni is a massive, brutal [yōkai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/y%C5%8Dkai "wikipedia:yōkai") with the horned head of a bull and the body of an octopus, a crab, or a spider. One legend says that it lives off the coasts of Japan and attacks fishermen.
- Gyūki is the only tailed beast that apparently has been shown to have a permanently damaged body.
- Alongside Chōmei, they are the only tailed beasts that do not have any known elemental affinity. Both are also the only beasts whose tails differ from the conventional ones that their siblings have.
