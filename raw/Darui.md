**Darui** (ダルイ) is a shinobi of [Kumogakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kumogakure "Kumogakure") and the right-hand-man of the [Fourth Raikage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Raikage "Fourth Raikage"),[^4] whom he later succeeds as the **Fifth [Raikage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Raikage "Raikage")** (五代目雷影, *Godaime Raikage*, *literally meaning:* Fifth Lightning Shadow).

[^3]

## Background

Darui was once a student of the [Third Raikage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Raikage "Third Raikage"). During his training, Darui learned, among other things, the Raikage's unique [black lightning](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Black_lightning "Black lightning") technique.[^5] Along with [C](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/C "C"), he also served as the Third Raikage's support.

In the anime, when [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure") and [Sunagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sunagakure "Sunagakure") invited other villages to an unscheduled [joint](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/In_Naruto%27s_Footsteps:_The_Friends%27_Paths "In Naruto's Footsteps: The Friends' Paths") [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams"), the Fourth Raikage was furious at this, taking it as an underhanded scheme to attack Kumogakure. While A flatly told his men to reject [Tsunade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsunade "Tsunade") 's offer, Darui informed A that he already informed [Killer B](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Killer_B "Killer B") of this. A quickly instructed Darui and [C](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/C "C") to prevent B, who was already on his way to leave for the exams, from attending.[^6]

## Personality

Darui is an easy-going and mellow individual as seen when the Fourth Raikage's over-the-top exit through a window, he tells [C](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/C "C") that he will simply use the door like normal people do. His personality is also evident through his lazy and languid way of speaking, often times referring to things or events as being "dull" (だるい, *darui*) — a pun on his name. While having a laid-back demeanour however, Darui isn't lazy, leaping to the Raikage's defence when the bodyguards of the other Kage reacted to the Raikage destroying the desk he was sitting at.[^7] Darui is a very apologetic individual, and will even apologise for other people's actions — a trait he stated that he hadn't noticed himself; this attribute leads him to saying "sorry" (すみません, *sumimasen*) just as much as the word "dull". He also possesses a strong loyalty to the Fourth Raikage whom he often addresses as "boss" (ボス, *bosu*), rather than adding the suffix "sama" to his title as others do. The Fourth in turn, considers Darui his "second right hand",[^4] and because of this, Darui will just as quickly verbally defend the Fourth as he would physically. Because of this trust, Darui was put in charge of the [First Division](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/First_Division "First Division") as well.[^8] Darui is also quite understanding. When [Naruto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto "Naruto") was late to the five Kage meeting, he quickly defended Naruto by saying that the life of a Kage as well as a father and husband is a busy one. Despite this, Darui still seems to retain his laid back attitude as he sat in a casual and laid back manner during the Kage meeting.

## Appearance

![Darui in Part II.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/ac/Darui%27s_full_body.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/150?cb=20160302072502)

Darui in Part II.

Darui is a fairly tall, dark-skinned man with a slightly bulbous nose, black eyes that usually look bored, and shaggy, platinum blonde (almost white) hair which covers his left eye. He wears a high-collared, sleeveless uniform with loose fitting pants, bandages on his wrists and the one-strap-over-one-shoulder [flak jacket](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flak_jacket "Flak jacket") of a Kumogakure shinobi.

Darui also has stylised characters for *water* (水) and *lightning* (雷) [tattooed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tattoo "wikipedia:Tattoo") on his right and left shoulders respectively, denoting his chakra's nature affinities, [Water Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water_Release "Water Release") and [Lightning Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_Release "Lightning Release"), as well as the fact that he has a kekkei genkai, the [Storm Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Storm_Release "Storm Release").[^9] The tattoo on his left arm also signifies that he has inherited the Third Raikage's [black lightning](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Black_lightning "Black lightning"). He carries a broad, foldable, [cleaver](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Cleaver_Sword "Cleaver Sword") - [like](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cleaver_\(knife\) "wikipedia:Cleaver (knife)") [sword](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sword "Sword"), strapped to his back, when outside the village.

Years after the Fourth Shinobi World War and becoming Raikage, Darui became noticeably taller and muscular. He also grew a small moustache, and gained creases at the corner of his mouth, which is similar to his predecessor. While his attire was mostly unchanged, he began wearing a hood in place of the high-collar he wore before.

## Abilities

![Darui simultaneously using two chakra natures.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/63/Raiton_Kangekiha.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150214212022)

Darui simultaneously using two chakra natures.

Having been trained by the [Third Raikage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Raikage "Third Raikage") himself, Darui is a highly skilled and capable shinobi: being chosen as one of the Fourth Raikage's bodyguards and his right-hand man, as well as be placed as leader of the [First Division](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/First_Division "First Division") of the [Allied Shinobi Forces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Allied_Shinobi_Forces "Allied Shinobi Forces") during the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"). His prowess ultimately allowed him to defeat the [Gold and Silver Brothers](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_and_Silver_Brothers "Gold and Silver Brothers"), who wielded the [Treasured Tools of the Sage of Six Paths](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Treasured_Tools_of_the_Sage_of_Six_Paths "Treasured Tools of the Sage of Six Paths"), nearly single-handedly.[^4] Years later, Darui gained a great increase in skill, which enabled him to become the Fifth Raikage,[^3] and could compete against [Ōtsutsuki Clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/%C5%8Ctsutsuki_Clan "Ōtsutsuki Clan") members like [Momoshiki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Momoshiki "Momoshiki").[^10]

### Physical and Chakra Prowess

Darui has shown impressive speed, being able to coordinate a combined assault on Taka alongside the [lightning-enhanced](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_Release_Chakra_Mode "Lightning Release Chakra Mode") Fourth Raikage, as well as protect C from a sneak attack by Suigetsu.[^11] By the time he had became Raikage, his taijutsu skills were compared to those of Naruto and [Sasuke](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke "Sasuke") by [Kinshiki Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kinshiki_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Kinshiki Ōtsutsuki").[^12] Darui also possesses considerable chakra reserves and stamina, as he did not die of exhaustion after using [three](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Benihisago "Benihisago") [of](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kohaku_no_J%C5%8Dhei "Kohaku no Jōhei") [the](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shichiseiken "Shichiseiken") [Treasured Tools of the Sage of Six Paths](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Treasured_Tools_of_the_Sage_of_Six_Paths "Treasured Tools of the Sage of Six Paths") and still had enough chakra to continue fighting effectively.[^13]

### Ninjutsu

#### Nature Transformation

![Darui using his Storm Release: Laser Circus.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/06/Ranton_Reiz%C4%81_S%C4%81kasu.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120705141609)

Darui using his Storm Release: Laser Circus.

Darui is proficient in [wind](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind "Wind"),[^1] [water](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water "Water") and [lightning](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning "Lightning") [nature transformations](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nature_transformation "Nature transformation"). He is able to effectively catch an enemy in a [water wall](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water_Release:_Water_Formation_Wall "Water Release: Water Formation Wall") and [channel lightning](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_Release:_Wave_of_Inspiration "Lightning Release: Wave of Inspiration") through it to electrocute them.[^14] Darui can also use Lightning Release to [enhance](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Flow "Chakra Flow") his weapons, giving it enough power to cut through a [metal blade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kumogakure_Swords "Kumogakure Swords").[^15] By combining his water and lightning-natures, he can utilise [Storm Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Storm_Release "Storm Release"), allowing him to fire [beams of light](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Storm_Release:_Laser_Circus "Storm Release: Laser Circus") that he can freely guide like missiles.

![Darui generating black lightning.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/04/Black_lightning.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150506014829)

Darui generating black lightning.

Darui can use black lightning — a special form of lightning passed down solely to him by his teacher, the Third Raikage,[^16] making his lightning techniques even more powerful.[^5] With the black lightning, he can use techniques such as the [Lightning Release: Black Panther](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_Release:_Black_Panther "Lightning Release: Black Panther") to immobilise or kill several enemies. In the anime, he can even increase the [power and effectiveness](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Double_Black_Panther "Double Black Panther") of the technique by using a [shadow clone](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_clone "Shadow clone").[^17]

#### Kenjutsu

Usually, Darui wields a [broad and fairly long sword](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Cleaver_Sword "Cleaver Sword") that is peculiar in comparison to others swords in that it folds in two to be sheathed and unfolds when in use. He has exceptional skills in [kenjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kenjutsu "Kenjutsu"), able to clash with Suigetsu's [Kubikiribōchō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kubikirib%C5%8Dch%C5%8D "Kubikiribōchō") and defeat him by pinning him to a wall with his lightning-imbued sword. He could also literally disarm [Ginkaku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ginkaku "Ginkaku") and [Kinkaku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kinkaku "Kinkaku") during their battle,[^18] and knock Ginkaku into Kinkaku's [Kōkinjō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/K%C5%8Dkinj%C5%8D "Kōkinjō") while quickly seizing the [Shichiseiken](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shichiseiken "Shichiseiken") and [Benihisago](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Benihisago "Benihisago").[^19] After becoming Raikage, his kenjutsu skills are capable of pressuring [Momoshiki Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Momoshiki_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Momoshiki Ōtsutsuki") and have him on the defensive.[^20]

## Part II

### Five Kage Summit

Main article: [Five Kage Summit](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Kage_Summit_\(Arc\) "Five Kage Summit (Arc)")

![The Kage's bodyguards on the offensive.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/d/da/Bodyguards_on_the_offensive.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150224122429)

The Kage's bodyguards on the offensive.

Darui and C were chosen to escort the Fourth Raikage to the [Kage Summit](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kage_Summit "Kage Summit") in the [Land of Iron](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Iron "Land of Iron"). Shortly after they had arrived and the meeting began, Darui and C had to rush to the Raikage's defence following his outburst and subsequent destruction of the table he was seated at, standing between the Raikage and the other Kage's bodyguards whom had interpreted this as an aggressive action towards their own respective leaders. Signalled to stand down, they each returned to their posts and things settled down, only to again come to the Raikage's side when [White Zetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/White_Zetsu "White Zetsu") appeared later during the meeting. White Zetsu revealed that Sasuke Uchiha, [Killer B](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Killer_B "Killer B") 's captor, was nearby, so Darui and C accompanied the Raikage to track Sasuke down. When the Raikage broke down a wall to leave, Darui emphatically apologised for both it and the table before departing.

![Darui clashing with Suigetsu.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/7/7c/Suigetsu_vs_darui.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150828152338)

Darui clashing with Suigetsu.

When they located Sasuke and his [team](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Taka "Taka"), [Suigetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Suigetsu "Suigetsu") immediately charged at C when the battle got underway. Darui however, intercepted his attack and the two men exchanged blows with their respective swords. Darui was ultimately victorious, as he imbued his sword with [lightning](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning "Lightning") -natured chakra and pinned Suigetsu to a wall. He then tended to C — who had been knocked unconscious by Sasuke's [genjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu:_Sharingan "Genjutsu: Sharingan") — and they both looked upon the Raikage as he grew increasingly enraged and reckless during his fight with Sasuke. The [Sunagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sunagakure "Sunagakure") [delegation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Three_Sand_Siblings "Three Sand Siblings") soon arrived to provide assistance, and Darui joined forces with them while C healed the Raikage, and bombarded Sasuke with a devastating attack.[^21] Using [Susanoo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Susanoo "Susanoo"), Sasuke survived their assault and destroyed the pillars that supported the building, causing the roof to collapse in on them; Darui was protected from the falling debris by the [Fifth Kazekage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fifth_Kazekage "Fifth Kazekage") 's [sand](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sand "Sand"). With Sasuke's escape and everyone unharmed, Darui and C returned to the Summit hall, where [Tobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobi "Tobi") explained his [Eye of the Moon Plan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eye_of_the_Moon_Plan "Eye of the Moon Plan") to them. As part of his explanation he revealed that B only pretended to be captured, leading Darui to state that this revelation did not surprise him at all.

After the formation of the [Allied Shinobi Forces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Allied_Shinobi_Forces "Allied Shinobi Forces"), Darui joined the Raikage in searching for B. They were drawn to the site of his battle with [Kisame Hoshigaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kisame_Hoshigaki "Kisame Hoshigaki") and arrived just in time for Darui to stop Kisame's attempt to cut off B's legs: breaking the [blade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kumogakure_Swords "Kumogakure Swords") with a lightning-infused [shuriken](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shuriken "Shuriken"). When the so-called ["Kisame"](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Substitute_Technique "Substitute Technique") was eliminated, they all returned to Kumogakure. Despite Darui's desire to rest, [Mabui](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mabui "Mabui") requested a full debriefing of what had happened since they left. Darui later attended a war council meeting convened by the Raikage.

### Fourth Shinobi World War: Confrontation

Main article: [Fourth Shinobi World War: Confrontation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War:_Confrontation "Fourth Shinobi World War: Confrontation") During the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"), Darui was placed in charge of the [First Division](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/First_Division "First Division") of the [Allied Shinobi Forces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Allied_Shinobi_Forces "Allied Shinobi Forces"). He and his platoon mobilised alongside the others but soon break off and headed for the coast of the [Land of Lightning](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Lightning "Land of Lightning") to defend against an approaching portion of the [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki") forces. Before the opposing army made landfall, Darui launched the first attack, using his [Lightning Release: Black Panther](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_Release:_Black_Panther "Lightning Release: Black Panther") technique to electrocute dozens of [White Zetsu Army](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/White_Zetsu_Army "White Zetsu Army") clones as they marched through the sea towards land. Darui confronted the [reincarnated](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Impure_World_Reincarnation "Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation") [Gold and Silver Brothers](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_and_Silver_Brothers "Gold and Silver Brothers") where he apologised to them for needing to defeat them and tarnish their reputations even further.

![Darui severs Kinkaku's arm.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/e/ea/Kinkaku%27s_Arm_Severed.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120705133838)

Darui severs Kinkaku's arm.

As the battle ensued, he is joined by [Samui](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Samui "Samui") and [Atsui](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Atsui "Atsui") as the two brothers revealed their weapons of choice: the [Treasured Tools of the Sage of Six Paths](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Treasured_Tools_of_the_Sage_of_Six_Paths "Treasured Tools of the Sage of Six Paths"). During the battle, few White Zetsu clones tried to ambush them, but they are easily defeated by Atsui's [attack](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Cloud-Style_Flame_Beheading "Cloud-Style Flame Beheading"). Darui tried to stop Atsui before he rushed off to face [Kinkaku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kinkaku "Kinkaku") and [Ginkaku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ginkaku "Ginkaku") and looked on as both Atsui and Samui were hit with the [Kōkinjō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/K%C5%8Dkinj%C5%8D "Kōkinjō") and had their ["word souls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kotodama "wikipedia:Kotodama") " (言霊, *kotodama*) severed later horrified when Atsui was sucked into the [Benihisago](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Benihisago "Benihisago") and Samui is taken hostage. Apologising to Samui for what he was about to do, he severed Kinkaku's arm and used his [Storm Release: Laser Circus](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Storm_Release:_Laser_Circus "Storm Release: Laser Circus") attack hitting both Kinkaku and Ginkaku — who are shocked to see that the missiles from the attack were guided. This assault ended somewhat in defeat as Samui is absorbed into the Benihisago and Darui was hit with the Kōkinjō and his word soul severed with the [Shichiseiken](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shichiseiken "Shichiseiken").

![Darui surprises Ginkaku after escaping the gourd.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/b0/Darui_Freed_from_Benihisago.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120705133836)

Darui surprises Ginkaku after escaping the gourd.

Having gained a good idea of how the tools worked by watching what happened to Atsui and Samui, Darui knew he could avoid being caught in the Benihisago by not saying his most-said word: "dull" (だるい, *darui*). However, he inadvertently uttered the word when he admonished the brothers and stated, "Ore wa Bosu no migiude **da. Rui** jihin janē! (「オレはボスの右腕だ。類似品じゃねェ!」, *I'm the Boss' right arm. I'm no imitation!*)". As he is sucked in, he started apologising to every-one saying "sorry" (すみません, *sumimasen*) three times, making that his most-said word and thus stopping the suction. After his escape, Darui launched an attack immediately with his sword and was able to knock Ginkaku into the Kōkinjō and commandeered both the Shichiseiken and the Benihisago. After looking at his changed most-said word, he realised that he was very apologetic by nature and stated that he'd accept that. He then launched a new tactic and distracted Kinkaku by throwing his [sword](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Cleaver_Sword "Cleaver Sword") at him and then followed up with his Laser Circus technique again which buffeted Kinkaku, giving him a chance to sever Ginkaku's word soul with the Shichiseiken.

![Darui sealing Ginkaku in the Benihisago.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/37/Ginkaku_sucked.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20210819022045)

Darui sealing Ginkaku in the Benihisago.

Ginkaku then said his most used word, "Kinkaku" (キンカク) in pleading for his brother to help him, and got sealed inside the Benihisago. As he began to suffer from the massive chakra drain of the Treasured Tools, Darui witnessed Kinkaku turn into his [six-tailed, Version 2 form](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Version_2 "Version 2") and rampaged on the battlefield. To stop Kinkaku, headquarters [sent](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Heavenly_Transfer_Technique "Heavenly Transfer Technique") Darui the [Kohaku no Jōhei](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kohaku_no_J%C5%8Dhei "Kohaku no Jōhei") and [Team 10](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Asuma "Team Asuma") to provide support for him. While [Shikamaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikamaru "Shikamaru") and [Chōji](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%8Dji "Chōji") distracted Kinkaku, [Ino](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ino "Ino") used her [Mind Body Switch Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_Body_Switch_Technique "Mind Body Switch Technique") to take possession of Kinkaku's body.

![Darui sealing Kinkaku in the Kohaku no Jōhei.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/03/Darui_sealing_Kinkaku.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160121000911)

Darui sealing Kinkaku in the Kohaku no Jōhei.

Darui then called out to Kinkaku and Ino forced Kinkaku to respond, activating the Kohaku no Jōhei and sucking Kinkaku in. As Kinkaku was sealed he wondered how he, and his sibling could've been defeated by " *copper* " pieces, and Darui responded stating that when you have a bunch of copper coins, together they can be worth just as much as a gold coin. With the [Kōkinjō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/K%C5%8Dkinj%C5%8D "Kōkinjō") getting sealed along with Kinkaku, Darui had no way to use the Benihisago but nevertheless hung onto it, hoping to find a way to free Samui and Atsui. In the meantime, he helped subdue the reincarnated [Kakuzu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakuzu "Kakuzu") and by nightfall, Kakuzu and the rest of Akatsuki's forces had been dealt with. Before they could claim victory however, Tobi appeared on the battlefield with the [Demonic Statue of the Outer Path](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Statue_of_the_Outer_Path "Demonic Statue of the Outer Path"). While the statue wreaked havoc, Tobi confronted Darui and tried to take the Benihisago and the Kohaku no Jōhei. Fearing Tobi's intentions, Darui and Shikamaru tried to stop him, only to be attacked by the statue. They are saved by Chōji and [Kitsuchi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kitsuchi "Kitsuchi") respectively but this allowed Tobi to escape with the tools. With this, they start retreating as night began to fall.

The next morning, Darui and the other shinobi were made to stand apart inside circles until [Naruto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto "Naruto") could arrive on the battlefield and weed out the [impostor](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Substitute_Technique "Substitute Technique") shinobi which had taken the form of their comrades during the night and was wreaking havoc and confusion on the allied shinobi. Darui later expressed his disbelief that the Raikage had actually agreed to let the [jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki") out of confinement upon hearing that Naruto was headed to the battlefield.

### Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax

Main article: [Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War:_Climax "Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax")

![Darui and the Allied Shinobi Force confront the Ten-Tails.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/a8/Allied_Forces_jutsu.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150914121326)

Darui and the Allied Shinobi Force confront the Ten-Tails.

Upon arriving on the battlefield where they were, Darui announced that the remainder of his division had arrived. With this, he and the other members of the [Allied Shinobi Forces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Allied_Shinobi_Forces "Allied Shinobi Forces") stood to face their opposition. Following the strategy relayed to them by [Shikaku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikaku "Shikaku"), Darui and C, along with several other Kumo-nin, begin the assault. With C and others amongst their ranks blinding the enemy with the [Lightning Release: Lightning Illusion Flash of Lightning Pillar](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_Release:_Lightning_Illusion_Flash_of_Lightning_Pillar "Lightning Release: Lightning Illusion Flash of Lightning Pillar") technique, Darui and several other Storm Release bearers, used the [Storm Release: Laser Circus](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Storm_Release:_Laser_Circus "Storm Release: Laser Circus") technique to aim for the beast's eye. Backed up by the [medic-nin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Medic-nin "Medic-nin") Sakura, Darui attempted to attack Obito Uchiha with a Storm Release attack but is quickly repulsed by the now fully-matured Ten-Tails who had escaped the hole which it had seemingly been trapped in.

## Creation and Conception

With Darui's design, [Kishimoto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kishimoto "Kishimoto") noted that he wanted to immediately convey a sense of sluggishness. His collar is worn upturned for a sense of stylishness, and the two tattoos on his shoulders are there as challenges to his opponents, as well as to signify that he possesses a kekkei genkai.

## Trivia

- "Darui" (怠い) is Japanese for "sluggish" or "dull", referring to his lazy, languid way of speaking.
- A running gag with Darui is that every time A destroys a wall when leaving a room, Darui apologises for A and tries to use the door while C thinks they should just use the hole in the wall as well.
- Darui is the only person to become Raikage who isn't named "A".
	- However, he is referred to as "A" in *[Naruto x Boruto: Ultimate Ninja Storm Connections](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_x_Boruto:_Ultimate_Ninja_Storm_Connections "Naruto x Boruto: Ultimate Ninja Storm Connections")* in his New Era costume, suggesting it is a name taken on. It is unclear if he is referred to as Boss as other successive Raikage have been.
- While Darui finds most things to be "dull", he is generally enthused by his unique hairstyle.[^1]
- According to his *[Naruto Mobile](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Mobile "Naruto Mobile")* profile, Darui had reached the acme of perfection with Lightning Release and kenjutsu, as well as being the only inheritor of Black Lightning.[^23]
