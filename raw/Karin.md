**Karin** (香燐) is an [Otogakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Otogakure "Otogakure") [shinobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinobi "Shinobi"), a former kunoichi of [Kusagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kusagakure "Kusagakure"), and a member of the [Uzumaki clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uzumaki_clan "Uzumaki clan").[^3] [^4] She assisted [Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru") in his experiments, and was left in charge as warden of his [Southern Hideout](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Southern_Hideout "Southern Hideout") while he was away. She was later recruited into [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha") 's group [Taka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Taka "Taka"), which was initially called "Hebi" at the time of its creation.

## Background

In the anime, Karin and her [mother](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Karin%27s_Mother "Karin's Mother") were allowed to take refuge in Kusagakure, on the condition that her mother [healed](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Heal_Bite "Heal Bite") the village's wounded under watch by [Zōsui](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Z%C5%8Dsui "Zōsui"). After the village suffered many casualties following a surprise attack, her mother upheld her duties and healed the wounded at the Kusagakure hospital, where she died after transferring too much chakra to the wounded. Immediately afterwards, the duty was passed onto Karin, much to her disapproval.[^5]

![Karin as a Kusagakure genin.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/ab/Genin_Karin.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150927064008)

Karin as a Kusagakure genin.

Some time after becoming a genin, Karin took part in the [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams") held in [Konoha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha "Konoha") on a [team](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Shigeri "Team Shigeri"). During the second phase, Karin lost track of her teammates and was attacked by a large [bear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/bear "wikipedia:bear"). She was saved by [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha"), who was disappointed to find she had an earth scroll as well, but she was able to catch a good glimpse of him, as he smiled at her.[^6] Her team later failed as they didn't make it to the tower with both scrolls by the fifth day.

Soon after during a war, Karin [sensed](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mind%27s_Eye_of_the_Kagura "Mind's Eye of the Kagura") a huge group of people approaching the village she was staying in and hid while they attacked. After the village was burned down and everyone was killed, she was harassed by two men in another town who, noticed her Uzumaki lineage, thought to capture her and sell her to the underground market, but she was saved by Orochimaru, who, noticing this, offered her protection, which she accepted.

Later, Orochimaru asked her how she survived her village being burned, to which she replied that she could tell how many large groups of people were approaching her. Orochimaru lead him to bring her back to [Otogakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Otogakure "Otogakure").[^1] During her time with Orochimaru, Karin's healing abilities were studied by Orochimaru and [Kabuto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kabuto "Kabuto"), with the latter successfully being able to replicate it.[^3] After being involved in experiments with [Jūgo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%ABgo "Jūgo") and [Kimimaro](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kimimaro "Kimimaro"), Orochimaru made her in charge of the Southern Hideout.[^7]

In the anime, when Karin saw Sasuke for the first time since joining Orochimaru, she realised he was the boy that rescued her during the Chūnin Exams.[^5] When the two formally met, Karin assisted Sasuke with recapturing one hundred and eighteen test subjects from one of Orochimaru's hideout, which they succeeded in doing.[^8] Sometime later, she was sent to the [Eastern Hideout](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eastern_Hideout "Eastern Hideout") to deliver a vial to help further research on [Suigetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Suigetsu "Suigetsu"). Soon after Sasuke completed his task, Suigetsu escaped his chamber, leading to Karin taking action to hunt him down and joined Sasuke in an effort to recapture him. When they found Suigetsu, she attempted to persuade him into coming back to the hideout, which led to him attacking her, and she repelled him with her [chakra chain](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tight_Binding_Golden_Chain_Technique "Tight Binding Golden Chain Technique"). After knocking him out, they brought him back to the hideout and parted ways.[^9]

## Personality

![Suigetsu being attacked by an enraged Karin.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/e/e4/Karin_attacks_Suigetsu.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130919141654)

Suigetsu being attacked by an enraged Karin.

Karin has a fluctuating personality; she will appear tough and in control at times, to the point of being violent and critical, and at other times will appear highly flirtatious. She possesses a strong dislike towards [Suigetsu Hōzuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Suigetsu_H%C5%8Dzuki "Suigetsu Hōzuki"), whom she attacks whenever he decides to mock or berate her. In the anime, she stated that she was attracted to men based on their chakra quality, and claims that Suigetsu's chakra disgusts her. Sasuke Uchiha's chakra used to dazzle her, but it now scares her, due to it getting colder and darker from his hatred. [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki") 's seems to calm and dazzle her as well, making her feel comfortable and relaxed due to it being warm and bright, though she also became scared after sensing [an evil chakra within him](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tailed_Demon_Fox "Nine-Tailed Demon Fox").[^10]

![Karin forgives Sasuke almost immediately for stabbing her.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/f0/Karin_forgives_Sasuke.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140731123627)

Karin forgives Sasuke almost immediately for stabbing her.

Karin develops a fondness for Sasuke from the first day they met; when he saved her life during the Chūnin Exams. In the anime, she has a flashback of when Orochimaru sends her to assist Sasuke in capturing a horde of escaped prisoners. There, she is captivated by the level of Sasuke's chakra and develops a sexual attraction to him. Karin's strong fondness bordered on fanaticism. For example, it was revealed that she had kept one of Sasuke's sweat-drenched shirts. Karin would typically insist that she has no feelings for Sasuke around others, while frequently suggesting to Sasuke that they should spend time alone with the intention of freely expressing her feelings to him, only to switch back to acting cold when other people are present. Despite Sasuke's chakra becoming increasingly "darker", she still found him attractive. However, after he stabbed her in order to kill [Danzō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Danz%C5%8D "Danzō") and tried to kill her, then even ordering Sakura to kill her to test Sakura's intent to join him, Karin stated that she no longer cares about him. Karin has also used others' perception of her feelings towards Sasuke to her advantage, feigning mental instability while imprisoned at Konoha to make the guards pay less attention to her. Upon her escape, she follows Sasuke's chakra with the intent of aggressively confronting him about the stab as she vents her rage by furiously beating Suigetsu, but with Sasuke's apology and Orochimaru's suggestion, she found herself unable to stay angry. She even puts Sasuke's well-being above her own. Despite not being a combatant of Taka, she rushed into the battlefield with virtually no weapons or protective gear when she senses that Sasuke was on the verge of death, paying no heed to how strong the enemy was.

![Karin dreams of Sasuke kindly smiling at her for once.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/44/To_See_That_Smile.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150924131845)

Karin dreams of Sasuke kindly smiling at her for once.

In the anime, as seen in her [Infinite Tsukuyomi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Infinite_Tsukuyomi "Infinite Tsukuyomi") dream, her greatest desire is simply to see Sasuke smile kindly to her. Years later, despite still having feelings for Sasuke, Karin is content with the knowledge that he is happy with his new family. Karin also seems to have a soft spot for Sasuke and Sakura's [daughter](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sarada_Uchiha "Sarada Uchiha"), whom she helped deliver when she was born, and was the one who gave their daughter a pair of glasses. Her temper has also lessened and she has more amicable interactions with [Suigetsu Hōzuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Suigetsu_H%C5%8Dzuki "Suigetsu Hōzuki").

## Appearance

![Karin in Part II.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/a4/Karin_full.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/130?cb=20140805024328)

Karin in Part II.

Karin has crimson eyes, fair skin, and the characteristic red hair of the [Uzumaki clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uzumaki_clan "Uzumaki clan") which she wears in an unusual hairstyle: her hair is short and spiky on the right side, while longer and straight on the left side. She wears brown narrow glasses, which have a serrated blade hidden in them,[^11] and her outfit consists of a lavender uniform that exposes her navel, short black shorts, and long black thigh high stockings with black sandals. Her chest, neck and arms are covered with bite marks that result from people biting her to [heal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Heal_Bite "Heal Bite"), and all of which are hidden by her uniform.

As she is allied with [Hebi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hebi "Hebi"), Karin begins to wear a pouch around her midsection, and later a black cloak. When allied with [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki"), Karin wears an Akatsuki cloak with a hood instead of a high collar. After the fight with [Killer B](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Killer_B "Killer B"), Karin briefly put her hair in a ponytail. She is seen wearing a black dress and black-rimmed glasses when it's decided that Taka will go to Konoha. Years after the Fourth Great Shinobi War, Karin began wearing a long sleeved white shirt with a purple tie and black cuffs on her wrists, black boots, and black shorts, and has also buzzed the left side of her head. Her body also apparently healed from all her bite marks.

When Karin was still living in Kusagakure, she wore a mesh shirt and stockings, a purple skirt, a light brown short sleeved jacket with an orange stripe going across the chest, and black sandals. She also had the Kusagakure forehead protector on her forehead.

As a child, Karin had short hair that reached her chin and wore a dark purple short sleeved shirt that had a brown collar, light brown pants, and brown sandals.

## Abilities

![Karin manifests chakra chains.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/cc/Adamantine_Attacking_Chains_2.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150528122847)

Karin manifests chakra chains.

Karin has been praised for her abilities by both [Obito Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha "Obito Uchiha") and Orochimaru, the latter deciding to take her with him for that reason. Despite being mostly a non-combatant, Orochimaru referred to Karin as one of his good experiments,[^4] and had enough faith in her skills to put her in charge of the Southern Hideout's prison, since she made escaping impossible. Karin later demonstrated these skills most predominantly during the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"), where she destroyed much of Tobi's [giant wooden statue](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kanzeon_Lotus_King "Kanzeon Lotus King").

### Sensory Perception

![Karin's "sense" of chakra.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/1/12/Karin%27s_%22Sense%22_Of_Chakra.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20151129231932)

Karin's " sense " of chakra.

Karin possesses a unique ability called the [Mind's Eye of the Kagura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mind%27s_Eye_of_the_Kagura "Mind's Eye of the Kagura"), which allows her to sense any targets' [chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra "Chakra") signature. Sasuke states that her sensory abilities are unlike any other.[^12] Unlike most sensors, who normally need to mould chakra beforehand, Karin appears able to detect chakra targets without any conscious effort.[^13] Using this ability, Karin can detect the presence of others, easily discerning a specific individual, even its species, from a great distance, letting her easily track the target.[^14] [^15] She can determine the size of a target's chakra reserves and its potency, and even tell when someone was lying because their dishonest emotions cause their chakra to fluctuate.[^16] Karin can also tell if one is trapped in genjutsu, including herself.[^17] Similarly, she can [completely conceal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Suppression_Technique "Chakra Suppression Technique") her own chakra from others.[^18] However, she cannot use her chakra for other feats while doing so, thereby disabling her own abilities.

### Life Force and Chakra Prowess

![Karin healing Sasuke.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/0b/Heal_Bite.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150304192939)

Karin healing Sasuke.

As a direct descendant of the [Uzumaki clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uzumaki_clan "Uzumaki clan"),[^3] [^4] Karin has a remarkably powerful life-force. This gives her an immense longevity, and tremendous reserves of chakra and vitality. Karin has also demonstrated a unique and powerful healing capability that allows her to mend wounds and regenerate quickly by [biting](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Heal_Bite "Heal Bite") herself and consuming some of her chakra allows her to heal instantaneous.[^19] Likewise, she can heal others by letting them bite her.[^20] The potency of Karin's healing chakra was enough to restore [Tsunade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsunade "Tsunade") 's [chakra reserves](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Strength_of_a_Hundred_Seal "Strength of a Hundred Seal"),[^21] as well as heal herself from Tobi's otherwise fatal [Wood Release: Cutting Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release:_Cutting_Technique "Wood Release: Cutting Technique"). However, it is dangerous for her to use that ability more than once a day,[^22] and as a consequence, she is left with presumably permanent bite marks.

### Ninjutsu

Stemming from her Uzumaki chakra, Karin is able to materialise an [incomplete version](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tight_Binding_Golden_Chain_Technique "Tight Binding Golden Chain Technique") of the [Adamantine Sealing Chains](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Adamantine_Sealing_Chains "Adamantine Sealing Chains") in combat. During the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"), she was able to use the chains to combat and destroy [Tobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobi_\(Zetsu\) "Tobi (Zetsu)") 's giant wooden statue and its [numerous arms](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Top_Transformed_Buddha "Top Transformed Buddha").[^23] Karin is also proficient in the use of [Earth](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth "Earth"), [Water](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water "Water"), [Yin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yin "Yin") and [Yang Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yang_Release "Yang Release").[^2] In the anime, she was also seen using the [Mystical Palm Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mystical_Palm_Technique "Mystical Palm Technique"), although it is unclear how skilled she is in its usage compared to a [medical-nin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Medical-nin "Medical-nin").[^24]

### Intelligence

Karin is quite intelligent. With her extensive knowledge at her disposal, she can come up with brilliant strategies that contribute to her missions.[^1] While being tracked by the Konoha shinobi, she was able to deduce they were accomplishing this feat by locking on to and following Sasuke's scent. From this, she quickly came up with a countermeasure to throw them off Hebi's tracks by tearing up Sasuke's sweat-soaked shirt and scattering them about in multiple directions attached to birds.[^25] She is analytical, capable of quickly deducing the basics of a complex technique such as [Izanagi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Izanagi "Izanagi"). She is also resourceful, as seen during her imprisonment, hiding a blade in the frame of her glasses as well as a lock-pick set within a portrait that she seemingly carries around with her, all the while putting up an act of fluctuating feelings for Sasuke in order to lessen the guards' sense of security around her. Furthermore, to ensure she kept the portrait, she feigned even more mental instability and caused less ruckus with the portrait, causing to guards to allow her to keep it.[^11]

### Other Skills

Although she usually takes a supporting role for her allies as the team tracker and unorthodox healer, Karin seems to have some competence in taijutsu, as shown when she tried to attack Danzō to save Sasuke, and also when losing her temper from Suigetsu's taunting.[^26] Furthermore, she was seen knocking out one of the prisoners in the Southern Hideout once.[^27] She has proven to be very nimble and evasive from the countless attacks from Tobi's giant wooden statue.

![Hebi begins searching for Itachi Uchiha.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/fd/Team_Hawk.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20210806023438)

Hebi begins searching for Itachi Uchiha.

Sasuke recruited Karin as a member of [Hebi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hebi "Hebi"). While Sasuke admitted that she might be troublesome sometimes, he also noted that her special abilities made her useful. While Sasuke tried to recruit Karin, she tried to seduce him, though without success. Once the team was completed, Sasuke sent Hebi out in multiple directions to try and locate [Itachi Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Itachi_Uchiha "Itachi Uchiha"). While on the search, Karin passed [Sakura Haruno](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sakura_Haruno "Sakura Haruno"), although the two did not acknowledge each other. After [Deidara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Deidara "Deidara") 's suicide explosion, the rest of the team regrouped and took Sasuke to an inn to recover. When Karin went out for supplies, she sensed the [Eight Man Squad](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight_Man_Squad "Eight Man Squad") approaching and warned the team. It was her plan to use Sasuke's old clothing as a means to distract the squad from catching them.

### Fated Battle Between Brothers

Main article: [Fated Battle Between Brothers](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fated_Battle_Between_Brothers "Fated Battle Between Brothers") When they finally got back on Itachi's trail, Karin stayed behind with Suigetsu, Jūgo, and [Kisame](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kisame "Kisame") as Sasuke went ahead to battle Itachi.

### Pain's Assault

Main article: [Pain's Assault](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Pain%27s_Assault_\(Arc\) "Pain's Assault (Arc)")

![Taka searching for Killer B.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/48/Team_Taka_Akatsuki_attire.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150224122610)

Taka searching for Killer B.

Hebi, now renamed [Taka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Taka "Taka"), later began working with [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki"). They were sent to [Kumogakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kumogakure "Kumogakure") to capture the [Eight-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight-Tails "Eight-Tails") ' [jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki"). When they found him, Karin stood on the sidelines and watched the others fight [Killer B](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Killer_B "Killer B"). When Sasuke was mortally wounded fighting Killer B, Karin immediately rushed to pull the dying Sasuke out of the fight, as Suigetsu and Jūgo kept Killer B busy. As she pulled the swords out of Sasuke's body she exposed her right arm, riddled with numerous bite marks. She then told Sasuke to bite her arm, and as he did, her [Heal Bite](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Heal_Bite "Heal Bite") healed his wounds.

As Killer B got bored and attempted to leave, Karin used her [Mind's Eye of the Kagura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mind%27s_Eye_of_the_Kagura "Mind's Eye of the Kagura") to locate him. Killer B then used his demonic chakra to charge at her, Jūgo, and Suigetsu, but Jūgo rocketed them away to safety. After Sasuke was yet again critically wounded by Killer B, Karin insisted on reviving Sasuke again. She was stopped by Jūgo, who claimed she didn't have enough chakra to save Sasuke again, so Karin watches as Jūgo transfers his flesh to Sasuke to heal him. When Killer B fully transformed into the Eight-Tails, Sasuke used [Amaterasu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Amaterasu "Amaterasu") to quell it. Karin quickly moved all of her teammates out of the way of one of Killer B's tentacles, but this resulted in her being hit by one, and some of the black flames spread to Karin's cloak. Sasuke used his [Mangekyō Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mangeky%C5%8D_Sharingan "Mangekyō Sharingan") to put them out. Karin was carried to safety by Jūgo. While the team was recovering, Karin briefly put her hair in a ponytail. She noticed that something was wrong with Sasuke, but as he brushed it off, she detected [an enemy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J "J") outside their base, which Sasuke and Jūgo went to investigate.

### Five Kage Summit

Main article: [Five Kage Summit](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Kage_Summit_\(Arc\) "Five Kage Summit (Arc)") After Taka recovered, they moved out for Konoha. Along the way they are stopped by [Tobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobi "Tobi"), who convinced Sasuke to go after [Danzō Shimura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Danz%C5%8D_Shimura "Danzō Shimura") at the [Kage Summit](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kage_Summit "Kage Summit"). When they arrived in the [Land of Iron](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Iron "Land of Iron"), they decided to wait until the meeting was over before they attack. [Zetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Zetsu "Zetsu") soon revealed their presence and the Kage mobilise against Taka. When the battle got under way, Karin noticed that Sasuke's chakra was now darker than when in his [cursed seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Cursed_seal "Cursed seal") level two state.

![Karin locates Danzō.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/52/Karin%27s_Mind_Eye_of_Kagura.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150914215553)

Karin locates Danzō.

When the [Fourth Raikage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Raikage "Fourth Raikage") arrived, he instructs [C](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/C "C") to locate and eliminate Karin, Taka's sensory ninja. Karin [hid her chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Suppression_Technique "Chakra Suppression Technique") to avoid detection, and once C was defeated by Sasuke she again began to look for Danzō's location. By the time Sasuke is able to elude the Raikage, Karin had found Danzō. She informs Sasuke that Jūgo and Suigetsu are still alive, but he said to leave them and had her lead him to Danzō. Danzō fled as soon as they arrived, and Sasuke was forced to fight the [Fifth Mizukage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fifth_Mizukage "Fifth Mizukage") and the [Third Tsuchikage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Tsuchikage "Third Tsuchikage") before he could follow. During the brief confrontation Karin grew concerned with Sasuke's deteriorating chakra levels. When his chakra signature disappeared, she broke into tears, believing him to be dead, with the Tsuchikage turning his sight on her.

Tobi soon appeared with Sasuke in tow before he sent them both to a [different realm](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kamui%27s_Dimension "Kamui's Dimension") so she could heal his wounds. He later returned and released them in front of Danzō so that Sasuke could fight him. Karin observed the battle and tried to determine how Danzō was able to survive a series of apparently fatal injuries. She immediately ruled out genjutsu or a clone technique, and eventually noticed that the [Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sharingan "Sharingan") embedded in Danzō's arm closed one by one as the battle progressed. Coupling this observation with frequent descents in Danzō's chakra levels, she learned the secret of his [technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Izanagi "Izanagi"). She told Sasuke about it and advised that he keep his distance until Izanagi expired.

![Sasuke stabs Karin in order to mortally wound Danzō.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/7/7c/Sasuke_hits_Danzo_and_Karin.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230408204036)

Sasuke stabs Karin in order to mortally wound Danzō.

Once Sasuke mortally wounded Danzō, Karin healed his injuries. Danzō then took Karin hostage as he refused to die. Karin called to Sasuke for help, but Sasuke [stabbed](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chidori_Sharp_Spear "Chidori Sharp Spear") her through the chest in order to pierce Danzō's heart. Sasuke simply commented that, if she can be taken hostage so easily, she was now only a burden to him. Karin collapsed and Tobi suggested that Sasuke finish her off since she had some knowledge of their plans. As Sasuke walked towards Karin, she has a flashback of her first encounter with him at the Chūnin Exams and remembered his smile. She took one last look at Sasuke, wishing to see his smile one more time, before he was about to kill her, but he was interrupted by the arrival of Sakura.

Sakura insisted that she wanted to be with Sasuke no matter what, leading Karin to realise that Sakura too loved Sasuke. Suspicious of her actual intentions, Sasuke ordered Sakura to prove her loyalty by killing Karin. While Sakura was thinking about how to attack Sasuke, Sasuke prepared to kill Sakura, and Karin managed to mutter a warning, just before [Kakashi Hatake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake "Kakashi Hatake") arrived to counter the attack. After it became clear that not even his words can alter Sasuke's motives, he ordered Sakura to take Karin away from the area and heal her enough to talk. While still barely conscious, Karin looked up at a sobbing Sakura and pleaded for her to hold in her tears while Sakura saved Karin's life. A tear fell down Karin's face as she sensed a further change in Sasuke's chakra, stating that Sasuke is no longer the person that Sakura once knew. As she asked Sakura what she would do, Sakura didn't respond and, emotionally unstable, returned to the fight despite Karin's warnings.

Later, when Sasuke prepared to fight with Naruto, Karin, [sensing](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mind%27s_Eye_of_the_Kagura "Mind's Eye of the Kagura") that Sasuke is going to overexert himself again, dwelled on the fact that she was only able to heal him a little from his earlier skirmishes, before catching herself in that thought; in light of the fact of everything that Sasuke had done to her, Karin decided to herself that she was now done with Sasuke. After Sasuke left, Kakashi told her that she was coming with them to Konoha; Karin resigned to her fate as she had no choice, being warned by Kakashi not to try anything. As Karin left with them, she made a special note of Naruto's character, apparently hearing of him before.

After escaping from the jail, Karin noticed Sasuke's chakra in the village and couldn't believe that it would be him. She followed the signal just as Taka, Orochimaru and the Hokage prepared to leave for the battlefield, attacking a fleeing Suigetsu over what had happened to her. Sasuke's apology for attacking her was met with a mix of anger and giddy appreciation as she noticed Orochimaru among the group. In the end, she found herself unable to stay angry at Sasuke and forgave him.

### Jūgo Arc

In the anime, when Jūgo abruptly left the base to deal with an outbreak of cursed seals infecting wildlife, Orochimaru tasked Suigetsu and Karin with retrieving him. They ultimately encountered [Sumire Kakei](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sumire_Kakei "Sumire Kakei"), whose [Nue](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nue "Nue") attacked them abruptly. After Suigetsu overexerted himself to knock her and her summoning out, they were approached by [Team 7](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_7_\(Konohamaru\) "Team 7 (Konohamaru)"), who revealed that Jūgo had been apprehended by the [Land of Rivers](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Rivers "Land of Rivers") ' researchers. While Karin treated Sumire, she voiced her joy at seeing Sarada again, saying that she is dear to her despite them not being related. When Sumire regained her consciousness, she explained that [two](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Momo "Momo") [assailants](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasami "Sasami") who captured [Wasabi Izuno](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wasabi_Izuno "Wasabi Izuno") and [Namida Suzumeno](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Namida_Suzumeno "Namida Suzumeno") had [special collars](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Cursed_Seal_Device "Cursed Seal Device") that let them harness cursed seals of their own. Realising that this duo was working with the Land of Rivers' researchers, they concluded the outbreak must have been started by them. While Suigetsu insisted on just assaulting the enemies and taking Jūgo back by force, the others insisted that they had to be more cautious due to the innocent bystanders, as negative outcomes due to Suigetsu's and Karin's involvement could endanger Orochimaru's already dubious truce with the world.

![Karin immobilises Sasami.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/67/Karin_immobilizes_Sasami.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20210922135023)

While initially working, the enemies quickly saw through the ruse and split up, with Sasami going further to face Karin and Sarada while Momo returned to the base. Once transformed, Sasami quickly began making sport of her opponents, treating them as prey. Karin's sensory prowess let her follow Sasami's movements enough to defend. However, when Sasami managed to land a powerful blow on Sarada, Karin immobilised Sasami with her [chakra chains](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tight_Binding_Golden_Chain_Technique "Tight Binding Golden Chain Technique") long enough for Sarada to [take](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Heal_Bite "Heal Bite") some of Karin's chakra. This let Sarada deliver a [devastating punch](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Enhanced_Strength "Chakra Enhanced Strength") that knocked Sasami out and destroyed her cursed seal collar. Karin complimented Sarada's skills, comparing her to her parents. While on the way back to the village, they changed course when they realised the wild geese were about to start migrating, spreading the cursed seal infection.

![Karin, Jūgo, and Suigetsu capture Tosaka.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/7/73/Taka_captures_Tosaka.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230422050129)

Karin, Jūgo, and Suigetsu capture Tosaka.

Karin and Sarada struggled to keep the infected geese from migrating, requesting Team 15's help when they arrived by the lake. While Jūgo defeated [Tosaka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tosaka "Tosaka") and later fought Nue and Boruto, Karin considered the possibility of having to kill the birds to prevent the cursed seal from spreading. She didn't think Sarada could do it, but she took the responsibility onto herself, as she was part of the team assigned to the mission. After Boruto managed to cause Jūgo's transformation to recede, Mitsuki arrived with Konohamaru and Suigetsu, having acquired a serum to remove the infection. Suigetsu consumed it and [merged](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hydrification_Technique "Hydrification Technique") with the lake to rain it over the birds. Karin left with Jūgo and Suigetsu, having also taken a powerless Tosaka as a prisoner, and was teased by Suigetsu over her concern for Sarada.

- In the [fourth databook](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jin_no_Sho "Jin no Sho"), [Hiden](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiden "Hiden") is not listed among Karin's special characteristics, despite her usage of [Mind's Eye of the Kagura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mind%27s_Eye_of_the_Kagura "Mind's Eye of the Kagura").
- The [third databook](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sha_no_Sho "Sha no Sho") reveals that when Karin met Orochimaru, it was after her village was attacked and burned down, while the anime portrays their meeting as her being rescued by him after two men tried to abduct her, due to her Uzumaki heritage.[^9]
- According to her [Naruto Mobile](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Mobile "Naruto Mobile") profile, Karin possessed powerful chakra.[^33]
