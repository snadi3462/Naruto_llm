**Karui Akimichi** (秋道カルイ, *Akimichi Karui*) is a [kunoichi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kunoichi "wikipedia:Kunoichi") from [Kumogakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kumogakure "Kumogakure"), and a member of [Team Samui](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Samui "Team Samui"). She would later marry [Chōji Akimichi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%8Dji_Akimichi "Chōji Akimichi") and immigrate to [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure"), thus becoming a member of the [Akimichi clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akimichi_clan "Akimichi clan").

## Personality

Karui is quite self-assured, outspoken, stubborn, combative, and impulsive, allowing her emotions to come out. This was shown when she angrily yelled at [Team Kakashi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Kakashi "Team Kakashi") for allowing the renegade [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha") to run loose and cause chaos in Kumo. She came to a violent point when she took out her rage on [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki") and beat him without hesitation,[^4] though, according to her partner [Omoi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Omoi "Omoi"), she felt remorse for doing so.

Karui has demonstrated great teamwork skills, able to coordinate attacks with Omoi without either needing to communicate.[^5] Nevertheless, she has a somewhat comedically antagonistic relationship with Omoi. During their début, they argued over the possible reasons that the Raikage could have called them. She assumed that he wanted them to get him more protein shakes. However, the argument started with Omoi, implying that she is flat-chested in comparison to their other teammate, [Samui](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Samui "Samui"). Like the rest of her team, Karui highly respects [Killer B](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Killer_B "Killer B") and was steadfast in her pursuit of revenge against Sasuke.

As a mother, Karui is close to her daughter [Chōchō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%8Dch%C5%8D "Chōchō"), who inherited her self-assured personality. She seems laid-back when it comes to the newest generation of [Ino-Shika-Cho](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Formation_Ino%E2%80%93Shika%E2%80%93Ch%C5%8D "Formation Ino–Shika–Chō") 's training, questioning the necessity for Chōchō and her peers to learn such jutsus in times of peace. She also is patient with her teenage daughter's complains and likes to spend time with her family, though she dislikes when her husband forgets to share food she buys.

## Appearance

Karui is a dark-skinned kunoichi with long spiky red hair and amber eyes. As noted by Omoi in Part II, she was flat-chested.

Karui wears a long, short-sleeved dress with frilly edges complete with the Kumogakure [flak jacket](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flak_jacket "Flak jacket"), two simple yellow earrings, fishnet stockings, thigh-high boots with white soles and a [forehead protector](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forehead_protector "Forehead protector") which she wears like a bandanna. She also carries a long sword on her back.

As an adult, Karui sports a more feminine figure and her hair has grown fairly longer. Her attire now consists of a long sleeveless dark dress held closed by a white obi. She also no longer wears her forehead protector, and sets her hair in a more neat, back-comb style. Karui has dangling earrings in the same colour as her previous ones. In the anime, however, Karui does not wear earrings.

## Abilities

![Karui using Lightning Release.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/7/7b/Karui%27s_Lightning_Release.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20190218221306)

Karui using Lightning Release.

As a [chūnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin "Chūnin"), Karui is undoubtedly a capable kunoichi. She has demonstrated great teamwork skills, able to coordinate attacks with her partner Omoi without either needing to communicate.[^6] In terms of fighting style, Karui mainly relies on [kenjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kenjutsu "Kenjutsu"), wielding a long katana in battle. Being trained by Killer B, she is quite skilled in kenjutsu. She could effectively defeat several [White Zetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/White_Zetsu "White Zetsu") clones with a [frontal slash](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Cloud-Style_Front_Beheading "Cloud-Style Front Beheading"). In the anime, she can use [Lightning Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_Release "Lightning Release").[^7]

## Part II

### Pain's Assault

Main article: [Pain's Assault](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Pain%27s_Assault_\(Arc\) "Pain's Assault (Arc)")

![Team Samui preparing to depart to Konoha.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/23/Team_Samui.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150224121450)

Team Samui preparing to depart to Konoha.

In her first appearance, she and Omoi argued over the possible reasons that they could have been summoned by the Raikage. Omoi thought he was going to be scolded for putting mayonnaise on his potatoes; the argument started, however, when Omoi suggested that Samui's back problems stemmed from her large breasts, something Karui doesn't need to worry about, due to her being flat-chested. Karui and her teammates are tasked with getting intel on Sasuke and Akatsuki from Konoha.

### Five Kage Summit

Main article: [Five Kage Summit](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Kage_Summit_\(Arc\) "Five Kage Summit (Arc)") Later, after Naruto's victory over [Nagato](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nagato "Nagato"), Karui and her team were seen travelling to Konoha, when Omoi worried that, once they got there, a girl would fall for him and, once he had to leave, she would commit suicide out of loneliness. During that short argument with him, Karui threw a rock at him. While Omoi worried that she could have buried Konoha had she caused an avalanche, Karui angrily yelled that he overreacted too much. After they arrived at Konoha, they were shocked to find a giant crater in its place (caused by Pain's [Shinra Tensei](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinra_Tensei "Shinra Tensei") shortly before his battle with Naruto). Omoi blamed Karui for the crater being there, while Samui said they should ask someone for information on what had happened.

Karui and Omoi overheard Naruto, [Sai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sai "Sai") and [Sakura Haruno](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sakura_Haruno "Sakura Haruno") talking about [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha"), so they attacked Naruto, Sakura and Sai. Later, they explained that Sasuke had attacked and captured their sensei, and that he is an Akatsuki member. Karui then insulted Sakura and asked her what Sasuke is to her. Unable to find an answer to the question, Sakura cries, but Karui was stopped by Sai pointing out that they should not pressure and blame her, before Naruto managed to calm them both down by saying that their master is most likely still alive.

![Karui assaulting Naruto.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/fa/Karui_Beating_Naruto.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20180428081333)

Karui assaulting Naruto.

Later, Karui and Omoi attempted to get Naruto talking about Sasuke, but Naruto refused, not wanting to sell him out and cause chains of hatred. Wondering what to do with their hatred, Naruto told them to take out their hatred on him by beating him up, which Karui did. Karui continued beating up Naruto until Sai interfered. She then threatened to beat up Sai too, but was stopped by Omoi. Samui arrived, and asked Karui and Omoi to help her copy down all of the Konoha's information on Akatsuki and Sasuke.

Once they left, Omoi wondered if Naruto was alright. Karui angrily told him to be quiet, through which Omoi could tell that Karui cared as well. She watched when Naruto pleaded with the Raikage not to go after Sasuke. Karui is seen welcoming back the Raikage, [Killer B](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Killer_B "Killer B"), [C](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/C "C") and [Darui](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Darui "Darui") after the [Kage Summit](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kage_Summit "Kage Summit"), and she is relieved that her master is safe.

### Fourth Shinobi World War: Confrontation

Main article: [Fourth Shinobi World War: Confrontation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War:_Confrontation "Fourth Shinobi World War: Confrontation")

![Karui assigned to the Second Division.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/d/d0/Karui_with_the_Second_Division.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20180801232659)

Karui assigned to the Second Division.

Karui is placed in the [Second Division](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Division "Second Division") in the [Allied Shinobi Forces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Allied_Shinobi_Forces "Allied Shinobi Forces"). She tells [Hinata Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hy%C5%ABga "Hinata Hyūga") that the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War") will be easy. Later, as the White Zetsu Army was unearthed, she attacked them using the [Cloud-Style Front Beheading](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Cloud-Style_Front_Beheading "Cloud-Style Front Beheading").

In the anime, during the second day of the war, Karui and her division were attacked by one of the [reincarnations](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Impure_World_Reincarnation "Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation") from [Kabuto Yakushi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kabuto_Yakushi "Kabuto Yakushi"): a former samurai named [Tatewaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tatewaki "Tatewaki"). As Karui began to grow anxious from the division being quickly overwhelmed by the reincarnated samurai's swordsmanship, Naruto arrived to aid them. Karui at first didn't recognise Naruto's [new form](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tails_Chakra_Mode "Nine-Tails Chakra Mode"), which greatly angered him. As Tatewaki recognised Naruto from three years ago, he asked what became of the [two](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sh%C5%AB_\(lord\) "Shū (lord)") [children](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chiyo_\(princess\) "Chiyo (princess)") he was protecting. Naruto and the reincarnated samurai clash, Naruto tells him that the children survived and are living happily. Relieved to hear this, Tatewaki suddenly dropped his sword. Now free of regrets, Tatewaki no longer had any emotional bonds to this world, letting him break the summoning contract and return to the afterlife. Watching this, Karui admiring the latter for taking back control.

### Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax

Main article: [Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War:_Climax "Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax")

![Karui and the Allied Shinobi Force confront the Ten-Tails.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/a8/Allied_Forces_jutsu.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150914121326)

Karui and the Allied Shinobi Force confront the Ten-Tails.

Along with the other remaining members of the Allied Shinobi Forces, Karui later made her way to the battlefield where the [jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki") were. Meeting up with Omoi, the two immediately went to B's side when they arrived, where the two proceeded to make sure that their sensei was all-right. They later stood alongside the others to face their opposition.

After B had regained enough chakra and flew towards the [Ten-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails "Ten-Tails") to launch a counter-measure against the Tailed Beast Ball the beast was preparing to launch, Karui emphatically cheered for her sensei to get them as he launched himself towards the beast.

### Kaguya Ōtsutsuki Strikes

Main article: [Kaguya Ōtsutsuki Strikes](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaguya_%C5%8Ctsutsuki_Strikes "Kaguya Ōtsutsuki Strikes") Ultimately, Karui and the rest of the world were freed from the [Infinite Tsukuyomi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Infinite_Tsukuyomi "Infinite Tsukuyomi") by Naruto and Sasuke after they ended the war. Eventually, Karui moved to Konoha and married Chōji, leading to her joining the Akimichi clan and having a daughter, whom they named Chōchō.

### Sarada Uchiha Arc

Main article: [Naruto Gaiden: The Seventh Hokage and the Scarlet Spring](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Gaiden:_The_Seventh_Hokage_and_the_Scarlet_Spring "Naruto Gaiden: The Seventh Hokage and the Scarlet Spring")

Karui and the others waiting for their children.

On the day of the Kage Summit in Konoha, Karui was present for the upcoming training session of the newest generation of the [Ino-Shika-Cho](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ino-Shika-Cho "Ino-Shika-Cho"). Despite her assistance, she questioned the necessity of Chōchō's traning alongside her classmates in such peaceful times, leading to [Ino Yamanaka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ino_Yamanaka "Ino Yamanaka") informing her of the importance of the custom to [their](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yamanaka_Clan "Yamanaka Clan") [clans](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nara_Clan "Nara Clan").

With graduation exams nearing for her daughter's class, Karui met with her family to eat out. While Chōchō began complaining again about how different she was from her parents, Karui took it in stride.

### Naruto Shinden: Parent and Child Day

Main articles: [Naruto Shinden: Parent and Child Day](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Shinden:_Parent_and_Child_Day "Naruto Shinden: Parent and Child Day") and [Parent and Child Day Arc](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Parent_and_Child_Day_Arc "Parent and Child Day Arc") **NOTE:** In the anime, the events of this arc occur after the [Chōchō Arc](#Chōchō_Arc).

![Karui spending Parent and Child Day with her family.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/56/Akimichi_family_dinner.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20190226062443)

Karui spending Parent and Child Day with her family.

During the new Konohagakure holiday, Parent and Child Day, Karui was annoyed at both how lazy Chōji was being and that he ate all the sweets she specifically bought for the family to eat together, ultimately kicking him out of the house to think about his actions. Later, she begrudgingly watched her husband and daughter eat in a food competition. When the dumplings began growing endlessly, Karui joined her family in fighting them off to no avail. Ultimately, Chōji enlarged himself to eat all the dumplings and apologised for eating all the sweets before, realising that food should be shared with family. Karui forgave him. At the end of Parent and Child Day, Karui watches on as Chōji and Chōchō eat.
