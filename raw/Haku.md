**Haku** (白) was an orphan from the [Land of Water](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Water "Land of Water"), and a descendant of the [Yuki clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yuki_clan "Yuki clan").[^4] He later became a [shinobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinobi "Shinobi") under [Zabuza Momochi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Zabuza_Momochi "Zabuza Momochi") 's tutelage whom he later partnered with, ultimately becoming a [Mercenary Ninja](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mercenary_Ninja "Mercenary Ninja").

## Background

Haku as a child.

Haku was born in a small, snowy village in the [Land of Water](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Water "Land of Water"), a land that had suffered from war not long before. In this war, different sides battled each other using ninja, some of whom possessed [kekkei genkai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kekkei_genkai "Kekkei genkai"). After the war ended, memories of the horrific battles still lingered in the minds of the people. Those with kekkei genkai quickly became feared and hated for their abilities, out of concern that their existence would only bring about more war.

Haku's father and mother were simple farmers, and they lived a peaceful life. They loved each other, and were kind to their child. Unfortunately, this would all change. Haku's mother was a wielder of a kekkei genkai: [Ice Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ice_Release "Ice Release"). She hid this fact from her husband, hoping that the love and peace that was shared in their small family would last forever. One day, Haku discovered the ability to manipulate water. Amazed by this, Haku proudly showed this to his mother, who was horrified by what she saw. She harshly scolded and slapped Haku for displaying his ability, though she tearfully apologized to him immediately afterwards. Unbeknownst to them, Haku's father had seen everything from the shadows. When Haku's father discovered that his wife and child possessed a kekkei genkai, he assembled a small mob of villagers. With tears flowing down his eyes, he killed his wife. He then attempted to kill Haku. But before he could, Haku used his Ice Release to create several large [ice spikes](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Certain-Kill_Ice_Spears "Certain-Kill Ice Spears") to kill his father and the rest of the mob.

Orphaned, Haku became a child who was wanted by no one and was forced to take to the cold streets and rummage through trash bins for scraps of food, even sometimes having to fight off the wild dogs that roamed the streets. In time, he was confronted by Zabuza Momochi, a man who had "the same eyes" as him. Zabuza later asked Haku to become his "weapon," which meant to become a dedicated [shinobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinobi "Shinobi") for Zabuza. Haku readily accepted this role, due to the purpose it gave him, and devoting his life to becoming the ultimate tool for Zabuza to use. In the anime, upon leaving the Land of Water, Haku and Zabuza met [Kimimaro](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kimimaro "Kimimaro"). Haku appeared to sympathise with Kimimaro for his loneliness and lack of purpose by looking into his eyes. However, he was forced to keep moving by Zabuza, and left Kimimaro to be.[^5] Zabuza then trained Haku in the ways of the shinobi, as well as every fighting technique that he knew.

## Personality

Haku was a good-hearted, compliant, and eager shinobi, believing that a person becomes truly strong when they have someone important to protect. Despite his talent for battle, and incredible abilities, Haku disliked needing to kill others in his role as a tool, and avoided doing so whenever possible, Zabuza told him he was too soft when Haku decided to face the two [Genin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genin "Genin"). When forced to face an opponent, Haku did all that he could to avoid inflicting great harm, even putting them into a death-like state to prevent them from fighting further, as he did to [Sasuke](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke "Sasuke") during their battle. Before he met [Zabuza](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Zabuza "Zabuza"), he suffered from a belief that he was unnecessary, and served Zabuza because he wanted to feel useful, when he couldn't defeat the [Kyubi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kyubi "Kyubi") 's power, he became so demoralized that he begged Naruto to kill him. His loyalty to Zabuza at times even appeared akin to seeing Zabuza as a father as upon hearing from Kakashi how much Zabuza actually cared for Haku, the boy cried in joy at learning this. Aside from his devotion to Zabuza, Haku can feel anger to those he perceived as disrespecting of him, breaking Gatō's arm and threatening to kill his two bodyguards with their own swords when they tried to attack him. Because of Haku's pure, kind-hearted personality, Zabuza believed, despite his deeds as his tool, Haku would be able to thrive in heaven while he was condemned to hell.

## Appearance

	Haku in his shinobi attire.

1/2

Haku was a 15-year-old boy with an androgynous appearance and was even viewed as being beautiful by Naruto, who exclaimed that he was "prettier than [Sakura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sakura "Sakura") ", even after he informed him that he was male.[^6] He had long black hair, pale skin and large, dark-brown eyes, and a slender frame.

Haku's normal shinobi outfit consisted of the standard Kirigakure pinstriped outfit which stopped at his knees. Over this he wore a green haori with white trimmings, and around his waist a brown sash with a fringed trail wrapped around his waist twice. He also wore light-brown platoon sandals with straps in the same colour as his kimono and nail polish on his fingernails and toenails in matching blue green colour. When wearing this outfit, Haku's long hair was gathered in a white bun holder, while two locks of his hair fell loose framing his face, bound with metal hair cuffs at the ends. Haku also wore a black forehead protector with the Kirigakure's symbol upon it. When in battle or moving covertly, he wore a hunter-nin mask on top of his forehead protector, which was white with thin, curved eye-holes and a red wavy design in place of the mouth, as well as the Kirigakure symbol etched in the top.

When Haku was not in combat, he wore his long hair loosely, and wore a pink, sleeveless kimono, with pale red edges and decorated with small plum-coloured swirls, that went to his ankles. Around his waist was a simple white obi tied in a bow, and he wore a pair of light brown sandals with dark straps. He also wore a dark-coloured choker around his neck.

When he was a child, Haku wore an overly-large, shredded light-brown shirt which fell off his shoulders, and a pair of shredded knee-length pants, as well as the dark choker. Later when taken in by Zabuza, Haku was spotted wearing a short blue kimono with light blue edges and a dark grey split skirt, similar to those ones he wore as a shinobi, and also similar to those he wore as a younger child when his parents were alive.

When he was [reincarnated](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Impure_World_Reincarnation "Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation"), his sclera turned black.

## Abilities

![Haku attacking shinobi.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/ae/Haku_attacks_shinobi.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160120050949)

Haku attacking shinobi.

A gifted individual of such prodigious talent that, having also been taught everything [Zabuza](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Zabuza "Zabuza") knew, Haku was a very dangerous, and powerful ninja for his age. According to Zabuza, in many ways, Haku had become more skillful than the [Anbu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Anbu "Anbu") and the former Seven Mist Ninja Swordsmen member himself.[^7] Even an elite Konoha [jōnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%8Dnin "Jōnin") like [Kakashi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi "Kakashi") believed the young shinobi to be stronger than himself at the time of their first meeting.[^8] According to both Sasuke and Zabuza, Haku could've killed both the former and Naruto if he wanted, but chose to hold back against them.[^9] [^10] His skills were great enough for [Kabuto Yakushi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kabuto_Yakushi "Kabuto Yakushi") to reincarnate him with the [Impure World Reincarnation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Impure_World_Reincarnation "Impure World Reincarnation") technique to fight during the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War").

### Physical and Chakra Prowess

![Haku disarms Zōri and Waraji before they can react.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/8e/Haku_vs_guardians.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240918031102)

Haku disarms Zōri and Waraji before they can react.

One of Haku's defining battle-traits was his sheer speed. His speed is so tremendous that he was able to quickly close the gap between [Zōri](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Z%C5%8Dri "Zōri") and [Waraji](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Waraji "Waraji") and take their swords from them before either of them realised what had happened.[^11] Zabuza was visibly shocked that Sasuke could outpace Haku, although this was only due to Sasuke applying chakra to his feet to increase his speed.[^12] Haku's speed, however, increases to incredible levels when using his [Demonic Mirroring Ice Crystals](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Mirroring_Ice_Crystals "Demonic Mirroring Ice Crystals"), making it appear like he was in all the mirrors at once, even though he was just travelling in between them at extraordinary speeds.[^13] His speed was such that Sasuke could not see nor react to his attacks until he awakened the [Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sharingan "Sharingan").[^14]

Like Zabuza, Haku was highly proficient with the [Silent Killing](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Silent_Killing "Silent Killing"), able to effectively strike down enemies within zero visibility, in some cases in perfect unison with his teacher. Haku was strong enough to carry Zabuza's body a safe distance away without losing speed.[^15] This strength is further highlighted when Haku nearly broke [Gatō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gat%C5%8D "Gatō") arm after casually grabbing it.[^16] [^17] Haku had noticeable [taijutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Taijutsu "Taijutsu") skill, able to effectively block a [kick](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Leaf_Coiling_Whirlwind "Leaf Coiling Whirlwind") from [Might Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Might_Guy "Might Guy") and [Rock Lee](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rock_Lee "Rock Lee") in unison.[^18] Haku also had the rare ability to perform [hand seals](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hand_seals "Hand seals") with only one hand, a skill that amazed and frightened even [Kakashi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi "Kakashi").[^19] Upon his [reanimation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Impure_World_Reincarnation "Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation"), he demonstrated having a powerful chakra as well, being visible to the naked eye.[^20]

### Ninjutsu

![Haku using Ice Release.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/c2/Ice_Release.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20170120070228)

Haku using Ice Release.

Haku is shown to be adept in [shurikenjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shurikenjutsu "Shurikenjutsu"), able to precisely hit key points in a person's body with [senbon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Senbon "Senbon") from great distances.[^21] Haku's most noticeable power was the utilisation of [water](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water "Water") and [wind](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind "Wind") -natured chakra to create [Ice Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ice_Release "Ice Release").[^22] His power over ice granted him special techniques, like the [Demonic Mirroring Ice Crystals](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Mirroring_Ice_Crystals "Demonic Mirroring Ice Crystals"), which surrounds a target with mirrors that Haku could travel between and attack from, even if his opponent is in the sky.[^13] During his flashbacks of his youth in the anime, Haku was shown to release [large spikes made of ice](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Certain-Kill_Ice_Spears "Certain-Kill Ice Spears") when he was about to be killed by his father. Defensively, he could create an [ice dome](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ice_Release:_Ice_Rock_Dome_of_Magnificent_Nothingness "Ice Release: Ice Rock Dome of Magnificent Nothingness") that is capable of blocking multiple explosive tags at once.[^23] Furthermore, it has been implied that Haku could affect the weather, causing localised snowfall.[^24] He could also use his two elements individually, such as his [Thousand Flying Water Needles of Death](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Thousand_Flying_Water_Needles_of_Death "Thousand Flying Water Needles of Death"), which uses his water nature to concentrate the surrounding water into needles to attack enemies from all angles.[^25]

### Intelligence

Haku possessed a very detailed knowledge of human physiology, thanks to Zabuza's former Anbu training. His knowledge was great enough that he could disable an opponent with [acupuncture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/acupuncture "wikipedia:acupuncture") by hitting a precise point on the body with a senbon, to cause immediate death or a temporary appearance of such.[^26] [^27] Haku was also very keen-minded, as he had been shown to have great observational and analytical abilities, capable of quickly determining a way around an opponent's technique or strategy. Haku also appears to have knowledge of some animals as he was able to detect the [Allied Shinobi Forces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Allied_Shinobi_Forces "Allied Shinobi Forces") ' [Surprise Attack Division](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Surprise_Attack_Division "Surprise Attack Division") based on the unusual flight pattern of the birds [Sai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sai "Sai") had made. Haku also knew a lot about herbalism, as he often collected plants to use for medical purposes.

## Part I

### Prologue — Land of Waves

When Zabuza was about to be killed by [Kakashi Hatake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi_Hatake "Kakashi Hatake"), Haku appeared, disguised as a [hunter-nin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hunter-nin "Hunter-nin"), playing the part by appearing to kill Zabuza and pretending to be a hunter ninja who was after Zabuza. After this, he is reprimanded by [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki") for the "death" of a strong rival, and Haku then proceeds to take away the unconscious body. Haku asks him not to be abrupt, but Zabuza asks when he will be wearing the Anbu mask but Haku replies that it reminded him of the old days and was useful in the rescue, adding that Zabuza would have died if he had not appeared, Zabuza replies that he attacked his neck, but Haku says that he did not want to leave scars on Zabuza's "beautiful body" also highlighting the lack of muscles in his neck. In this Haku adds that Zabuza will not be able to move for a week, Zabuza praises him for being pure and intelligent but Haku argues that he is still a child and then realizes that the mist disappeared, leaving Zabuza to rest a week before he could have a rematch with Kakashi.

During that time, a disenchanted [Gatō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gat%C5%8D "Gatō") appears, mocking Zabuza, and his bodyguards, [Zōri](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Z%C5%8Dri "Zōri") and [Waraji](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Waraji "Waraji") prepare to unsheathe their swords, what caught Haku's attention. The tycoon tells them to wait as they approach Zabuza, but Haku threatened Gatō not to touch Zabuza as he recovered. After an attempt to attack him, he held Gato's subordinates' swords against their own necks warning them not to continue since he was starting to get angry. After the incident, Gatō gives them an ultimatum that if they fail again they will be expelled. After Gatō leaving the refuge, Zabuza replied that they shouldn't interfere, but Haku argued that if they cause a stir, they would be hunted down again, choosing to be patient. Later, he changes into his casual attire to look for medicinal plants and finds Naruto unconscious from training and wakes him up, warning him that he's about to catch a cold. After he had a conversation with him about their purposes in life and those they care about, Haku finishes collecting plants showing faith that the ninja will become stronger and that they will see each other very soon, and he finally clarifies that he's a man.

Haku facing Naruto and Sasuke.

During Zabuza's second fight with Kakashi, Haku (wearing the same mask as when he saved Zabuza) was drawn into battle with Kakashi's students: first Sasuke and then Naruto. He trapped Sasuke with his [Demonic Mirroring Ice Crystals](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Mirroring_Ice_Crystals "Demonic Mirroring Ice Crystals"), and started bombarding him with needles, until Naruto came, with the same happening to him. With both tired out, when Sasuke appeared to have died protecting Naruto from a "fatal" blow (in actuality being put in a death-like state by Haku), Naruto, in anger, tapped into the power of the [Nine-Tailed Demon Fox](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tailed_Demon_Fox "Nine-Tailed Demon Fox") sealed within him. He was able to attack Haku, and break through the mirrors, in turn breaking Haku's mask. Recognising Haku from earlier, Naruto regained his senses in time to stop himself from killing Haku.

![Haku's death.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/c5/Kakashi_Impales_Haku.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20221003120202)

Haku's death.

Naruto recognizes him and reprimands him for following someone like Zabuza but Haku tells him about the persecution of people with Kekkei Genkai and how it involved his tragic childhood, he states that when Zabuza asked him to come with him for his unique abilities, he said that it made him very happy, Naruto reprimands him for following someone like Zabuza. Haku, defeated, and thus of no further use to Zabuza, asked Naruto to kill him anyway. Though reluctant, Naruto tried to comply, if it would make Haku happy. Before Naruto could do so, however, Haku sensed that Zabuza was about to be killed by Kakashi. Finding one final purpose, he parried Naruto's attack and went to Zabuza, using himself as a human shield against Kakashi's [Lightning Cutter](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_Cutter "Lightning Cutter"). Before he died, he grabbed Kakashi's arm, hoping that in death he would be useful to Zabuza in defeating his opponent. Zabuza thanked him, and tried to cut through his body to kill Kakashi, but Kakashi was able to pull away from the attack.

![Haku in death beside Zabuza.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/93/Zabuza_and_Haku_HD_Shot.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160124002556)

Haku in death beside Zabuza.

After both of Zabuza's arms got injured, Zabuza's employer, [Gatō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gat%C5%8D "Gatō"), arrived soon afterwards, and chose to end his contract with Zabuza, having brought his men to kill him. Gatō was quick to defile Haku's body. While initially unperturbed by this, Zabuza was brought to tears by the act when Naruto told him of how Haku felt about him. In a final act of repentance, Zabuza took one of Naruto's kunai, went through all the men, and stabbed and slashed Gatō several times, at the cost of his own life. As he died from the injuries given to him by Gatō's men, Zabuza asked to be brought beside Haku in death to see his face one more time, and he explained how he'd wish to go to where Haku will go in the afterlife. Kakashi finally claimed that he promises they both will go together. In the aftermath, Haku was buried next to Zabuza.

## Part II

### Fourth Shinobi World War: Confrontation

Main article: [Fourth Shinobi World War: Confrontation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War:_Confrontation "Fourth Shinobi World War: Confrontation") In preparation for the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"), Haku was [reincarnated](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Impure_World_Reincarnation "Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation") by [Kabuto Yakushi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kabuto_Yakushi "Kabuto Yakushi") to fight against the [Allied Shinobi Forces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Allied_Shinobi_Forces "Allied Shinobi Forces"), before being mobilised alongside [Gari](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gari "Gari"), [Pakura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Pakura "Pakura") and [Toroi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Toroi "Toroi"). Conversing with Zabuza, Haku noted their situation to be unusual, as his body was moving of its own accord despite his mind being clear.

![Haku protects Zabuza.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/95/Protective_Ice_Dome.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160120050415)

Haku protects Zabuza.

Ambushed by the [Surprise Attack Division](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Surprise_Attack_Division "Surprise Attack Division"), Haku quickly [protected](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ice_Release:_Ice_Rock_Dome_of_Magnificent_Nothingness "Ice Release: Ice Rock Dome of Magnificent Nothingness") both himself and Zabuza from the [explosive tags](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Explosive_tag "Explosive tag") carried by [Sai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sai "Sai") 's [ink birds](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Super_Beast_Imitating_Drawing "Super Beast Imitating Drawing"). Locating the enemy's position in the sky, Haku created a [mirror](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Mirroring_Ice_Crystals "Demonic Mirroring Ice Crystals") above them and shot them down with his [senbon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Senbon "Senbon"), before rejoining the other reincarnated shinobi below. Involuntarily storming the grounded opposition, Haku was intercepted by [Rock Lee](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rock_Lee "Rock Lee") before he could deal the deadly blow. As Zabuza talked with Kakashi, the latter revealed that Naruto had become an admirable shinobi since they had fought him, to which Haku happily remarked that he believed Naruto would only continue to improve.

![Haku stands with the others as his consciousness is erased.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/d/d1/Kabuto_eradicates_their_personalities.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120531114914)

Haku stands with the others as his consciousness is erased.

As Kabuto erased their consciousness, Haku pleaded with Kakashi to stop them again while stating his belief that he had failed as Zabuza's tool, since the latter was also dead. When Kakashi rebuffed this stating that he had in fact succeeded, that Zabuza died avenging Haku because he truly viewed Haku as more than a mere tool, a tearful Haku silently thanked Zabuza as the last of his consciousness faded away. A mindless Haku then launched into an assault, killing several [Third Division](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Division "Third Division") shinobi, until he was [repelled](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Leaf_Coiling_Whirlwind "Leaf Coiling Whirlwind") by [Might Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Might_Guy "Might Guy") and Rock Lee.

![Haku gets bisected by Zabuza.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/91/Zabuza_cuts_down_Haku.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150825022109)

Haku gets bisected by Zabuza.

After the previous generation of the [Seven Ninja Swordsmen of the Mist](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Seven_Ninja_Swordsmen_of_the_Mist "Seven Ninja Swordsmen of the Mist") are [summoned](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning_Technique "Summoning Technique") to the battlefield, Haku returned to his mirrors, as the swordsmen began their [silent](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Silent_Killing "Silent Killing") massacre on the [mist-covered](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiding_in_Mist_Technique "Hiding in Mist Technique") battlefield. Kabuto later moved Haku to intercept Kakashi's attempt to [incapacitate](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_Cutter "Lightning Cutter") Zabuza — reflecting how he had blocked Kakashi in their first encounter. An impaled Haku then kept Kakashi still so that Zabuza could attack; bisecting Haku in the process. [Ensui Nara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ensui_Nara "Ensui Nara") then [immobilised](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Imitation_Technique "Shadow Imitation Technique") Haku, giving [Maki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Maki "Maki") the opportunity to [bind](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Cloth_Binding_Technique "Cloth Binding Technique") and [seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sealing_Tag "Sealing Tag") him and Zabuza after his defeat.

### Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax

Main article: [Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War:_Climax "Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax") Later, with the release of the Impure World Reincarnation technique, a light of sorts encased Haku's sealed body as the technique was dispelled, and his soul was returned to the [afterlife](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Pure_Land "Pure Land").

## Legacy

![Zabuza and Haku's grave.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/62/Zabuza_and_Haku%27s_grave.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20210811200444)

Zabuza and Haku's grave.

Both Haku and Zabuza had a profound influence in Naruto's life and their interactions were the factor of Naruto's [nindō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nind%C5%8D "Nindō") of never abandoning his comrades. During his and Zabuza's reincarnation in the midst of the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"), Kakashi admitted that it was because of him and Zabuza that Naruto strove to become the ninja and hero that he is.[^28] Naruto himself credits Haku and Zabuza as people who had a great influence on his life and his views on the world.[^29]

## Trivia

- The name "Haku" means "white" (白), while his family name "Yuki" means "snow" (雪).
- Despite his early death, and short appearances in the story, Haku has remained a relatively popular character in the story. He routinely appears in favourite character lists, but has fallen out of the top 10 in more recent polls.
- Haku and his Ice Release ability were made reference to in Part II, when Naruto was learning about different kinds of chakra.
- Haku's mask made a cameo appearance in the ending omake of *Shippūden* [episode 129](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Infiltrate!_The_Village_Hidden_in_the_Rain "Infiltrate! The Village Hidden in the Rain"), along with Tobi's mask and Baki's mask. He also appears in person alongside Zabuza, Hayate Gekkō, and several Amegakure ninja in [episode 183](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto:_Outbreak "Naruto: Outbreak") of Shippūden.
- In *Naruto: Shippūden* [episode 146](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/The_Successor%27s_Wish "The Successor's Wish"), a [hunter-nin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hunter-nin "Hunter-nin") appears with a mask and hair style similar to Haku's.
- In the Ishikawa Prefecture of Japan, there is a snow-covered, potentially active volcano known as [Mount Haku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Haku "wikipedia:Mount Haku").
- In Part I, Haku wasn't depicted wearing lipstick neither in the manga nor anime. In Part II, however, when he is reincarnated, he is seen wearing lipstick (dark shade in the manga and light shade in the anime) in the present and in flashbacks.
- In 2019, [Masashi Kishimoto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Masashi_Kishimoto "Masashi Kishimoto") revealed that Haku was originally going to be a bear, before his editor suggested that they change him into a human.
- The way Naruto destroys Haku's mask was later mirrored when Naruto destroyed [Obito](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito "Obito") 's mask.[^30] [^31]
- Studio Pierrot's Settei sheets of Haku show that his sandals add 5 cm on to his height.
- The manner in which Kakashi killed Haku mirrored the way Kakashi killed [Rin Nohara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rin_Nohara "Rin Nohara"), as both times were accidental, due to both Haku and Rin moving in front of Kakashi's attack and being impaled through the chest.
- According to *[Naruto x Boruto: Ultimate Ninja Storm Connections](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_x_Boruto:_Ultimate_Ninja_Storm_Connections "Naruto x Boruto: Ultimate Ninja Storm Connections")*, Haku was a former Kiri-nin.
