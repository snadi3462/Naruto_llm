**Matatabi** (又旅), more commonly known as the **Two-Tails** (ニ尾, *Nibi*), is one of the nine [tailed beasts](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_beasts "Tailed beasts"). It was last sealed within [Yugito Nii](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yugito_Nii "Yugito Nii") of [Kumogakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kumogakure "Kumogakure").

## Background

![The young tailed beasts with Hagoromo.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/bb/Young_Bijuu_and_Hagoromo.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150728221849)

The young tailed beasts with Hagoromo.

Matatabi and the tailed beasts first came into being in the waning days of [Hagoromo Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hagoromo_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Hagoromo Ōtsutsuki"), who used his [Creation of All Things Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Creation_of_All_Things_Technique "Creation of All Things Technique") to divide the [Ten-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails "Ten-Tails") ' chakra into nine separate bodies and giving each a name. Some time after being created, the Sage explained to the young tailed beasts that a link existed between them despite their separation. He also foretold of a day when they would be brought together again - though not as they were - and that at that time someone would emerge to show them what true power is.[^5] [^6] In the anime, before his death, Hagoromo sent Matatabi to live in a temple built for the purpose of protecting it in a canebrake region.[^7] Over time, many people came to believe that Matatabi, like [Shukaku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shukaku "Shukaku"), was once a human that turned into a [living ghost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ikiry%C5%8D "wikipedia:Ikiryō") (生霊, *ikiryō*).[^1]

![Matatabi's wall mural inside the Falls of Truth.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/0a/Nibi_mural.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20210805003640)

Matatabi's wall mural inside the Falls of Truth.

At some point after the creation of Konohagakure, [Hashirama Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama_Senju "Hashirama Senju") captured eight of the tailed beasts and sold Matatabi and [Gyūki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gy%C5%ABki "Gyūki") to Kumogakure at the [Five Kage Summit](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Kage_Summit "Five Kage Summit"), as a sign of friendship, peace, and to ensure a balance of power amongst their new villages.[^8] That peace didn't last, and the [First Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/First_Shinobi_World_War "First Shinobi World War") started, In a cavern behind the [Falls of Truth](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Falls_of_Truth "Falls of Truth"), where [jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki") learn to control their tailed beasts' power, there are wall murals of Matatabi and Gyūki, which indicates how long they were associated with the [Land of Lightning](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Lightning "Land of Lightning").[^9] [^10]

The tailed beast would end up being sealed within [Yugito Nii](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yugito_Nii "Yugito Nii") when she was two years old. She was placed into a detestable training programme in order to learn to control Matatabi's power.[^11] In the anime, Yugito agreed to have [Nekobaa](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nekobaa "Nekobaa") collect Matatabi's paw print for the [Paw Encyclopaedia](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Paw_Encyclopaedia "Paw Encyclopaedia").[^12]

## Personality

Compared to most of the tailed beasts, Matatabi has shown to be respectful and polite towards others, speaking in a formal manner using "watashi" (私) when referring to itself and using [honourifics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_honorifics "wikipedia:Japanese honorifics") when addressing Naruto while introducing itself to the young shinobi.

## Appearance

![A young Matatabi.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/44/Young_Matatabi.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130913102847)

A young Matatabi.

Matatabi is two-tailed, like a [nekomata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nekomata "wikipedia:Nekomata"), and is completely engulfed in flames, like a [Kasha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasha_\(folklore\) "wikipedia:Kasha (folklore)") (though it's flames are an animated cobalt blue and black). It also has heterochromatic eyes, the right being yellow and the left being green, similar to an [odd-eyed cat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/odd-eyed_cat "wikipedia:odd-eyed cat") without pupils. When forced to transform by [Tobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobi "Tobi"), Matatabi increased in size compared to when Yugito unleashed it.[^13] During the last remaining days of the Sage of Six Paths' life, Matatabi was a young flaming kitten much smaller than its present-day self, but was still much larger than the Sage.[^6]

## Abilities

*See also: [Jinchūriki Forms](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki_Forms "Jinchūriki Forms")*

![Matatabi creating a Tailed Beast Ball.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/1/16/Matatabi_using_Tailed_Beast_Ball.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150218015751)

Matatabi creating a Tailed Beast Ball.

As a [tailed beast](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_beast "Tailed beast"), Matatabi has an enormous supply of [chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra "Chakra") that it can [transfer](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Transfer_Technique "Chakra Transfer Technique") to others. It also has powerful healing capacities, able to mend injuries in themselves and others in seconds.[^14] Likewise, Matatabi is effectively immortal, as when it dies, it will eventually revive, albeit in a younger form. Also like all its kind, Matatabi create the [Tailed Beast Ball](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Ball "Tailed Beast Ball"). Matatabi also has flexible muscles, which grant it great reflexes and agility despite its large size.[^1] Matatabi's raw strength can easily [crush](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Cat_Claw_Attack "Great Cat Claw Attack") an opponent or send them flying with one swipe of its paw.[^15] When combined with the other tailed beasts' tails, it could shatter and completely obliterate a [senjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Senjutsu "Senjutsu") -enhanced complete [Susanoo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Susanoo "Susanoo").[^16] As its appearance suggest, it can use [Fire Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire_Release "Fire Release"), able to create [fireballs](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Cat_Flame_Roaring_Fire "Cat Flame Roaring Fire").[^17]

## Part II

### Akatsuki Suppression Mission

Main article: [Akatsuki Suppression Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki_Suppression_Mission "Akatsuki Suppression Mission")

![The Akatsuki extracting the Two-Tails from Yugito.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/44/Akatsuki_extracting_Matatabi.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150203070627)

The Akatsuki extracting the Two-Tails from Yugito.

Soon after her introduction, Yugito was captured by two [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki") members, [Hidan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hidan "Hidan") and [Kakuzu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakuzu "Kakuzu"), although it is unknown who was specifically assigned to capture her. Despite luring her two pursuers into a closed space, sealing the exits, and transforming herself into the Two-Tails, Yugito was overpowered and rendered unconscious by the two Akatsuki members. [Zetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Zetsu "Zetsu") later arrived to take her off their hands. After three days of the Akatsuki extracting the Two-Tails into the [statue](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Statue_of_the_Outer_Path "Demonic Statue of the Outer Path") from Yugito and getting sealed, its jinchūriki died afterwards as a result of having her tailed beast extracted.

### Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax

Main article: [Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War:_Climax "Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax")

![The remaining jinchūriki transform into their tailed beasts.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/59/Biju_full_transformation.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130905164030)

The remaining jinchūriki transform into their tailed beasts.

After [Son Gokū](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Son_Gok%C5%AB "Son Gokū") was resealed into the [Demonic Statue of the Outer Path](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Statue_of_the_Outer_Path "Demonic Statue of the Outer Path"), Tobi, who was intent on going all-out from that point on, forced Yugito to fully transform into the Two-Tails. The opposing tailed beasts prepared to attack the shinobi, but they were [rebuffed](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Roar "Great Roar") by a fully-transformed [Naruto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto "Naruto"). Wasting no time in their attacks, the Two-Tails attempted to pounce from behind while Naruto and [Kurama](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kurama "Kurama") were incapacitated by the [Six-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Six-Tails "Six-Tails"), but both the giant cat and slug got slammed with such force that they were sent flying. The opposing beasts regrouped and then prepared a collaborative Tailed Beast Ball, which was met by one from Naruto and Kurama.

![Matatabi, alongside the other jinchūriki and tailed beasts, encounters Naruto.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/6b/Seishin_Sekai.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130905162801)

Matatabi, alongside the other jinchūriki and tailed beasts, encounters Naruto.

As Naruto entered a deeper plane of the tailed beasts' consciousness, he met the remaining five tailed beasts and their jinchūriki. Summoning Naruto before it, the giant cat told the young shinobi that it intended to keep the promise it made to Son Gokū. As it told Naruto to stretch out his hand, Matatabi introduced itself. Later, as Matatabi was freed from the [chakra receiver](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_receiver "Chakra receiver"), it affirmed Kurama's belief that Naruto was the person the [Sage of the Six Paths](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_of_the_Six_Paths "Sage of the Six Paths") had spoken about so long ago before being resealed into the Demonic Statue of the Outer Path.

### Birth of the Ten-Tails' Jinchūriki

Main article: [Birth of the Ten-Tails' Jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Birth_of_the_Ten-Tails%27_Jinch%C5%ABriki "Birth of the Ten-Tails' Jinchūriki")

![The tailed beasts being freed from Obito.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/21/Biju_released.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250919211345)

The tailed beasts being freed from Obito.

Matatabi appeared alongside the other tailed beasts with the exception of Gyūki and Shukaku when the Ten-Tails looked down at Naruto. The image of the tailed beasts, along with a silhouette of the Sage of Six Paths, drove the Ten-Tails to a fit as it evolved further. Later, Naruto, with the help of the remaining [Allied Shinobi Forces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Allied_Shinobi_Forces "Allied Shinobi Forces"), managed to free Matatabi by pulling its chakra out of Obito's body, alongside that of the other tailed beasts.

![The tailed beasts working together to attack Madara.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/53/Tailed_Beasts_vs_Madara.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250919131223)

The tailed beasts working together to attack Madara.

When Madara ran towards the tailed beasts, both Shukaku and [Gaara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gaara "Gaara") conjured [numerous sand bullets](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Release:_Sand_Buckshot "Wind Release: Sand Buckshot") and were able to restrain the Uchiha's movements. Using the opportunity to attack, the tailed beasts each took a swing at their opponent, giving sand-manipulating duo an opening to use their [Desert Layered Imperial Funeral Seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Desert_Layered_Imperial_Funeral_Seal "Desert Layered Imperial Funeral Seal"), but Madara managed to escape the sand pyramid with his Susanoo. As Naruto in [Tailed Beast Mode](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Mode "Tailed Beast Mode") pinned Madara down with a tail, Matatabi and the other tailed beasts joined in and did the same to increase the damage, but the Uchiha survived their attacks and retreated to a safe distance. After he received his own right Rinnegan eye from a White Zetsu clone, Madara quickly summoned the Demonic Statue of the Outer Path from Obito's body, and used the Rinnegan to [repel](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Limbo:_Border_Jail "Limbo: Border Jail") the tailed beasts. With the tailed beasts stunned, Madara then [chained](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Statue_Chains "Demonic Statue Chains") them up and sealed them back into the Demonic Statue.

![The tailed beasts reunite with Hagoromo and Naruto.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/bd/Tailed_Beast_Reunion.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150730121101)

The tailed beasts reunite with Hagoromo and Naruto.

Later, the chakra Matatabi transferred to Naruto earlier, manifested within Naruto's subconscious along with the other tailed beasts to speak with the young jinchūriki and the spirit of Hagoromo Ōtsutsuki. There, they all voiced their shared belief to the Sage that Naruto was the child of prophecy.

### Kaguya Ōtsutsuki Strikes

Main article: [Kaguya Ōtsutsuki Strikes](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaguya_%C5%8Ctsutsuki_Strikes "Kaguya Ōtsutsuki Strikes")

![The tailed beasts being freed from the Ten-Tails.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/af/Tailed_Beast_separate_from_Kaguya.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160901155526)

The tailed beasts being freed from the Ten-Tails.

After Madara's ambitions were cut short by [Black Zetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Zetsu "Black Zetsu"), who revealed to be the silent manipulator of shinobi history since it was first created by [Kaguya Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaguya_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Kaguya Ōtsutsuki"), the black entity used Madara's body as a medium to resurrect its creator. During the battle against the tyrannical princess, Matatabi and the other tailed beasts continued to support Naruto by giving him [their combined power](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_Art:_Super_Tailed_Beast_Rasenshuriken "Sage Art: Super Tailed Beast Rasenshuriken") to overwhelm her. Ultimately, Kaguya was defeated by Naruto and [Sasuke](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke "Sasuke"), who used the [Six Paths — Chibaku Tensei](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Six_Paths_%E2%80%94_Chibaku_Tensei "Six Paths — Chibaku Tensei") to eject all the tailed beasts from her and seal her away inside a newly-created [moon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Moon "Moon") within her own [dimension](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaguya%27s_Dimensions "Kaguya's Dimensions").

After the spirits of Hagoromo and the past Kage summoned both the tailed beasts and [Team 7](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Kakashi "Team Kakashi") back to the real world, the tailed beasts began to discuss their plans for the future since they were finally free. Sasuke, however, made his own intentions known: removing as many people as possible who could get in the way of his plans of leading the world through a revolution to do away with current shinobi systems, including killing all the current Kage and tailed beasts. Just as Sasuke swiftly [subdued](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu:_Sharingan "Genjutsu: Sharingan") the tailed beasts with his Rinnegan and sealed them within his [Chibaku Tensei](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chibaku_Tensei "Chibaku Tensei"), Naruto reassured the tailed beasts that he would save them once again. After Naruto was able to defeat and reform Sasuke, the two dispelled the Infinite Tsukuyomi and Sasuke released Matatabi, along with the other tailed beasts, from their prisons.

## Trivia

- "Matatabi" (又旅) literally means "travel again", which is probably an adaptation to not only the nekomata (猫又), but also the Japanese name for [silver vine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Actinidia_polygama "wikipedia:Actinidia polygama"), a plant noted for having an effect on cats much like that of [catnip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepeta_cataria "wikipedia:Nepeta cataria").
- A [bakeneko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bakeneko "wikipedia:Bakeneko") (化け猫, *monster cat*) is a popular animal [yōkai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Y%C5%8Dkai "wikipedia:Yōkai") in Japanese folklore, and is known for its shape-shifting abilities and being able to walk and speak like a human. It was believed that when a bakeneko reaches a certain age, its tail would split in two, becoming a [nekomata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nekomata "wikipedia:Nekomata") (猫又, *forked-cat*). Matatabi also draws inspiration from the [kasha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasha_\(folklore\) "wikipedia:Kasha (folklore)"), a yōkai that is often depicted as a giant flaming cat, which it can interchange with the bakeneko and nekomata. In addition, all these yōkai were said to have the powers of [necromancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Necromancy "wikipedia:Necromancy"), and had been associated with [strange fires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atmospheric_ghost_lights "wikipedia:Atmospheric ghost lights") and other unexplainable occurrences.
- In *[Naruto Mobile](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Mobile "Naruto Mobile")*, within the dream world created by the [Infinite Tsukuyomi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Infinite_Tsukuyomi "Infinite Tsukuyomi"), Matatabi is able to [transfer](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Transfer_Technique "Chakra Transfer Technique") the blue flames of its body into Yugito's weapon to deliver a powerful strike.[^18]
