**Ao** (青) was a former [Anbu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Anbu "Anbu") of [Kirigakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kirigakure "Kirigakure") 's [Hunter-nin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hunter-nin "Hunter-nin") Corps, that served as the [Fifth Mizukage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fifth_Mizukage "Fifth Mizukage") 's right hand.[^2] For his services, he was regarded as a hero of his village, before retiring and secretly working as an Outer for [Kara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kara "Kara").

## Background

Ao fought on the battlefields ever since he was a young boy.[^4] During the [Third Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Shinobi_World_War "Third Shinobi World War"),[^1] he defeated a [Hyūga clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hy%C5%ABga_clan "Hyūga clan") member and took one of their [Byakugan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Byakugan "Byakugan"), which he implanted into his right eye socket. With it, he saw through the [genjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu "Genjutsu") placed on the [Fourth Mizukage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Mizukage "Fourth Mizukage"), who would lose his life to it after its dispel.[^5] Years after the war, he encountered [Shisui Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shisui_Uchiha "Shisui Uchiha"), which left enough of an impact on Ao for him to clearly remember Shisui's abilities and his [chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra "Chakra") colour.[^6]

In the anime, shortly after Yagura's passing and [Mei Terumī](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mei_Terum%C4%AB "Mei Terumī") was made the Fifth Mizukage, Ao was present when the village received an invitation from [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure") and [Sunagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sunagakure "Sunagakure") to join an impromptu [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams") they would be hosting collaboratively. Ao noted that as the village was still recovering from the Fourth's passing and thus shouldn't waste time on an event seemingly designed to showcase [Gaara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gaara "Gaara") as the new [Kazekage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kazekage "Kazekage"). After Mei threatened Ao's life after misinterpreting his words as an insult to her marital status, it was ultimately decided the Kiri-nin would not take part in this event.[^7]

## Personality

Ao was calm, prideful, stern and was the epitome of a stereotypical masculine individual. He believed that men should be tough and decisive, constantly scolding [Chōjūrō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%8Dj%C5%ABr%C5%8D "Chōjūrō") for his pessimistic and weak demeanour. This attitude seemed to be caused by his upbringing during the times when Kirigakure was known as the "Village of the Bloody Mist". He had a tendency of bringing up how things were done during those days, usually while fussing over Chōjūrō's behaviour. Despite his fussing, Ao did seem to have some type of friendship with Chōjūrō as he offered to treat him to a meal for luck in the upcoming war.

After surviving the Fourth Shinobi World War, while outwardly becoming mellow and at peace with his glory days behind him, grateful to even be alive, Ao had in reality grown bitter and disillusioned with the Shinobi world due to his belief of having no further purpose in life due to his disability and also due to the current peaceful era. Despite his previous loyalty to Kirigakure, he had not returned to the village since the war's end. Ao also seemed to possess a colder and battle-hungry outlook, seen when he looked forward to taking part in combat for the first time in sixteen years.

Openly saying he abandoned his identity as a shinobi long ago, he preferred to rely on the more modern combat methods using [Scientific Ninja Tools](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Scientific_Ninja_Tools "Scientific Ninja Tools"), noting that they offered great power to those who could never achieve it naturally. At the same time, Boruto noted that Ao still retained some of his former shinobi outlook, particularly being critical of his Water Release usage as a former Kiri-nin. After his defeat at the hands of [Boruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Boruto_Uzumaki "Boruto Uzumaki"), the young genin's mercy and words of using the tools available properly, along with still seeing Ao as a living person with worth resonated with him. This apparently restored his former shinobi pride, as he saved Boruto from an attack meant to kill him and the Konoha-nin and doing so with ninjutsu.

## Appearance

- ![Ao's appearance in Part II.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/41/UNSG_Ao.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/49?cb=20241004210508)
	Ao's appearance in Part II.

He was a middle aged man with blue hair which was styled in a moused-up manner. His left eye was blue and his right eye was covered with an eye-patch, under which he concealed the Byakugan that he obtained from a Hyūga clan member he defeated. He wore a talisman in each ear with the kanji for a humble form of "to hear" (承, *shō*) written on them twice on each side. These talismans reacted when his Byakugan was under threat and would protect it from anyone, even himself. He also wore the standard striped, grey suit, with a green haori that had white trimmings that stop halfway down, over them, and a [forehead protector](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forehead_protector "Forehead protector"). With the outbreak of the Fourth Shinobi World War, he donned the standard outfit of the shinobi alliance with a Kirigakure [flak jacket](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flak_jacket "Flak jacket") over it.

Sixteen years after the [Fourth Great Shinobi War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Great_Shinobi_War "Fourth Great Shinobi War"), Ao had aged noticeably, with wrinkles lining his face. He wore a dark suit jacket over a pinstripe turtleneck shirt and matching pinstripe pants. The attack from the Ten-Tails cost him his Byakugan. Also, his left arm and leg had been replaced with experimental cybernetic prosthetics. The right side of his face was heavily scarred, and a craniofacial prosthetic covered his right eye, cheek, and ear. The back of his right hand and right wrist were also heavily scarred. He no longer wore a forehead protector nor his talisman earrings.

## Abilities

As a veteran of Kirigakure's hunter-nin, Ao was a highly capable tracker and has extensive knowledge of the human body. Ao had rather impressive speed and reaction, able to grab and redirect the [kama](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kama "Kama") back at [the assailant](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_Puppet_Switch_Cursed_Seal_Technique "Mind Puppet Switch Cursed Seal Technique") before the latter could sneak attack him from behind.[^8] His prowess was such, that he could survive a fight with Shisui Uchiha, a feared shinobi who was regarded as a genius even amongst the Uchiha. Even in old age, [Koji Kashin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Koji_Kashin "Koji Kashin"), an Inner of [Kara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kara "Kara"), held Ao's abilities in high regard, viewing him mighty enough on his own to not need additional weapons. Ao was shown to be very fast, able to strike with blazing speed that can easily catch his foe off guard. He also had advanced taijutsu prowess. In the anime, it was shown that after his rehabilitation, his physical abilities greatly declined, having failed to hit a single target with shuriken.[^9]

### Ninjutsu

#### Nature Transformation

Common among Kiri-nin, Ao could utilise [Water Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water_Release "Water Release"), able to create a [vortex of water](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water_Release:_Water_Colliding_Wave "Water Release: Water Colliding Wave"). He could also use [Yin Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yin_Release "Yin Release") to perform [genjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu "Genjutsu"), as he was capable of controlling an [individual](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Katasuke_T%C5%8Dno "Katasuke Tōno") for a extended duration of time, using a fake identity of "Kusa" while doing so.

### Body Modifications

![Ao activates the Byakugan.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/d/d8/Ao%27s_byakugan_activated.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150307065539)

Ao activates the Byakugan.

After defeating a Hyūga, Ao transplanted one of his eyes with the latter's Byakugan. According to *[School Trip Bloodwind Records](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/School_Trip_Bloodwind_Records "School Trip Bloodwind Records")*, Ao possessed a special Kirigakure ninjutsu that allowed him to do this.[^10] He wore [talisman seals](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Barrier_Talisman:_Armoured_Eye "Barrier Talisman: Armoured Eye") as earrings, which would protect the [dōjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/D%C5%8Djutsu "Dōjutsu") if anyone tried to take or attack it. Once activated, the seal could only be deactivated by a hunter-nin like himself.[^11] With the Byakugan, he could distinguish [chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra "Chakra") colours of certain individuals and determine if an individual was under the influence of genjutsu.[^12] One of the many physical injuries Ao received during the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War") was the loss of his Byakugan.[^13]

![Ao firing ninjutsu bullets.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/ce/Ao%27s_Bullets.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20210201064518)

Ao firing ninjutsu bullets.

After his body was modified with [Shinobi-Ware](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinobi-Ware "Shinobi-Ware") and he completed his rehabilitation, Ao's body became capable of withstanding a [cave collapsing](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Release:_Rock_Lodging_Destruction "Earth Release: Rock Lodging Destruction") on top of him. Further advances on his modified body was the usage a mechanism embedded in his chest to fire [Fire Release Bullets](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire_Release_Bullet:_%22Blaze%22 "Fire Release Bullet: \"Blaze\""), and was equipped with a double-sided sabre below his left arm, denoted a "Style Three Shinobi Gauntlet".[^14]

### Bukijutsu

During his battle against [Team 7](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_7_\(Konohamaru\) "Team 7 (Konohamaru)"), Ao utilised condenser-type [Scientific Ninja Tools](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Scientific_Ninja_Tools "Scientific Ninja Tools") given by [Koji Kashin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Koji_Kashin "Koji Kashin"). He wielded a [minigun](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire_Gatling "Fire Gatling") that can rapid-fire Fire Release Bullets, and four (nine in the anime) [Mirror Drones](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_Drone "Mirror Drone") he could control with chakra to fire powerful chakra blasts. He also took one of Katasuke's [gauntlets](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jutsu_Absorption_Arm "Jutsu Absorption Arm") that can create [Absorption Sphere](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Absorption_Sphere "Absorption Sphere") to absorb incoming jutsu, and briefly used a prototype [Chakra Blade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Blade "Chakra Blade") of Katasuke's, before being forced to cast it aside due to the huge chakra consumption.

### Sensory Perception

Ao was a highly skilled [sensor-type](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sensor-type "Sensor-type") shinobi, which allowed him to be appointed as the leader of the [Sensor Division](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sensor_Division "Sensor Division") during the Fourth Shinobi World War. With his sensory skills, he could even pick out the [reincarnated](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Impure_World_Reincarnation "Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation") shinobi out of thousands of [White Zetsu clones](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/White_Zetsu_Army "White Zetsu Army"), a feat that his counterparts could not perform.[^15] Along with [Inoichi Yamanaka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Inoichi_Yamanaka "Inoichi Yamanaka"), he was able to erect an immense [Sensing Barrier](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sensing_Barrier "Sensing Barrier"), which allowed him to monitor the battle between the Allied Shinobi Forces and the White Zetsu Army. Ao also counted as one the few sensors that have shown the ability to sense [natural energy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Natural_energy "Natural energy").[^16]

## Part II

### Five Kage Summit

Main article: [Five Kage Summit](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Kage_Summit_\(Arc\) "Five Kage Summit (Arc)") Ao sets off with Chōjūrō to escort the Fifth Mizukage to the Five Kage Summit, called for by the [Fourth Raikage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Raikage "Fourth Raikage"). There he scolds Chōjūrō for his indecisiveness. After travelling for some time, the small group stopped at an inn to eat and rest. After being told that the Fourth Mizukage's teachings are to be forgotten, by both the Fifth Mizukage and Chōjūrō, he yells at the latter for not being able to make up his mind as to what to order for dinner and sends him to get food.

![The Kage's bodyguards on the offensive.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/d/da/Bodyguards_on_the_offensive.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150224122429)

The Kage's bodyguards on the offensive.

After arriving at the summit in the Land of Iron, Ao sits behind the Kiri curtain listening as the Kage have their meeting. When the Fourth Raikage crushes the table in a fit of anger, Ao, along with the other attendants, move to protect their respective Kage. The commotion ends and the attendants return to their seats. Later while the Kage are discussing the possibility of making [Danzō Shimura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Danz%C5%8D_Shimura "Danzō Shimura") the head of the alliance, Ao notices his chakra with his Byakugan; he then points out that Danzō confiscated his [Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sharingan "Sharingan") from Shisui Uchiha, someone he'd fought in the past. Before he can prove the accusation, Zetsu appears and Ao moves to defend the Mizukage. After the Raikage seemingly kills white Zetsu, he tells Ao to watch Danzō. When Danzō states that he cannot use the mind controlling technique that many times in one day, Ao says that he is unsure if he's lying and that he can no longer trust Danzō.

When [Fū Yamanaka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/F%C5%AB_Yamanaka "Fū Yamanaka") and [Torune](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Torune "Torune") try to go and battle Sasuke, Ao tells them to stay right there as the Raikage told him to watch over them and when they get into battle position Danzō tells them to stop. He later demands that Danzō and Fū stop whispering, revealing that he's also a sensor and knows that they have been located. When Sasuke finally reaches the meeting room, Ao is the first to notice his presence. When Sasuke is attacked, Ao and Karin simultaneously notice that Danzō is attempting to escape. Ao asks permission to follow, and is told not to follow him too far. He begins his pursuit after telling the Mizukage he would leave the Akatsuki (Sasuke and his comrades) to her. Sasuke attempts to follow with Karin in tow, but is promptly stopped by the Mizukage.

![Ao caught in Fū's trap.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/87/Ao_trapped1.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160227003008)

Ao caught in Fū's trap.

On his way, Ao is also trapped by Zetsu's [Spore Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Spore_Technique "Spore Technique"), but he manages to get free from it; only to be suddenly trapped by Fū's [Mind Puppet Switch Cursed Seal Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_Puppet_Switch_Cursed_Seal_Technique "Mind Puppet Switch Cursed Seal Technique"). Fū, following Danzō's orders, tries to retrieve the Byakugan. However, Ao tells Fū that there is a special seal in his eyes, making it impossible for enemies to try to take his eye. As Fū decided to improvise by making Ao decapitate himself, Ao warned Fū to time it carefully as too soon would allow Ao to dodge and too late would take Fū down as well. Before the true outcome could happen, the Mizukage and Chōjūrō arrive and save Ao. Fū, still in control of Ao, tried to trick the Mizukage into removing the seal on his eye, but she saw through Fū's deception when he acted kindly to Chōjūrō's insult. The Mizukage then punches Ao in the face for calling her an old hag, unaware that Fū already ended the technique.

As they are arriving back in Kirigakure, the three are informed that the [Water Daimyō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water_Daimy%C5%8D "Water Daimyō") had approved the alliance. Ao is later relieved that former Kirigakure shinobi [Kisame Hoshigaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kisame_Hoshigaki "Kisame Hoshigaki") had fallen to the hands of [Killer B](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Killer_B "Killer B"), not having to worry about any future conflicts. As a sign of good luck, he insists that once they settle down that he treat Chōjūrō to a meal before the upcoming war. When he is rejected, he chides him for his dislike of oily foods and to keep it up if he wanted to "fail at the crucial moment". He is later seen ordering Kirigakure shinobi to gather all ninja [tools](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tools "Tools") currently available to them in preparation for the war.

### Fourth Shinobi World War: Confrontation

![Ao and Inoichi creating a Sensing Barrier.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/b3/Sensing_System_Barrier_erection.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160307033820)

Ao and Inoichi creating a Sensing Barrier.

Ao was appointed captain of the [Sensor Division](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sensor_Division "Sensor Division") of the Allied Shinobi Forces. When he met with his division, he told a perturbed [C](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/C "C") who was participating in war for the first time in his life, that a great deal depended on how well they did their job as they were the eyes of the Alliance. Ao later assisted Inoichi in creating the [barrier](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sensing_Barrier "Sensing Barrier") which the Sensor Division would be using. When the war officially breaks out, he notes that if it had not been for [Muta Aburame](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Muta_Aburame "Muta Aburame") 's information they would have had no idea that the enemy were travelling underground. He then tells his division members to shift the barrier downwards to pick up on the enemy's exact location.

Upon hearing of the large amount of enemy forces heading towards the [First Division](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/First_Division "First Division"), Ao uses his sensory abilities to hone in on the revived shinobi on the battlefield while the others maintaining the barrier focused on the large scale sensing. Ao discovered that they were many reincarnated shinobi amongst the troop all of which were infamously strong during their lifetimes. He then reports that there are two persons on the battlefield with immense chakra reserves tinged with the chakra of the [Nine-Tailed Demon Fox](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tailed_Demon_Fox "Nine-Tailed Demon Fox"). They were later identified as Kumogakure's [Gold and Silver Brothers](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_and_Silver_Brothers "Gold and Silver Brothers").

Later after the [Demonic Statue of the Outer Path](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Statue_of_the_Outer_Path "Demonic Statue of the Outer Path") is summoned to the battlefield, Ao senses it and identifies it as a "gargantuan mass of chakra". He wonders to himself what they were up against. As night falls and the Alliance is thrown into turmoil from the reports of spies who had entered their ranks and were murdering the shinobi forces, Shikaku surmises that the enemy might be using some sort of illusion on the shinobi to cause them to act this way, Ao states that [Itachi Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Itachi_Uchiha "Itachi Uchiha") was the only person with the kind of prowess to even take control of someone outside the range of their sensors, but also states that even for him it'd be impossible to be controlling so many persons at once. He then looks to Shikaku for orders telling them that if they didn't come up with something they'd end up having their own shinobi killing each other.

### Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax

Main article: [Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War:_Climax "Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax") Though after hearing the progress of the battles from Inoichi leads to some celebration. After a tremor is felt at headquarters, Ao reports that a huge number of shinobi from the Fourth Division had been killed all at once.

Reporting on the battle between [Tobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobi "Tobi"), [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki") and [Killer B](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Killer_B "Killer B"), Ao tells headquarters that B had seemingly transformed into his full Eight-Tails form but was still being pressured. As the Nine-Tails' chakra is released, Ao and the other sensors manning the barrier comment on the sheer power. Thinking to himself, he states that it felt somewhat different because there was something else that he could feel. He then reports to Shikaku the situation on the battlefield, telling him that the four men there were actually beating the " [masked Madara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Masked_Madara "Masked Madara") ". Later, when the Sensing Water Sphere in HQ became distorted, Ao hastily followed through with Shikaku's order to check out the situation.

In shock of the still-displaced [Sensing Water Sphere](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sensing_Water_Sphere "Sensing Water Sphere"), he alerts everyone in [HQ](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/HQ "HQ") to the fact that the [Ten-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails "Ten-Tails") had changed its form another time, and then wondered why the beast was firing off [Tailed Beast Balls](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Ball "Tailed Beast Ball") in far-off directions. Realising the direction of the fourth attack, Ao confirmed Shikaku's suspicions that the attack was headed towards the HQ. With this, Ao and the others resolved to perform the duties they were assigned until the end, and were able to relay one last strategy before the attack reached HQ.

## Blank Period

Ao managed to be the sole survivor the Ten-Tails' devastating attack on the Allied Shinobi Forces’ HQ though it left him severely wounded. In the anime, Ao was discovered buried under debris by the [Logistical Support and Medical Division](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Logistical_Support_and_Medical_Division "Logistical Support and Medical Division"). Afterwards, he would be taken to the [Konoha Hospital](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Hospital "Konoha Hospital") for treatment, where he would spend many years in a coma.[^17]

![Katasuke showing Ao's prosthetics.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/33/Katasuke_showing_Ao%27s_prosthetics.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20201228075736)

Katasuke showing Ao's prosthetics.

Due to his injuries, Ao became a patient of [Katasuke Tōno](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Katasuke_T%C5%8Dno "Katasuke Tōno"), who used Scientific Ninja Tools to rehabilitate him. While equipped with an advanced prosthetic left leg and arm, Katasuke credited Ao's speedy recovery to his own strong conviction, ultimately allowing Ao to resume living a normal life, though he chose not to return to Kirigakure, nor to keep up to date with its affairs. In the anime, while feeling that his life no longer had meaning, Ao was approached by [Victor](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Victor "Victor") who recruited him to [Kara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kara "Kara") as an Outer. To which, Ao's body was further modified and upgraded with various weapons, giving the retired shinobi a new purpose. Ao would come to work directly under Victor after joining the organisation.

### Ao Arc

Main article: [Ao Arc](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ao_Arc "Ao Arc")

![Ao and Mugino talk at the memorial.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/7/76/Ao_and_Mugino_talk.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20201228075006)

Ao and Mugino talk at the memorial.

In the anime, Ao came to Konoha, looking for Katasuke to aid in some maintenance for his replacement limbs. He met [Mugino](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mugino "Mugino"), who revealed that the doctor was attending the memorial service for the fallen people of the Fourth Shinobi World War. Ao joined the man to the event, only to arrive late as everyone was already leaving. There, Ao voiced his relief to finally pay his respect to his fallen allies. Upon leaving, he met [Shikamaru Nara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikamaru_Nara "Shikamaru Nara") and [Ino Yamanaka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ino_Yamanaka "Ino Yamanaka"). Shikamaru asked the sole survivor of the Allied Shinobi Force's HQ about the true feelings of their fathers before their demise, having longstanding doubts about Shikaku's confidence in his abilities. Ao assured him that Shikaku and Inoichi never had any doubt about their kids' abilities and was proud of them to the end, leaving Shikamaru and Ino truly grateful.

While on board a [Thunder Rail](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Thunder_Rail "Thunder Rail"), Ao encountered Katasuke and his [genin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genin "Genin") [escorts](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Konohamaru "Team Konohamaru"). As Katasuke repaired Ao's prosthetic hand, he told the genin of his days as a shinobi. Questioning [Boruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Boruto_Uzumaki "Boruto Uzumaki") if he still disliked Scientific Ninja Weapons after seeing the good they did in his rehabilitation, Ao grabbed Katasuke's screwdriver to threaten Boruto, and informed him that the technology isn't good or evil, it's how you use it. When the train arrived at Ryūtan City, Ao remained on board while saying his farewells to Boruto. Afterwards, [Koji Kashin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Koji_Kashin "Koji Kashin") approached Ao on the train. In the anime, he revealed to Koji that he went to Konoha recently to make sure Katasuke remembered nothing of his usage of genjutsu on the scientist, ready to kill Katasuke if he did as a liability. Koji informed Ao that the vessel has been lost. Tasking Ao with recovering the vessel, Koji left a crate containing intel and weapons for Ao to use on the mission before departing.

Not receiving an answer, Ao aimed his gun at the group to kill them. Ao then unleashed a barrage of [Fire Release Bullets](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire_Release_Bullet:_%22Blaze%22 "Fire Release Bullet: \"Blaze\"") from his gun. Konohamaru, with help from Katsuke, was able to destroy Ao's gun. As Konohamaru asked if this had anything to do with the organisation [Kara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kara "Kara"), Ao unleashed a hidden weapon from his prosthetic shoulder, blasting Konohamaru. He then attacked Katsuke and stole one of his technique-absorbing gauntlets. After testing it himself on Boruto's Rasengan, Ao concluded that now they had to die to keep the organisation a secret. Mugino then held Ao down long enough to [collapse](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Release:_Rock_Lodging_Destruction "Earth Release: Rock Lodging Destruction") the cave on him and Ao to save his allies. His sacrifice however turned futile as Ao quickly clawed his way out of the rubble, prompting Boruto and his team to retreat, while Ao was committed to making sure not a single one of them survived.

Along the way, he was confronted by Koji, who was displeased by Ao's recent sloppiness. As he questioned Ao's resolve at fighting fellow ninja again, Ao bluntly stated that any compassion he had died a long time ago. Content with the answer, Koji reminded him of his mission to retrieve the target and warned them about [Team Konohamaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Konohamaru "Team Konohamaru") preparing an ambush on him and left. Once Ao found Team Konohamaru, he was attacked by Konohamaru, Mitsuki, and Sarada, who began cornering him with a relentless barrage of ninjutsu assaults. While calmly absorbing them all with the gauntlet, Ao deduced that Boruto was waiting for the right moment to strike at his blindside. Seeing through Boruto's sneak-attack, he blocked Boruto's chakra blade with his prosthetic arm, forcing the boy to drop it. Ao then quickly grabbed it and stabbed Boruto through the chest, gloating at Boruto's failure. Boruto however was revealed to be a shadow clone and grabbed onto Ao's blade-wielding hand, holding him in place long enough for Mitsuki to [restrain](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Soft_Physique_Modification "Soft Physique Modification") Ao's same arm. Quickly, Ao realised that he was tricked as the prototype chakra-blade was quickly draining his chakra from extended usage. Ao managed to break free and was enraged at being played. He was then attacked by Boruto with his Rasengan. Ao attempted to absorb the attack, but Boruto used the other absorbing gauntlet to cancel the two out, leaving Ao open and hit by the Rasengan.

Ao easily repelled their attacks and subdued the only foe still equipped with the advanced gauntlet, knowing that shadow clones cannot replicate tools of such complex nature. This however was a ruse by Boruto, who also knew that fact and gave the clone his gauntlet in the dust cloud, giving him the opening to strike Ao down with his chakra blade (in the anime, using a Rasengan). Defeated, Ao watched as Boruto loomed over him expecting him to finish the battle. Boruto however instead placed a screwdriver next to Ao, encouraging him to fix himself. Ao discouraged Boruto's blind mercy, saying it would kill him. Boruto however insisted that as a former hero of the Kirigakure, he had the potential to do good things with the tools at his disposal. Shocked at Boruto's words of encouragement, Ao noticed Koji approaching. Realising his associate's next move, Ao used his Water Release to push Boruto away from the impending doom of Koji's crushing summoning at the cost of his own life.

## Legacy

In the anime, after Koji's withdrawal from battle, Konohamaru reluctantly gave Ao a proper burial. He did this not to forgive Ao for killing Mugino, but in gratitude for saving Boruto, noting that he died as a hero and shinobi. [Shikamaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikamaru "Shikamaru") was also rather saddened that Ao became a member of Kara.

Ao's defeat helped Konoha gain a better understanding of the threat from Kara, seeing one of their Inners in action. The same battle also gave a better understanding of Boruto's [Kāma](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/K%C4%81ma "Kāma").

Initially Boruto remained somewhat bitter at Koji for killing Ao, recalling said event when Koji saved Boruto from [Konoha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha "Konoha") pursuers.

## Creation and Conception

When designing Ao, [Kishimoto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kishimoto "Kishimoto") wanted to create a character with an air of severity and masculinity while still seeming tranquil. His eye-patch is supposed to convey masculine trendiness, and his haori is to serve a dual purpose in that it should convey his quiet and mature personality while the sleeves were to signify that Ao was not a close-range fighter. Kishimoto also noted that he deliberately made Ao's attire similar to [Haku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Haku "Haku") 's and was even going to go as far as to have him wear a mask, but ultimately decided against it.
