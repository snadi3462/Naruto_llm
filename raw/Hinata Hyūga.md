**Hinata Uzumaki** (うずまきヒナタ, *Uzumaki Hinata*, [née](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/n%C3%A9e "wikipedia:née") **Hyūga** (日向)) is a kunoichi of [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure"). Formerly the heiress of the [Hyūga clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hy%C5%ABga_clan "Hyūga clan"), she lost the position upon being deemed unsuited for the responsibilities of leading the clan. Nonetheless, Hinata [persevered](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nind%C5%8D "Nindō") and from her observation of [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki") especially, Hinata found an example to follow. Through her membership with [Team 8](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_8 "Team 8"), she sought to become strong enough to change herself, if even a little at a time. A few years after the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"), Hinata joined the [Uzumaki Clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uzumaki_Clan "Uzumaki Clan") after marrying Naruto.

## Background

![Hinata meets Naruto.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/c4/Naruto%27s_first_encounter_with_Hinata.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150711120110)

Hinata meets Naruto.

Hinata is the oldest daughter of [Hiashi Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiashi_Hy%C5%ABga "Hiashi Hyūga"). When she was still a child, Hinata was almost kidnapped by the-then [Head Ninja](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Head_Ninja "Head Ninja") of [Kumogakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kumogakure "Kumogakure"), who was visiting Konoha under the guise of signing a peace treaty between their two villages. Hiashi killed him and saved Hinata, setting in motion the events that would become known as the " [Hyūga Affair](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hy%C5%ABga_Affair "Hyūga Affair") ".[^9]

On the day of her enrolment in Konoha's [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy"),[^10] Hinata was picked on by three bullies because of her [unusual eyes](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Byakugan "Byakugan"). Naruto, despite not knowing her, immediately came to her defence, but he was outnumbered and knocked unconscious, and the bullies damaged the red scarf he was wearing. When he regained consciousness, Hinata thanked him for helping her and returned his scarf to him, but he let her keep it. Hinata started watching Naruto from that point onwards, and by doing so saw Naruto's desire to gain attention and his struggle to exceed the extremely low expectations others had for him. Despite this, Naruto never lost faith in himself or his dream to become the [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage"). Hinata was inspired by this and started emulating Naruto, adopting his refusal to never give up as a means of earning her father's approval. In the anime, Hinata's growing affections for Naruto were noticed by the Hyūga's elders, who warned her to stay away from him.[^11]

Because Hiashi was the head of the Hyūga clan, Hinata was raised to be its heiress and her family expected great things from her. But even at a young age, her father came to believe that Hinata was not suited for the role of heiress, as she struggled under his gruesome training regime, on top of him also believing that her timid personality prevented her from truly excelling. When Hinata's younger sister [Hanabi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hanabi "Hanabi") started displaying superior talent, Hiashi began to reconsider Hinata's selection as heiress. In the anime, Hinata and Hanabi were pitted against each other to determine who would one day lead the Hyūga. Because Hinata was unwilling to harm Hanabi - a hesitation Hanabi did not share - she was defeated, disappointing her father.[^12] Hinata was then disinherited and turned over to the care of [Kurenai Yūhi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kurenai_Y%C5%ABhi "Kurenai Yūhi").[^13]

## Personality

![Hinata's anxiety kicks in.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/5b/Hinata%27s_panic.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20251010210451)

Hinata's anxiety kicks in.

As a child, Hinata was soft-spoken and polite, always addressing people with proper [honourifics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_honorifics "wikipedia:Japanese honorifics"). She is kind, always thinking of others more than for herself, caring for their feelings and well-being. She doesn't like being confrontational for any reason. This led to her being meek or timid to others, as her overwhelming kindness can render her unable to respond or act for fear of offending somebody.[^14] Her father's constant push to alter this personality trait when she was younger only made it worse, eroding Hinata's self-confidence and making her even more bashful because she gained so little faith in herself and opinions.[^3] Despite being disinherited, Hinata loves her family very much, as shown when she "agreed" to marry Toneri in order to rescue Hanabi even though she knew it meant breaking Naruto's heart and the possibility she and Toneri actually would get married.

Hinata's outlook sees a shift after her appointment to Team 8. Whereas her father and much of the Hyūga clan had decided she was a lost cause who would never amount to anything,[^15] her team considers her a valuable member with her special strong points. Originally, she would over-rely on her teammates, looking to them in times of difficulty to save her from situations that make her uncomfortable. It is for her teammates - among other reasons - that Hinata starts seeking to change herself, as she wishes to help the team rather than burden it.[^16]

![Hinata dreams of being with Naruto.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/7/70/Hinata%27s_dream.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160303115312)

Hinata dreams of being with Naruto.

Hinata's primary motivation is Naruto Uzumaki, with whom she has been fascinated since meeting him. He initially gained her attention because of his kindness to her and outspoken nature, and then kept her attention because of similarities that Hinata perceived to herself: Naruto had a painful childhood without the love of parents, a fate worse than the mere difficulty she had with her own father, and he craves the attention and acknowledgement of anybody, just as Hinata wants to please her father.[^17] But whereas Hinata lost faith in herself, Naruto always believed he can accomplish any given task if he tries hard enough. She admires him deeply for this courage and over time fell deeply in love with him, which manifests as increased shyness and speechlessness when around him, something that became apparent to everyone, apart from ironically Naruto himself. Hinata strives to prove herself to others, but mostly to gain the attention and worthiness of Naruto. She emulates him to this purpose, adopting his [nindō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nind%C5%8D "Nindō") of never giving up to improve herself. To that end, she stops running from confrontation, instead doing all that she can to face it, even in the face of failure to keep trying, and willing to risk her life for her loved ones and village.[^18] By the end of Part I, her clan's faith in her is restored and strengthened.[^19]

![Hinata's intimidating demeanour.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/d/de/Scary_Hinata.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20170607101125)

Hinata's intimidating demeanour.

Throughout the years, Naruto remains her primary motivation; she willingly faced Pain to protect Naruto, despite knowing she has no chance of winning.[^20] She even defends Naruto from himself when he's in doubt, repeating to him his refusal to never give up.[^21] [Two years](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/The_Last:_Naruto_the_Movie "The Last: Naruto the Movie") after the war, upon achieving her [greatest dream](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Infinite_Tsukuyomi#Known_Dreams "Infinite Tsukuyomi") of having her love reciprocated by Naruto to form a deep and committed relationship together, she became more confident in herself and expressive about her feelings. In the anime, she is able to laugh openly because of it.[^22] After marrying Naruto and he becomes Hokage, Hinata remains supportive of him and his increased responsibilities, even though she yearns for him to be home more often. Valuing her family so much, Hinata turned down becoming a jōnin just so she could focus more time caring for her family. As a mother and wife, she has become a master homemaker, keeping the family well-structured yet well-adjusted and happy. While very loving and supportive of her family, she is equally strict and assertive. Her newfound structured nature made her very intimidating and more easily angered when her family acts up, easily keeping them in line and swiftly punishing them for their misconduct.[^23] [^8] She is also insightful and wise with her words, realising that her children, particularly Boruto, take after Naruto in the desire to prove themselves to others and that she must believe in them to do things on their own.[^24]

Hinata is a slender girl of fair-complexion and average height who, as a Hyūga, her most distinguishing trait is the [Byakugan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Byakugan "Byakugan"), giving her featureless white eyes (lavender in the anime). She is noted to be beautiful by Naruto and [Toneri Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Toneri_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Toneri Ōtsutsuki").[^10] She has blue-black hair that she keeps in a bob-cut of different lengths: in Part I, her hair is cut just above her forehead akin to a bowl-cut with chin-length strands framing her face; in Part II, her hair reaches her lower back and the framing strands reach shoulder-length; in *The Last: Naruto the Movie*, her hair extends beyond her hips and she would at times tie it into a ponytail. By the time Boruto entered the Academy, she cut it into a bob-style reaching her shoulders.[^25]

In Part I, Hinata usually wears a cream-coloured hooded-jacket with a fire symbol on the upper sleeves and fur around the cuffs and hem. She also wears navy blue pants and keeps her [forehead protector](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forehead_protector "Forehead protector") loosely around her neck. In Part II, she dons a lavender and cream hooded-jacket that shows no skin for a cute and frumpy look with lavender cuffs over mesh armour.[^26] She also wears navy blue pants, black, low-heeled sandals, and changes the cloth of her forehead protector from blue to black. Hinata switches to the standard Konoha infantry [flak jacket](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flak_jacket "Flak jacket") and assorted clothing during the Fourth Shinobi World War, though she does not appear to wear a forehead protector.

Two years after the war, Hinata's mission attire is a form-fitting light lavender, sleeveless kimono-style blouse with vertical lines, tied with a dark purple obi around her waist and short dark navy shorts. She also wore thigh-high stockings with black, open-toed boots. She does not wear her forehead protector at all. Her outfits become more varied after that, changing to match the occasion or simply being more casual than her prior garb. During Hinata's and Toneri's wedding, she wore a black long and sleeveless dress with a high collar, a long grey sash around her waist and matching long gloves. She also wore short puffy pants with black lace socks and sandals. On her head was a black turban with a black and light yellow veil over and yellow crescent moon earrings. ## Abilities

![Hinata repelling a Ten-Tails mini-clone with the Eight Trigrams Sixty-Four Palms.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/92/Hinata_fights_a_Ten-Tails%27_replica.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140821154835)

Hinata repelling a Ten-Tails mini-clone with the Eight Trigrams Sixty-Four Palms.

Originally, Hinata's meek nature hindered her performance, to which she was deemed too weak and unworthy for the role of Hyūga Clan heir.[^27] After committing herself to getting stronger in her training for Naruto, she began receiving consistent praise from her team and clan for her improving skills and strengthening character.[^28] [^20] [^18] Two years after the war, Hinata, having been recognised by [Hamura Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hamura_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Hamura Ōtsutsuki") as the Byakugan Princess, proves to be crucial in the destruction of the [Tenseigan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tenseigan_\(Energy_Vessel\) "Tenseigan (Energy Vessel)"), even though she herself can't destroy it.[^7]

Over a decade later, Hinata's prowess continued to improve, reaching [Jōnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%8Dnin "Jōnin") -level prowess despite declining the promotion to it. Likewise, while unprepared and reluctant to fight Hanabi, an active jōnin and the next Hyūga Clan leader, Hinata proved was able to decisively defeat her sister in a match.[^29]

### Chakra Prowess and Control

![Hinata's Gentle Step: Twin Lion Fist using Hamura's chakra.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/7/74/Twin_Lions_Crumbling_Attack.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150711131915)

Hinata's Gentle Step: Twin Lion Fist using Hamura's chakra.

As a member of the Hyūga Clan, Hinata possesses an innate ability to expel chakra from any of the 361 [tenketsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tenketsu "Tenketsu") in her body.<sup>[<span title="This information is unverifiable or unsourced and may be false.">citation needed</span>]</sup> Likewise, from her special clan training, Hinata has advanced control of her chakra. In the anime, Hinata's additional training during Part I allowed her to free herself from constraints [^30] and manipulate nearby water sources, turning them into [projectiles](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water_Needle "Water Needle").[^31] By Part II of the anime, Hinata's control proved on par with a [medical-nin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Medical-nin "Medical-nin") 's, for which reason she is assigned to help perform the [Four-Corner Sealing Barrier](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Four-Corner_Sealing_Barrier "Four-Corner Sealing Barrier").

When she met Hamura, Hinata inherited some of his [chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra "Chakra") as a member of the Hyūga Clan's main house. It is the strength and purity of her chakra that attracts [Toneri Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Toneri_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Toneri Ōtsutsuki"). It is also because she is a member of the Hyūga clan's main house and direct bloodline from Hamura that she can touch and find the Tenseigan, something that someone without both could not do. However, she couldn't destroy it by herself, so she instead combined her efforts with Naruto to accomplish such. Hinata's chakra levels increased tremendously after meeting Hamura, able to replenish Naruto's drained chakra reserves in an instant after Toneri stole his chakra, something that [Sakura Haruno](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sakura_Haruno "Sakura Haruno") required three days to do previously.

### Byakugan

![Hinata's Byakugan.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/a8/Hinata%27s_byakugan.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160901044559)

Hinata's Byakugan.

As a Hyūga, Hinata possesses the Byakugan, a [dōjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/D%C5%8Djutsu "Dōjutsu") that grants her penetrative vision in a near-360° around herself, with a small blind spot at the back of the neck above the first [thoracic vertebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/thoracic_vertebrae "wikipedia:thoracic vertebrae").[^32] With it, she is able to assist others frequently in tracking, scouting the area for their target(s); by focusing her vision in a single direction, Hinata can see across vast distances, able to see at least 1 kilometre during her first Chūnin Exam, and reaching as far away as 20 kilometres by the battle against Toneri. Hinata's Byakugan grants her attacks pinpoint accuracy, which she can share with others to also improve their own.[^33] She can also use it in order to identify [genjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu "Genjutsu"), searching for the caster in the nearby area.[^34] In the anime, Hinata can use [infrared](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/infrared "wikipedia:infrared") vision and magnify in to see tiny targets.[^35] By adulthood, the chakra sensitivity provided by the Byakugan allows her to pick up on the slightest presence of chakra.[^36]

### Physical Prowess

As is traditional in her clan, Hinata has been extensively trained in the [Gentle Fist](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gentle_Fist "Gentle Fist") fighting style. In unison with her Byakugan, Hinata can attack accurately target an individuals' [chakra pathway systems](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_pathway_system "Chakra pathway system") and the 361 tenketsu that run along it, constricting or cutting off their chakra flow from only minimal contact.[^15] Although this enables Hinata to attack an opponent's internal organs, she can also use it in a supplementary function, such as by setting dislocated shoulders back in place.[^37] Even by Hyūga standards, Hinata has exceptional agility and coordination in her movements, able to seamlessly maneuver around obstacles with no wasted effort or loss of balance.[^38]

![Hinata and Neji combining their Eight Trigrams Vacuum Palms.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/ae/Hakke_K%C5%ABhekish%C5%8D.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150221070203)

Hinata and Neji combining their Eight Trigrams Vacuum Palms.

While Hinata hadn't yet mastered her clan's more advanced techniques in Part I, in the anime, she created the [Protecting Eight Trigrams Sixty-Four Palms](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Protecting_Eight_Trigrams_Sixty-Four_Palms "Protecting Eight Trigrams Sixty-Four Palms") as an alternative, which effectively protects her and attacks anything within range with sharp streams of chakra emitted from her palms.[^39] By Part II, she had grown accustomed to more of her clan's techniques. This included the [Gentle Step Twin Lion Fists](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gentle_Step_Twin_Lion_Fists "Gentle Step Twin Lion Fists"), a technique that drains the target of their chakra where struck. Hinata can use the [Eight Trigrams Vacuum Palm](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight_Trigrams_Vacuum_Palm "Eight Trigrams Vacuum Palm") to attack targets outside her physical range or deflect incoming attacks,[^40] which increases in power if used in [conjunction with others](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight_Trigrams_Vacuum_Wall_Palm "Eight Trigrams Vacuum Wall Palm").[^41] She also mastered the [Eight Trigrams Sixty-Four Palms](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight_Trigrams_Sixty-Four_Palms "Eight Trigrams Sixty-Four Palms") in the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War").[^42] Two years later, despite her chakra being suppressed and unable to access her Byakugan, she was able to physically fend of many of Toneri's puppets be he needed to intervene. By adulthood, she became well-known for her mastery of the Gentle Fist,[^43] her knowledge and understanding able to instantly determine the nature and extent of one's damage caused by it.[^44] As an adult, she could even knock out a target with a single strike to their tenketsu.[^45] [^46]

### Other skills

While Hinata doesn't usually use [ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ninjutsu "Ninjutsu"), by Part II, her [nature transformations](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nature_transformation "Nature transformation") included [Fire](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire "Fire") and [Lightning Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_Release "Lightning Release").[^4] In the anime, she also demonstrated proficiency in [medical ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Medical_ninjutsu "Medical ninjutsu"), able to use the [Mystical Palm Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mystical_Palm_Technique "Mystical Palm Technique") to heal the wounded.[^47] Hinata is also proven to be a very talented knitter and cook.

## Part I

### Prologue — Land of Waves

Main article: [Prologue — Land of Waves](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Prologue_%E2%80%94_Land_of_Waves "Prologue — Land of Waves") In the anime, while the graduates of her [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy") class are being assigned to teams, Hinata hopes that she will be on the same team as [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki"). To her disappointment, she is instead added to [Kurenai Yūhi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kurenai_Y%C5%ABhi "Kurenai Yūhi") 's [Team 8](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_8 "Team 8"), along with [Kiba Inuzuka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kiba_Inuzuka "Kiba Inuzuka") and [Shino Aburame](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shino_Aburame "Shino Aburame"). Kurenai reports this to Hinata's father, [Hiashi Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiashi_Hy%C5%ABga "Hiashi Hyūga"), but he is unconcerned and in fact abandons Hinata to Kurenai's care, feeling she is unworthy of the [Hyūga clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hy%C5%ABga_clan "Hyūga clan").[^15]

### Chūnin Exams

Main article: [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams_\(Arc\) "Chūnin Exams (Arc)")

![Hinata lets Naruto copy her test.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/86/Naruto_and_hinata_at_first_exam.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150815204913)

Hinata lets Naruto copy her test.

Despite how recently they graduated from the Academy, Kurenai enters Team 8 in the [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams") being held in [Konoha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha "Konoha"); Hinata agrees to participate because she wants to change herself. For the exams' first stage, Hinata is seated next to Naruto as they take a written test. The objective in the first stage is to cheat off of other examinee's paper without getting caught, but Naruto doesn't realise this and agonises about potentially failing. Seeing this, Hinata offers to let him copy off of her paper. He suspects she's trying to trick him, but she assures him that she only wants to be helpful. Although he considers taking her up on her offer, Naruto ultimately declines, fearing that he'll get her disqualified if they're caught and claiming that, besides, he isn't the type to cheat.

![Team 8 paralyzed with fear from Gaara's bloodlust.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/8b/Team_8_hides_from_gaara.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250815195030)

Team 8 paralyzed with fear from Gaara's bloodlust.

Although Naruto is given the opportunity to forfeit, he refuses to do so, and for his determination, he passes the first stage along with Hinata and their teams. For the second stage, participants enter the [Forest of Death](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forest_of_Death "Forest of Death") with the objective of obtaining a set of two scrolls, one of which they are given at the start. Team 8 manages to collect their second scroll right after the second stage has started, and so they head for the centre of the forest where they're supposed to turn in the scrolls. Along the way, Kiba's partner, [Akamaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akamaru "Akamaru"), senses the [Three Sand Siblings](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Three_Sand_Siblings "Three Sand Siblings") ' fight with [Team Shigure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Shigure "Team Shigure"). Team 8 watches the fight from the cover of some nearby bushes and is horrified when [Gaara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gaara "Gaara") mercilessly kills all of Team Shigure. After they're certain Gaara won't kill them too, they proceed to the centre of the forest.

After passing the second stage, the remaining participants take part in one-on-one preliminary matches. During Naruto's fight with Kiba, Hinata's loyalties are torn; she wants to cheer for Naruto, but fears that would be rude to Kiba. When Naruto wins the fight, Hinata gives both him and Kiba some of the [Hyūga Clan Secret Ointment](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hy%C5%ABga_Clan_Secret_Ointment "Hyūga Clan Secret Ointment") so that their wounds will heal faster. Neji, like Kiba, tries to convince Hinata to forfeit, telling her that she's too meek and insecure to be a ninja. She begins to offer arguments in her defence, but Neji persists, claiming that she is fated to be a failure, a fact that he believes she's aware of due to her frightened reaction to what he says.

Although her attacks are ineffective against him, Hinata continues fighting, in the process enduring mounting internal trauma from Neji's counterattacks. Neji repeatedly tells her to forfeit, but Hinata refuses, unwilling to back down both as a matter of principle and because Naruto continues to cheer for her.

### In Naruto's Footsteps: The Friends' Paths

After the initial testing period is over, they are given a bonus question: each team must unanimously select one of their members to disqualify from the rest of the exams. Team 8 selects nobody, which is the correct answer and which qualifies them for the next phase.[^49]

Ultimately, [Team 8](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_8 "Team 8") were among the first 30 teams to make it. The following night, as all the genin were ready to eat dinner, everyone grew nervous about the overly-sweaty [Burami](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Burami "Burami") ruining the food, which caused the genin to quickly try to stop him, resulting in [Neji](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Neji "Neji") using the [Eight Trigrams Palms Revolving Heaven](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight_Trigrams_Palms_Revolving_Heaven "Eight Trigrams Palms Revolving Heaven") and accidentally destroying the food. ![Hinata faces off against Kazami.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/d/dd/Flag_Current_1.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150305164143)

They regroup and then reengage battle against Team Kazami, with Kiba ultimately defeating them. With their own scroll back and with Team Kazami's scroll now in their possession, Team 8 heads to the centre of the desert to turn in the scrolls.[^52]

Team 8 eventually gets lost in a sandstorm along the way.[^53] They are rescued by one of the exam's proctors, who brings them to where all of the other examinees are being assembled. They are informed that the Chūnin Exams have been cancelled; reports on the participants' performances will be sent back to their villages, leaving their promotion up to their superiors. Although the Zetsus suffer heavy casualties, their numbers are too great and the fight becomes one of attrition. After a day of fighting, Neji collapses from exhaustion, at which point Hinata takes responsibility for monitoring the Second Division's perimeter with her Byakugan. Shino encourages her not to over-exert herself, but she assures him that she doesn't mind since she's fighting to keep Naruto safe.

They discover several White Zetsus within Allied territory, and in the fight to destroy them Hinata is knocked unconscious. One Zetsu [impersonates her](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Substitute_Technique "Substitute Technique") and successfully pretends to be her for a time using its knowledge of her feelings for Naruto, but Kiba and Shino eventually discover it and, after defeating it, rescue Hinata.[^56] On the second day of the war, Hinata helps members of the Second Division escape the area of Neji's fight with the [reincarnated](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Impure_World_Reincarnation "Impure World Reincarnation") [Kidōmaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kid%C5%8Dmaru "Kidōmaru"). She reports this to [Inoichi Yamanaka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Inoichi_Yamanaka "Inoichi Yamanaka"), who is able to use the information to help Naruto save them. Hinata tearfully hugs Neji when he wakes up.[^57]

Although Neji is suspicious that Naruto may also be a White Zetsu, Hinata insists it's the real Naruto, which she can tell by looking in his eyes. Hinata apologises to Naruto for the suspicion he's receiving and regrets that he is still needing to save her when she's the one trying to fight for him. Naruto reassures her that she's strong enough to look after herself; he's merely returning the favour for all the times in the past she's helped him. She silently thanks him as he leaves.

### Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax

Main article: [Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War:_Climax "Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax")

![Hinata preparing to sacrifice her life to shield Naruto.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/97/Hinata_Shielding_Naruto.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20180417164610)

Hinata and the rest of the Allied Shinobi Forces are sent to assist Naruto in his battle with the [Ten-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails "Ten-Tails"). Recognising how crucial Naruto is to the war's victory, Hinata, Neji, and Hiashi join forces to block attacks directed at him. Neji and Hiashi quickly become preoccupied protecting other Allies from the Ten-Tails too, leaving only Hinata to defend Naruto. The Ten-Tails fires several volleys of [wood](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood "Wood") projectiles at him, which Hinata uses her body to defend him from. Neji in turn uses his body to defend her and is mortally wounded because of it. Watching Hinata crying for him as he dies, Neji observes that Hinata was willing to die for Naruto, which he uses as evidence that Naruto is responsible for more lives than his own. He then dies from his injuries.

![Naruto shares Kurama's chakra with Hinata.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/be/Naruto_gives_Hinata_power.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140605131235)

Naruto shares Kurama's chakra with Hinata.

Naruto is disheartened by the deaths of Neji and others in the Alliance. When he starts to show signs of giving up, Hinata brings him back senses by reminding him of his vow to not let any of his friends die. Although Neji may be dead, he will live on so long as others continue the fight that he gave his life for. Therefore, Naruto must stay true to the creed he has been using for so long and that she has long since adopted for herself: to not give up. Naruto's resolve is renewed and he thanks her for standing by his side. He then takes her hand and transfers some chakra to her, giving her a [version 1](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Version_1 "Version 1") -like shroud that protects her and augments her attacks against the Ten-Tails.

As Naruto leads the Alliance's attacks against the Ten-Tails, his arm becomes dislocated. Hinata sets it back in place, allowing him to remove the Ten-Tails from the control of [Madara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Madara "Madara") and [Obito Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha "Obito Uchiha"). The extended fighting eventually starts to tire Naruto out, so Hinata rallies a defence around him while Sakura Haruno heals him. She later joins Team 8's attack on the [Ten-Tails' clones](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails_Fission "Ten-Tails Fission"): she starts performing the [Eight Trigrams Sixty-Four Palms](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight_Trigrams_Sixty-Four_Palms "Eight Trigrams Sixty-Four Palms") on a [Ten-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails "Ten-Tails") [mini-clone](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails_Fission "Ten-Tails Fission"), but stumbles midway and fears she may not be able to complete it. By thinking of Neji and Naruto, she finds the resolve to follow through to its end and defeats the clone. She then prepares to use her [Gentle Step Twin Lion Fists](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gentle_Step_Twin_Lion_Fists "Gentle Step Twin Lion Fists").

In later instalments of the [*Clash of Ninja* series](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Clash_of_Ninja_\(series\) "Clash of Ninja (series)"), Hinata is playable in an "awakened" form. In this form, her clothes are similar to what she wore during the [Bikōchū Search Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Bik%C5%8Dch%C5%AB_Search_Mission "Bikōchū Search Mission"), though the form itself is not based on the events of that arc. Awakened Hinata has a personality similar to Neji's during the Chūnin Exams, arrogantly challenging opponents and boasting about the power of the main branch of the Hyūga clan. While in this form, she is also able to use techniques such as [Eight Trigrams Sixty-Four Palms](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight_Trigrams_Sixty-Four_Palms "Eight Trigrams Sixty-Four Palms") and [Eight Trigrams Palms Revolving Heaven](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight_Trigrams_Palms_Revolving_Heaven "Eight Trigrams Palms Revolving Heaven"). Most *Clash of Ninja* games require that this awakened Hinata be selected separately, but in *[Clash of Ninja Revolution 3](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Clash_of_Ninja_Revolution_3 "Clash of Ninja Revolution 3")*, normal Hinata can transform into her in the middle of battle by activating her Byakugan. A similar form appears in *[Ultimate Ninja 2](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ultimate_Ninja_2 "Ultimate Ninja 2")*, dubbed "Byakugan Hinata", with the main difference being she wears the traditional Hyūga attire.

In several of the *Ultimate Ninja Storm* games, the team combination of Hinata and Naruto is titled "Secret Lovers" or "Joined Hands".

Hinata is featured in the online game, *[Fortnite](https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortnite)*, as a playable skin, and she can be used with her Part II look as well as her new era look. Her Part II skin is labelled as "Hinata Hyuga", whereas her new era skin is labelled as "Hinata Uzumaki".

## Trivia

| ![Springtime of Youth](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/39/Youth_symbol.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/40?cb=20130120020002)  Springtime of Youth | The [*Springtime of Youth* wiki](https://springtimeofyouth.fandom.com/wiki/ "w:c:springtimeofyouth") has an article about this topic:  **[Hinata Hyūga](https://springtimeofyouth.fandom.com/wiki/Hinata_Hy%C5%ABga "w:c:springtimeofyouth:Hinata Hyūga")** |
| --- | --- |

- The name "Hinata" means "place in the sun". It can be written with the same kanji as her family name "Hyūga" (日向).
- In the *[Live Spectacle Naruto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Live_Spectacle_Naruto "Live Spectacle Naruto")*, the role of Hinata was played by Saki Takahashi.
- Hinata placed well in the [*Naruto* character popularity polls](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Character_Popularity_Polls "Naruto Character Popularity Polls"), reaching an all-time high of sixth most popular.
- According to the databook(s):
	- Hinata's hobby is [pressing flowers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pressed_flower_craft "wikipedia:Pressed flower craft").
		- Hinata wishes for a rematch with her cousin [Neji](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Neji "Neji"), and for a match with her father, [Hiashi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hiashi "Hiashi").
		- Hinata's favourite foods are [zenzai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_bean_soup "wikipedia:Red bean soup") and [cinnamon rolls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/cinnamon_roll "wikipedia:cinnamon roll"), while her least favourite foods are dishes such as [crabs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crab#Culinary_use "wikipedia:Crab") and [shrimp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shrimp#Human_consumption "wikipedia:Shrimp").
		- Hinata has completed 33 official missions in total: 10 D-rank, 14 C-rank, 8 B-rank, 1 A-rank, 0 S-rank.
		- Hinata's favourite phrase is "self-confidence" (自信, *jishin*).
		- Hinata's name is referred as a [pure white lily](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lilium_candidum "wikipedia:Lilium candidum"),[^4] the same flowers that were put on Neji's grave.[^59]
- During a 2010 interview, Kishimoto stated that many who work with him have told him Hinata is more of a heroine than Sakura.[^60]
	- In the [omake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Omake#Who's_the_Heroine? "Omake") for [*Naruto: Shippūden* episode 165](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tails,_Captured! "Nine-Tails, Captured!"), Sakura accuses Hinata of trying to become the main heroine of the series because of her popularity, much to Hinata's confusion.
		- In a series of interviews from 2015,[^61] Kishimoto revealed he had planned on Naruto and Hinata getting together since the middle of the manga, even commenting that Naruto and Hinata were meant to be since she acknowledged him before even [Iruka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iruka "Iruka"). Not only that, Kishimoto confirmed that Hinata was his favourite female character during an interview with Viz in October 2015 due to her kindness.[^62]
- According to [Hyō no Sho](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hy%C5%8D_no_Sho "Hyō no Sho"), while in the [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy"), Hinata had an A in taijutsu, cooperation, and classroom attitude, a B in ninjutsu and genjutsu, and an F in positivity.
- In the anime, Hinata holds the title of "Legendary Queen of Gluttony" (伝説の大食い女王, *Densetsu no Ōgui Joō*) at Ichiraku Ramen, having finished a record of 46 bowls of ramen in a single sitting.[^63]
- Studio Pierrot's Settei's sheets show that Hinata was 120 cm when Naruto saved her from three bullies.
