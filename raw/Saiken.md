**Saiken** (犀犬), more commonly known as the **Six-Tails** (六尾, *Rokubi*), is one of the nine [tailed beasts](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_beasts "Tailed beasts"). It was last sealed within [Utakata](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Utakata "Utakata") of [Kirigakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kirigakure "Kirigakure").

## Background

![The young tailed beasts with their creator Hagoromo.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/bb/Young_Bijuu_and_Hagoromo.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150728221849)

The young tailed beasts with their creator Hagoromo.

Saiken and the tailed beasts first came into being in the waning days of [Hagoromo Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hagoromo_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Hagoromo Ōtsutsuki"), who used his [Creation of All Things Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Creation_of_All_Things_Technique "Creation of All Things Technique") to divide the [Ten-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails "Ten-Tails") ' chakra into nine separate bodies and giving each a name. Some time after being created, the Sage explained to the young tailed beasts that a link existed between them despite their separation. He also foretold of a day when they would be brought together again - though not as they were - and that at that time someone would emerge to show them what true power is.[^2] [^3] In the anime, before his death, Hagoromo sent Saiken to live in a temple built for the purpose of protecting it in a humid cavernous region.[^4]

During the [First Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/First_Shinobi_World_War "First Shinobi World War"), [Hashirama Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama_Senju "Hashirama Senju") captured eight of the tailed beasts and sold Saiken and [Isobu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Isobu "Isobu") to Kirigakure at the [Five Kage Summit](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Kage_Summit "Five Kage Summit"), as a sign of friendship and to ensure a balance of power amongst their new villages. The tailed beast would end up being sealed within Utakata decades later. In the anime, [Harusame](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Harusame "Harusame") tried and failed to remove Saiken from within his student, intending to save his Utakata from being a living weapon for their village. Utakata, however, having not heard what his master had said to him, later came under the assumption after the incident that his master had tried to kill him. This failed removal resulted in Utakata transforming into Saiken and killing Harusame.

## Personality

Though much has not been seen of Saiken's personality, the giant slug uses "ore" (オレ) when referring to itself, "ore" being a boastful way of saying "I". It also speaks with a dialect which may possibly be [Ōsakaben](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kansai_dialect "wikipedia:Kansai dialect") in origin. In the anime, Saiken also seemed to be kinder and not as self-serving and malicious as most other tailed beasts, as when Utakata asked for its help to save Hotaru it seemed to willingly comply; and later it simply receded back into Utakata without trying to take him over. However, it was not above killing for self-preservation, as seen when it killed [Harusame](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Harusame "Harusame") when he attempted to extract it from its jinchūriki. In the anime, it speaks with a high pitched voice and appears to be very energetic.

## Appearance

![A young Saiken.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/43/Young_Saiken.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130913103248)

A young Saiken.

Saiken is an enormous white — with a light blueish tint — bipedal [slug](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Slug "Slug") with stubby arms, feet and six long tails. It has two prominent optical tentacles (eyes) and hole-like openings as a mouth. Its entire body is covered in a thick, slimy substance.

When seen during the last days of the Sage of Six Paths' life, Saiken was much smaller than its present-day self, but was still much larger than the Sage.[^3]

## Abilities

*See also: [Jinchūriki Forms](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki_Forms "Jinchūriki Forms")*

![Saiken using Water Release: Starch Syrup Capturing Field.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/7/75/Water_Release_Starch_Syrup_Capturing_Field.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160110005745)

Saiken using Water Release: Starch Syrup Capturing Field.

As a [tailed beast](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_beast "Tailed beast"), Saiken has a massive amount of [chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra "Chakra") that it can freely [share with others](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Transfer_Technique "Chakra Transfer Technique"). It also has powerful healing capacities, able to mend injuries in themselves and others in seconds.[^5] Likewise, Saiken is effectively immortal, as when it dies, it will eventually revive, albeit in a younger form. Saiken has shown the ability to emit corrosive substances that can instantly disintegrate its target on contact in the form of [liquid](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Leech_Gap "Leech Gap") or [gas](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wisdom_Wolf_Decay "Wisdom Wolf Decay").

Saiken is seen having a great deal of tremendous durability, able to withstand being thrown a great distance by Kurama. Saiken possesses an affinity for [Water Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water_Release "Water Release"). It can expel a [sticky, adhesive substance](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water_Release:_Starch_Syrup_Capturing_Field "Water Release: Starch Syrup Capturing Field") from its mouth, which is capable of trapping its targets. In the anime, it can use [Soap Bubble Ninjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Soap_Bubble_Ninjutsu "Soap Bubble Ninjutsu"). Like the rest of its kind, Saiken can create a [Tailed Beast Ball](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Ball "Tailed Beast Ball").

## Part II

### Six-Tails Unleashed

Main article: [Six-Tails Unleashed](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Six-Tails_Unleashed "Six-Tails Unleashed") In the anime, in an attempt to stop the [Fury](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fury "Fury") technique from causing untold destruction, Utakata went into his full Six-Tails form to shield the blast. Later, Utakata was confronted and captured by [Nagato](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nagato "Nagato") 's [Six Paths of Pain](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Six_Paths_of_Pain "Six Paths of Pain"). Afterwards, Saiken was extracted from Utakata's body, being sealed into the [Demonic Statue of the Outer Path](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Statue_of_the_Outer_Path "Demonic Statue of the Outer Path"), thus killing the jinchūriki in the process.

### Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax

Main article: [Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War:_Climax "Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax")

![Saiken being forced by Tobi to attack Kakashi and Guy.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/c8/Acid_Spray.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130823190816)

Saiken being forced by Tobi to attack Kakashi and Guy.

After Utakata's full transformation into Six-Tails, he attacks [Kakashi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kakashi "Kakashi") and [Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Guy "Guy"), emitting a corrosive alkali from its mouth, which Kakashi warned Guy not to touch. After Guy dissipated the gas with his [Morning Peacock](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Morning_Peacock "Morning Peacock") technique, the Six-Tails prepared to attack him again. After [Son Gokū](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Son_Gok%C5%AB "Son Gokū") was resealed into the Demonic Statue of the Outer Path, Tobi, who was intent on going all-out from that point on, forced Utakata to fully transform into the Six-Tails. The opposing tailed beasts prepared to attack the shinobi, but they were [rebuffed](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Roar "Great Roar") by a fully-transformed Naruto.

![The tailed beasts and jinchūriki encounter Naruto.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/6b/Seishin_Sekai.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130905162801)

The tailed beasts and jinchūriki encounter Naruto.

Wasting no time in their attacks, the Six-Tails incapacitated Naruto and [Kurama](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kurama "Kurama") by swallowing the arm, but the slug was slammed into the [Two-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Two-Tails "Two-Tails"), sending them both flying away. The opposing beasts regrouped and then prepared a collaborative Tailed Beast Ball, which was met by one from Naruto and Kurama. As Naruto entered a deeper plain of the tailed beasts' consciousness, Saiken introduced itself to the young shinobi. Later, as Saiken was freed from the [chakra receiver](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_receiver "Chakra receiver"), it affirmed Kurama's belief that Naruto was the person the [Sage of the Six Paths](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_of_the_Six_Paths "Sage of the Six Paths") had spoken about so long ago before being resealed back into the Demonic Statue.

### Birth of the Ten-Tails' Jinchūriki

Main article: [Birth of the Ten-Tails' Jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Birth_of_the_Ten-Tails%27_Jinch%C5%ABriki "Birth of the Ten-Tails' Jinchūriki")

![The tailed beasts being freed from Obito.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/21/Biju_released.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250919211345)

The tailed beasts being freed from Obito.

Saiken appeared alongside with the other tailed beasts with the exception of [Gyūki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gy%C5%ABki "Gyūki") and [Shukaku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shukaku "Shukaku") when the [Ten-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails "Ten-Tails") looked down at Naruto. The image of the tailed beasts, along with a silhouette of the [Sage of the Six Paths](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_of_the_Six_Paths "Sage of the Six Paths"), drove the Ten-Tails to a fit as it evolved further. Later on, the [Allied Shinobi Forces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Allied_Shinobi_Forces "Allied Shinobi Forces") managed to pull the tailed beasts out of Obito, thereby freeing them, including Saiken. When Madara ran towards the tailed beasts, both Shukaku and [Gaara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gaara "Gaara") conjured [numerous sand bullets](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Release:_Sand_Buckshot "Wind Release: Sand Buckshot") and were able to restrain the Uchiha's movements.

![The tailed beasts working together to attack Madara.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/53/Tailed_Beasts_vs_Madara.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250919131223)

The tailed beasts working together to attack Madara.

Using the opportunity to attack, the tailed beasts each took a swing at their opponent, giving sand-manipulating duo an opening to use their [Desert Layered Imperial Funeral Seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Desert_Layered_Imperial_Funeral_Seal "Desert Layered Imperial Funeral Seal"), but Madara managed to escape the sand pyramid with his Susanoo. As Naruto in [Tailed Beast Mode](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Mode "Tailed Beast Mode") pinned Madara down with a tail, Saiken and the other tailed beasts joined in and did the same to increase the damage, but the Uchiha survived their attacks and retreated to a safe distance. After he received his own right Rinnegan eye from a White Zetsu clone, Madara quickly summoned the Demonic Statue of the Outer Path from Obito's body, and used the Rinnegan to [repel](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Limbo:_Border_Jail "Limbo: Border Jail") the tailed beasts. With the tailed beasts stunned, Madara then [chained](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Statue_Chains "Demonic Statue Chains") them up and sealed them back into the Demonic Statue.

![The tailed beasts reunite with Hagoromo and Naruto.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/bd/Tailed_Beast_Reunion.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150730121101)

The tailed beasts reunite with Hagoromo and Naruto.

Later, the chakra Saiken transferred to Naruto earlier, manifested within Naruto's subconscious along with the other tailed beasts to speak with the young jinchūriki and the spirit of Hagoromo Ōtsutsuki. There, they all voiced their shared belief to the Sage that Naruto was the child of prophecy.

### Kaguya Ōtsutsuki Strikes

Main article: [Kaguya Ōtsutsuki Strikes](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaguya_%C5%8Ctsutsuki_Strikes "Kaguya Ōtsutsuki Strikes")

![The tailed beasts being freed from the Ten-Tails.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/af/Tailed_Beast_separate_from_Kaguya.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160901155526)

The tailed beasts being freed from the Ten-Tails.

After Madara's ambitions were cut short by [Black Zetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Zetsu "Black Zetsu"), who revealed to be the silent manipulator of shinobi history since it was first created by [Kaguya Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaguya_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Kaguya Ōtsutsuki"), the black entity used Madara's body as a medium to resurrect its creator. During the battle against the tyrannical princess, Saiken and the other tailed beasts continued to support Naruto by giving him [their combined power](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_Art:_Super_Tailed_Beast_Rasenshuriken "Sage Art: Super Tailed Beast Rasenshuriken") to overwhelm her. Ultimately, Kaguya was defeated by Naruto and [Sasuke](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke "Sasuke"), who used the [Six Paths — Chibaku Tensei](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Six_Paths_%E2%80%94_Chibaku_Tensei "Six Paths — Chibaku Tensei") to eject all the tailed beasts from her and seal her away inside a newly-created [moon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Moon "Moon") within her own [dimension](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaguya%27s_Dimensions "Kaguya's Dimensions").

After the spirits of Hagoromo and the past Kage summoned both the tailed beasts and [Team 7](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Kakashi "Team Kakashi") back to the real world, the tailed beasts began to discuss their plans for the future since they were finally free. Sasuke, however, made his own intentions known: removing as many people as possible who could get in the way of his plans of leading the world through a revolution to do away with current shinobi systems, including killing all the current Kage and tailed beasts. Just as Sasuke swiftly [subdued](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu:_Sharingan "Genjutsu: Sharingan") the tailed beasts with his Rinnegan and sealed them within his [Chibaku Tensei](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chibaku_Tensei "Chibaku Tensei"), Naruto reassured the tailed beasts that he would save them once again. After Naruto was able to defeat and reform Sasuke, the two dispelled the Infinite Tsukuyomi and Sasuke released Saiken, along with the other tailed beasts, from their prisons.
