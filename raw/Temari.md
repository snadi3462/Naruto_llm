**Temari Nara** (奈良テマリ, *Nara Temari*) is a [kunoichi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kunoichi "Kunoichi") of [Sunagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sunagakure "Sunagakure") 's [Kazekage Clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kazekage_Clan "Kazekage Clan") and the eldest of the [Three Sand Siblings](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Three_Sand_Siblings "Three Sand Siblings"). After marrying [Shikamaru Nara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikamaru_Nara "Shikamaru Nara"), she becomes a member of the [Nara Clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nara_Clan "Nara Clan") and emigrates to [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure"), where she works as a kunoichi and ambassador for the village.

## Background

![Temari as a child.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/d/d3/Young_Temari.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20141127205821)

Temari as a child.

Temari was born the eldest child of [Karura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Karura "Karura") and the Fourth [Kazekage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kazekage "Kazekage"): [Rasa](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rasa "Rasa"). While it was customary for close relations to a Kage to be a [jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki"), neither she nor her younger brother [Kankurō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kankur%C5%8D "Kankurō") were compatible with the [One-Tail](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/One-Tail "One-Tail"), so [Gaara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gaara "Gaara") was chosen.[^7]

In the anime, growing up as a relative to the [Fourth Kazekage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Kazekage "Fourth Kazekage") and Gaara, Temari had a hard time making friends as everyone was always intimidated by her lineage. One boy, however, [Daimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Daimaru "Daimaru"), would spend much time around her, albeit always to play pranks on her.[^8] She also used to play with [Sen](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sen "Sen") and [Yome](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yome "Yome") when they were little girls.[^9]

## Personality

Temari is a prudent, stoic and blunt individual who is rarely afraid to speak her mind, a trait she would later pass on to her son. In Part I, similar to her younger siblings, she was rather dismissive with a cruel streak which was demonstrated when Gaara killed [Team Shigure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Shigure "Team Shigure"); she simply smiled and waved goodbye to them before they were crushed.[^10] However, she values peace, as she questioned the reasoning of starting a war with [Konoha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha "Konoha") in Part I, and was saddened by the countless lives that would be lost as a result. In Part II, her value for peace increased, as she acts as a diplomatic liaison between Sunagakure and Konohagakure to prepare for the next Chūnin Exams, as well as by risking her life several times during the [Kage Summit](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kage_Summit "Kage Summit") and the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War") to save her comrades, and in her eyes, it didn't matter if they were from her village or not as it initially did for other shinobi. She continues her efforts towards to keep peaceful relations between the villages in her adulthood, holding the position of ambassador in the [Shinobi Union](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinobi_Union "Shinobi Union") and for Suna in Konoha.

![Temari dreams of her siblings trusting her and occasionally asking her for advice.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/e/eb/Temari%27s_Dream.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20191008003213)

Temari dreams of her siblings trusting her and occasionally asking her for advice.

During the [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams"), it is revealed that the one person Temari is afraid of is her own youngest brother, Gaara. When Gaara and Kankurō were fighting, she showed a light, happy, mood to make sure Gaara didn't harm her or Kankurō. Despite this, during the short time between then and the Sasuke Retrieval Arc, she no longer expressed fear around him, having been one of the first to accept the new Gaara. Like Kankurō, her relationship with Gaara has greatly improved after he sought redemption after being reformed by Naruto. Temari is shown to be a loving older sister who deeply cares for her siblings, hurrying back to the village to try to save when Gaara was captured by the [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki") and watching over Kankurō's recovery from poison. In the anime, as revealed from her [Infinite Tsukuyomi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Infinite_Tsukuyomi "Infinite Tsukuyomi") dream, Temari cares greatly for her siblings and her greatest desire is for them to trust and rely on her.[^11]

![Temari and Shikamaru being shy around each other after coming to terms with their feelings.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/f8/Temari_and_Shikamaru.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230321211139)

Temari and Shikamaru being shy around each other after coming to terms with their feelings.

Temari frequently appears alongside [Shikamaru Nara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikamaru_Nara "Shikamaru Nara"). Though they were opponents during the Chūnin Exams, they came to each others' aid at different points in Part I. Despite her usual tough demeanour, she has a tendency to show a softer nature around Shikamaru, such as showing sympathy for him after his father scolded him for questioning if he should be a ninja after the [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha") rescue mission failed.[^12] When [Naruto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto "Naruto") returned to Konoha at the start of Part II and saw the two of them walking together, he asked them if they were on a date, which both of them denied.[^13] Later, after Shikamaru had escorted her to the village gates near the beginning of Part II, she told him that he should take his duties more seriously, and to quickly become a jōnin like herself.[^14] This topic was continued when she was assigned to the [Fourth Division](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Division "Fourth Division") of the [Allied Shinobi Forces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Allied_Shinobi_Forces "Allied Shinobi Forces") with Shikamaru. She tells him to "look alive" and act more like a leader now that he was essentially the division's acting general in Gaara's place.[^15] In the anime, it is implied that Temari has feelings for Shikamaru, as she became very flustered when [Yukata](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yukata "Yukata") and [Matsuri](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Matsuri "Matsuri") made note of it.[^8] This would turn out to be true, as after the war, she was clearly angered by how Shikamaru would not tell her the secret mission he was about to do, apparently having considered them to be able to confide in each other anything, to the point of slapping him and crying. She eventually started a relationship with Shikamaru and eventually even married him and gave birth to their son Shikadai.

![Temari's strict demeanor as a mother.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/61/Temari_strict.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230322005913)

Temari's strict demeanor as a mother.

Temari is shown to be quite strict as a mother, as she is very upset to hear her son get involved in any trouble at school and sharply tells him to give his uncle, Gaara, a proper greeting. She is not above physically disciplining her son, albeit in comedic moments rather than properly abuse-portrayed moments.[^16] She is also shown to have just the same relationship with her husband as Shikaku and Yoshino Nara, her parents-in-law, had, with Shikamaru becoming "whipped" by Temari just like Shikaku was by Yoshino. Temari is usually annoyed by Shikamaru's lack of discipline towards their son and finds it unacceptable. She is also just as annoyed whenever he focuses too much on teaching their son shōgi to the point of resorting to violence, as shown when she blasted both him and Shikadai out of the house after they repeatedly ignored her due to being so focused in playing shōgi. Overall, she is shown to be the matriarch of her family, as both men greatly fear her wrath and she is easily able to guess their inner thoughts. In fact, she has became very loyal to the Nara clan, caring very much about the their future and reputation.[^17]

At same time, Temari is not without having a carefree side for her family. For example, she smiled at watching her son compete in the Chūnin Exams and chuckled after seeing her son forfeit in an manner almost identical to Shikamaru during their own match years earlier. While strict and fully willing to admonish her son in public, as she did when Shikadai showed hesitance to engage the Byakuya Gang as her backup, she does have limits to what she is willing to push her son into, as she disapproved of Shikamaru sending Shikadai to capture Boruto and Sarada thinking it would be too much for him to fight his friend. In fact, she genuinely trusts her son's judgement and ends up respecting his choices in serious matters, as seen when she reluctantly let Shikadai consider drop out being a ninja for a few years to study politics as the heir of a clan. Likewise, Temari is still very in love with Shikamaru, even blushing when her husband stated at times he wanted to eat with his family.

Temari bears a great resemblance to her late mother [Karura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Karura "Karura"); she has green eyes (once coloured black [^18]) and blonde hair, which is gathered into four consecutive pigtails.

In addition to incorporating fishnet worn over her shoulders and legs, specifically on her right calf and her left thigh, she also wore her black forehead protector around her neck. During the [Sasuke Recovery Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Recovery_Mission "Sasuke Recovery Mission") and for the remainder of Part I, Temari wore a long-sleeved purple blouse under a grey top, a dark blue skirt, and a longer sash arranged in a bow.

She is also seen in her previous outfit after the summit. During the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"), Temari, like all the other shinobi, donned the standard attire of her village inclusive of a [flak jacket](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flak_jacket "Flak jacket").

## Abilities

Being one of the [Fourth Kazekage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Kazekage "Fourth Kazekage") 's children who underwent the gruelling training of Sunagakure, Temari is a highly talented [^4] and skilled kunoichi who specialises in long-range combat. Even as a [genin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genin "Genin"), she easily managed to kill [Tayuya](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tayuya "Tayuya"), the second strongest member of the [Sound Four](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sound_Four "Sound Four"), without a single scratch.[^19] She rises to the rank of [jōnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%8Dnin "Jōnin") by Part II, and her skills allowed her to be chosen to guard the Fifth Kazekage during the [Five Kage Summit](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Kage_Summit "Five Kage Summit"). As an adult, she continues to display great prowess, being able to single-handedly handle the Byakuya Gang, forcing them to retreat,[^20] and easily outclass Team 10.[^21]

#### Tessenjutsu and Nature Transformation

![Temari gliding on her fan.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/3c/Temari_Riding_Fan.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20180425153135)

Temari gliding on her fan.

Temari wields a giant [iron fan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Giant_Folding_Fan "Giant Folding Fan") (鉄扇, *tessen*) in battle, which she uses in tandem with her [Wind Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Release "Wind Release") nature manipulation to create severe torrents of wind. In addition to being used as a makeshift club or to block attacks, the fan can be used to glide upon for transportation or intimidation,[^22] and it seems to be made of some sort of steel, since it is able to deflect kunai and shuriken when opened.

Inscribed on her fan are three purple circles spaced equally across its length, which she calls "stars" (星, *hoshi*, *English TV:* moons). As she opens the fan to reveal each star in sequence, the fan's power increases greatly. Once all three moons are revealed, Temari can create [slicing whirlwinds](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sickle_Weasel_Technique "Sickle Weasel Technique") that she can guide with her chakra. Her winds allow her to attack from afar and alter the battlefield to her liking, as well as deflect or divert most ranged attacks. A [stronger variant](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Sickle_Weasel_Technique "Great Sickle Weasel Technique") of her whirlwinds can be used as a counter against sound wave attacks that can cause [genjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu "Genjutsu"), making it both a defence and an attack at the same time.[^23] In the anime, Temari showed the ability to mix [sand](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sand "Sand") into her attacks to [catch the enemy off-guard](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Dust_Wind_Technique "Dust Wind Technique").[^24] She could also create a [deadly tornado](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Release:_Great_Task_of_the_Dragon "Wind Release: Great Task of the Dragon").[^25]

![Temari using the Wind Release: Great Sickle Weasel Technique.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/f0/Cyclone_Scythe_Jutsu1.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150615204344)

Temari using the Wind Release: Great Sickle Weasel Technique.

In Part II, Temari is shown to have far greater control of the winds she creates with her fan. She demonstrates the ability to slice through multiple points on a Samurai's armour without injuring the man inside.[^26] She also developed a [stronger variant](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Scythe_Weasel "Scythe Weasel") of the Sickle Weasel Technique.[^27] By the time of the Fourth Shinobi World War, Temari was claimed to be the best [Wind Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Release "Wind Release") user in the [Fourth Division](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Division "Fourth Division"),[^28] which was shown when she could [injure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Release:_Cast_Net "Wind Release: Cast Net") the [Third Raikage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Raikage "Third Raikage"), whose body is known to be extremely durable,[^29] and counter the resulting wind force caused by a swipe of one of the [Ten-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails "Ten-Tails") ' tail by using the [Wind Release: Wind Cutter Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Release:_Wind_Cutter_Technique "Wind Release: Wind Cutter Technique") alongside the other [tessenjutsu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tessenjutsu "wikipedia:Tessenjutsu") users.[^30] Likewise, she could [counter](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Release:_Great_Cast_Net "Wind Release: Great Cast Net") the Ten-Tails' arms alongside the rest of the Allied Shinobi Forces.[^31] In the anime, she has used Wind Release even without her fan, being able to create a [swirl of air](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Release:_Air_Current_Wild_Dance "Wind Release: Air Current Wild Dance") using her palm.[^32]

Two years after the war, Temari was recognised as the best Wind Release user in Suna, and possibly the world.[^33] She is also proven to be a skilled wind nature transformation teacher, having taught a range of Wind Release techniques to [Mirai Sarutobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mirai_Sarutobi "Mirai Sarutobi").[^6]

### Intelligence

Temari is a keen analyst, able to easily deduce an opponent's strategies and weaknesses soon after a battle begins. During her Chūnin Exams finals fight, she easily calculated the distance it would take for Shikamaru to reach her with the [Shadow Imitation Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Imitation_Technique "Shadow Imitation Technique"), and was able to consistently keep out of its grasp.[^34] Despite ultimately being out-witted in her fight against Shikamaru, she was praised by the [jōnin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%8Dnin "Jōnin") [Asuma Sarutobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Asuma_Sarutobi "Asuma Sarutobi") who called her "an excellent strategist".[^35] She has also shown to have keen senses, able to tell when a Wind Release technique is being used,[^36] as well as when [Naruto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto "Naruto") was attempting to use the [Tailed Beast Ball](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Ball "Tailed Beast Ball").[^37] She is also apparently very studious as since marrying into the Nara clan, she has become well-versed in its techniques (or least the knowledge of them) and helped develop a [new technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Imitation_Field_Technique "Shadow Imitation Field Technique").

### Other skills

![Temari summoning Kamatari.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/c6/Summoning_Beheading_Dance.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250405213731)

Temari summoning Kamatari.

Temari can [summon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning_Technique "Summoning Technique") [Kamatari](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kamatari "Kamatari"), a one-eyed weasel, able to aid her in battle by [creating strong gusts of wind in unison with her own](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Quick_Beheading_Dance "Summoning: Quick Beheading Dance").[^38] Temari is also a capable short-range combatant, able to easily block Rock Lee's [Leaf Whirlwind](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Leaf_Whirlwind "Leaf Whirlwind") with her fan, much to the latter's surprise,[^39] and even deflect Tayuya's [sound-speed genjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Flute:_Phantom_Sound_Chains "Demonic Flute: Phantom Sound Chains") by swinging her fan before the sound waves reach her.[^40] In the anime, she can use her fan in tandem with [taijutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Taijutsu "Taijutsu") to defeat several of [Konohamaru Sarutobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohamaru_Sarutobi "Konohamaru Sarutobi") 's [shadow clones](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_clone "Shadow clone").[^41]

## Part I

### Chūnin Exams

Main article: [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams_\(Arc\) "Chūnin Exams (Arc)") Temari is first introduced with her brothers Kankurō and Gaara. At first, she is shown to have an infatuation for [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha") by blushing and becoming mildly excited when she thought he had asked who she was. When questioned by [Sakura Haruno](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sakura_Haruno "Sakura Haruno") why they were in their village, Temari chastised her, calling her ignorant.

![Temari defeats Tenten.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/48/Temari_vs_Tenten.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150209080346)

Temari defeats Tenten.

During the preliminary matches of the [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams"), Temari was pitted against [Tenten](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tenten "Tenten"). Since the bulk of their fight went unseen in the manga, the anime adaptation explained the circumstances.[^42] Tenten began the match by throwing wave after wave of weapons at Temari, although Temari easily blew them away with her fan. After Tenten ran out of weapons, Temari used her fan to trap Tenten in a cyclone and propel her into the air. She finished off the match quickly, as the cyclone dispersed and Tenten fell to the ground. Temari made it a point to have Tenten land on her closed fan, knocking Tenten unconscious. Even after she was declared the winner of the match, she threw Tenten off of the fan in an attempt to have her land on many weapons on the ground. However, [Rock Lee](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rock_Lee "Rock Lee") jumped in to save Tenten before she hit the ground.[^43] Lee attempted to attack Temari, although she easily stopped him with her fan.

![Temari facing Shikamaru at the Chūnin Exams.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/2a/Temari_faces_Shikamaru.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230430044830)

Temari facing Shikamaru at the Chūnin Exams.

During the final rounds of the Chūnin Exams, Temari faced Shikamaru Nara.[^44] Knowing Shikamaru used shadows to trap his opponents, Temari made sure to stay out of his shadow's range.[^45] Despite this precaution on Temari's part, Shikamaru used various tactics to increase the distance his shadow could reach, all the while buying time for the sun to set and give him more shadows to work with.[^46] As Temari evaded his attacks and prepared to finish the match, she was caught by his shadow, Shikamaru, having navigated her to a position where he could easily trap her without her notice.[^47] Although Shikamaru appeared to be in a position where he could win the match, he tactically forfeited, saying he had used too much chakra and could no longer use his technique.[^48]

### Konoha Crush

Main article: [Konoha Crush](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Crush_\(Arc\) "Konoha Crush (Arc)")

![Temari battling Sasuke.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/d/d0/Temari_vs_Sasuke.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20240908041242)

## New Era

Upon getting married to Shikamaru and taking residence in Konoha, Temari would later give birth to their son, [Shikadai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikadai "Shikadai"). At some point, after [Mirai Sarutobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mirai_Sarutobi "Mirai Sarutobi") was promoted to chūnin, Shikamaru requested Temari's help in teaching her Asuma's Wind Release techniques. Temari agreed and also went on to also teach her a wide range of her own techniques.[^6]

### Academy Entrance Arc

Main article: [Academy Entrance Arc](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy_Entrance_Arc "Academy Entrance Arc")

![Temari and Shikamaru discuss Shikadai's actions at the Academy.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/02/Nara_Family.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230322010116)

Temari and Shikamaru discuss Shikadai's actions at the Academy.

In the anime, during the early part of Shikadai's first semester in the [Academy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Academy "Academy"), he was punished along with some other students for causing a commotion and Temari angrily confronted him in home when he was playing video games. While Shikadai insisted he was unintentionally caught in [Boruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Boruto_Uzumaki "Boruto Uzumaki") 's antics, Temari gave him a serious scolding for being involved at all, noting that it was unmanly to not take responsibility. When Shikamaru came home from work and learned about it, despite Temari's attempts to make him scold their son as well, he took the news of Shikadai's school punishment indifferently, viewing it as yet another learning curve for him. Temari was annoyed by this attitude and it only grew when she sensed they were thinking her lecture was a drag, furiously deciding to deprive both men dinner for the night. Later, when Temari learned that her son skipped classes one day, she soon disciplined her son with a harsh slap to the face, leaving a deep red mark across his check.

### Byakuya Gang Arc

Main article: [Byakuya Gang Arc](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Byakuya_Gang_Arc "Byakuya Gang Arc")

![Temari saves Team 15 from the Byakuya Gang.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/4a/Temari_saves_Team_15.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230322012803)

Temari saves Team 15 from the Byakuya Gang.

In the anime, as the [Byakuya Gang](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Byakuya_Gang "Byakuya Gang") began doing regular heists throughout Konoha, all active chūnin and above shinobi were mobilised to intercept. Temari met her son in the way when he was waiting for a friend and informed him that she would be late coming home but had prepared dinner and he just had to heat the food up. During one such heist, Temari discovered the thieves being spied on by [Team 15](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_15 "Team 15"). She came to their aid, driving the thieves on the retreat. The Konoha-nin followed the thieves into the park where they found Boruto and Shikadai. Temari instructed her son to help detain them, only for him to stumble. This ultimately led to the thieves escaping. Temari sternly lectured her son for being too careful and calculating to approach situations quickly, pointing out that Shikadai was clearly hesitant to fight the Byakuya Gang even as just backup for a jōnin, telling him that he has to commit himself to his goal and be willing to take risks or else he could lose things precious to him, before leaving to give report.

Temari protecting people from the falling debris.

While meeting with [Ino Yamanaka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ino_Yamanaka "Ino Yamanaka") and Sakura Uchiha for tea one day, Temari watched as Naruto used his shadow clones to help villagers as he was too busy in his office. During the final round of the Chūnin Exams, Temari sat with Sakura and Ino and watched her son face off against [Boruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Boruto_Uzumaki "Boruto Uzumaki"). When Shikadai admitted defeat during the battle, much like his father did when he was younger, Temari laughed. ### Mitsuki's Disappearance Arc

Main article: [Mitsuki's Disappearance Arc](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mitsuki%27s_Disappearance_Arc "Mitsuki's Disappearance Arc")

![Temari brings Shikamaru his lunch.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/1/15/Temari_brings_lunch.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230322011547)

Temari brings Shikamaru his lunch.

In the anime, when [Mitsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mitsuki "Mitsuki") apparently betrayed Konohagakure by attacking two gate guards and leaving, the village was put on lockdown to handle the matters. Later, Boruto and [Sarada](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sarada "Sarada") left to find their team-mate despite orders. When Temari learned that Shikamaru sent Team 10 to retrieve the two genin before they were branded missing-nin, she confronted her husband about such a decision, not liking the idea of putting their son in such a difficult situation with his best friend. Shikamaru insisted that it would both aid Shikadai in his growth and because Shikadai thinks so much like Shikamaru, their son would probably realise the inconsistencies in the situation and could help figure out the truth, something that Shikamaru would have wanted to do himself if not too busy.

![Temari and her family meeting with the Nara clan's chief advisor.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/8c/Nara_meeting.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230322013627)

Temari and her family meeting with the Nara clan's chief advisor.

Later, as the elder sister of Fifth Kazekage, Temari was invited to join Gaara at the Wind Daimyō's banquet. While preparing for it, the Nara Clan's chief adviser, [Enchū Nara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ench%C5%AB_Nara "Enchū Nara") was deeply annoyed that Shikamaru, as head of the Nara Clan, would not be joining. He voiced his disdain at how much the Nara Clan appeared to slipping since the Fourth World Shinobi War and the constant gossip of the Land of Wind that the Nara Clan was not worthy of the unity between the Kazekage Clan ever since Shikamaru married Temari. While Shikadai was offered by Enchū to take up learning politics under the Fire Daimyō, he was deeply tempted to agree and decided to drop out being a ninja for two years, much to Temari's dismay as she tried in vain to make him stop it and was further shocked when Shikamaru agreed to allow it without much complaint, though she let her son choose his path at the end. However, he eventually decided to prove the worth the Nara Clan by mastering Wind Release techniques from Temari, which she happily agreed to teach him.

### One-Tail Escort Arc

Main article: [One-Tail Escort Arc](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/One-Tail_Escort_Arc "One-Tail Escort Arc")

![Temari and Shikadai set out to Sunagakure.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/fc/Temari_and_Shikadai_Thunder_Train.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230322013303)

Temari and Shikadai set out to Sunagakure.

In the anime, Temari decided to take a reluctant Shikadai to her home in Sunagakure, insisting that he should visit his uncle once in awhile. Later, Temari grew concerned for her brothers when they failed to return after so long after meeting with Sasuke. Deciding to investigate, she took Shikadai with her. They ultimately found [Yodo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yodo "Yodo") and [Araya](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Araya "Araya") carrying Gaara back to the village. They explained how during a battle with [Urashiki Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Urashiki_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Urashiki Ōtsutsuki"), Sasuke was teleported to a different dimension and Gaara was injured while managing to [temporarily seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Desert_Layered_Imperial_Funeral_Seal "Desert Layered Imperial Funeral Seal") the enemy. After being told how Kankurō was joining Boruto and Shinki in escorting Shukaku to Konohagakure, Temari trusted the Suna-nin to take care of Gaara while she and Shikadai went ahead.

![Temari talks to Boruto about Shinki.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/95/Temari_and_Boruto.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230322012632)

Temari talks to Boruto about Shinki.

They soon found the site of Kankurō's fight against Urashiki's puppets, having ended in a huge explosion. While searching for Kankurō, they found Boruto had returned, desperate to help the older ninja. While Boruto explained he had to come back to help Kankurō, despite Shinki's cold protests, Temari explained that Shinki wasn't as uncaring as he appears, but rather was determined to make his family proud. While showing gratitude for Boruto wanting to help her brother, Temari insisted that as ninja, they had to think of the bigger picture, including respecting their allies' wishes. They found evidence of a hole being dug as a means to escape the explosion, prompting the Konoha-nin to catch up with Shinki.

![Temari saving Shikadai from Urashiki's puppet.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/f8/Temari_Saves_Shikadai.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20190917115039)

Temari saving Shikadai from Urashiki's puppet.

They ultimately caught up to Shinki and Shukaku as they were attacked by the last of Urashiki's last [puppet](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Autonomous_Puppet "Autonomous Puppet"). While Boruto helped Shinki and Shukaku escape, the puppet's large array of weapons and attack styles made it difficult for the shinobi to battle off as Temari was ultimately wounded and partially paralysed while protecting her son. Through the team-work of Boruto and Shikadai, they ultimately managed to destroy the puppet. Afterwards, it was decided for Boruto to head on with Shinki and Shukaku while Temari tended to her wounds with Shikadai and resumed their search for Kankurō. Shortly afterwards, they were attacked by Urashiki, who stole some of their chakra and knocked them out. The mother and son were later found by Suna-nin, who revealed that Kankurō was alive and safe, and were brought to Konohagakure for treatment.

- In the omake at the end of *Shippūden* episode 75, Shikamaru told Asuma that *Naruto: Shippūden* would now be called *Shikamaru: Shippūden*. The logo appeared with the title, and two silhouettes appeared facing each other. One of them was Shikamaru and the other was Temari.
- Temari was ranked as the 16th most popular character in the sixth and most recent *Naruto* character popularity poll. Temari was the 11th most popular character in *Naruto* according to the fifth poll. She finished 15th in the fourth poll. In the "Naruto Databook", Temari was the 14th most popular character overall.
- According to the databook(s):
	- Temari's hobby is flora appreciation.
		- Temari wishes for a rematch with [Shikamaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shikamaru "Shikamaru").
		- Temari's favourite foods are sweet [chestnuts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chestnut#Culinary "wikipedia:Chestnut") and [kenchin soup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenchin-jiru "wikipedia:Kenchin-jiru"), while her least favourite foods are [squid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Squid#As_food "wikipedia:Squid") and [octopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octopus#As_food "wikipedia:Octopus").
		- Her favourite phrases are " *Sharpen your sickle at sunset* " (夕焼けに鎌をとげ, *Yūyake ni kama wo toke*) [^1] and " *One look, a thousand [li](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Li_\(unit\) "wikipedia:Li (unit)")* " (一望千里, *Ichibō senri*).[^2] [^3] The latter idiom refers to a sweeping view of the eye or a boundless expanse.
		- Temari has completed 42 official missions in total: 0 D-rank, 9 C-rank, 12 B-rank, 20 A-rank, 1 S-rank.
- Studio Pierrot's Settei sheets show that Temari's heeled sandals in *The Last: Naruto the Movie* add 1 cm onto her height.
