**Killer B** (キラービー, *Kirā Bī*, *Viz:* Killer Bee) is a [shinobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinobi "Shinobi") from [Kumogakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kumogakure "Kumogakure"). He is the most recent [jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki") of the Eight-Tails, [Gyūki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gy%C5%ABki "Gyūki"), though, unlike his predecessors, he was able to befriend it and hone its power for Kumo's benefit. Despite being responsible for the village's protection, B aspires to be the world's greatest rapper.

## Background

![B and A officially become brothers.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/43/A_and_B_brothers.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20210721214802)

B and A officially become brothers.

At a young age, he was along with several other children who were gathered by the [Third Raikage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Raikage "Third Raikage") and [Dodai](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Dodai "Dodai") in order to select a tag partner for his son [A](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/A_\(Fourth_Raikage\) "A (Fourth Raikage)"). Out of all the children gathered, he was the only person able to successfully perform the [Lightning Release: Double Lariat](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_Release:_Double_Lariat "Lightning Release: Double Lariat") with A. As such he was given the name "B", and A stated they would be brothers from that day on.[^2] Since the age of five, B has been best friends with [Motoi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Motoi "Motoi"), whom he always greeted with a fist bump. After [A's cousin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Blue_B "Blue B"), the jinchūriki of [Gyūki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gy%C5%ABki "Gyūki") lost control and killed [Motoi's father](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Furui "Furui") along with seven other ninja on that day, B was chosen as its next [jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki").[^3] Soon afterwards, B suffered from similar prejudices against jinchūriki, not too different from that the villagers of [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure") and [Sunagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sunagakure "Sunagakure") had against [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki") and [Gaara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gaara "Gaara") respectively. However, he kept smiling and pushed himself to be the best jinchūriki that he could be, so that his brother wouldn't be hurt politically as the Fourth Raikage.[^4]

After his father's death, Motoi tried to kill B, believing that B's death would also kill Gyūki and avenge his late father, but B disarmed him easily and held no ill will against Motoi. Motoi however, was ashamed of his actions and did not speak to B for over thirty years because of it.[^5] B would later train with his brother on an [island](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Island_Turtle "Island Turtle") in the [Land of Lightning](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Lightning "Land of Lightning"), where there he would find the [Falls of Truth](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Falls_of_Truth "Falls of Truth"). There he learned how to control Gyūki in an amazingly short period of time. Some time after his training, B, alongside A, and other Kumo shinobi confronted [Minato Namikaze](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Minato_Namikaze "Minato Namikaze") and his small squad. When Minato moved to counter A's attack, B used a tentacle to push A out of the way, causing shock to the Konoha shinobi that he was Gyūki's jinchūriki. The two from this encounter Minato would come to have much respect for B and his abilities. Minato even stated that B acted as a true shinobi killer, thus giving him the title of "Killer".[^6] [^7] Some time since then, much like Naruto, B has become beloved in Kumo as the village's hero, or, as Motoi called him, the "Hero of Heroes".[^8]

When A became the Raikage, he forbade B from fully transforming into the Eight-Tails and restricted him from leaving the village, saying instead that he would become the guardian of the village and attack from within its walls, to protect him from meeting the same fate as his adoptive father.[^9] Sometime later, when B learned from pestering [Darui](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Darui "Darui") that Konoha and Suna were hosting together an impromptu Chūnin Exams, B saw this as an opportunity to give a show for the attendees. A however anticipated his young brother's actions and had Darui and [C](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/C "C") stop him.[^10]

## Personality

![B writing in his rhyme book during battle.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/61/B_Writing.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20180428034108)

B writing in his rhyme book during battle.

Killer B is very proud of his own power and by extension of who he is, regardless of his status and upbringing as a jinchūriki. Like [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki"), who has great overconfidence at times, Killer B can be quite cocky and stubborn, especially in terms of his own strength, demanding respect from others and asks to be called " **Lord Jinchūriki** " (人柱力様, *Jinchūriki-sama*) and his tailed beast be called " **Lord Eight-Tails** " (八尾様, *Hachibi-sama*). B has a special way of expressing his pride through song, more specifically in rap-style and tends to use "Bakayarō! Konoyarō!" (「バカヤロー!コノヤロー!, *Viz:* Fool! Ya Fool!) when referring or speaking to others. He will even write his rhymes in the middle of a fight, or during an important meeting, even if they are no good, which is usually the case. He gets his rapping ideas from his opponents in battle. This sometimes causes the people around him to become greatly annoyed by him. B thinks very highly of his rapping skills and doesn't respect anyone who would make fun of rap or his lyrics.

The Raikage has noted that Killer B is not the type of person to work with the strategies created by people and that he would only bring chaos into a battlefield;[^11] in his youth he was already seen completing missions, that were supposed to be assigned for both him and his brother, without A's knowledge. The Raikage also notes that he is the only being that B will listen to, as he is able to keep him in check. Despite all this, B knows talent when he sees it, and respects those with it. The primary example is his fellow Kumo jinchūriki, [Yugito Nii](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yugito_Nii "Yugito Nii"), who he looked up to for her extreme skills.[^12] He had also praised [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha") after their fight, stating he was one of the strongest shinobi he had ever fought. B has a very close relationship with his older brother, the Raikage. According to [Motoi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Motoi "Motoi"), the reason behind him becoming a jinchūriki was for the sake of his brother, even if it meant being shunned by the villagers. He didn't complain, and was always cheerful and determined.

![B offering to bump fists.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/34/B_adolescent.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20180428024618)

B offering to bump fists.

Due to the fact that he was confined to the village very soon after his brother assumed office and made the village's guardian,[^9] B desperately wished for a vacation and was even ready to fake his capture for one. When the [Fourth Raikage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Raikage "Fourth Raikage"), [Darui](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Darui "Darui") and [C](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/C "C") learned that B was not captured by Akatsuki, they immediately guessed his intentions and were not surprised by it, though they were very displeased. This leads to him receiving his brother's " [Iron Claw](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iron_Claw "Iron Claw") " as punishment. B also tends to [fist bump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fist_bump "wikipedia:Fist bump") with others. This ties into his rapper personality and also serves as a means of communication of sorts, with others without the need to speak.

However, when someone close to him is in danger, Killer B has no qualms about putting himself at risk to save them, if necessary, as seen when he prioritised the safety of his singing master Sabu and his pet, [Ponta](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ponta "Ponta") over confronting [Kisame Hoshigaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kisame_Hoshigaki "Kisame Hoshigaki") head-on when the Akatsuki confronted his entourage. This caring nature differentiates him from a typical jinchūriki, most of whom are cynical and uncaring towards others due to their circumstances. However, it was hinted that his past as a jinchūriki may not have been unlike Naruto's, since Motoi claimed that being a fellow jinchūriki, B understands what Naruto went through. He has a casual relationship with Gyūki, frequently engaging in friendly conversation with it, even sitting upon its nose when not in combat.

![B's attraction to older women.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/3f/B_attracted_to_Tsunade.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230422042414)

B's attraction to older women.

He was also completely unaffected by Naruto's [Harem Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Harem_Technique "Harem Technique"), resisting lustful advances and even appearing indifferent.[^13] However, he is not completely unaffected by such things, as he kept staring at Tsunade's chest while visibly flustered, which his brother reprimanded him for [^14] and he later stared at Yugito's own chest when her clothes were torn open during his fight with Tobi's Six Paths, openly commenting on them despite the intense battle he was currently engaged in.[^15] This is later explained as having a preference toward older women, at one point confessing it during the fight against Tobi, much to his tailed beast's surprise and chagrin.

From the time of his battle with Sasuke to the Fourth Shinobi World War, Killer B is shown to have matured a great deal. A as well as Darui and C believed having Killer B on the battlefield would be chaotic both from the battlefield and from chaos he himself would create. However, this would end up to be proven wrong as he was a good mentor and role model to Naruto, who himself matured a great deal with his help, and together ended up helping the Allied Shinobi Forces turn the tide against the Akatsuki. B is also very kind and compassionate, having befriended all the giant animals on the [Island Turtle](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Island_Turtle "Island Turtle") which may indicate that he has a liking for animals, as well as never expressing any ill will towards Motoi who tried to take his life, nor against the villagers who used to shun him. B is shown to have taken a liking to Naruto during their time together, even supporting his desire to join the war after getting past Iruka, based on what he learned about Naruto while training him and knowing that there would be no point trying to stop him if Iruka couldn't. B even wanted to visit Naruto in Konoha after the latter became Hokage even though Gyūki talked him out of it stating that Naruto had little free time on his hands, unlike B.

## Appearance

- ![Killer B's appearance as a child](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/49/Kid_B_Mobile.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/162?cb=20210423192150)
	Killer B's appearance as a child

1/3

Killer B has dark skin and a muscular build, as well as white hair (blond in the anime) and a goatee. On his right shoulder, he has a tattoo of the kanji for "iron" (鉄, *tetsu*), which is the [Iron Armour Seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iron_Armour_Seal "Iron Armour Seal") that was used to seal Gyūki into him, and on his left cheek, he has a tattoo of a bull's horn (correspondingly, Gyūki has horns like a bull, and the left one is partially missing).[^16] His top lip also has a slightly darker hue than his bottom one.

Killer B wears oval-shaped sunglasses and a white-coloured [forehead protector](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forehead_protector "Forehead protector"). He also has his village's standard one-strap-over-one-shoulder [flak jacket](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flak_jacket "Flak jacket"), and a long, red rope belt tied around his waist, the standard Kumo hand and shin guards, shinobi sandals, and a white scarf around his neck. In the anime the trim on his flak jacket is white, but in the manga it is yellowish.[^17] [^18] He originally carried seven swords on his back, but now he only carries two swords on his back and stores the rest inside Samehada.

As a child, B's hair was much shaggier and he wore a simple suit along with his trademark sunglasses and scarf along with Kumo-nin shin-guards. In his adolescent years, his hair grew much longer and he wore it in a stylised, combed-back manner. In these years he donned the standard uniform of the Kumo-nin inclusive of shirt and started carrying two [particular swords](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kumogakure_Swords "Kumogakure Swords"). As he got older, he started wearing his [forehead protector](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forehead_protector "Forehead protector") like a bandanna.

Years after the Fourth Shinobi World War, B's attire consisted of a long, dark, sleeveless vest that left his abdomen exposed along with baggy white pants held up by a cord and standard shinobi sandals. His hair had also gotten noticeably longer, his goatee became shaggier and covered his lower jaw, plus his brow becomes hairless and more prominent.

## Abilities

![Killer B enhancing his physical attacks with Gyūki's chakra.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/f5/Lariat.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150203070452)

Killer B enhancing his physical attacks with Gyūki's chakra.

As the guardian of Kumo and having long-since mastered his unique powers, Killer B is an extremely powerful shinobi, able to single-handedly overwhelm the entire team [Taka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Taka "Taka") consisting of [other](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha") [dangerous](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Suigetsu_H%C5%8Dzuki "Suigetsu Hōzuki") [fighters](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J%C5%ABgo "Jūgo"). As a child, he accomplished missions by himself that were meant to be executed as a team with his partner A,[^19] causing him to note that his younger brother has more talent than he did.[^20] Even [Minato Namikaze](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Minato_Namikaze "Minato Namikaze") stated that B was a ninja with a great power rather than simply a jinchūriki.[^21] B could hold his own against [Kisame Hoshigaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kisame_Hoshigaki "Kisame Hoshigaki") of [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki"),[^22] and later two tailed beasts during the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War").[^23]

### Jinchūriki Transformations

Main article: [Jinchūriki Forms](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki_Forms "Jinchūriki Forms")

![B in Tailed Beast Mode.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/57/Killer_B%27s_Tailed_Beast_Mode.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150127164116)

B in Tailed Beast Mode.

B is a jinchūriki of Gyūki. Even more, he is one of the few individuals to achieve complete [control](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Control "Tailed Beast Control") of his tailed beast,[^24] [^25] with Kisame Hoshigaki considering him to be a "perfect jinchūriki" (完璧な人柱力, *kanpeki na jinchūriki*, *Viz:* epitome of jinchūriki) for his skills and ability therein,[^26] even after having already faced a host before. Because he has a strong relationship with Gyūki, it can aid B by disturbing his chakra to dispel [genjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu "Genjutsu") or granting B more of its own chakra, making it more of a willing partnership than actual dominance. B can easily access and utilise Version 1 and 2 transformations with all eight tails, and even his most powerful form, the [Tailed Beast Mode](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Mode "Tailed Beast Mode"), all of which while retaining complete control of himself. In this state, he can fire [multiple](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Continuous_Tailed_Beast_Balls "Continuous Tailed Beast Balls") [Tailed Beast Balls](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Ball "Tailed Beast Ball") and generate [powerful tornadoes](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Eight_Twists "Tailed Beast Eight Twists") by spinning its tentacles rapidly. B can also regenerate any of Gyūki's tentacles if they are lost; this ability, however, does not extend to its horns, as one of them was cut off by A.

![B demonstrating his ability to produce ink.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/7/77/Ink_Creation.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160226032118)

B demonstrating his ability to produce ink.

B can also partially transform parts of his body into those of Gyūki's, such as his own arm or quickly forming all eight tentacles on his back. These partial transformations can be used for various purposes.[^27] [^28] [^29] [^30] Stemming from Gyūki's "octopus" trait, B can produce [ink](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ink_Creation "Ink Creation") from his mouth to write lyrics as a makeshift pencil, blind his enemies, or form [ink clones](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ink_Clone_Technique "Ink Clone Technique") that can immobilise and [seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sealing_Technique:_Octopus_Hold "Sealing Technique: Octopus Hold") his targets.[^31]

### Chakra and Physical Prowess

![B overcomes A in physical strength.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/e/e7/B_overpowering_A.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150215215902)

B overcomes A in physical strength.

As a [jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki"), Killer B possesses a massive quantity of strong chakra and stamina, enough to fight throughout an entire day, even while [fully transformed](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki_Forms#Tailed_Beast_Mode "Jinchūriki Forms"). He could quickly recover and return to battle with no ill effects shortly after being hit by his own [Tailed Beast Ball](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Ball "Tailed Beast Ball"). His tantalising chakra even caused [Samehada](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Samehada "Samehada") to betray its master Kisame to join B. Even the [Shinju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinju "Shinju") was attracted to B's reserves and chased him with far more roots and branches than other shinobi in the area.

B's fighting style consists of fast unpredictable movement, aptly named, [Disturbance Taijutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Disturbance_Taijutsu "Disturbance Taijutsu"). Like his brother, B generally makes use of [wrestling moves](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_Release:_Lariat "Lightning Release: Lariat"), instead of standard taijutsu, which is supplemented by his immense strength. He could successfully perform a [Lightning Release: Double Lariat](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_Release:_Double_Lariat "Lightning Release: Double Lariat") with A as a child,[^32] and defeat a [giant bear](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kintoki "Kintoki") in a sumō match as an adult. During the Fourth Shinobi World War, B's natural raw strength had surpassed his brother's, even in his [fully-powered state](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_Release_Chakra_Mode "Lightning Release Chakra Mode").[^33] He is also durable enough to withstand being [electrocuted](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chidori "Chidori") by Sasuke, [punched hard](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Piston_Fist:_Style_One "Piston Fist: Style One") by Jūgo, and even [hit](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_Release:_Lariat "Lightning Release: Lariat") by A with no visible damage.[^33] He has also shown remarkable speed and reflexes, able to surprise the [Yellow Flash](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yellow_Flash "Yellow Flash") when they fought.[^34] [^35]

### Ninjutsu

#### Kenjutsu

![B's unique kenjutsu.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/81/Acrobat.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150124180516)

B's unique kenjutsu.

While carrying only two swords in his youth, B has greatly developed his [kenjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kenjutsu "Kenjutsu") in adulthood. Combining kenjutsu with his unique fighting style, Disturbance Taijutsu, B can make use of another technique, [Acrobat](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Acrobat "Acrobat") — a unique style in which B holds seven blades in between his left armpit, both elbows, right leg, stomach, the right side of his neck, and in his mouth. As strange as it appears, B is very skilled in this style, easily pressuring his enemies with unpredictable swings from various angles in a manner similar to hip-hop dance. When he finds an opening, he can quickly impale his opponent with several blades. His ability to impale his opponents is extremely pinpoint accurate, often hitting vital spots. Killer B mentioned Sasuke only survived being impaled by him because he used [Chidori Current](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chidori_Current "Chidori Current") to deflect the blades that impaled him.

![B wielding Samehada.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/84/Samehada.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160306203535)

B wielding Samehada.

B's kenjutsu could overwhelm even the [most](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Suigetsu_H%C5%8Dzuki "Suigetsu Hōzuki") [trained](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha") [swordsmen](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Itachi_Uchiha "Itachi Uchiha"), some of whom possess the Sharingan.[^36] [^37] He can also use the blades as mid-range projectiles, create an "Eighth" sword by using Gyūki's chakra, and quickly slice rocks into various shapes. After acquiring [Samehada](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Samehada "Samehada") as his main weapon, B has integrated it into his fighting style by using the sword's ability to 'cut' chakra.[^38] B can also coordinate attacks with Samehada,[^39] and use it to store some of his swords when not in use. He is also a talented teacher in kenjutsu, having taught [Omoi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Omoi "Omoi") and [Karui](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Karui "Karui") tactics like [feint attacks](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Cloud-Style_Deception_Beheading "Cloud-Style Deception Beheading") and deceptions with [shadow clones](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_clones "Shadow clones") or the [Body Replacement Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Body_Replacement_Technique "Body Replacement Technique") as setups for a [final blow](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Reverse_Mist_Beheading "Reverse Mist Beheading").[^40]

#### Nature Transformation

![B's pencil charged with his lightning chakra.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/bc/Charged_a_sharp_but_weak_pencil-point.PNG/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20210805025526)

B's pencil charged with his lightning chakra.

B is capable of using [Lightning](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning "Lightning"), [Fire](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire "Fire"), [Water](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water "Water"), and [Yang Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yang_Release "Yang Release") [nature transformations](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nature_transformation "Nature transformation").[^1] With lightning, he has displayed the ability to [flow lightning-natured chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Flow "Chakra Flow") through his blades to increase their cutting power or clad himself in it to increase his strength and speed.[^41] B's skill with Lightning Release can even charge a pencil to pierce straight through a large tree trunk and even through the ground. As stated by Kisame, B does this by increasing the vibration frequency of his lightning chakra high enough to surpass the penetrative force of [Wind Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Release "Wind Release") techniques.[^42]

### Intelligence

Despite his carefree nature, Killer B possesses sharp wit, as shown when he used the [Body Replacement Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Body_Replacement_Technique "Body Replacement Technique") to switch places with one of Gyūki's tentacle so he could escape Sasuke's [Amaterasu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Amaterasu "Amaterasu"). He can also be very persuasive and deceptive, as he quickly turned Naruto's attention away from leaving the Tailed Beast Temple by offering to train him. He also has the ability to interpret someone's "heart" as well as transmit his own feelings merely through touching someone (which is usually done through a fist bump). This ability was first demonstrated when he was training with Naruto in the [Tailed Beast Temple](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Temple "Tailed Beast Temple"), again when Iruka expressed his wishes to protect Naruto to him [^43] and when he stopped his brother from attacking Naruto.[^44] B is quite knowledgeable in fūinjutsu, being able to distinguish between the superiority of the [Four Symbols Seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Four_Symbols_Seal "Four Symbols Seal") and his own [Iron Armour Seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iron_Armour_Seal "Iron Armour Seal") [^45]

## Part II

### Pain's Assault

Main article: [Pain's Assault](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Pain%27s_Assault_\(Arc\) "Pain's Assault (Arc)")

![Killer B stabs Sasuke.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/a6/B_stabs_Sasuke.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130919143748)

Killer B stabs Sasuke.

### Five Kage Summit

Main article: [Five Kage Summit](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Kage_Summit_\(Arc\) "Five Kage Summit (Arc)")

![B charges at Kisame with a Version 2 Lightning Release: Lariat.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/f0/Lariat_Version_2.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150816112425)

B charges at Kisame with a Version 2 Lightning Release: Lariat.

Free from his confinement to Kumogakure, B sought out [Sabu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sabu "Sabu") to advance his music career. In the midst of training B was found by [Kisame Hoshigaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kisame_Hoshigaki "Kisame Hoshigaki"), who tracked his powerful chakra with his [Samehada](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Samehada "Samehada"). While Sabu attacked him, B struck from behind with one of his lightning-imbued swords. Not only was Kisame able to block their attacks with Samehada, but B's lightning disappeared when it makes contact. B entered a Version 1 form and attempts to [Headbutt](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Headbutt "Headbutt") him, but his chakra shroud disappeared too. Sabu realised that Samehada was absorbing B's chakra. B entered a seven-tailed Version 1 form and attacked Kisame again but Samehada absorbs most of his chakra. Upon realising that Samehada could only absorb six tails at a time, B entered a [Version 2](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki_Forms#Version_2 "Jinchūriki Forms") form and created chakra-bones to use in a more powerful Lightning Release: Lariat. Kisame was badly damaged and Samehada was only able to absorb his Version 2 chakra, returning B to a Version 1 form.

![B and A finish off "Kisame".](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/63/Lightning_Release_Double_Lariat.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150408193733)

B and A finish off "Kisame".

Samehada used the chakra it absorbed from B to heal Kisame, who quickly merged with Samehada and created a [dome of water](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water_Release:_Great_Exploding_Water_Colliding_Wave "Water Release: Great Exploding Water Colliding Wave") around them. B tried to get Sabu and [Ponta](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ponta "Ponta") out of the dome before they drown, but the water dome moved with Kisame. B drew Kisame away from Sabu and Ponta, in order to force him to release them to pursue B. Once they were free, B confronted Kisame, only for Kisame to render all of B's counterattacks ineffective and slowly absorb his chakra all the while. When B lost consciousness, Kisame dissipated the water dome. To prevent B from escaping, Kisame tried to cut off his legs with Samehada. However, Samehada had grown fond of B's tailed beast chakra during the battle and betrayed Kisame; in addition to not letting Kisame harm B, it transfered some of the chakra it stole back to him. Kisame kicked it away and tried to cut off B's legs with one of B's own swords, but the Raikage and his bodyguards arrived to stop him, having noticed Kisame's water dome. Samehada continued to replenish B's reserves, allowing B and A to perform [Lightning Release: Double Lariat](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_Release:_Double_Lariat "Lightning Release: Double Lariat") to decapitate Kisame. A then reprimanded B for running away from the village.

When they returned to Kumogakure, B now carrying Samehada, they were greeted by the villagers at the main gate. B's students, [Omoi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Omoi "Omoi") and [Karui](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Karui "Karui"), ask about Samehada. They and B were unaware that Kisame, having faked his death, was hiding inside Samehada to gather intel on Kumo and the [Shinobi Alliance](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinobi_Alliance "Shinobi Alliance") during the meetings B later attended (and didn't pay attention to).

### Fourth Shinobi World War: Countdown

Main article: [Fourth Shinobi World War: Countdown](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War:_Countdown "Fourth Shinobi World War: Countdown")

![B attacks the Giant Squid.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/b1/Hachi-B_fights_Squid.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150202192221)

B attacks the Giant Squid.

Shortly before the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War") began, the Alliance decided to send the remaining jinchūriki to Kumogakure's [Island Turtle](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Island_Turtle "Island Turtle") to hide. B arrived there first, allowing him to defend fellow jinchūriki [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki") from the island's [Giant Squid](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Giant_Squid "Giant Squid") when he arrived. Shortly after this, Naruto approached B for help gaining control of the [Nine-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tails "Nine-Tails"). B refused, having noticed an [evil](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Naruto "Dark Naruto") within Naruto, and also due to Naruto accidentally insulting his rap. Later, when the squid attacked [Motoi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Motoi "Motoi"), B came to his aid. After the squid was subdued, Motoi asked B why he saved him, despite surely knowing about Motoi's earlier attempt on his life. B's reaction was to act like it had never happened, and did not hesitate to reconcile with Motoi.

![B and Motoi reconcile.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/47/B_and_Motoi.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20210804202004)

B and Motoi reconcile.

After observing the reunion between B and Motoi, Naruto was able to overcome his inner hatred. With it vanquished, B took him to the [secret temple](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Temple "Tailed Beast Temple") behind the [Falls of Truth](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Falls_of_Truth "Falls of Truth"), to allow him to battle the Nine Tails for control of its chakra. When Naruto first opened the [seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight_Trigrams_Sealing_Style "Eight Trigrams Sealing Style") and released the Nine-Tails, B tried to help him contain it. His abilities were limited while in Naruto's subconscious and he was only able to block a tailed beast ball from the Nine-Tails before being knocked out of the fight. Naruto eventually emerged victorious and communicated all that he went through to B with a fist-bump. Naruto then demonstrated his new control of the Nine-Tails, allowing him to expose Kisame's presence.

Kisame fled with Samehada and B pursued him. Samehada tried to return to B, allowing Kisame an opportunity to absorb his chakra. When he recovered, B flung [Might Guy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Might_Guy "Might Guy") after Kisame. Guy was able to capture him but Kisame committed suicide to prevent the group from interrogating him. They tried to check the intel Kisame had with him, only to be [imprisoned](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Water_Prison_Technique "Water Prison Technique") while a [shark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shark "wikipedia:Shark") swam away with the intel. B tried to catch the shark after he freed himself, but was unsuccessful. Fearing an imminent Akatsuki attack, the island's animal population was moved indoors. Because Naruto was not to know about the ongoing war, B helped keep Naruto distracted while the other members of the group fought the Akatsuki outside.

### Fourth Shinobi World War: Confrontation

Main article: [Fourth Shinobi World War: Confrontation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War:_Confrontation "Fourth Shinobi World War: Confrontation")

![B demonstrating his tailed beast control to Naruto.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/63/Killer_B_In_Control.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150722180238)

B demonstrating his tailed beast control to Naruto.

To keep Naruto occupied, B started helping him gain control of his new [Nine-Tails Chakra Mode](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tails_Chakra_Mode "Nine-Tails Chakra Mode"), first by having him use his [chakra arms](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Chakra_Arms "Tailed Beast Chakra Arms") to stack blocks in a perfect column. When Naruto mastered this, they moved on to transforming into the Nine-Tails. The Nine-Tails would not cooperate with Naruto on this, leaving him unable to use the [Tailed Beast Ball](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Ball "Tailed Beast Ball"). They instead decided to practise with Naruto's standard techniques to see how the Nine-Tails' chakra affected them. Naruto began with his [Rasengan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rasengan "Rasengan"), which B recognised as being based on the Tailed Beast Ball. B encouraged him to focus on mastering the Rasengan in this new form.

During training, Naruto sensed some of the Nine-Tails' chakra [elsewhere](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_and_Silver_Brothers "Gold and Silver Brothers"). B tried to convince him that it was nothing, determined to keep Naruto in the training room so he wouldn't find out about the war. He nevertheless let Naruto leave to go to the bathroom, unaware that it was a lie from Naruto. When Naruto didn't return, B went looking for him, but instead found [Iruka Umino](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iruka_Umino "Iruka Umino"), Naruto having found out about the war, and headed off to try and stop it. Iruka requested that B protect Naruto, and after recognising Iruka as a positive influence from fist-bumping with Naruto, B agreed, and went after Naruto. When the Eight Tails protested, B reminded him that they were still training, and stated that real world experience would always be faster than training. He caught up to Naruto and the two teamed up to break through the [Self-Repairing Barrier](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Self-Repairing_Barrier "Self-Repairing Barrier") to escape from the island. They then headed for the battlefield.

![B protecting Naruto from A.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/a6/B_intercepts.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150504190940)

B protecting Naruto from A.

While en route, they were intercepted by A and the [Fifth Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fifth_Hokage "Fifth Hokage"). B stated he thought [Tsunade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsunade "Tsunade") was attractive. B tried to convince his brother to let them pass, promising to keep Naruto safe from Akatsuki. A was not convinced by his words and, when Naruto promised to use force to escape, the Raikage threatened to kill him. B blocks the latter's attack and informed his brother that he must be willing to kill both of them. A claimed to have no problem with this but B, unbelieving, bumped fists with him to remind him of all they had been through together. B further stated that since A became the Raikage, he has failed to see B's development in strength, and has therefore underestimated him. Still unconvinced, both he and Naruto tried to combat him but struggle doing so as A manages to fight them off. B and A later simultaneously hit each other with their respective Lightning Release: Lariats, with B overpowering his brother. B explained how he overpowered A with his Lightning Release: Lariat; his true strength, along with Naruto's, came from the people that believed in them, not just the beasts inside of them. A eventually pushed B out of the way and activated his Lightning Release Armour again, seemingly intending to kill Naruto. Naruto dodged A, who revealed that he attacked in order to test Naruto. Impressed, he and the Hokage let them pass.

B and Naruto later encountered several shinobi, including [F](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/F "F") who told B that they were to return to headquarters as back-up. Before he could continue however, Naruto attacked him revealing that F and the other shinobi were transformed [enemies](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/White_Zetsu_Army "White Zetsu Army") much to B's shock. After taking out a few of the clones, B watched on with amazement and pride as Naruto took out the enemy, while noting that in response to Naruto's Tailed Beast Chakra mode the Zetsu were turning into trees in the same manner Yamato's wood creations did at the [Tailed Beast Temple](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Temple "Tailed Beast Temple"). Later while Naruto arrived just in time to save two shinobi from [Toroi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Toroi "Toroi") 's onslaught, he had a conversation with one of his fellow Kumo-nin before setting out once again.

B clashes with Itachi.

While en route with Naruto, the two encountered [Itachi Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Itachi_Uchiha "Itachi Uchiha") and [Nagato](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nagato "Nagato"). As the battle commenced suddenly with Itachi being forced to use the [Fire Release: Great Fireball Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire_Release:_Great_Fireball_Technique "Fire Release: Great Fireball Technique"), B used Samehada to slice through the attack effectively saving both him and Naruto, although Samehada was unhappy about being used to cut through hot flames. As Itachi appeared behind B suddenly and used the [Fire Release: Phoenix Sage Flower Nail Crimson](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire_Release:_Phoenix_Sage_Flower_Nail_Crimson "Fire Release: Phoenix Sage Flower Nail Crimson") technique, B countered with his [transformed arm](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki_Forms#Partial_Transformation "Jinchūriki Forms"). B then used the tentacle to grab Itachi, only for him to disappear in a flurry of crows. As Itachi told B not to look into his eyes, Gyūki broke B out of the genjutsu he had already been caught in. He then charged Itachi using his [seven swords](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Acrobat "Acrobat"). Itachi retreated onto Nagato's summoned [bird](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Giant_Drill-Beaked_Bird "Giant Drill-Beaked Bird").

![B attacks Nagato.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/b7/B_attacks_Nagato.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140206183837)

B attacks Nagato.

As Itachi activates his [Mangekyō Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mangeky%C5%8D_Sharingan "Mangekyō Sharingan") B tried to stop him by throwing his sword but it was repelled by Nagato. Nagato appeared behind B and tried to attack him, but B went into his Version 2 transformation and countered Nagato's attack with a Lightning Release: Lariat. Nagato used the Preta Path's ability to [absorb](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Blocking_Technique_Absorption_Seal "Blocking Technique Absorption Seal") B's chakra, causing him to regain a youthful appearance.

Seeing Naruto in dire straits with his soul being [removed](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Absorption_Soul_Technique "Absorption Soul Technique"), B moved to attack Nagato. Nagato, sharing vision with his own chameleon summon and the King of Hell, intercepted B by using his [Asura Path](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Asura_Path "Asura Path") abilities. Before Nagato could fatally injure B and remove Naruto's soul, they were rescued by Itachi's [Susanoo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Susanoo "Susanoo"). As Nagato used [Chibaku Tensei](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chibaku_Tensei "Chibaku Tensei") in an effort to trap them, Itachi told them to attack the centre of the sphere in an attempt to dispel the technique. B watched on as Itachi's [Sword of Totsuka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sword_of_Totsuka "Sword of Totsuka") began to seal Nagato who passes on his final words to Naruto, before passing onto the afterlife.

After Itachi claims that he will stop the [Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Impure_World_Reincarnation "Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation") technique, B voiced his doubts and recalled that they were not able to kill the reincarnated ninja, just seal them. Itachi reminded them again that no matter how infallible a technique may seem, it always had a weak point. As Naruto stated that he would end the war and attempted to create shadow clones he reverted to his normal self, having used up too much of the Nine Tails's chakra. After Itachi told Naruto not to try to shoulder everything himself, B told him that he had made a promise to Iruka to protect him.

B was later scolded by Gyūki for taking too long to urinate and for letting Naruto go ahead of them. When Naruto's clone contacts them mentally, wanting help for fighting the reincarnated Third Raikage, he asked B to let him speak to Gyūki to which B refused while urinating. However, the clone still got to speak to the beast because it could overhear mentally.

Seeing that Blue B's demon powers and immortal body were too much for him to handle alone, B decided to retreat. Motoi tried to help, only to be defeated quickly. Seeing Motoi in trouble, B quickly counterattacked to save his childhood friend. Shocked that B had actually found true friends who accept him for who and what he is, Blue B realised that B had in fact found what Blue B could not to become a fully-realised jinchūriki. ### Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax

Main article: [Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War:_Climax "Fourth Shinobi World War: Climax")

![B and Naruto clash with Yugito and Han.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/7/7a/Jinch%C5%ABriki_clashes.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150221103933)

B and Naruto clash with Yugito and Han.

After catching up to Naruto, the two jinchūriki ran into [Tobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobi "Tobi") with his [Six Paths of Pain](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Six_Paths_of_Pain "Six Paths of Pain"). After a short skirmish with the other jinchūriki, and conversing with Gyūki about the jinchūriki and Yugito's fate, B enquired about the "other [Madara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Madara "Madara") " that Naruto was speaking of. Looking on smiling as Naruto told Tobi that it wasn't all bad living with a tailed beast inside them, he declared that he would go first before staring in shock as the other jinchūriki began their tailed beast transformations. Shocked that they still had their tailed beast skills, B attacked Fū head-on nonetheless. After she dodged several of his and [Samehada](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Samehada "Samehada") 's attacks, Naruto informed him of the shared field of vision that the [Six Paths of Pain](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Six_Paths_of_Pain "Six Paths of Pain") possessed using the [Rinnegan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rinnegan "Rinnegan"). Catching Naruto, B fled the vicinity after seeing Yugito unleash her [Mouse Hairball](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mouse_Hairball "Mouse Hairball") technique. Clashing with her while escaping, he was able to land a somewhat shallow attack on her that immediately begins repairing. This opportunity however, allowed B to see where Tobi had implanted the [black receivers](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Black_receiver "Black receiver") in his Six Paths.

After Naruto's attempt to destroy the receivers in Rōshi's chest was thwarted, B agreed with Gyūki that the forest was too much of a hindrance for them and transformed into Gyūki to [level the entire forest](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Eight_Twists "Tailed Beast Eight Twists"). B immediately moved to seal the revived jinchūriki with his [Octopus Hold](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sealing_Technique:_Octopus_Hold "Sealing Technique: Octopus Hold") technique but it was unsuccessful as Tobi caused the jinchūriki to enter Version 2-like states. As they began counter-attacking, B and Naruto tried to fend them off, but began feeling the pressures of fighting six powerful opponents. B was attacked by a fully transformed [Han](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Han "Han"), who [gored](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Horn_Breaking "Horn Breaking") him with his horns and sent him flying, seemingly knocking him unconscious. ## Creation and Conception

In a 2012 Shōnen Jump interview, Kishimoto stated that one of his past editors who really likes pro wrestling greatly influenced his decision to create a character that — even though he was a shinobi — used pro wrestling moves as part of his techniques. He also stated that he wanted to make him into an interesting character and one of the most powerful shinobi in the series. Kishimoto went on to say that he has trouble writing B's lines at times because he always wants them to rhyme due to his rapping style.

## Trivia

- His name is a reference to the famed rap group, the [Wu-Tang Clan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wu-Tang_Clan "wikipedia:Wu-Tang Clan"), often referred to as the "Killer Bees".
- Bī is the Japanese pronunciation of the letter "B" (as well as the Japanese pronunciation of "Bee"). This parallels the names of other Kumogakure shinobi such as [F](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/F "F") and [J](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/J "J") for example.
- During a fierce battle with [Taka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Taka "Taka"), B quoted [Muhammad Ali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad_Ali "wikipedia:Muhammad Ali") 's famous line, " *float like a butterfly, sting like a bee* ".[^50]
- Killer B makes various references to bees. This is a pun on the number of tails his tailed beast has. Eight in Japanese is hachi (八), a homophone to the Japanese word for bee, hachi (蜂).
- Though not confirmed, Killer B was given the name of Kira Hachi by fans. It comes from the word " *Kirābī* " incorrectly translated into Japanese (Hachi is the Japanese word for " *bee* ", as well as " *Eight* ". Kira is a loanword of the English " *killer* "). Kira Hachi, can be translated to " *Killer Eight* ", following the homophone of bee.
- B's nickname may be a reference to [The Killer Bees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Killer_Bees_\(professional_wrestling\) "wikipedia:The Killer Bees (professional wrestling)"), a professional wrestling team in the [World Wrestling Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Wrestling_Entertainment "wikipedia:World Wrestling Entertainment"). Additionally, the [Lightning Release: Double Lariat](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Lightning_Release:_Double_Lariat "Lightning Release: Double Lariat") resembles the "Cross Bomber" move used by the Hell's Missionaries tag-team in Kinnikuman, a wrestling manga.
- B has used or has possession of two of the [Seven Swordsmen of the Mist](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Seven_Swordsmen_of_the_Mist "Seven Swordsmen of the Mist") blades: [Kubikiribōchō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kubikirib%C5%8Dch%C5%8D "Kubikiribōchō") and [Samehada](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Samehada "Samehada") respectively.
- In chapter [542](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/The_Secret_Story_of_the_Strongest_Tag_Team!! "The Secret Story of the Strongest Tag Team!!"), when A and B face off against several [Iwagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iwagakure "Iwagakure") shinobi while on a mission,[^51] B is first depicted with longer hair and looks a bit older. However, in subsequent panels his hair is once again short and spiky and looks much younger.[^52] Oddly, this was not corrected in the tankōbon.
- In a 2012 Shōnen Jump interview, [Masashi Kishimoto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Masashi_Kishimoto "Masashi Kishimoto") stated that [Chōji Akimichi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%8Dji_Akimichi "Chōji Akimichi") and B were currently his favourite characters in the *Naruto* series.
- Studio Pierrot's Settei sheets of Killer B show that he was 112 cm at age 5, and 122 cm at age 7. As an adult Studio Pierrot has him at 192.7 cm.
- His birth date corresponds to [Taurus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taurus "wikipedia:Taurus"), the bull in the western zodiac, further referencing his connection with Gyūki.
