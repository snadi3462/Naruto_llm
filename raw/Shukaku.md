**Shukaku** (守鶴), more commonly known as the **One-Tail** (一尾, *Ichibi*), is one of the nine [tailed beasts](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_beasts "Tailed beasts"). It was last sealed within [Gaara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gaara "Gaara") of [Sunagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sunagakure "Sunagakure"), after being sealed in two other [jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki") before him.[^8]

## Background

![The young tailed beasts with Hagoromo.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/bb/Young_Bijuu_and_Hagoromo.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150728221849)

The young tailed beasts with Hagoromo.

Shukaku and the tailed beasts first came into being in the waning days of [Hagoromo Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hagoromo_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Hagoromo Ōtsutsuki"), who used his [Creation of All Things Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Creation_of_All_Things_Technique "Creation of All Things Technique") to divide the [Ten-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails "Ten-Tails") ' chakra into nine separate bodies and giving each a name. Some time after being created, the Sage explained to the young tailed beasts that a link existed between them despite their separation. He also foretold of a day when they would be brought together again - though not as they were - and that at that time someone would emerge to show them what true power is.[^9] [^10] In the anime, before his death, Hagoromo sent Shukaku to live in a temple built for the purpose of protecting it in a desert region that would later become part of the [Land of Wind](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Wind "Land of Wind").[^11]

Long before the [First Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/First_Shinobi_World_War "First Shinobi World War"), Shukaku was captured by disciples of a temple and kept in Sunagakure, to which the [First Kazekage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/First_Kazekage "First Kazekage"), who was invited along with other first Kage by [Hashirama Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama_Senju "Hashirama Senju") into the first [Five Kage Summit](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Kage_Summit "Five Kage Summit"), attempted to use his possession of the tanuki as leverage to gain wealth for his nation, as opposed to gaining another tailed beast.[^12]

Shukaku's first [jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki") was a Suna priest named [Bunpuku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Bunpuku "Bunpuku"), who was locked away in a dungeon with the giant tea kettle, and was kept under constant guard. Over time, the citizens of Sunagakure came to believe that Shukaku itself was a [living ghost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ikiry%C5%8D "wikipedia:Ikiryō") (生霊, *ikiryō*) of a corrupted priest sealed inside a tea kettle.[^4] Despite the animosity the village showed for Shukaku and its host, the elderly priest held no ill-will towards the tanuki nor the villagers, viewing it as an equal and was content as long as Shukaku hadn't forgotten his name. Shukaku eventually came to appreciate Bunpuku and would later remember the words he imparted to it: that someday someone would appear in the world to teach it about the heart and acceptance.[^13] Shukaku had another jinchūriki who inspired the [Third Kazekage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Kazekage "Third Kazekage") to create his [Iron Sand](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Iron_Sand "Iron Sand") [fighting style](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fighting_style "Fighting style").[^8] [^14] Shukaku was eventually removed from their bodies, resulting in their respective deaths.[^15]

![Shukaku goes on a rampage after Gaara loses control.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/08/Gaara_lost_control.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20211030011449)

Shukaku goes on a rampage after Gaara loses control.

When [Karura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Karura "Karura") became pregnant with Gaara, her husband, the [Fourth Kazekage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Kazekage "Fourth Kazekage"), had [Chiyo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chiyo "Chiyo") seal Shukaku into Gaara while he was still in the womb because, out of his [three children](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Three_Sand_Siblings "Three Sand Siblings"), Gaara was the only one compatible with the tailed beast.[^16] [^17] Before Karura died after giving birth, she passionately told her son that she would protect him, and, according to her husband, her love seeped into the sand and was noted to form his automatic defence.[^18] [^19] However, Gaara couldn't properly [control](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Control "Tailed Beast Control") Shukaku's powers, killing or injuring several of Suna's shinobi and citizens by accident. Shukaku would even cite its hatred toward humans, warning Gaara not to sleep too long if he didn't want to give Shukaku an opening. Deemed a failure, the Fourth Kazekage chose to test his son one last time, ordering [Yashamaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yashamaru "Yashamaru") to assassinate him and also tell him that his mother never loved him, and that she was used as a human sacrifice for the sealing process. Gaara failed the test, fully releasing the rampaging Shukaku, to which the Fourth eventually used his [Gold Dust](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_Dust "Gold Dust") to subdue the tanuki.[^20]

## Personality

![Shukaku threatening a young Gaara.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/89/Shukaku_and_young_Gaara.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20141218124918)

Shukaku threatening a young Gaara.

Shukaku is characterised as a childish and short-tempered individual, and often speaks in a bombastic and incoherent manner, giving it the demeanour of a drunkard. It uses "ore-sama" (オレ様, *literally meaning:* my esteemed self) when referring to itself, as it expresses great pride in its own abilities, especially its sealing techniques and defences.[^21] [^22] According to Gaara, prior to its introduction, Shukaku relishes the opportunity to kill anyone in sight, even more so during a full [moon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Moon "Moon").[^23] Shukaku has also shown to harbour a bitter grudge towards [Kurama](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kurama "Kurama") due to the fox's beliefs that the tailed beasts' strength is measured by the number of tails, therefore viewing the tanuki as the weakest.[^24] Because of this, Shukaku strives to find any opportunity to surpass its arrogant rival.

Shukaku's hatred towards humans appears to stem from centuries of being viewed and treated as a monster that must either be destroyed or captured and weaponized. This led it to spark fear in whomever was its [jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki"), reminding them that if they go into a deep sleep, the tanuki would possess their body and murder all humans; this resulted in Gaara's developing insomnia. Rude as Shukaku can be, it could still be respectful to those who show genuine respect and kindness to the tanuki, as seen from its original harsh attitude toward [Bunpuku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Bunpuku "Bunpuku"); when its jinchūriki treated the tanuki as an equal, an impressed Shukaku complimented him by comparing him to the Sage of Six Paths. Likewise, during the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War"), as Gaara defiantly fought to protect Shukaku from being sealed and openly thanked Shukaku for helping him find his path in life, Shukaku came to see Gaara in a similar light to Bunpuku and fought to protect Gaara just as earnestly.

Years after the Fourth Great Shinobi War, Shukaku's relationship with Sunagakure was relatively peaceful as it mellowed down. As with the other tailed beasts, Shukaku also came to respect Naruto as well, commenting on his son [Boruto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Boruto "Boruto") with interest when he displayed a similar drive. Shukaku is also not above developing affection as after Naruto's daughter [Himawari](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Himawari "Himawari") risked her own safety to save Shukaku, the tanuki began enjoying her company and when finally leaving, bid her a genuine and friendly goodbye.

## Appearance

![A young Shukaku.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/55/Young_Shukaku.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20151015140632)

A young Shukaku.

Shukaku is a sandy-brown coloured [tanuki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/tanuki "wikipedia:tanuki"), with black (dark blue in the anime) [cursed seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Cursed_seal "Cursed seal") markings all over its face, body, and tail. It has a jagged, concave mouth with no tongue, and the sclerae of its eyes are black, with yellow irides and pupils that each takes the shape of a black four-pointed star with four black dots around it. During the last days of the Sage of Six Paths, Shukaku in its youth looked basically the same as it does presently, only much smaller with shorter ears and more spaced out markings.[^10]

## Abilities

*See also: [Jinchūriki Forms](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki_Forms "Jinchūriki Forms")*

![Shukaku creating a Tailed Beast Ball.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/60/Shukaku%27s_Tailed_Beast_Ball.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150917123944)

Shukaku creating a Tailed Beast Ball.

Shukaku is such a potential threat, As a precaution, if its jinchūriki lost control and fully transformed into Shukaku inside of Suna, the villagers were to either evacuate beforehand or seek shelter due to the risk of mass casualty from its rampage.[^25] Shukaku's power is quite impressive to the likes of the [Ōtsutsuki Clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/%C5%8Ctsutsuki_Clan "Ōtsutsuki Clan"), with [Urashiki Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Urashiki_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Urashiki Ōtsutsuki") expressing glee in taking it.[^26]

### Physical and Chakra Prowess

Shukaku has great physical strength while using its tail, as seen when overlapping it with the other tailed beasts to shatter and even obliterate a [senjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Senjutsu "Senjutsu") -enhanced humanoid [Susanoo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Susanoo "Susanoo").[^27] As a tailed beast, Shukaku possesses a large quantity of [chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra "Chakra"). It also has powerful healing capacities, able to mend injuries in themselves and others in seconds.[^28] Likewise, Shukaku is effectively immortal, as when it dies, it will eventually revive, albeit in a younger form.

### Ninjutsu

Shukaku has the ability to control its jinchūriki whenever they sleep, leading its host to develop [insomnia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/insomnia "wikipedia:insomnia").[^29] Like the rest of its kind, Shukaku can also perform the [Tailed Beast Ball](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Ball "Tailed Beast Ball"). It is capable of performing [fūinjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/F%C5%ABinjutsu "Fūinjutsu") by using its natural cursed seal markings across its own sand body, which gives off a magnetic field to bind its targets. One of its techniques, the [Desert Layered Imperial Funeral Seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Desert_Layered_Imperial_Funeral_Seal "Desert Layered Imperial Funeral Seal"), is strong enough to temporarily restrain and withstand powerful techniques, like the Susanoo.

#### Sand Manipulation

![Shukaku enforcing a fūinjutsu with its curse seal markings.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/e/e0/Sabaku_Sotaisu_Fuin.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20141218123738)

Shukaku enforcing a fūinjutsu with its curse seal markings.

Shukaku's most notable ability is its [sand](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sand "Sand") manipulation, using it as an effective weapon and shield. Since its whole body is completely made of sand, the tanuki can ignore most damage that would otherwise be detrimental, as shown when it had one of its arms [chopped off](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Toad_Sword_Beheading "Toad Sword Beheading") by [Gamabunta](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gamabunta "Gamabunta") and was still able to keep on fighting. Taking pride in its motto of absolute defence, Shukaku's sand is durable enough to block a senjutsu-enhanced Susanoo sword with ease.

#### Nature Transformation

![Shukaku using Wind Release.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/4/41/Shukaku%27s_Wind_Release.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20190902100717)

Shukaku using Wind Release.

Shukaku can use [Wind](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind "Wind") and [Earth Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Release "Earth Release") [nature transformations](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nature_transformation "Nature transformation"). It can rapidly fire [wind bullets](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Release:_Drilling_Air_Bullet "Wind Release: Drilling Air Bullet") with enough force to level entire forests. Likewise, it can combine its wind abilities with Gaara's sand in order to produce a [mass of fast-moving sand bullets](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Release:_Sand_Buckshot "Wind Release: Sand Buckshot"). He could also charge these wind bullets to the point they could fire like a [Tailed Beast Ball](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Ball "Tailed Beast Ball"), and wipe out entire landscapes.[^30] Shukaku can combine wind and earth elements to use [Magnet Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Magnet_Release "Magnet Release"), and is the only tailed beast which can use [cursed seals](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Juinjutsu "Juinjutsu") from its markings and infuse them into the [Rasengan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rasengan "Rasengan") to create the [Sage Art: Magnet Release Rasengan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_Art:_Magnet_Release_Rasengan "Sage Art: Magnet Release Rasengan"), which was powerful enough to restrain [Madara Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Madara_Uchiha "Madara Uchiha") 's [shadow](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Limbo:_Border_Jail "Limbo: Border Jail") when he was the Ten-Tails' jinchūriki.

## Part I

### Konoha Crush

Main article: [Konoha Crush](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Crush_\(Arc\) "Konoha Crush (Arc)")

![Shukaku clashes with Gamabunta.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/e/e9/Bunta_vs_Shukaku.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150124090301)

Shukaku clashes with Gamabunta.

During the [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams "Chūnin Exams"), Gaara partially transformed into Shukaku during his fight with [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha"), lashing out with the tailed beast's arm after being injured for the first time and terrifying Sasuke with a glimpse of its eye. During the [Konoha Crush](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konoha_Crush "Konoha Crush"), Gaara later transformed into his [full Shukaku form](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Mode "Tailed Beast Mode") during his fight with [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki"), who summoned [Gamabunta](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gamabunta "Gamabunta") to counteract it.

When Gaara exposed himself on top of Shukaku's head and used the [Feigning Sleep Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Feigning_Sleep_Technique "Feigning Sleep Technique"), Shukaku took full control of its body and battled them. Shukaku's power was too much for Gamabunta to handle, resulting in both him and Naruto using the [Combination Transformation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Combination_Transformation "Combination Transformation") to take the form of a giant fox (the Nine-Tails in the anime). Gamabunta used the form's claws and teeth to grab and restrain Shukaku, in order for Naruto to wake Gaara up with a punch, which suppressed Shukaku's power.

## Part II

### Kazekage Rescue Mission

Main article: [Kazekage Rescue Mission](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kazekage_Rescue_Mission "Kazekage Rescue Mission")

![Shukaku being extracted from Gaara.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/3/39/Shukaku_Extracted_from_Gaara.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150917211420)

Shukaku being extracted from Gaara.

During Gaara's time as the Fifth Kazekage, [Deidara](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Deidara "Deidara") and [Sasori](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasori "Sasori") from the [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki") were assigned to capture Shukaku. During the battle between Gaara and Deidara, Gaara manifested Shukaku's arms several times to attack the Akatsuki member.

After Deidara defeated Gaara in combat and took him to Akatsuki's lair in the [Land of Rivers](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Rivers "Land of Rivers"), Shukaku was forcefully [extracted](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sealing_Technique:_Phantom_Dragons_Nine_Consuming_Seals "Sealing Technique: Phantom Dragons Nine Consuming Seals") from Gaara and placed within the [Demonic Statue of the Outer Path](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Statue_of_the_Outer_Path "Demonic Statue of the Outer Path"). Though the extraction killed him, Gaara was resurrected through [Chiyo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chiyo "Chiyo") 's [One's Own Life Reincarnation](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/One%27s_Own_Life_Reincarnation "One's Own Life Reincarnation") technique and has still retained the ability to control sand.

### Birth of the Ten-Tails' Jinchūriki

Main article: [Birth of the Ten-Tails' Jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Birth_of_the_Ten-Tails%27_Jinch%C5%ABriki "Birth of the Ten-Tails' Jinchūriki")

![The tailed beasts being freed from Obito.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/21/Biju_released.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250919211345)

The tailed beasts being freed from Obito.

Shukaku was the only tailed beast sealed within the Demonic Statue to have not been resealed into [Obito Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Obito_Uchiha "Obito Uchiha") 's [Six Paths of Pain](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Six_Paths_of_Pain "Six Paths of Pain") and as such, did not meet Naruto during his encounter with the other tailed beasts. When Naruto proceeded in his plan to pull all the beasts from Obito's body in order to halt the tree form of the Ten-Tails' flower from blooming and completing the [Eye of the Moon Plan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eye_of_the_Moon_Plan "Eye of the Moon Plan"), its chakra rejected Naruto as he contained none of the tanuki's chakra. Gaara, having been bonded with Shukaku, instead took hold and began pulling the chakra himself. Through the efforts of the Kazekage, Shukaku was freed from its captivity alongside the other tailed beasts. Upon being freed, Shukaku started to recognise Naruto. Despite showing reluctance to Gaara's request for its help in sealing [Madara Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Madara_Uchiha "Madara Uchiha"), Shukaku decided to comply only for the opportunity to surpass Kurama.

![The tailed beasts working together to attack Madara.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/53/Tailed_Beasts_vs_Madara.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20250919131223)

The tailed beasts working together to attack Madara.

When Madara ran towards them and the other tailed beasts, both Shukaku and Gaara conjured [numerous sand bullets](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Release:_Sand_Buckshot "Wind Release: Sand Buckshot") and were able to restrain the Uchiha's movements. Using the opportunity to attack, the tailed beasts each took a swing at their opponent, giving the sand-manipulating duo an opening to use their [Desert Layered Imperial Funeral Seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Desert_Layered_Imperial_Funeral_Seal "Desert Layered Imperial Funeral Seal"), but Madara managed to escape the sand pyramid with his Susanoo. As Naruto in [Tailed Beast Mode](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Mode "Tailed Beast Mode") pinned Madara down with a tail, Shukaku and the other tailed beasts joined in and did the same to increase the damage, but the Uchiha survived their attacks and retreated to a safe distance. After he received his own right Rinnegan eye from a White Zetsu clone, Madara quickly summoned the Demonic Statue of the Outer Path from Obito's body, and used the Rinnegan to [repel](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Limbo:_Border_Jail "Limbo: Border Jail") the tailed beasts.

![Shukaku protecting Gaara from Madara's attack.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/1/10/Shukaku_defending_Gaara.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150113025028)

Shukaku protecting Gaara from Madara's attack.

With the tailed beasts stunned, Madara then proceeded to [chain](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Statue_Chains "Demonic Statue Chains") them up and prepared to seal them back into the statue. As Madara began the process on Shukaku, Gaara [intervened](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Desert:_Hand "Desert: Hand"), declaring his refusal to let Madara get his former tailed beast. Shocked at how determined Gaara was to protect Shukaku despite their negative past, the tanuki remembered [Bunpuku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Bunpuku "Bunpuku") 's words of wisdom. From that, it also recalled Gaara explaining that he'd been grateful to Shukaku as it was thanks to the tanuki that Gaara had met his first friend, Naruto. As Madara launched an attack on Gaara, Shukaku quickly blocked the attack for its former jinchūriki. Despite their new-found team-work, Madara still rebuffed their efforts and all the tailed beasts were resealed within the Demonic Statue.

![The tailed beasts reunite with Hagoromo and Naruto.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/bd/Tailed_Beast_Reunion.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150730121101)

The tailed beasts reunite with Hagoromo and Naruto.

Later, after Madara succeeded in becoming the Ten-Tails' jinchūriki, a reformed Obito managed to steal some of Shukaku's chakra along with some of Gyūki's. Then Obito sealed the [Yin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yin "Yin") half of Kurama into a dying Naruto to save his life, Obito also sealed Shukaku and Gyūki chakra portion into Naruto. Inside Naruto's subconscious, Shukaku, Gyūki, and Yin-Kurama found the young jinchūriki talking with the spirit of [Hagoromo Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hagoromo_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Hagoromo Ōtsutsuki"). As the other six tailed beasts manifested to join in the meeting, they all voiced their shared belief to the Sage that Naruto was the child of prophecy. As Naruto fought Madara, Shukaku aided him by lending its Magnet Release chakra, allowing Naruto to use a juinjutsu technique against Madara.

### Kaguya Ōtsutsuki Strikes

Main article: [Kaguya Ōtsutsuki Strikes](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaguya_%C5%8Ctsutsuki_Strikes "Kaguya Ōtsutsuki Strikes")

![The tailed beasts being freed from the Ten-Tails.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/af/Tailed_Beast_separate_from_Kaguya.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160901155526)

The tailed beasts being freed from the Ten-Tails.

After Madara's ambitions were cut short by [Black Zetsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Zetsu "Black Zetsu"), who revealed to be the silent manipulator of shinobi history since it was first created by [Kaguya Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaguya_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Kaguya Ōtsutsuki"), the black entity used Madara's body as a medium to resurrect its creator. During the battle against the tyrannical princess, Shukaku and the other tailed beasts continued to support Naruto by giving him [their combined power](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_Art:_Super_Tailed_Beast_Rasenshuriken "Sage Art: Super Tailed Beast Rasenshuriken") to overwhelm her. Ultimately, Kaguya was defeated by Naruto and Sasuke, who used the [Six Paths — Chibaku Tensei](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Six_Paths_%E2%80%94_Chibaku_Tensei "Six Paths — Chibaku Tensei") to eject all the tailed beasts from her and seal her away inside a newly-created [moon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Moon "Moon") within her own [dimension](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaguya%27s_Dimensions "Kaguya's Dimensions").

After the spirits of Hagoromo and the past Kage summoned both the tailed beasts and [Team 7](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_Kakashi "Team Kakashi") back to the real world, the tailed beasts began to discuss their plans for the future since they were finally free. Sasuke, however, made his own intentions known: removing as many people as possible who could get in the way of his plans of leading the world through a revolution to do away with current shinobi systems, including killing all the current Kage and tailed beasts. Just as Sasuke swiftly [subdued](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Genjutsu:_Sharingan "Genjutsu: Sharingan") the tailed beasts with his Rinnegan and sealed them within his [Chibaku Tensei](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chibaku_Tensei "Chibaku Tensei"), Naruto reassured the tailed beasts that he would save them once again. After Naruto was able to defeat and reform Sasuke, the two dispelled the Infinite Tsukuyomi and Sasuke released Shukaku, along with the other tailed beasts, from their prisons.

### Versus Momoshiki Arc

Shukaku warning Gaara.

After having never initiated contact with Gaara since the two parted ways, Shukaku [contacted](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Telepathy "Tailed Beast Telepathy") the Kazekage to inform him that it had lost contact with [Gyūki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gy%C5%ABki "Gyūki"). Telling him that its connection with Naruto was still intact, Shukaku said it had a bad feeling about the matter before leaving Gaara to deal with the matter as he saw fit.

### One-Tail Escort Arc

Main article: [One-Tail Escort Arc](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/One-Tail_Escort_Arc "One-Tail Escort Arc")

![Shukaku facing Urashiki.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/2/27/Shukaku_Vs_Urashiki.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20190902100919)

Shukaku facing Urashiki.

In the anime, as Gaara was battling [Urashiki Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Urashiki_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Urashiki Ōtsutsuki"), Shukaku arrived to aid its former host. Together, the two were able to [seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Desert_Layered_Imperial_Funeral_Seal "Desert Layered Imperial Funeral Seal") away the foe. Knowing that the seal won't hold Urashiki for long, it was decided that [Kankurō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kankur%C5%8D "Kankurō"), [Shinki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinki "Shinki"), and [Boruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Boruto_Uzumaki "Boruto Uzumaki") escort Shukaku to Konoha for its safety while Gaara would return to Suna to recover. Also during which, to make sure Urashiki would not sense its chakra, Shukaku willingly sealed itself inside a small tea kettle. However, the seal still allowed Shukaku to move about as a miniature version of itself, wearing the kettle like an armour. During their journey, they were intercepted by four of Urashiki's puppets. Kankurō decided to face the threat alone, while Boruto and Shinki went on ahead with Shukaku.

Along the way, Boruto was amazed at how cold Shinki was. Shukaku noted that Gaara was a lot like this back in the day, until Naruto's kindness swayed him to find a better path. Soon afterwards, they noticed a massive explosion from the battlefield. While Shinki insisted to move on, Boruto refused leave Kankurō when he needed them most, saying it would be a trashy thing to do. Boruto went back, while Shinki continued on with Shukaku. Before long, they were engaged by one of Urashiki's [puppets](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Autonomous_Puppet "Autonomous Puppet"). While Shukaku was concerned about this threat, Shinki was determined to avenge Kankurō.

Quickly, the puppet proved itself to be very versatile and dangerous as is easily breached Shinki's defenses and injected him with a paralysing poison. Shinki was forced to retreat with Shukaku, but was saved by the timely arrival of Boruto, Shikadai and Temari. While Shikadai and Temari held off the puppet, Boruto helped Shinki and Shukaku to safety. There, Boruto apologised to Shinki for calling him uncaring about his uncle, but still insisted that one should never willingly sacrifice an ally, proving his belief by returning to help Shikadai and Temari. Shukaku found the whole event very exciting, viewing Boruto to be similar yet smarter and more skilled than his father was at the time. It decided to watch the fight, but Shinki quickly picked up the tanuki and resumed his mission to Konoha.

After Boruto caught up with Shukaku and Shinki, they were soon intercepted by Urashiki. While the genin knew they were no match for him, they were able to pull off a strategy to again temporarily [seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Secret_Technique:_Black_Iron "Black Secret Technique: Black Iron") him. Knowing Urashiki would soon escape and intercept them again at the border to the Land of Fire, they decided Boruto would distract Urashiki with a clone disguised as Shinki and carrying an empty tea kettle while Shukaku and Shinki went on ahead. During the plan, Shukaku noticed how hesitant he was with this plan that had a high chance with death for Boruto.

Once arriving at the border of the Land of Fire, Shukaku and Shinki were intercepted by [Mirai Sarutobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mirai_Sarutobi "Mirai Sarutobi") who interrogated Shinki for unsanctioned entrance. While Shinki refused to answer questions to a shinobi not above chūnin, [Sai Yamanaka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sai_Yamanaka "Sai Yamanaka") arrived, whose high-ranking prompted Shinki to explain the situation. Sai took over delivering Shukaku to Naruto. Once arriving at the Hokage's office, Shukaku explained the situation for Boruto. Before the Hokage left, Sasuke arrived to reveal that he already rescued Boruto and Urashaki had escaped. They decided to keep Shukaku at Naruto's home for protection.

![Himawari playing with Shukaku.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/a/ad/Himawari_and_Shukaku.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20190929214716)

Himawari playing with Shukaku.

Upon arriving to meet Boruto's family, [Himawari](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Himawari "Himawari") immediately took a liking the miniaturised tailed beast, treating the tanuki as a talking Shukaku toy and kept it close to her. While Shukaku found the girl's attachment annoying, it was intrigued to learn that she once knocked out Kurama. Hoping to learn how she did as a chance to have some mocking rights on the fox, Shukaku decided to play nice with the young girl. Ultimately however, when learning that Himawari had no knowledge of the incident, Shukaku quickly turned cold again and told her to leave it alone, much to her sadness. While alone and taking a nap, its tea kettle form was mistaken for trash and taken to a scrapyard. As Shukaku was on the verge of being throwing into a trash compactor, Himawari, who was desperately looking for the tanuki, activated her [Byakugan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Byakugan "Byakugan") again and quickly saved Shukaku. Amazed at how genuinely concerned Himawari was for it, Shukaku became kinder to her and openly admitted to Kurama that humans can be very entertaining. Later, when Naruto proceeded to move Shukaku to a new safehouse, the tanuki waved Boruto and Himawari goodbye with its tail.

## Trivia

- A [tanuki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanuki "wikipedia:Tanuki") (狸, *literally meaning:* raccoon dog) is a popular animal [yōkai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Y%C5%8Dkai "wikipedia:Yōkai") in Japanese folklore and is famous for its mischievous nature and abilities to shape-shift. They are also said to be rivals to the [kitsune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/kitsune "wikipedia:kitsune"), which created an old Japanese proverb: "A fox and a tanuki matching their cleverness" (狐と狸の化かし合い, *kitsune to tanuki no bakashi ai*), meaning two sly characters trying to outsmart one another. Shukaku being sealed inside a [tea kettle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kama_\(Japanese_tea_ceremony\) "wikipedia:Kama (Japanese tea ceremony)") is a reference to one Japanese tale called *[Bunbuku Chagama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bunbuku_Chagama "wikipedia:Bunbuku Chagama")*. In addition, Shukaku being claimed to be a corrupted Suna priest earlier in the series, has ties to a legend in [Kenchō-ji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kench%C5%8D-ji "wikipedia:Kenchō-ji") of a tanuki disguised as the shrine's priest.
	- Furthermore, "Shukaku" (守鶴), which literally means "protector crane", was also the name of the [tanuki](https://yokai.com/shukaku/) featured in *Bunpuku Chagama*.
- Shukaku is the first tailed beast to have its name revealed in the series. In addition, Shukaku is the only tailed beast that hasn't been shown giving its jinchūriki the characteristic red chakra-shrouds ([Version 1](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Version_1 "Version 1") or [Version 2](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Version_2 "Version 2") forms). This is presumably because Shukaku appeared early in the story as a sand spirit, since Kishimoto had not yet created the idea of multiple tailed beasts and Shukaku being one of them.
- When chapter 629 was first released, Shukaku's image was seen among the tailed beasts, despite not having the chance to give Naruto its chakra like the others, suggesting an error.[^34] In the tankōbon version, it was removed.
