**Kurama** (九喇嘛 クラマ), more commonly known as the **Nine-Tails** (九尾, *Kyūbi*), is one of the nine [tailed beasts](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_beasts "Tailed beasts"). Centuries of being regarded as a mindless monster and sought after as a tool for war caused Kurama to hate humans. After being sealed into [Naruto Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki "Naruto Uzumaki"), Kurama attempts to maintain its cynical perspective about the world, but with Naruto's insistence on treating it with respect, the fox overturns its hatred and willingly strives to use its power for the world's salvation.

## Background

![Kurama and the other young tailed beasts with Hagoromo.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/bb/Young_Bijuu_and_Hagoromo.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150728221849)

Kurama and the other young tailed beasts with Hagoromo.

Kurama and the tailed beasts first came into being in the waning days of [Hagoromo Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hagoromo_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Hagoromo Ōtsutsuki"), who used his [Creation of All Things Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Creation_of_All_Things_Technique "Creation of All Things Technique") to divide the [Ten-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails "Ten-Tails") ' chakra into nine separate bodies and give them each a name. Sometime after being created, the Sage explained to the young tailed beasts that a link existed between them despite their separation. He also foretold of a day when they would be brought together again—though not as they were—and that at that time someone would emerge to show them what true power is.[^6] [^7] In the anime, before his death, Hagoromo sent Kurama to live in a temple built for the purpose of protecting it in a forest region with mountains that would later become part of the [Land of Fire](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Land_of_Fire "Land of Fire").[^8] Over time, Kurama gained a fearsome reputation as a natural disaster that can desolate areas where the darkest aspects of human nature collect and fester.[^9] [^10]

During the time of the founding of the shinobi villages, the [Gold and Silver Brothers](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_and_Silver_Brothers "Gold and Silver Brothers") of [Kumogakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kumogakure "Kumogakure") were assigned to capture Kurama, but they were both swallowed whole by the fox. However, the two brothers survived and gained some of its power by eating the flesh of its stomach for two weeks, forcing Kurama to regurgitate them.[^11] [^12] [^13]

![Kurama and Madara vs. Hashirama.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/6/64/Hashirama_vs_Madara.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150215010118)

Kurama and Madara vs. Hashirama.

After [Madara Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Madara_Uchiha "Madara Uchiha") 's defection from [Konohagakure](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konohagakure "Konohagakure"), he found Kurama and used his [Sharingan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sharingan "Sharingan") to control it, forcing the fox to help him fight against [Hashirama Senju](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hashirama_Senju "Hashirama Senju"), the First [Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage "Hokage"). The epic battle left a colossal scar in the earth that would come to be called the [Valley of the End](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Valley_of_the_End "Valley of the End").[^14] [^15] After his victory over Madara, Hashirama's wife, [Mito Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Mito_Uzumaki "Mito Uzumaki"), sealed the fox within herself, becoming its first [jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki").[^16]

Over the period of time that included the [First](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/First_Shinobi_World_War "First Shinobi World War") and [Second](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Shinobi_World_War "Second Shinobi World War") Shinobi World Wars, Mito kept Kurama at bay except when the seal nearly broke when she was going through childbirth. Towards the final years of Mito's life, it was decided that a new jinchūriki would have to be found, and that was another member of [her clan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Uzumaki_Clan "Uzumaki Clan"), [Kushina Uzumaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kushina_Uzumaki "Kushina Uzumaki"). Although Kushina was terrified when she learned of it, Mito comforted her young successor by telling her that by filling herself with love, Kushina would overcome the fox's hatred.[^16] This was shown years later that despite Kurama's constant attempts to destroy her hope for happiness as its jinchūriki and coerce her to break the seal, Kushina's love for [Minato Namikaze](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Minato_Namikaze "Minato Namikaze") and his love for her were proven to be strong enough to contain the fox.[^17]

![Kurama, extracted from Kushina.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/f/fe/Kurama_extraction.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20120210135747)

Kurama, extracted from Kushina.

Soon after the end of the [Third Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Shinobi_World_War "Third Shinobi World War"), the [Third Hokage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Hokage "Third Hokage") made special arrangements for Minato to ensure that Kurama would remain sealed inside Kushina while she gave birth to [Naruto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto "Naruto") on the night of October 10. However, despite keeping the childbirth a secret, a masked man called [Tobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tobi "Tobi") tracked down their location, killed their escorts, and held Naruto hostage, forcing Minato to quickly rescue him and [teleport](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flying_Thunder_God_Technique "Flying Thunder God Technique") him to safety. Right after Tobi captured Kushina, he [extracted](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tail_Releasing_Method "Tail Releasing Method") and seized control of Kurama and tried to have the fox kill its barely surviving jinchūriki, but Minato made it in time to save her. Soon afterwards, the masked man [summoned](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning_Technique "Summoning Technique") Kurama into Konoha and ordered it to [destroy the village](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tailed_Demon_Fox%27s_Attack "Nine-Tailed Demon Fox's Attack").[^18]

![Kurama attacks Konoha.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/b/b2/Kyuubi_attack_on_Konoha.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230523204403)

Kurama attacks Konoha.

While Kurama was rampaging in the village, Minato fought against Tobi and soon managed to place a [Contract Seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Contract_Seal "Contract Seal") on him, which freed the fox from his control. Despite that, Kurama still harboured resentment against Konoha for sealing it away and continued decimating the [shinobi](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinobi "Shinobi") forces that tried to repel it. As it prepared a [Tailed Beast Ball](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Ball "Tailed Beast Ball"), Minato [summoned](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Food_Cart_Destroyer_Technique "Summoning: Food Cart Destroyer Technique") [Gamabunta](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gamabunta "Gamabunta") on top of the fox and quickly teleported it to Kushina and Naruto's location, letting its Tailed Beast Ball detonate away from the village. Already dying from the extraction, Kushina used her remaining strength to [restrain](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Adamantine_Sealing_Chains "Adamantine Sealing Chains") Kurama, planning to seal the fox back inside her so it would die with her. However, knowing that Kurama would revive without a host and how great a threat Tobi was, Minato instead came up with a plan to give his son the means to combat the masked man in the future.[^19]

![Kurama's Yang half, moments after being sealed within Naruto.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/9a/Nine-Tails_ep.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130830000552)

Kurama's Yang half, moments after being sealed within Naruto.

Because it was impossible for Minato to seal Kurama entirely on his own, he first used the [Dead Demon Consuming Seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Dead_Demon_Consuming_Seal "Dead Demon Consuming Seal") to separate and seal the fox's [Yin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yin "Yin") half within himself and then prepared the [Eight Trigrams Seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight_Trigrams_Seal "Eight Trigrams Seal") to imprison the [Yang](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yang "Yang") half within Naruto.[^20] [^21] Realising what they were planning, Kurama attempted to kill Naruto, but both of the parents used themselves as shields and completed the sealing.[^22] Although greatly frustrated of being sealed again, Kurama decided to wait for the time when it could influence Naruto to use its power to break the seal.[^23] Throughout the years, Kurama weakened the seal through the cracks of its formation by constantly sending out small portions of its chakra, which had positive side-effects on Naruto, such as amplifying his naturally large chakra reserves and healing ability, being able to heal from minor wounds within seconds to major injuries within a day.

In the anime, it was revealed that Kurama had left behind large amounts of its chakra during the attack. [Kazuma](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kazuma "Kazuma"), a former member of the [Twelve Guardian Ninja](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Twelve_Guardian_Ninja "Twelve Guardian Ninja"), collected and sealed the chakra within his son, [Sora](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sora "Sora"), hoping to make use of its power for his own agenda.[^24]

## Personality

Kurama is characterised as a snarky and shrewd individual. It uses "washi" (ワシ) when referring to itself, which is generally used by older men. Kurama expresses great pride in its power, believing itself to be the strongest of the tailed beasts due to its belief that their strength is determined by their number of tails, which earned Kurama nothing but disapproval from its brethren, especially [Shukaku](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shukaku "Shukaku").[^25] The fox would also react out of jealousy whenever Naruto was not using its power.[^26] Conversely, Kurama openly acknowledged the fact that it alone doesn't stand a chance against the Ten-Tails,[^27] and from its past experience with Madara, the fox knew full well he was not an opponent to be underestimated, even when he was seemingly pushed into a corner.[^28] Kurama holds great respect to [Hagoromo Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hagoromo_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Hagoromo Ōtsutsuki"); during the tailed beasts' youth, the fox shed tears after the Sage had imparted his final words to them.[^7] Kurama's manner of speech is often harsh, but deep down it has a supportive disposition, an ability to express concern for its allies and give some words of encouragement.[^29] [^30] In addition, thanks to its exploits during the [Fourth Shinobi World War](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War") and working alongside Naruto, Kurama's peaceful existence expanded to the [Five Great Shinobi Countries](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Great_Shinobi_Countries "Five Great Shinobi Countries"), as it would talk directly to the other [Kage](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kage "Kage") and assist over important matters.[^31]

Kurama also has a comical side; the fox brazenly teased Naruto about his first kiss with Sasuke,[^7] argued with him about each other's stubbornness,[^32] and nervously lashed out at a joyful Naruto after the tailed beasts were freed from [Kaguya Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kaguya_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Kaguya Ōtsutsuki") while flipping him off.[^33] In the anime, Kurama came to fear [Himawari](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Himawari "Himawari") after being incapacitated by her [Gentle Fist](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gentle_Fist "Gentle Fist") attack during an incident where Naruto had to shield [Boruto](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Boruto "Boruto") from Himawari, who attacked him in anger for damaging her panda plushy.[^34]

![The change in dynamics between Naruto and Kurama.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/8f/Naruto_and_Kurama_fist_bump.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20130905155834)

The change in dynamics between Naruto and Kurama.

Due to centuries of being perceived as a terrifying monster or a powerful tool of war that must either be sealed or weaponised, Kurama developed intense hostility and distrust towards humans, even going as far as proclaiming itself to be the living embodiment of hatred.[^35] Ever since its sealing within Naruto, Kurama plotted to use his dependence on its power to break free, becoming an ever-looming threat to both Naruto and the people he cared about. Even after Naruto managed to best Kurama in combat,[^36] the fox tried to instill doubt in him about his ability to deal with the hatred in both the [war](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Shinobi_World_War "Fourth Shinobi World War") and Sasuke, but Naruto was determined to find a way and hoped to resolve Kurama's hatred someday.[^37] Despite reproaching him for making such a claim, Kurama was visibly shocked by the gesture, later choosing to assist Naruto against the [reincarnated](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning:_Impure_World_Reincarnation "Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation") Madara and allowing him to utilise the [Nine-Tails Chakra Mode](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tails_Chakra_Mode "Nine-Tails Chakra Mode") without consequence during the early stages of the war. Soon, after listening to Naruto's conversation with [Son Gokū](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Son_Gok%C5%AB "Son Gokū") about his sincere desire to save the tailed beasts and become friends with Kurama,[^38] the fox, who witnessed all of the hardships and deeds Naruto had in his life, decided to stand by and see if he could truly aid its brethren.[^39] When Naruto successfully freed Son Gokū from Obito's control, Kurama finally became convinced of Naruto's word and, in return, extended its hand to him as a form of partnership, to which Naruto then removed the [seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Torii_Seal "Torii Seal") that restrained it and happily acknowledged the fox as his teammate and a member of Konoha.[^40]

Ultimately, Kurama [formed a strong bond](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Uzumaki%27s_Relationships#Kurama "Naruto Uzumaki's Relationships") with Naruto; the fox proudly declared itself his friend and admitted that it had the utmost faith in Naruto's ability to carry on the legacy Minato and [Jiraiya](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jiraiya "Jiraiya") had left him, therefore abandoning its grudge against the Fourth Hokage for sealing it.[^41] Naruto trusted Kurama enough to allow the fox to [control](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Control "Tailed Beast Control") his body and switch back without conflict.[^42] When Naruto became notably distraught as [Neji Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Neji_Hy%C5%ABga "Neji Hyūga") died in his arms, Kurama fiercely reaffirmed Neji's sacrifice, reminding Naruto of his parents' similar sacrifices for him and how they shouldn't be in vain.[^43] Later, despite being free from its seal, Kurama didn't turn down Hagoromo's request to stay with Naruto.[^44] Kurama even went as far as to sacrifice its own life for Naruto, as the fox intentionally hid the actual risk of using [Baryon Mode](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Baryon_Mode "Baryon Mode") from him because it knew he would oppose it if he knew the truth.[^45]

Kurama even came to care for Naruto's family, finding them entertaining to be around, further indicating that the fox saw it as its responsibility to look after them akin to being a parent.[^46] Three years after its demise and being reborn within Himawari, Kurama displayed a rather mentoring nature towards the young girl, calmly explaining the situation and offering advice. Likewise, the fox appreciated Himawari's acceptance and respect towards it.

Kurama is a red-orange coloured [kitsune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/kitsune "wikipedia:kitsune") with black fur around its red eyes that stretch up to its ears, and nine long swiping tails. It also has an upper-body structure of a human, complete with opposable thumbs on its clawed hands.

During the last remaining days of Hagoromo, Kurama in its youth was much smaller in size, yet still towering over an adult, with leaner and softer while less-defined in shape with a rounder face, akin to a fox cub.[^7] Over time, Kurama's size grew to roughly equaling the [Hokage Rock](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hokage_Rock "Hokage Rock"), and [Gamabunta](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gamabunta "Gamabunta"), the largest of the Mount Myōboku's toads, was only the size of Kurama's torso. Its pupil alone was bigger than a tall human.[^47] When Minato separated Kurama's [Yin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yin "Yin") chakra and [Yang](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Yang "Yang") chakra, it was split into two entities, both of which were shrunken more than half its original size, yet still roughly the size of other massive tailed beasts.[^22] [^48] While the two were basically identical to the other, Yang-Kurama retained its original colouring while Yin-Kurama was darker coloured.[^49] [^50]

While manifesting its [Nine-Tails Chakra Mode](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tails_Chakra_Mode "Nine-Tails Chakra Mode"), the fox's entire body took on a transparent golden colour, its whiskers were much thicker, and black circular markings appeared on its shoulders, stomach, as well as the front and back of its paws. Black lines also ran the length of its spine, arms, legs and tails. Additionally, while Naruto is in [Sage Mode](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_Mode "Sage Mode"), Kurama gained similar thick dark patches circling around its eyes.[^51] Upon gaining Naruto's [Six Paths Chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra "Chakra"), Kurama's [Tailed Beast Mode](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Mode "Tailed Beast Mode") avatar grew to tremendous proportions, roughly the same size as [Sasuke Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sasuke_Uchiha "Sasuke Uchiha") 's Six Paths Complete Body — [Susanoo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Susanoo "Susanoo"), with its tails becoming bushier.[^52]

After both halves of Kurama were reunited, the fox grew even larger than ever before, to the point it was towering over a stump of a [God Tree](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/God_Tree "God Tree").

Three years after its demise from over-usage of the [Baryon Mode](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Baryon_Mode "Baryon Mode"), Kurama reformed as a cub like it did when first created by Hagoromo, albeit much smaller, roughly the size of a large pet dog, with very long tails.

### Chakra and Physical Prowess

![Kurama's chakra is enough to power up an entire shinobi army.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/8a/Naruto_powered_the_Alliance_up.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20140612131231)

Kurama's chakra is enough to power up an entire shinobi army.

Kurama possesses vast and powerful [chakra](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra "Chakra"), the most of the nine tailed beasts.[^56] It could be [passed on](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Chakra_Transfer_Technique "Chakra Transfer Technique") to the entire [Allied Shinobi Forces](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Allied_Shinobi_Forces "Allied Shinobi Forces"), be felt countries away by non-sensors,[^57] and was required to be sealed last into the [Demonic Statue of the Outer Path](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Demonic_Statue_of_the_Outer_Path "Demonic Statue of the Outer Path") to avoid overloading it.[^58] It can also accumulate a huge amount of chakra shortly after it has used up most of its own. Due to Naruto's compatibility with Kurama's chakra, any chakra it possessed is limited for its jinchūriki alone, unless it was moulded to fit the recipient's unique chakra signature.[^59]

When used by others, the chakra became dense enough to be seen as a visible shroud that can further protect the wearer. Using its chakra, Kurama could shroud itself within its [Nine-Tails Chakra Mode](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tails_Chakra_Mode "Nine-Tails Chakra Mode") to increase its power. As a last-ditch ability, Kurama could enter [Baryon Mode](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Baryon_Mode "Baryon Mode"). In this state, similar to [nuclear fusion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_fusion "wikipedia:Nuclear fusion"), Kurama's chakra was consumed as raw materials to produce a new type of energy. When in use, it dramatically boosted Kurama's reflexes, raw might, and chakra to extreme levels. Because all chakra is connected, the same power that is produced from bits of their lives also works to reduce the lifespan of opponents on contact. Due to its extreme energy consumption, Kurama must remain focused and not waste energy of extraneous thoughts and actions, to maximise the amount of time Baryon Mode can be maintained.[^60] As a tailed beast, Kurama has powerful healing capacities, able to mend injuries in itself and others in seconds.[^61] Likewise as such, it is effectively immortal. When it dies, it will eventually revive, albeit in a younger form.

Kurama has a great deal of brute force, reputedly able to raise [tsunamis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/tsunami "wikipedia:tsunami") and flatten mountains with just a single swipe of a tail.[^62] Even with half its strength, it could overpower five of Obito's controlled tailed beasts,[^55] smash Madara's [senjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Senjutsu "Senjutsu") -enhanced [Susanoo](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Susanoo "Susanoo") with a tail,[^63] and with the Six Paths Chakra, battle Sasuke's Six Paths Complete Body — Susanoo to a standstill.[^64] Kurama is deceptively fast, able to track Naruto in Sage Mode.[^65] The fox is also very durable, enough to withstand [several senjutsu-enhanced attacks](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_Art:_Many_Ultra-Big_Ball_Spiralling_Serial_Spheres "Sage Art: Many Ultra-Big Ball Spiralling Serial Spheres"),[^66] and block a Tailed Beast Ball from the [Ten-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails "Ten-Tails") ' initial form.[^67]

### Ninjutsu

![Kurama supercharges a Tailed Beast Ball even on low chakra.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/8/88/Kurama_forming_Bijuudama.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150829213430)

Kurama supercharges a Tailed Beast Ball even on low chakra.

As a tailed beast, Kurama can create a [Tailed Beast Ball](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Ball "Tailed Beast Ball"), and fire it either as a [beam](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tailed_Beast_Heavy_Cannon "Tailed Beast Heavy Cannon") or [rapid blast of spheres](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Continuous_Tailed_Beast_Balls "Continuous Tailed Beast Balls"). Kurama can also supercharge it, creating one many times its own size for much greater power, even on wisps of chakra. One of these was powerful enough to counter a collaborated Tailed Beast Ball from five tailed beasts.[^55] [^7] At full power, Kurama's Tailed Beast Ball could blast [Toneri Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Toneri_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Toneri Ōtsutsuki") 's [giant golem](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hamura_%C5%8Ctsutsuki_Golem "Hamura Ōtsutsuki Golem") through the [moon](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Moon "Moon") and into orbit.[^68] With unity between the jinchūriki and itself, Kurama can manifest itself outside for a period of time, allowing the two to fight independently and away from each other.

![Kurama accumulating natural energy.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/0/06/Kurama_Accumulating_Energy.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20160930065149)

Kurama accumulating natural energy.

Kurama can unleash [destructive roars](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Roar "Great Roar"), capable of dispersing a [massive Rasengan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ultra-Big_Ball_Rasengan#Senjutsu_Influence "Ultra-Big Ball Rasengan"), knocking back five tailed beasts at once, and destroying entire city blocks.[^69] [^55] Even when limited through Naruto's Version 1 and 2 states, they were strong enough to destroy an entire bridge and repel Pain's [Shinra Tensei](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Shinra_Tensei "Shinra Tensei") respectively.[^70] [^71] Kurama also has adept [sensor](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sensor "Sensor") skills, able to detect chakra from great distances and uniquely able to [sense negative emotions](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Negative_Emotions_Sensing "Negative Emotions Sensing"),[^72] as well as [natural energy](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Natural_energy "Natural energy"), which it could accumulate and transfer to Naruto to create [senjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Senjutsu "Senjutsu") chakra.[^73] In the anime, it is shown using [Wind](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wind_Release "Wind Release") and [Fire Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Fire_Release "Fire Release") [nature transformations](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nature_transformation "Nature transformation"), being able to generate [twisters](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tails_Twister "Nine-Tails Twister") and breathe fire.[^74]

### Intelligence

Kurama is very cunning and could formulate strategies in the midst of battle. Through Naruto's body, the fox tricked Obito into using [Kamui](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kamui "Kamui") to absorb Kakashi into [Kamui's dimension](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kamui%27s_dimension "Kamui's dimension"), only for Obito to become vulnerable to Kakashi's attacks as he phased through Naruto's punch.[^75] Kurama has also shown remarkable leadership skills, being able to calmly and effectively instruct its allies on how best to engage the Ten-Tails.[^76] In the anime, it has enough knowledge of [fūinjutsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/F%C5%ABinjutsu "Fūinjutsu") to determine the natures and weaknesses of the [Eight Trigrams Seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Eight_Trigrams_Seal "Eight Trigrams Seal") that bound it inside Naruto.[^23] In addition, Kurama has an advanced understanding of nuclear physics, particularly the nuclear fusion that occurs within the Sun.[^60]

Naruto's first use of the Nine-Tails' chakra.

### Chūnin Exams

Main article: [Chūnin Exams](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ch%C5%ABnin_Exams_\(Arc\) "Chūnin Exams (Arc)") Within the [Forest of Death](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Forest_of_Death "Forest of Death"), [Team 7](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_7 "Team 7") was ambushed by a disguised [Orochimaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Orochimaru "Orochimaru") and his large [snake](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Snake "Snake") summon. Realising Naruto was the Nine-Tails' [jinchūriki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jinch%C5%ABriki "Jinchūriki"), Orochimaru countered by using the [Five Elements Seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Elements_Seal "Five Elements Seal") on him, which disrupted the flow of both the fox's and Naruto's chakra. Despite the impairment, Naruto was able to beat [Kiba Inuzuka](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kiba_Inuzuka "Kiba Inuzuka") and [Akamaru](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akamaru "Akamaru") during the preliminary rounds.

Later, during his training to prepare for the finals, Naruto encountered [Jiraiya](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jiraiya "Jiraiya"), whom the Nine-Tails recognised as the Fourth Hokage's teacher.[^23] After Jiraiya deduced who Naruto was and believed it was time for Naruto to learn how to take advantage of the Nine-Tails' power, he [removed](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Five_Elements_Unseal "Five Elements Unseal") Orochimaru's seal and offered to teach Naruto the [Summoning Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Summoning_Technique "Summoning Technique").

![The Nine-Tails meets Naruto.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/9/99/Naruto_meets_Kurama.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20150222031656)

The Nine-Tails meets Naruto.

As the month of training was nearing its end, Naruto was still struggling to summon large [toads](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Toads "Toads"). In order to accelerate Naruto's slow progress, Jiraiya, without warning, pushed him off a cliff. In his act of desperation, Naruto, for the first time, abruptly entered his subconscious and encountered the Nine-Tails, who showed no hesitation in expressing both its hostility and desire to devour Naruto. Despite initially being terrified, Naruto boldly demanded that the Nine-Tails give him its chakra as a form of "rent money" for living in his body, since his death would also result in the fox's own. Realising this, the amused Nine-Tails complied, giving Naruto enough chakra to summon [Gamabunta](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Gamabunta "Gamabunta").

With Naruto finally becoming aware of its presence, the scheming Nine-Tails was convinced that it could influence him even more to use its power.[^23]

At the time of the finals, the first match was Naruto facing [Neji Hyūga](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Neji_Hy%C5%ABga "Neji Hyūga"). After Neji sealed his [tenketsu](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tenketsu "Tenketsu") with [Sixty-Four Palms](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sixty-Four_Palms "Sixty-Four Palms"), Naruto called upon the Nine-Tails for aid. Without hesitating, the fox gave Naruto enough chakra to force his tenketsu to open and defeat Neji.

Main article: [Search for Tsunade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Search_for_Tsunade "Search for Tsunade") While on his search for [Tsunade](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Tsunade "Tsunade") with Jiraiya, Naruto was confronted by [Itachi Uchiha](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Itachi_Uchiha "Itachi Uchiha") and [Kisame Hoshigaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kisame_Hoshigaki "Kisame Hoshigaki") of [Akatsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Akatsuki "Akatsuki"). The Nine-Tails' chakra manifests as an image of itself.

Just as Sasuke, enhanced by his [cursed seal](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Cursed_Seal_of_Heaven "Cursed Seal of Heaven"), pierced through Naruto's right shoulder, the Nine-Tails quickly gave Naruto enough chakra to enter his initial jinchūriki form, instantly healing his large wound. ### Time Slip Arc

Main article: [Time Slip Arc](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Time_Slip_Arc "Time Slip Arc") In the anime, [Urashiki Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Urashiki_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Urashiki Ōtsutsuki") decided that the best course of stealing Kurama's chakra was to use [Karasuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Karasuki "Karasuki") to time-travel to the past, and attack Naruto when he was too weak and inexperienced to fight back. He arrived in the time period shortly after Sasuke's defection from Konoha.

When failing to steal past-Naruto's chakra due to the seal on him, Urashiki entered into Naruto's subconscious to forcibly remove it. Once finding Kurama's domain, the fox demanded to know what his intentions were. Shortly after he replied that he was "taking back" its chakra, the angered Kurama used its power to reject Urashiki from Naruto's subconscious and subsequently burned the intruder's hand. This outburst also forced Naruto to enter his Version 1 state. Ultimately, Jiraiya was forced to use an extra seal to subdue Naruto back into normal.

### Kawaki Arc

Main article: [Kawaki Arc](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kawaki_Arc "Kawaki Arc")

![Kurama appearing before Kawaki.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/59/Kurama_%26_Kawaki.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20210525122142)

Kurama appearing before Kawaki.

While Naruto was sleeping, Kurama manifested its head from his body to warn [Kawaki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Kawaki "Kawaki") against wandering off from Naruto. While shocked, Kawaki remained composed enough for Kurama to explain its role in watching over him, and corrected certain misconceptions Kawaki had about Naruto, explaining the various hardships he had to overcome. Moved by the story, Kawaki wondered if he could become someone like Naruto, to which Kurama believed that it would be all up to him, before directing him to go to sleep.

![Kurama facing Jigen with Naruto and Sasuke.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/c/c2/Jigen_vs_Shinobi.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20260627042552)

Kurama facing Jigen with Naruto and Sasuke.

Later, using the connection between his and Kawaki's [Kāma](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/K%C4%81ma "Kāma"), [Jigen](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jigen "Jigen") teleported to Naruto's house instantly to bring back Kawaki. When Jigen subdued Naruto with black chakra draining rods, Kurama immediately chastised him for shaming the Hokage title, causing Naruto to playfully retort to the fox as he activated Six Paths Sage Mode. Jigen then soon teleported himself and Naruto to another dimension and attempted to strand him there, but Sasuke arrived and stopped him.

Despite their best efforts, Kurama, Naruto, and Sasuke were swiftly overwhelmed and subdued by Jigen, who was later revealed to be possessed by [Isshiki Ōtsutsuki](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Isshiki_%C5%8Ctsutsuki "Isshiki Ōtsutsuki"). When Isshiki was going to kill Sasuke, Naruto managed to hold Isshiki off with his shadow clones while Sasuke escaped with his [Rinnegan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Rinnegan "Rinnegan"). As Naruto continued to taunt Isshiki, Kurama quickly told him off, stating that from the beginning Isshiki never had any intentions to kill him, but could just easily change his mind and slaughter them both, to which the Ōtsutsuki commended the fox before sealing them away in a giant coffin. The seal was then put under safeguard by [Boro](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Boro "Boro").

Determined to save them, [Team 7](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_7_\(Konohamaru\) "Team 7 (Konohamaru)") and Kawaki later used the power of Kawaki and Boruto's Kāma to enter the dimension. While struggling to fight against Boro, Boruto and Kawaki used their Kāma to create a rift in the seal, freeing the unconscious Naruto. After the team defeated Boro and returned to Konoha with Naruto, they were all taken to the hospital for treatment, with Naruto making a swift recovery.

![Kurama preparing to activate Baryon Mode.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/5/59/Kurama_activates_Baryon_Mode.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20210919194406)

Kurama preparing to activate Baryon Mode.

Later, after Boruto teleported the [resurrected](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/K%C4%81ma "Kāma") Isshiki and himself to a separate dimension, Kurama, Naruto, and Sasuke managed to join Boruto for their second confrontation against Isshiki. However, the Ōtsutsuki once again proved too powerful as he knocked out Sasuke and blocked Naruto's sensory abilities by manifesting giant black cubes, a fact that Kurama noticed. Seeing how determined Naruto was to stop Isshiki, even if it cost him his own life, Kurama suggested using a last resort while [misleading him into thinking it would result in his death](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambiguity#Lexical_ambiguity "wikipedia:Ambiguity"). Naruto agreed to it, declaring that the moment he decided to become Hokage, he knew the risks and was prepared to die. Naruto took on a new form of [Baryon Mode](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Baryon_Mode "Baryon Mode").

Kurama explained to Naruto that the form is like nuclear fusion, consuming all their respective energy and that he must be careful not to make any unnecessary movements or thoughts. The new form baffled everyone with its sheer power. Naruto quickly began overwhelming Isshiki. Soon after, the strain of Baryon Mode began catching up as Naruto began to tire. Kurama noted this form gradually drains all life-forces, including Naruto's. However, it would also drain Isshiki's with each contact, meaning they just had to keep pressuring Isshiki until his already diminished lifespan ran out.

![Kurama's life fades away after a final conversation with Naruto.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/naruto/images/7/7b/Kurama%27s_Life_Fades_Away.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20211003131558)

Kurama's life fades away after a final conversation with Naruto.

After Isshiki's defeat, Kurama spoke with Naruto inside his subconscious for a final conversation as Baryon Mode proved too much to handle. In the manga, Naruto told the kitsune that he had no ill will to Kurama despite killing his parents. In the anime, they reminisce about their history together as Naruto prepared to die. However, Kurama revealed that Naruto would be fine, as it was Kurama's life that was gambled from using Baryon Mode. Kurama revealed the double meaning behind its earlier words and Naruto interpreted it as Baryon Mode resulting in his death rather than Kurama's. Before Kurama's life finally faded away, it warned Naruto that he would lose all access to Kurama's chakra and abilities, meaning he would have to be more careful from now on.

Soon after, the remaining kernels of Kurama's chakra found its way into [Himawari](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Himawari "Himawari").

## New Era: Part II

### Boruto's Return Arc

Main article: [Boruto's Return Arc](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Boruto%27s_Return_Arc "Boruto's Return Arc") Three years after its temporary death, Kurama is revived as a much smaller, younger form. Its presence drew the attention of the Human [God Trees](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/God_Tree "God Tree"), [Jura](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Jura "Jura") and [Hidari](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Hidari "Hidari"), who had mistaken Himawari for her father. As [Team 10](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Team_10_\(Moegi\) "Team 10 (Moegi)") retreated with Himawari from the God Trees, Kurama reached out to her within her mental realm, surprised that incarnations of the [Ten-Tails](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Ten-Tails_\(Kara\) "Ten-Tails (Kara)") were able to sense it.

As Kurama introduced itself, it explained the situation to Himawari. Kurama surmised that its rebirth inside her came from her unique heritage, both being born of two strong bloodlines and inheriting some of its chakra already as the daughter of Kurama's previous jinchūriki. Himawari merely welcomed the fox, impressing Kurama at her maturity and acceptance of the situation. It also revealed to Himawari's compatibility to the fox's power is even greater than her father's was. As Himawari regained consciousness, Jura caught up to Himawari and forced them to the ground. When [Inojin](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Inojin "Inojin") was impaled by Jura's [Wood Release](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Release "Wood Release"), the sight of her maimed friend enraged Himawari enough to access Kurama's power. Proud of how quickly Himawari adapted, it encouraged her to fight. Using her newfound abilities, Himawari healed herself and Inojin. She then proceeded to pummel Jura about, until the God Tree decided to take the fight more seriously and swiftly subdued her, prompting Kurama to admit that Himawari could not win. Instead, Boruto arrived to help his sister and [teleported](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Flying_Thunder_God_Technique_\(Boruto\) "Flying Thunder God Technique (Boruto)") them away.

Ultimately, deciding that Himawari needed time to process this news, it called the training for the day done.

- Kurama was the first character shown in the series.
- "Kurama" (九喇嘛) literally means "nine [lama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/lama "wikipedia:lama") ". Kishimoto was mainly inspired to create Kurama based on the [character with the same name](https://yuyuhakusho.fandom.com/wiki/Kurama "w:c:yuyuhakusho:Kurama") from the manga series *[Yū Yū Hakusho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YuYu_Hakusho "wikipedia:YuYu Hakusho")*.[^79] The name may also reference [Mount Kurama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Kurama "wikipedia:Mount Kurama") (鞍馬山, *Kurama-yama*), the sacred mountain said to be the home of [Sōjōbō](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C5%8Dj%C5%8Db%C5%8D "wikipedia:Sōjōbō"), a [tengu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tengu "wikipedia:Tengu") who taught the arts of swordsmanship to [Minamoto no Yoshitsune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minamoto_no_Yoshitsune "wikipedia:Minamoto no Yoshitsune").
	- Furthermore, in the *jōruri* and kabuki play *[Yoshitsune and the Thousand Cherry Trees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoshitsune_Senbon_Zakura "wikipedia:Yoshitsune Senbon Zakura")*, Minamoto no Yoshitsune is accompanied by [Genkurō](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genkur%C5%8D "wikipedia:Genkurō"), a *kitsune* disguising himself as Yoshitsune's retainer, [Satō Tadanobu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sat%C5%8D_Tadanobu "wikipedia:Satō Tadanobu").
- [Kitsune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kitsune "wikipedia:Kitsune") (狐, *fox*) are popular animal [yōkai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Y%C5%8Dkai "wikipedia:Yōkai") in Japanese folklore, and are very well-known to be cunning tricksters with shape-shifting abilities, to which on occasions, use to transform themselves into beautiful women in order to seduce men (possibly the inspiration for the [Sexy Technique](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Sexy_Technique "Sexy Technique"); Naruto even thought Kushina would be Kurama, when they first met). They are also said to be rivals to the [tanuki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanuki "wikipedia:Tanuki"), which created an old Japanese proverb: "A fox and a tanuki matching their cleverness" (狐と狸の化かし合い, *kitsune to tanuki no bakashi ai*), which meant the two sly characters trying to outsmart one another. However, kitsune are known to keep their promises, a trait Naruto has with his [nindō](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Nind%C5%8D "Nindō"). Stories often depict kitsune growing additional tails as they age, which increases their magical power; once a kitsune reaches 1,000 years of age and grows their ninth and final tail, their fur turns gold and becomes a [tenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenko_\(fox\) "wikipedia:Tenko (fox)") (天狐, *celestial fox*), making themselves equivalent to a [kami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/kami "wikipedia:kami").
- In the [manga pilot](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Naruto_Manga_Pilot "Naruto Manga Pilot"), Kurama was actually Naruto's father, who attacked the village of Mt. Oinari and required nine shinobi to defeat him. Hiruzen was the only survivor and had taken pity on Naruto, who had taken human form, and allowed him to live in the village.
- Kurama's death was featured in *Boruto* episode 218, on the 19th anniversary of the debut of the *Naruto* anime, where Kurama debuted.
- Despite being a kitsune in appearance, Kurama has never once been called the Kyubi no Kitsune in the official translation, but is erroneously called that in the English anime and Viz translations.
